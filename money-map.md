# The Money Map

Derived from first principles, not copied advice. 2026-08-15.

---

## 1. The Village (what money IS)

```
A village of 100 people
│
├── Everyone needs: food, water, shelter, health, education...
├── Nobody can do everything alone → they divide the work
├── Money = a RECEIPT for value given to society
│     └── you gave value → society owes you value
└── No value given = no receipts. Ever.
```

**Correction that matters:**

> Money does NOT measure how much you worked.
> It measures how much others VALUE what you supplied.
> 12 hours of unwanted work = 0 receipts.

---

## 2. The Formula (what money you GET)

```
PAY = value of problem solved
    x how few people can solve it   (scarcity)
    x how many times your work is copied (leverage)
```

- Hours appear nowhere in the formula.
- The 12-hr worker earning thousands: small problem, common skill, zero copies.
- The 2-hr owner earning crores: big problem, rare position, work copied endlessly.

```
Village proof:
├── 10 can cook, 1 can heal → 1 heal-hour = 20 cook-hours (scarcity)
└── One cook builds a mill → feeds 100 while sleeping (leverage)
```

## 3. The Stack (WHERE money is earned in 2026)

```
L4  AI          → amplifies technology
L3  Technology  → amplifies business
L2  Business    → serves human needs
L1  Human needs → ground floor. Never changes.

Service flows DOWN. Money flows UP.
```

**Money is earned at the ARROWS, not the layers:**

```
L1→L2  cook food for people        → hotel
L2→L3  build software for hotels   → booking system
L3→L4  add AI to booking systems   → auto-pricing engine
```

- Higher arrow = fewer players = more scarcity = the vertical version of the healer rule.
- Gaps (a business with no tech helping it, tech with no AI helping it) = open doors.
- Warning: money only ENTERS the stack at L1 — a human paying for a need.
  AI that never traces down to a human need collects nothing.

## 4. The Grid (the complete map)

```
          food  health  educ  entert  comm  security  shelter ...
L4-AI      ·      ·      ·      ·      ·      ·        ·
L3-Tech    ·      ·      ·      ·      ·      ·        ·
L2-Biz     ·      ·      ·      ·      ·      ·        ·
L1-Need    ■      ■      ■      ■      ■      ■        ■

~10 domains x 3 arrows = every way money has ever been earned.
```

- Every rich person stands on one dot of this grid.
- Old world: pick one domain, stand at L1→L2 for life.
- 2026: one person can stand at a higher arrow in any domain.
- The domains never changed. The stack grew taller.

## 5. What remains (in order)

```
1. PICK a dot        → domain x layer. A test, not a marriage.
2. BUILD proof there → one finished, visible, showable thing.
3. COLLECT receipts  → smallest possible money, fast.
```

Dot-picking rule for my position: choose for **speed to first receipt**, not maximum size.
