# Chapter 17
## Paise ka rasta — Delhi se gaon tak, aur beech ke haath

---

### Ek nadi, ek naksha

Ab tak humne paisa kai jagah dekha: builder ka paisa (ch.4), chunav ka paisa (ch.8), sanstha ka paisa (ch.11), MPLADS (ch.15), vibhag ka budget (ch.16).

Aaj hum **ek hi nadi** ka poora rasta dekhenge — sarkari paisa. Woh kahan paida hota hai, kis-kis haath se guzarta hai, aur gaon tak pahunchte-pahunchte usme kya hota hai.

Yeh chapter is kitab ka sabse "systems" wala chapter hai. Iske baad har sarkari yojana ki khabar tumhe alag dikhegi.

### Paisa aata kahan se hai

Sabse pehle source. Sarkar ke paas paisa aata hai:

```
CENTRE ka paisa                  STATE ka paisa
- Income tax, corporate tax      - Sharab par tax (excise)
- GST ka centre wala hissa       - GST ka state wala hissa
- Customs                        - Zameen/property par
- (aur udhaar)                     stamp duty aur registration
                                 - Vehicle tax
                                 - (aur udhaar)
```

Ek baat yahan note karo, kyunki woh poori kitab se judti hai: **sharab aur zameen — do cheezein jo state ki kamai ka bada hissa hain — wahi do cheezein hain jinme politics ka sabse gehra lena-dena hai** (ch.8 ki funder list yaad karo: sharab, mining, zameen, builder). Yeh sanyog nahi hai. Jis cheez se sarkar kamati hai, us cheez par sarkar ka control hota hai — aur jis par control hai, wahi politics ko funding karti hai.

### Sawaal 1: har mod par kaun khada hai?

```
MOD                          WAHAN KAUN KHADA HAI

Centre → State               Central mantri, MP ka asar
                             (kaun sa state, kitna, kab)

State budget                 CM aur Finance mantri
                             (kis vibhag ko kitna)

Vibhag → tender              Vibhag ka mantri + officer
                             (kaunsa kaam, kis niyam se)

Tender → thekedar            Officer + local neta
                             (kis thekedar ko)

Zila mein execution          Collector + MLA/MP ki
                             sifarish (kis gaon mein)

Gaon mein kaam               Sarpanch + thekedar
                             (kaise bana, kitna bana)
```

Aur ab woh line jo poore chapter ka nichod hai:

> **Har mod par ek FAISLA hai, aur har faisle ke peeche ek aadmi hai. Paisa upar se neeche seedha nahi girta — woh har mod par ek aadmi se poochkar aage badhta hai.**
>
> **Aur jahan-jahan koi poochta hai, wahan-wahan taakat paida hoti hai.**

Isliye poori kitab mein har neta ka kaam ek jaisa dikhta hai — chahe woh sarpanch ho ya CM. Sab ek hi cheez kar rahe hain: **apne mod par khade hokar yeh tay kar rahe hain ki paisa kis taraf mudega.**

### Sawaal 2: paisa rasta bhar ghisata kyun hai

Ab honest baat. Jab Rs 100 upar se chalta hai, toh gaon tak Rs 100 nahi pahunchta. Kyun?

Do alag wajah hain, aur inko alag samajhna zaroori hai:

**Wajah A — system ka kharcha (yeh chori nahi hai)**

```
- Har level par staff, office, transport
- Survey, design, supervision
- Audit aur paperwork
- Aur der: paisa saal ke aakhir mein aata hai,
  kaam jaldi mein hota hai, quality girti hai
```

Yeh legal kharcha hai. Har bade sangathan mein hota hai.

**Wajah B — leakage (yeh chori hai)**

Yeh reported hai, aur iske teen aam roop hain:

```
1. KAAM KA "KAM" HONA
   Kaagaz par 10 cm sadak, zameen par 6 cm.
   Kaagaz par 100 bori cement, zameen par 70.
   Yeh sabse aam hai kyunki pakadna mushkil hai —
   sadak ke andar kaun dekhega?

2. FARZI KAAM
   Kaagaz par bana, zameen par kuch nahi.
   (Yeh kam hota hai kyunki pakda jaata hai)

3. NAKLI LIST
   Beneficiary list mein aise naam jo hain hi nahi,
   ya jinko milna nahi chahiye tha.
   (Ch.3 yaad karo — sarpanch ke paas LISTS ki
    taakat thi. Yeh wahi taakat hai, bade size mein.)
```

Aur ab sabse zaroori structural baat:

> **Leakage sabse zyada wahan hoti hai jahan (a) cheez naapna mushkil ho, aur (b) jisko milna tha usko pata hi na ho ki usko kya milna tha.**

Isliye sadak aur building mein leakage aasan hai (kaun naapega?), aur seedha khaate mein aane wale paise mein mushkil (aadmi ko pata hai kitna aana tha).

Yehi wajah hai ki pichle kai saalon mein sarkar ne bahut si cheezein **seedha bank khaate mein** bhejni shuru ki. Woh technology ka faisla nahi tha — **woh is leakage-mechanism ka jawab tha.** Beech ke haath hata do, toh beech ka ghisav bhi hat jaata hai.

### Sawaal 3: scheme ki khabar ko kaise padhein

Ab tumhare kaam ki cheez. Jab bhi koi khabar aaye — "sarkar ne 5,000 crore ki yojana ghoshit ki" — toh yeh 5 sawaal poocho:

```
1. YEH PAISA KISKA HAI?
   Centre ka, state ka, ya dono ka (aur kis anupaat mein)?
   Kyunki jiska paisa hai, uska credit hai — aur uska
   control bhi.

2. YEH KIS RASTE SE JAYEGA?
   Vibhag ke through? District ke through? Seedha
   khaate mein? Har rasta alag taakat banata hai.

3. FAISLA KAUN KAREGA — "kis gaon mein"?
   Yeh sabse important sawaal hai. Paisa bada ho
   ya chhota, asli taakat "kahan lagega" ke faisle
   mein hai.

4. KAB AAYEGA?
   Ghoshna aur paisa aane mein aksar mahine-saal
   lagte hain. Chunav se pehle ki ghoshna aur
   chunav ke baad ka paisa — dono alag cheezein hain.

5. NAAP KAUN SAKTA HAI?
   Agar koi naap nahi sakta, toh us paise ka anjaam
   pehle se pata hai.
```

Yeh 5 sawaal poochne ke baad tum har yojana ki khabar ko **structure** ki tarah padhoge, headline ki tarah nahi.

### Ek aur cheez: paisa hamesha kam hi hota hai

Ek baat jo bahar wale kam samajhte hain: **har MLA ke ilaake ki demand hamesha budget se badi hoti hai.**

```
Ek MLA ke ilaake mein:
  50-150 gaon
  Har gaon mein: sadak, naali, paani, school ki
                 marammat, community hall, shamshan,
                 bijli ka transformer

Aur uske paas MLALADS ka chhota fund + vibhag mein
sifarish ka asar.
```

Matlab **woh sabka kaam kabhi nahi kar sakta** — chahe woh kitna hi imaandaar ho.

Aur jab resource kam ho aur demand zyada, toh distribution ka faisla **political** ho jaata hai apne aap. Woh kis gaon mein kaam bhejega? Wahan jahan uska block hai, jahan pichhli baar vote mila, jahan uska mukhiya hai.

> **Yeh corruption nahi hai. Yeh scarcity hai. Aur scarcity apne aap politics paida karti hai — kyunki jab sabko nahi mil sakta, toh "kisko milega" hi asli sawaal ban jaata hai.**

Yeh baat samajh lo toh tum kisi bhi neta se milte waqt ek galti nahi karoge: usse yeh mat poochho ki "aapne yeh kyun nahi kiya." Woh sab nahi kar sakta. Poocho: "aap kis cheez ko pehle rakhte ho, aur kyun."

### Contested corner

**Kya seedha khaate mein paisa bhejna leakage ka hal hai?**

- **Camp A:** Haan, aur yeh sabse bada sudhaar hai. Beech ke haath hat gaye. Jisko milna hai usko seedha milta hai aur usko pata hai kitna milna tha.
- **Camp B:** Aadha hal hai. Cash transfer un cheezon mein kaam karta hai jo paisa hain (pension, subsidy). Lekin sadak, school, aspataal — yeh cheezein cash nahi hain, aur unme purana system hi chalta hai. Aur upar se: list mein naam hona hi asli darwaza hai, aur woh list abhi bhi neeche ke logon ke haath mein hai.

Dono sahi hain. Sabse imaandaar version: **cash transfer ne un jagah leakage kam ki jahan cheez naapi ja sakti thi. Jahan naapna mushkil hai, wahan kuch nahi badla.**

### Baban ki kahani — part 17

Baban 56 ka. Uske gaon Nimgaon mein — jahan se woh 34 saal pehle nikla tha — ek sadak ka kaam mansoor hua. 2.8 kilometre. Uske purane ghar ke saamne se.

Usne pehli baar apni zindagi mein ek kaam ko **poore raste** follow kiya. Sirf jaanne ke liye.

```
Usne dekha:

- Yojana central thi, paisa centre aur state dono ka
- State ke vibhag ne district ko allot kiya
- District mein Executive Engineer ne estimate banaya
- Tender nikla. Teen thekedaar aaye.
  (Baban ne teenon ke naam dekhe. Do ek hi aadmi
   ke the — bhai aur saala. Teesra "cover" ke liye tha.)
- Kaam mila. Kaam shuru hua.
```

Sadak bani. Uske purane padosi ne phone karke kaha: "bahut achhi bani hai."

Aath mahine baad usi padosi ne phir phone kiya: "kinare tootne lage hain."

Baban gaon gaya. Sadak dekhi. Aur usne woh cheez dekhi jo usne 30 saal se sun rakhi thi lekin kabhi apni aankh se nahi dekhi thi.

Sadak ke ek kinare par jahan mitti dhas gayi thi, wahan cross-section dikh raha tha. Uski moti layer kaagaz wali motai se saaf kam thi. Kitni kam, yeh koi engineer hi bata sakta tha. Lekin kam thi.

Baban ne kisi ko shikayat nahi ki. Usne sirf hisaab lagaya, aur register mein likha:

> **Aaj maine paise ko poora rasta chalte dekha. Centre se chala, state se guzra, vibhag se guzra, district se guzra, thekedaar tak aaya, aur zameen par pahuncha.**
>
> **Har mod par kisi ne kuch nahi churaya — bas har mod par kisi ne apna FAISLA liya. Kaunsi yojana, kaunsa zila, kaunsa gaon, kaunsa thekedaar, kaunsa estimate. Aur har faisla thoda sa apne aadmi ki taraf jhuka.**
>
> **Aakhir mein sadak 6 saal ki jagah 3 saal chalegi. Kisi ek aadmi ne bada paap nahi kiya. Sabne chhota-chhota jhukav diya, aur unka jod ek kamzor sadak ban gaya.**

Aur do line neeche, jo poore chapter ka asli sabak thi:

> **Isliye "bhrashtachar" shabd galat hai. Woh ek bade chor ki tasveer banata hai. Asli cheez ek chor nahi hai — asli cheez 8 mod hain, aur har mod par ek aadmi jiske paas thoda sa faisla hai aur bahut sara dabav.**
>
> **Ek chor ko pakadna aasan hai. 8 mod ko theek karna mushkil hai. Isliye yeh cheez 75 saal se chal rahi hai.**

Aur aakhri line, jo usne apne college ke liye likhi:

> **Aur ab main khud us nadi mein khada hoon — mere college ko bhi grant mili hai. Ab mere paas bhi ek mod hai. Dekhte hain main kya karta hoon.**

### Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

**1. Paisa upar se neeche seedha kyun nahi girta, aur usse taakat kahan paida hoti hai?**

> *Jawab:* Har mod par ek faisla hai — kaunsa state, kaunsa vibhag, kaunsa tender, kaunsa thekedaar, kaunsa gaon. Har faisle ke peeche ek aadmi hai. Jahan-jahan koi "poochta" hai, wahan-wahan taakat paida hoti hai. Isliye sarpanch se CM tak sabka kaam ek jaisa dikhta hai.

**2. Leakage sabse zyada kahan hoti hai?**

> *Jawab:* Wahan jahan (a) cheez **naapna mushkil** ho (sadak ki motai, cement ki bori) aur (b) jisko milna tha usko **pata hi na ho** kitna milna tha. Isliye sadak/building mein aasan, aur seedha khaate mein aane wale paise mein mushkil.

**3. Seedha bank khaate mein paisa bhejna kis samasya ka jawab tha?**

> *Jawab:* Beech ke haath hataana. Jab aadmi ko pata hai kitna aana tha aur woh seedha aata hai, toh naapna aasan ho jaata hai. Lekin yeh sirf un cheezon mein kaam karta hai jo paisa hain (pension, subsidy) — sadak/school/aspataal mein purana system hi chalta hai.

**4. Yojana ki khabar padhne ke 5 sawaal kaunse hain?**

> *Jawab:* (1) Paisa kiska hai (centre/state/dono)? (2) Kis raste se jayega? (3) "Kis gaon mein" ka faisla kaun karega? (4) Kab aayega? (5) Naap kaun sakta hai? Paanchwa sawaal anjaam bata deta hai.

**5. "Scarcity apne aap politics paida karti hai" — kaise?**

> *Jawab:* Har MLA ke ilaake ki demand hamesha budget se badi hoti hai — woh sabka kaam kabhi nahi kar sakta, chahe kitna imaandaar ho. Jab sabko nahi mil sakta, toh "kisko milega" hi asli sawaal ban jaata hai — aur woh faisla apne aap political hota hai (apna block, apna mukhiya, apna vote).

### What this means for you

Do cheezein.

**Ek, khabar padhne ke liye:** ab jab bhi koi yojana ki ghoshna dekho, headline mat padho — **rasta** padho. Paisa kiska, kis raste se, kaun tay karega ki kahan lagega, aur naap kaun sakta hai. In 4 jawabon se tumhe pata chal jayega ki woh yojana zameen par kya banegi.

**Do, neta se milne ke liye:** kisi bhi MLA/MP ki sabse badi rozana problem yeh hai ki **uske paas demand se bahut kam resource hai**, aur usko roz tay karna padta hai ki kisko na bole. Woh kaam nahi kar paane se utna pareshan nahi hota jitna is baat se ki **usko yaad nahi rehta kisko kya bola tha, aur kiska number ab aana chahiye.** Yeh uska roz ka dard hai — aur ise samajhne wala aadmi uske liye bahar wale se alag hota hai.
