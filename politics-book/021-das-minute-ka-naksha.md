### Ab method — kisi bhi neta ko 10 minute mein padhna

Chaar sawaal. Isi order mein. Har sawaal ka jawab agla sawaal kholta hai.

#### Sawaal 1: LEVEL — yeh kis danda par hai? (2 minute)

```
sarpanch / corporator  →  chhota base, roz ka sampark,
                          zyada available, ticket ka bhookha

MLA                    →  2-4 lakh voters, 250-350 booth,
                          teen darr, machine + sanstha ka khel

MP                     →  ~18 lakh voters, Delhi ka rishta,
                          ghar par kamzor (roz ka kaam state
                          ke paas hai), apne MLAs ki machine
                          par ladta hai → lehar par depend

mantri / CM            →  FILE + POSTING. Asli taakat yahan
                          hai. Vibhag dekho, naam nahi.
                          CM ki kamzori: ginti
```

**Aur saath mein ek zaroori sub-sawaal: yeh SATTA mein hai ya VIRODH mein?**
Yeh ek jawab uski aadhi haalat bata deta hai (ch.6 ki table).

#### Sawaal 2: POWER SOURCE — iski taakat kahan se aati hai? (3 minute)

Yeh sabse important sawaal hai. Chaar mein se koi ek (ya zyada):

```
A. PARTY KA NISHAAN
   → lehar par jeeta. Ticket kata toh khatam.
   → PARTY ka aadmi hai. Party se darta hai.

B. APNA EHSAAN KA KHAATA (ch.10)
   → 15-25 saal ke chhote kaam. Apna vote hai.

C. SANSTHA (ch.11)  ← sabse pakka
   → college/cooperative/mill. Naukri + admission.
   → haarne par bhi zinda rehta hai.

D. JAATI/SAMUDAY KA NETRITVA
   → block uske saath party badalne par bhi jaata hai
```

**Test:** *"agar iska ticket kat jaye aur yeh nirdaleeya lade, toh kitne vote layega?"*
Kam = party ka aadmi. Bahut = **party uska aadmi**.

#### Sawaal 3: RISK — yeh abhi kis khatre mein hai? (3 minute)

```
Ticket ka khatra?     → uske hi ilaake mein apni party ka
                        koi naya ladka bheed dikha raha hai?
                        (ch.7 — application form yehi hai)

Seat ka khatra?       → pichhla margin kitna tha?
                        machine ka kya haal hai?

Gut ka khatra?        → uska gut party ke andar upar hai
                        ya neeche? (ch.12)
                        Gut neeche = uska phone uthna kam,
                        uske project "review" mein, uske
                        log khiskna shuru

Sanstha ka khatra?    → uski sanstha par jaanch/audit?
                        (yeh sabse gehri chot hai — uska
                         permanent base hil raha hai)
```

**Jitna zyada khatra, utna zyada woh khula hai** — kisi bhi aisi cheez ke liye jo usse bacha sake. Yeh baat business ke liye bhi sach hai aur samajhne ke liye bhi.

#### Sawaal 4: NEED — isko abhi kya chahiye? (2 minute)

Upar ke teen jawab jodo, aur zaroorat apne aap nikal aayegi:

```
Ticket ke khatre mein         → party ko dikhane layak
                                kuch: bheed, data, "mere
                                paas woh hai jo doosron
                                ke paas nahi"

Seat ke khatre mein           → machine ka sach: kaun
                                sach bol raha hai, kaunsa
                                booth khali hai

Gut neeche hai                → upar tak apni awaaz,
                                aur waqt kaatna

Naya/pehli baar               → pehchaan aur ehsaan ka
                                khaata banane ke tareeke

Purana aur surakshit          → izzat, virasat, "legacy"
                                — aur bete ka rasta

Sanstha wala                  → uski sanstha ki brand aur
                                usse nikalne wale ehsaan
```

### Do rules jo hamesha kaam aayenge

**Rule 1 — Har behaviour ke peeche incentive dhoondho, niyat nahi.**

Jab bhi koi political cheez ajeeb lage — koi wafadaar ka ticket kat gaya, koi neta ne party badal di, koi bade neta ne chhoti si baat par itna zor lagaya — yeh mat poochho "yeh aisa kyun kar raha hai, kitna galat hai." Yeh poocho:

> **"Is aadmi ki jagah par khade hokar, iske teen darr aur iske resources ko dekhte hue — yeh faisla rational hai kya?"**

95% baar jawab haan hoga. Aur jab jawab haan hota hai, tab tumhe system samajh aata hai, sirf ghatna nahi.

**Rule 2 — Yaad rakho ki naksha kahan dhundhla hai.**

Kuch cheezein settle nahi hain, aur unpe pakka bolna hi outsider ki nishaani hai:

```
- Kya paisa/sharab sach mein vote khareedta hai?
  (Shayad turnout ka tool hai, persuasion ka nahi)
- Kya "kaam" se vote milta hai?
  (Zaroori base hai, akela kaafi nahi)
- Kya jaanch agencies hathiyaar hain?
  (Sabse garam bahas — lekin DAR ka asar asli hai,
   chahe istemal ho ya na ho)
- Kya karyakarta system kamzor ho raha hai?
  (Dhaancha abhi bhi decisive, lekin uska tareeka badla)
- Kya anti-defection law kaam kar raha hai?
  (Stability aayi, lekin MLA party ka gulaam bhi ban gaya)
```

Veteran ki nishaani yeh nahi hai ki uske paas har sawaal ka jawab hai. **Veteran ki nishaani yeh hai ki usko pata hai kaunse sawaal ka jawab kisi ke paas nahi hai.**

### Baban ki kahani — aakhri part

Baban 58 ka hai.

MLA ka beta jeet gaya tha — do baar. Baban ne dono baar uska campaign chalaya. Teesri baar usne mana kar diya.

Uski credit society ab 9,000 members ki hai. Usne 4 saal pehle ek chhota ITI college shuru kiya — 200 students, 30 staff. Chhota hai. Lekin uske naam par hai.

Us saal ek 24 saal ka ladka uske paas aaya. Padha-likha, sheher se, kuch karna chahta tha politics mein. Usne poocha: "Dada, shuru kahan se karun?"

Baban 20 minute chup raha. Phir usne apna purana register nikala — woh hi jo 15 saal ki umar mein school ki copy tha — aur usme se paanch line padh kar sunayi:

**Ek:** *"Akela vote bekaar hai. Block sab kuch hai. Aur block ki backing bharosa hai — jo mukhiya apna word nahi rakhta, uska block ginti mein nahi aata."*

**Do:** *"Election ke din koi election nahi jeeta jaata. Us din sirf ginti hoti hai us kaam ki jo 5 saal mein hua ya nahi hua."*

**Teen:** *"Politics mein sabse mehngi cheez woh nahi hai jo tum kharch karte ho. Sabse mehngi cheez woh hai jahan tum maujood nahi the."*

**Chaar:** *"Party paisa nahi deti. Party rasta deti hai. Aur jo aadmi rasta le leta hai, woh apne raste se bandha jaata hai."*

Aur phir usne register band kar diya aur aakhri baat bina padhe kahi — kyunki woh use zubani yaad thi:

**Paanch:** *"Aur sabse zaroori — jo kaam tum karo, woh apne NAAM par karo. Main 26 saal duniya ki sabse achhi machine chalata raha, kisi aur ke naam par. Log mera chehra dekhte the aur kisi aur ka naam lete the. Us din mujhe pata chala ki main mehnat kar raha tha, poonji nahi bana raha tha."*

Ladke ne poocha: "Toh aapko afsos hai?"

Baban hansa — pehli baar us poore saal mein.

"Afsos? Nahi. Maine sab kuch seekh liya. Bas 20 saal der se apne liye lagana shuru kiya. Tu meri baat sun raha hai — matlab tere 20 saal bach gaye. Ab ja, aur chhoti si cheez shuru kar. Kuch bhi — 200 logon ka koi kaam. Lekin apne naam par."

Us raat, 43 saal baad, Baban ne us purani copy ke aakhri page par ek aakhri line likhi:

> **Yeh machine kisi ne banayi nahi hai. Yeh apne aap bani hai — kyunki har aadmi apna sahi kaam kar raha tha. Voter apna, karyakarta apna, neta apna, builder apna. Kisi ne sazish nahi ki. Aur isiliye yeh itni majboot hai.**
>
> **Jo aadmi isko "galat" kehkar samajhna chhod deta hai, woh isse kabhi nahi ladh sakta aur na hi isme kaam kar sakta. Aur jo isko samajh leta hai — usko phir yeh chunna padta hai ki isme khada kahan hona hai.**

### Aakhri self-test — poori kitab ka

Yeh 8 sawaal poori kitab ka test hain. Bina peeche dekhe jawab do.

**1. Neta ki job ki shakal kya hai, aur usse kya nikalta hai?**
> Chhoti salary + badi taakat → kamai taakat se hogi, salary se nahi. Yeh corruption ka design hai, niyat nahi.

**2. Neta ka asli customer kaun hai aur kyun?**
> Mukhiya/block-leader. Ek voter = 1 vote (retail), ek mukhiya = 40-500 vote (wholesale). Limited time mein wholesale hi rational hai.

**3. Karyakarta muft kyun kaam karta hai?**
> Umeed, suraksha, pehchaan, dhandha. Aur party ka unlimited zero-cost product — pad/izzat — yehi kharidta hai.

**4. Ticket kis cheez par milta hai?**
> Sirf jeetne ke chance par. Wafadaari ginti mein nahi aati kyunki wafadaar ka vote already party ka hai. Party woh khareedti hai jo abhi uska nahi.

**5. Election ke din ka kaam kya hai?**
> Turnout, persuasion nahi. Apne bane hue vote ko ghar se nikalna. Parchi = tracking, polling agent = aankh, counting = report card.

**6. Neta institution kyun banata hai?**
> Kursi nazuk hai (80% asar, jo sarkar badalte hi khatam). Sanstha taakat ka permanent version hai — naukri, admission, bheed, izzat, zameen — jo haarne par bhi chalti rehti hai.

**7. Dal-badal ka asli kaaran kya hai?**
> Virodhi dal mein machine ka compounding decay. Machine ke bina wapasi namumkin hai, isliye toot survival hai. Aur akela tootne wale ka bhaav aadha — keemat machine mein hai, naam mein nahi.

**8. Dynasty kyun banti hai?**
> Retirement ka koi rasta nahi. 25 saal ki poonji transfer karne ka sirf ek bharosemand raasta hai — khoon. Aur party ko bhi ready machine sasti padti hai.

Agar aathon ka jawab bina peeche dekhe aa gaya — **naksha tumhare dimaag mein hai.** Ab koi bhi political khabar tumhare liye shor nahi rahegi; har baat ka ek khaana hoga.

### What this means for you — aakhri baar

Is kitab ne tumhe do cheezein di hain.

Pehli: **ek naksha**, jisme har nayi political jaankari ka ek khaana hai. Ab jab koi khabar aaye — koi neta toota, koi ticket kata, koi sarkar bani — tumhe pata hoga kahan dekhna hai: uska power source kya tha, uski machine ka kya haal tha, uska gut kahan khada tha.

Doosri, aur zyada zaroori: **ek nazariya.** Yeh machine kisi ne design nahi ki. Woh sirf incentives ka jod hai — har aadmi apna rational kaam kar raha hai, aur unka total ek aisa system banata hai jo kisi ek aadmi ki niyat se nahi chalta.

Aur isi wajah se, jab tum kisi neta ke saamne baithoge, tum us kamre mein ek alag aadmi hoge. Tum us aadmi ko "neta" ki tarah nahi dekhoge. Tum dekhoge: iska level, iska power source, iska abhi ka khatra, aur isliye iski abhi ki zaroorat.

**Aur jab tum kisi ki zaroorat theek se dekh lete ho, tab tumhe usse kuch bechne ki zaroorat nahi padti. Tum sirf usse woh dete ho jo usko waise bhi chahiye tha.**

*Yeh kitab ka aakhri chapter hai. Agar tumne yahan tak padha hai, toh tumhare paas ab woh naksha hai jo shuru mein ek gaon ke jhagde se shuru hua tha aur Delhi tak pahuncha. Isko istemaal karo — khabar padhte waqt, kisi se milte waqt, aur khaas kar tab jab koi tumse kahe ki politics samajhna mushkil hai. Woh mushkil nahi hai. Woh sirf likha hua nahi tha.*
