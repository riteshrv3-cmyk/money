# Chapter 11
## MLA ka business model — college kyun kholte hain

---

### Chapter 1 ka sawaal, ab poora jawab

Sabse pehle sawaal yaad karo: chhoti salary, badi taakat, crores ka kharcha. Chapter 8 mein humne loop dekha — kursi se theke, zameen, permission. Lekin woh sab **ek-baar-ka** paisa hai.

Ab asli sawaal: **ek neta APNI TAAKAT ko sthayi (permanent) kaise banata hai?**

Kyunki taakat nazuk hai. Chapter 6 yaad karo — 80% asar hai, adhikaar nahi. Aur asar us din khatam ho jaata hai jis din:
- sarkar chali gayi, ya
- ticket kat gaya, ya
- seat haar gaye

Toh ek samajhdar neta kya karega? Woh apni taakat ko kisi aisi cheez mein **convert** karega jo kursi ke jaane par bhi na jaye.

Us cheez ka naam hai **institution** — sanstha.

Aur yehi wajah hai ki tumhare MLA ke paas ek college hai.

### Sabse pehle: institution kaam kaise karta hai

Ek shikshan sanstha (education trust) lo. Bahar se: seva. Andar se, **political engine** ki tarah dekho:

```
EK COLLEGE / SANSTHA MLA KO KYA DETA HAI:

1. NAUKRI  (sabse bada — machine ka fuel)
   Ek college mein: professors, clerk, peon, driver,
   security, safai, canteen, hostel warden.
   Ek achhe size ki sanstha mein 100-500 naukriyaan.

   Ab chapter 5 wapas dekho. Karyakarta ko chahiye tha
   umeed, suraksha, pehchaan. Naukri teenon ek saath
   deti hai — permanent, uske BETE ke liye.

   Aur ek naukri ek parivaar ko 20 saal ke liye
   bandh deti hai. Yeh sabse sasti aur sabse pakki
   loyalty hai jo paisa khareed sakta hai.

2. ADMISSION  (ehsaan ka kaarkhana)
   Har saal seats. Har seat ek parivaar ka ehsaan.
   Aur admission ka season chunav se pehle bhi aata hai.
   Chapter 10 wala ehsaan ka khaata — yeh uski factory hai.

3. BHEED  (machine ka raw material)
   Students, staff, unke parivaar. Rally mein bheed
   chahiye? Bus bhej do. Yeh bheed kiraye ki nahi hai —
   yeh "apni" hai, aur sasti hai.

4. IZZAT  (sabse safed hissa)
   "Sanstha ke adhyaksh" hona MLA hone se zyada
   respectable hai. Woh shiksha ka seva-bhaavi aadmi
   ban jaata hai, neta nahi.

5. PAISA  (legal aur grey, dono)
   Fees, grant, aur — yeh reported hai aur bar-bar
   ilzaam laga hai, khaas kar professional colleges mein —
   donation/capitation. Iske alawa building ke theke,
   supply, transport — sab apne logon ko.

6. ZAMEEN  (sabse chhupa hua, sabse bada)
   Shikshan sansthaon ko aksar sasti/riyayati sarkari
   zameen milti hai, ya kheti ki zameen ko sanstha
   ke naam par convert karwana aasan ho jaata hai.
   Aur zameen — chapter 8 yaad hai — sabse bada
   long-term return hai.
```

Ab in 6 ko ek saath dekho. **Ek college ek machine hai jo naukri, ehsaan, bheed, izzat, paisa aur zameen — ek saath banati hai. Aur woh chunav haarne par band nahi hoti.**

Yeh sirf paisa kamane ka zariya nahi hai. **Yeh taakat ka permanent version hai.**

### Isliye teen dhandhe baar-baar milte hain

Neta log har dhandhe mein nahi jaate. Woh un teen-chaar mein jaate hain jinme yeh property hoti hai:

```
DHANDHA              KYUN NETA LOG ISME HOTE HAIN

Shikshan sanstha     naukri + admission + bheed + izzat
(school/college)     + sasti zameen

Cooperative bank /   gaon ke har kisaan ka isse kaam
credit society       padta hai. Loan kisko mile, kiska
                     maaf ho — yeh taakat rozana chalti hai.
                     Aur iske chunav hote hain — matlab
                     ek aur election jeetne ka mauka,
                     chhota lekin pakka.

Sugar mill /         (Maharashtra, UP, Karnataka jaise
cooperative          ilaakon mein) Hazaaron kisaan
                     supplier hain. Kab bhugtan hoga,
                     kiska pehle — yeh taakat hai.
                     Aur mill ke chunav se poora
                     political base ban jaata hai.

                     sabse emotional roop

Dairy / mandi /      roz ka lena-dena, roz ka rishta
```

In sabme ek common cheez dhoondho. Sab mein **bahut saare aam logon ka rozmarra ka lena-dena** hai. Yeh IT company ya factory nahi hai jahan 50 log kaam karte hain aur baaki duniya ko farak nahi padta.

**Neta ka business woh hota hai jahan zyada se zyada logon ka roz ka kaam usse hokar guzre.** Kyunki uska product paisa nahi hai — uska product rishta hai.

### Cooperative — India ka sabse chhupa hua political school

Ek section iske liye alag se, kyunki bahar wale isko sabse kam samajhte hain.

Cooperative society (sahakari sanstha) — bank, sugar mill, dairy, credit society — ek aisi sanstha hai jiske **apne chunav hote hain**, jiske apne members hote hain, aur jiske paas asli paisa hota hai.

Ab dekho ek naujawan neta ke liye yeh kya hai:

```
1. Yeh ek CHHOTI VIDHAN SABHA hai
   Yahan woh chunav ladna seekhta hai — blocks banana,
   vote ginna, panel banana — lekin risk kam hai
   aur paisa kam lagta hai.

2. Yeh ek READY-MADE BLOCK hai
   Society ke 5,000 members = 5,000 parivaar jinka
   isse kaam padta hai. Jo society chalata hai, uske
   paas woh 5,000 pehle se hain.

3. Yeh PAISE ka rasta hai
   Loan, khareed, bhugtan, theke — sab andar.

4. Yeh SEEDHI ka danda hai
   Society ka chairman → zila bank ka director →
   MLA ka ticket. Yeh raasta itna common hai ki
   kuch ilaakon mein isko "normal career path"
   samjha jaata hai.
```

Isliye jab tum kisi MLA ki biography padho aur usme likha ho "sahakari sanstha ke adhyaksh rahe" — woh ek chhoti line nahi hai. **Woh uski poori taakat ki jad hai.**

### Zameen — sabse bada, sabse chhupa

Ab sabse honest section. Politics ka sabse bada wealth-creator theke nahi hain. **Zameen hai.**

Wajah simple hai — MLA ke paas ek cheez hoti hai jo market mein sabse mehngi hai: **pehle se pata hona.**

```
JO USKO PEHLE PATA HOTA HAI:
  - road/highway kahan se nikalegi
  - naya industrial area kahan aayega
  - zone kab badlega (kheti → residential/commercial)
  - koi bada project kahan lagega
  - reservation/acquisition kis zameen par aayega

Aur zameen ka bhaav is ek information par 5x-50x
tak badal sakta hai.
```

Yeh khareed apne naam par ho zaroori nahi — rishtedaar, trust, sanstha, ya kisi partner ke naam par ho sakti hai. Iske ilzaam har party ke netaon par lage hain, jaanch bhi hui hain, aur bahut mamle adaalaton mein hain. *(Yeh reported pattern hai. Main kisi specific aadmi par ilzaam nahi laga raha — main mechanism bata raha hoon, kyunki mechanism samjhe bina system samajh nahi aata.)*

Aur ek structural baat samjho: **isko rokna bahut mushkil hai**, kyunki information leak hone ka koi sabooti nishaan nahi hota. Zameen legally khareedi gayi, paisa cheque se gaya, kaagaz saaf hai. Bas timing "achhi" thi.

Aur yahan poori kitab ka ek bada insight hai:

> **Pehla chunav sabse mehnga hota hai. Har agla chunav sasta hota jaata hai — kyunki tumhari machine ab tumhare institutions se apne aap chalti hai.**

Isliye 3-4 baar ka MLA hataana itna mushkil hota hai. Woh sirf ek MLA nahi hai — woh ek **poora local economy** hai jisme sekdon parivaar ka rozgaar aur hazaaron ka lena-dena juda hua hai.

Aur yehi baat chapter 14 ka darwaza hai: **jab yeh sab ek parivaar ke naam par ho jaata hai, use dynasty kehte hain.**

### Ab tumhare MLA ki taraf

Tumhare MLA ke paas ek college hai. Ab tumhe dikhna chahiye ki woh college kya hai:

```
Woh uska "side business" nahi hai.
Woh uska ASLI business hai, aur MLA-giri usko
chalane ka tareeka hai.

College se usko milta hai:
  - staff aur unke parivaar ka pakka vote
  - har saal admission ka ehsaan
  - rally ki bheed
  - "shiksha samrat" wali izzat
  - aur ek base jo chunav haarne par bhi rehta hai
```

Aur ab ek aur cheez samjho, jo tumhare liye seedha kaam ki hai: **usne apne college mein tumhare career-app ka pilot kyun offer kiya?**

Uske hisaab se woh 3 cheezein khareed raha hai:
1. **Ehsaan ka naya source** — "hamare college ke bachchon ko naukri lagwai."
2. **Izzat/PR** — "modern, AI, placement" wali baat uske sanstha ki brand value badhati hai.
3. **Aur sabse bada** — agar sach mein 50 bachchon ki naukri lagi, toh woh 50 parivaar hain jo uska naam lenge. **Naukri lagwana Indian politics ka sabse taakatwar ehsaan hai.**

Aur usne paisa kyun nahi offer kiya? Kyunki uske model mein **paisa dena zaroori hi nahi hai** — usne tumhe wahi diya jo woh sabko deta hai: mauka aur pahunch. Chapter 5 wapas dekho, jahan corporator ne Baban se kaha tha "paise nahi, **rasta** dunga."

**Tumhare saath bilkul wahi hua jo Baban ke saath hua tha.** Aur ab tumhe pata hai ki uske baad kya hota hai: woh rasta asli hota hai, lekin uski keemat tab hoti hai jab tum uspe apna kuch banao — kisi aur ka nahi.

### Contested corner

**Kya institution rakhna corruption hai?**

- **Camp A:** Haan. Sarkari zameen sasti lena, apne logon ko naukri dena, admission bechna — yeh public resource ka private istemal hai.
- **Camp B:** Nahi. Bahut si sansthaayein sach mein achhi shiksha/ilaaj deti hain, aur un ilaakon mein kuch nahi tha jab tak in netaon ne nahi banaya. Sarkar wahan school nahi khol rahi thi.

Dono mein sach hai, aur yeh alag-alag sanstha par depend karta hai. **Is kitab ka position:** mechanism dono cases mein SAME hai — chahe niyat achhi ho ya buri, ek sanstha politically wahi kaam karti hai (naukri, ehsaan, bheed, izzat). Isliye humein sanstha ko "achhi ya buri" se nahi, **function** se padhna chahiye.

### Baban ki kahani — part 11

Baban 46 ka. Woh ab bhi kisi aur ka aadmi hai.

Us saal uske MLA ne ek engineering college shuru kiya. Trust bana, zameen mili — sarkari, riyayati rate par, "shiksha ke prayojan" ke liye. Building ka theka MLA ke saale ki company ko gaya.

Udghatan ke din 4,000 log the. Do mantri aaye. Akhbaar mein poore page ka photo.

Us raat MLA ne Baban ko bulaya aur kaha: "Ab bata, tujhe kya chahiye?"

Baban ne kaha: "Naukri chahiye. Mere ladke ke liye nahi — mere 40 karyakartaon ke ladkon ke liye. College mein clerk, driver, security, lab assistant. 40 naukri."

MLA hansa: "40 nahi, 22 de sakta hoon."

"22 chalega."

Ghar aakar Baban ne register mein woh line likhi jo uski poori zindagi ki sabse thandi calculation thi:

> **Maine aaj 22 naukri li. Iska matlab 22 parivaar 20 saal ke liye bandh gaye. Aur woh parivaar mere naam se bandhe hain, MLA ke naam se nahi — kyunki naukri MLA ne di, lekin SIFARISH maine ki. Log hamesha usko yaad rakhte hain jisne unka naam liya, usko nahi jisne dastakhat kiya.**

Do saal baad Baban ne apna pehla kaam khud shuru kiya — ek chhota sa credit society, apne mohalle mein. Members: 600. Paisa kam, taakat kam.

MLA ne poocha: "Yeh chhoti si cheez kyun kar raha hai?"

Baban ne kaha: "Sahab, aapka college hai. Meri koi cheez nahi hai. Main 20 saal se aapki machine chala raha hoon. Machine achhi hai — lekin woh aapki hai."

MLA kuch der chup raha. Phir bola: "Samajhdar aadmi hai tu. Isiliye khatarnak bhi hai."

Us raat ki register entry — teen line, aur teeno is chapter ka nichod:

> **1. Har neta ka asli sawaal yeh nahi hai ki "main kitna kama raha hoon." Asli sawaal yeh hai: "kursi chali gayi toh mere paas kya bachega?"**

> **2. Jiske paas sanstha hai, uske paas kursi ke baad bhi machine hai. Jiske paas nahi hai, woh haarte hi khatam ho jaata hai — 6 mahine mein log uska phone uthana band kar dete hain.**

> **3. Aur isliye main aaj 46 saal ki umar mein ek chhoti si society se shuru kar raha hoon. Der ho gayi. Lekin na karne se der behtar hai.**

### Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

**1. Neta institution kyun banata hai — ek line mein asli wajah?**

> *Jawab:* Kursi nazuk hai (5 saal, aur taakat ka 80% sirf "asar" hai jo sarkar badalte hi khatam ho jaata hai). Institution taakat ka **permanent version** hai — woh chunav haarne par bhi naukri, ehsaan, bheed aur izzat deta rehta hai. Yeh shauk nahi, insurance hai.

**2. College se neta ko 6 cheezein kya milti hain?**

> *Jawab:* Naukri (staff = pakki 20-saal loyalty), admission (har saal ehsaan ki factory), bheed (students/staff/parivaar = sasti rally), izzat (shiksha ka seva-bhaavi chehra), paisa (fees/grant/theke, aur reported donation), aur zameen (riyayati sarkari zameen + conversion).

**3. Neta log sirf kuch hi dhandhon mein kyun jaate hain (college, cooperative, mill, hospital)?**

> *Jawab:* Kyunki in sabme **bahut saare aam logon ka rozmarra ka lena-dena** hai. Neta ka product paisa nahi, rishta hai — isliye woh dhandha chahiye jisse zyada se zyada parivaar roz guzarte hon. Ek factory jisme 50 log kaam karte hain, politically bekaar hai.

**4. Zameen sabse bada return kyun hai, aur usko rokna mushkil kyun hai?**

> *Jawab:* Kyunki MLA ke paas **pehle se pata hona** hota hai — road, zone change, project, acquisition. Us information par bhaav 5x-50x badal sakta hai. Rokna mushkil hai kyunki khareed legal hoti hai, paisa cheque se, kaagaz saaf — sirf timing "achhi" hoti hai, aur information leak ka koi nishaan nahi banta.

**5. "Pehla chunav sabse mehnga hota hai" — kyun, aur isse kya explain hota hai?**

> *Jawab:* Kyunki pehli baar machine kharidni padti hai. Baad mein institution woh machine apne aap chalata hai (naukri, ehsaan, bheed free mein aate hain). Isse explain hota hai ki 3-4 baar ka MLA hataana itna mushkil kyun hai — woh sirf neta nahi, ek local economy hai jisse sekdon parivaar ka rozgaar juda hai.

### What this means for you

Kisi bhi neta ko 10 minute mein padhna ho toh yeh mat poochho ki uski party kya hai. Yeh poochho: **"iske paas kaun si sanstha hai?"** Uska jawab batayega uska base kahan hai, uska paisa kahan se aata hai, aur haar jaane par woh khatam hoga ya nahi.

Aur tumhare apne MLA ke liye, seedha: uska college uska business model hai, aur uska pilot offer usi model ka hissa tha — usne tumhe paisa nahi, **rasta** diya, kyunki uske system mein wahi currency hai. Woh galat nahi tha. Bas yaad rakhna ki us raste par tumhe apna kuch banana hoga, warna tum 20 saal baad Baban ki tarah 46 ki umar mein shuru karoge.
