# Chapter 19
## Lehar, media, WhatsApp — jahan naksha dhundhla hai

---

### Yeh chapter alag hai

Ab tak har chapter ne tumhe kuch **bataya**. Yeh chapter tumhe kuch **nahi** batayega — aur wahi iska maqsad hai.

Kyunki ek sawaal aisa hai jispe 30-30 saal ke tajurbewale log ek doosre se ulta bolte hain:

> **Election sach mein jeetaata kya hai?**

Is sawaal ka koi settled jawab nahi hai. Aur jo aadmi ispe pakka jawab deta hai, woh yaa toh kuch bech raha hai, yaa usne kaafi elections nahi dekhe.

Toh is chapter mein main tumhe **teen theories** dunga, teenon ka evidence dunga, aur phir batunga ki practitioners aakhir mein kya karte hain.

**Yeh clarity ka ulta nahi hai. Yeh clarity ka sabse upar ka level hai** — kyunki asli samajh yeh jaanne se aati hai ki kaunsa sawaal khula hai.

---

### Theory 1: ZAMEEN — machine jeetati hai

**Daawa:** Election booth par jeeta jaata hai. Jiske paas 300 booth par apne log hain, jo apna voter ghar se nikaal sakta hai, wahi jeetta hai. Baaki sab shor hai.

**Iske paksh mein:**
- Ch.9 ka ganit: 80,000 pakke samarthak par 60% vs 75% turnout ka antar 12,000 vote hai. Bahut si seats isse kam par tay hoti hain.
- Wahi candidate, wahi party, wahi lehar — lekin do alag seats ke result alag aate hain. Antar zameen ka hota hai.
- Har badi party pichhle dashak mein booth-level sangathan par zyada kharch kar rahi hai, kam nahi. Agar machine bekaar hoti toh yeh ulta hota.

**Iske khilaf:**
- Kuch elections mein poori-poori machine wale log saaf ho gaye. Agar machine hi sab kuch hoti, toh 20 saal ke sangathan wale kabhi na haarte.

---

### Theory 2: LEHAR — hawa jeetati hai

**Daawa:** Log candidate ko nahi, mahaul ko vote dete hain. Jab lehar chalti hai, tab machine, jaati, paisa — sab bekaar ho jaate hain. Log party ka nishaan dekh kar button dabate hain.

**Iske paksh mein:**
- Wave elections mein aise log jeet jaate hain jinka naam bhi seat mein kisi ko pata nahi tha. Unke paas na machine thi, na khaata, na sanstha.
- Aur usi baar bade-bade "unbeatable" neta haar gaye.
- Yeh baar-baar hua hai, alag-alag saalon mein, alag-alag partiyon ke saath.

**Iske khilaf:**
- Lehar har baar nahi aati. Zyadatar elections normal hote hain, aur normal elections mein zameen chalti hai.
- Aur lehar ke andar bhi: kuch seats bach jaati hain. Kaun bachta hai? Aksar wahi jiske paas apna vote hai (ch.14).

---

### Theory 3: MUDDA/KAAM — performance jeetati hai

**Daawa:** Voter ab dekhta hai ki kaam hua ya nahi. Sadak, paani, bijli, ration, gas, ghar — jo sarkar deti hai, uska hisaab hota hai.

**Iske paksh mein:**
- Jin sarkaron ne dikhne wali cheezein di hain (bijli, sadak, seedha khaate mein paisa — ch.17), unhone dobara jeeta hai kai baar.
- Aur incumbency har jagah anti nahi hai — kai sarkarein dobara aayi hain.

**Iske khilaf:**
- Bahut se achha kaam karne wale MLA haar jaate hain aur bahut se nikamme jeet jaate hain (ch.10 ka contested corner).
- Aur "kaam" ka credit aksar upar chala jaata hai — MLA kaam karta hai, credit CM ya PM ko milta hai. Toh local performance ka reward local aadmi ko milega, yeh pakka nahi.

---

### Ab WhatsApp aur digital — naya kya hai

Ab 2026 ki layer. Yahan bhi do camp hain, aur pehle honest structure samajh lo:

**Jo sach mein badla hai:**

```
1. RAFTAAR
   Pehle ek baat gaon tak pahunchne mein din
   lagte the. Ab minute lagte hain.

2. KHARCHA
   Ek video banakar 50,000 logon tak pahunchna
   ab lagbhag muft hai. Pehle iske liye rally,
   gaadi, poster chahiye tha.

3. PARTY KA APNA CHANNEL
   Pehle party ki baat akhbaar/TV se hokar jaati thi.
   Ab party seedha logon ke phone tak pahunch
   sakti hai — beech mein koi nahi.

4. DATA
   Voter list + phone number + local information
   ka jod. Ab yeh naapa aur target kiya ja sakta hai.

5. AUR SABSE ZAROORI — MACHINE KA UPGRADE
   Yeh sabse kam samjha jaata hai. WhatsApp ne
   karyakarta ki jagah nahi li — usne karyakarta ko
   TEZ kar diya. Ab booth pramukh ko har din ki
   line, har din ka message, aur har din ka kaam
   phone par milta hai. Machine wahi hai, uska
   nervous system naya hai.
```

**Jo nahi badla:**

```
1. Vote abhi bhi ghar se nikalna padta hai.
   Koi message aadmi ko booth tak nahi le jaata —
   aadmi le jaata hai (ch.9).

2. Bharosa abhi bhi aadmi se aata hai. Gaon mein
   log message par utna vishwas nahi karte jitna
   us aadmi par jo woh message lekar aaya.

3. Aur sabse badi baat: digital ne machine ko
   REPLACE nahi kiya. Jinke paas machine hai,
   unka digital kaam karta hai. Jinke paas nahi,
   unke liye woh sirf shor hai.
```

**Aur ek naya khatra jo pehle nahi tha:**

Galat khabar (misinformation) ab utni hi tez failti hai jitni sahi. Aur woh ilaake-ilaake mein alag hoti hai — jo baat ek gaon ke group mein chal rahi hai, woh doosre gaon mein nahi. Isliye ab campaign ko woh cheez ladni padti hai jo **dikhti hi nahi.**

### EVM — chhoti, imaandaar baat

Yeh sawaal is chapter mein aana chahiye kyunki log ise "kya jeetaata hai" ke saath jodte hain.

- Machine se voting hoti hai, VVPAT parchi voter ko dikhti hai aur box mein girti hai; kuch booths ki parchiyon ka milaan bhi hota hai.
- Ilzaam lage hain; EC aur adaalaton ne bar-bar iski jaanch ki hai.
- **Is kitab ka position:** yeh ek contested public bahas hai aur main ise settle nahi karunga.

Lekin ek architectural baat pakki hai, jo bahas se alag hai aur ch.9 mein bhi thi:

> **Jis party ke paas booth par apne log nahi hain, woh kisi bhi system mein — kaagaz ya machine — kamzor rahegi. Technology gawaah ki zaroorat khatam nahi karti.**

### Toh practitioner karta kya hai

Ab sabse kaam ki baat. Agar teeno theories mein sach hai, toh zameen par log kya karte hain?

**Woh teeno maante hain, alag-alag anupaat mein:**

```
NORMAL ELECTION (koi badi lehar nahi):
  zameen (machine)  ████████████  sabse bhaari
  mudda/kaam        ██████
  hawa              ████

WAVE ELECTION:
  hawa              ████████████  sab kuch kha jaati hai
  zameen            ██████        (bas bachav karti hai)
  mudda             ████

Aur problem yeh hai: PATA NAHI CHALTA ki yeh
wave election hai ya normal — jab tak result na aaye.
```

Aur isliye har samajhdar candidate ek hi strategy chalata hai:

> **Machine par kaam karo jaise lehar aayegi hi nahi. Aur narrative par kaam karo jaise machine kaafi nahi hogi. Kyunki tumhe pehle se nahi pata ki is baar kaunsa election hai.**

Yeh strategy inefficient hai — dono jagah kharcha hota hai. Lekin yeh **rational** hai, kyunki uncertainty asli hai.

### Contested corner (poora chapter hi contested hai, toh yahan sabse gehra)

**Kya karyakarta ka zamana khatam ho raha hai?**

- **Camp A:** Haan. Naya ladka 5 saal muft kaam nahi karega jab uske paas naukri aur Instagram dono hain. Ab paisa aur digital hi machine hai.
- **Camp B:** Nahi. Jo party booth-level sangathan sabse achha banati hai, wahi jeetti hai — aur yeh pichle dashak mein zyada sach hua hai, kam nahi. Digital ne machine ko badla hai, hataya nahi.

**Beech ka version, aur shayad sabse imaandaar:** karyakarta ka **fuel** badal raha hai. Purana fuel tha "umeed + pehchaan" (ch.5). Naye ladke ke liye woh kam kaam karta hai — usko zyada seedha fayda chahiye. Isliye machine ab pehle se **mehngi** hoti ja rahi hai. Woh khatam nahi ho rahi; uska rate badh raha hai.

### Incentive equation

```
CANDIDATE:
  Nahi jaanta: yeh wave election hai ya normal
  Isliye:      dono par kharch karta hai (machine +
               narrative). Inefficient lekin rational.

PARTY (bade level par):
  Uske paas narrative banane ki taakat hai (national
  campaign, media, digital)
  Isliye:      party lehar par kaam karti hai,
               candidate zameen par — aur dono ek
               doosre ko blame karte hain jab haarte hain

KARYAKARTA:
  Naya fuel maang raha hai (seedha fayda)
  Isliye:      machine mehngi ho rahi hai

CONSULTANT/VENDOR:
  Woh hamesha kehta hai ki uska wala factor decisive
  hai (data wala data kahega, ground wala ground)
  Isliye:      is sawaal par kisi vendor ki baat
               ka weight kam rakhna
```

Woh aakhri box practical hai. Jab bhi koi tumse kahe "aaj kal elections X se jeete jaate hain," pehle poocho: **X bechne wale kaun hain?**

### Baban ki kahani — part 19

Baban 57 ka. Aur us saal ek 26 saal ka ladka uske paas kaam maangne aaya — Sameer. Laptop, phone, aur ek presentation.

Sameer ne kaha: "Dada, aapka tareeka purana hai. Ab main aapko dikha sakta hoon ki kaunse booth par kaunsa mudda chal raha hai, kis ghar mein kitne voter hain, aur kis message ka kya asar hua."

Baban ne poori presentation dekhi. Phir usne ek sawaal poocha:

"Booth number 114 — Shivaji Nagar. Wahan 780 vote hain. Tumhara data kya kehta hai?"

Sameer ne screen dekhi: "612 households mapped, 71% reachable, top issue: water supply."

Baban ne kaha: "Theek. Ab main batata hoon. Us booth mein 780 vote hain, lekin 40 ghar ek hi khaandaan ke hain aur unka mukhiya Ganesh hai. Ganesh ki ladki ki shaadi pichhle saal atki thi kyunki ladke wale dahej mein gaadi maang rahe the. Maine woh mamla settle karwaya tha. Us din se woh 40 ghar mere hain — chahe koi bhi khada ho, chahe paani aaye ya na aaye."

Sameer chup ho gaya.

Baban ne aage kaha, aur bina taunt ke kaha: "Tumhara data mujhe batata hai ki wahan 780 log hain aur unko paani chahiye. Yeh mujhe pehle se pata hai. **Tumhara data mujhe yeh nahi batata ki kis ghar mein kis aadmi ka ehsaan hai.** Aur election us ehsaan se chalta hai, us paani se nahi."

Sameer ne kaha: "Toh mera kaam bekaar hai?"

Aur ab Baban ne woh kiya jo usne apni zindagi mein pehle kabhi nahi kiya tha — usne apni hi baat ko kaata.

"Nahi. Baith."

Usne register nikala, purane wala. Aur bola:

"Mere paas 34 saal ka hisaab hai, lekin woh MERE dimaag mein hai aur is copy mein. Jis din main mar jaunga, yeh sab khatam. Aur ek aur baat — main 11 booth theek se jaanta tha. Ab mere paas 40 booth ka kaam hai aur main sach bataun toh mujhe 40 booth ka detail yaad nahi rehta. **Main bhool raha hoon, aur yeh mujhe kisi ko batana nahi hai.**"

"Tumhara laptop mere dimaag ki jagah nahi le sakta. Lekin woh meri COPY ki jagah le sakta hai. Aur agar tum us copy mein woh column bana sako jo maine 40 saal pehle banaya tha — 'word ka pakka?' — toh tumhara data kaam ka ho jayega."

Us raat register mein:

> **Sameer galat nahi hai aur main bhi galat nahi hoon. Woh dekh sakta hai ki KITNE log hain. Main jaanta hoon ki KAUN kis ka hai. Dono zaroori hain.**
>
> **Lekin ek farak hai jo mere haq mein nahi jaata: mera gyaan mere saath khatam ho jayega, uska gyaan file mein rahega. Aur jo cheez likh li jaati hai, woh agli peedhi ko mil jaati hai.**
>
> **Maine 34 saal apna sabse keemti maal apne dimaag mein rakha, isliye ki wahi meri suraksha tha. Aur usi wajah se woh kabhi kisi ko de nahi paya.**

Aur aakhri line, jo poore chapter ka nichod thi:

> **Naya tareeka purane ko nahi harata. Naya tareeka purane ko TEZ karta hai. Jo aadmi sirf naya jaanta hai, uske paas raftaar hai lekin pata nahi kahan jaana hai. Jo sirf purana jaanta hai, usko rasta pata hai lekin woh dheere chalta hai — aur ek din woh bhoolne lagta hai.**

### Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

**1. "Kya jeetaata hai" ki teen theories aur unka core daawa?**

> *Jawab:* (1) **Zameen** — machine aur turnout; jo apna voter nikaal sakta hai wahi jeetta hai. (2) **Lehar** — mahaul; jab hawa chalti hai toh machine/jaati/paisa sab bekaar. (3) **Mudda/kaam** — voter dikhne wali cheezon ka hisaab karta hai. Teenon ke paas evidence hai; koi settled nahi.

**2. Practitioner is uncertainty ka kya jawab deta hai?**

> *Jawab:* "Machine par kaam karo jaise lehar aayegi hi nahi, aur narrative par jaise machine kaafi nahi hogi." Kyunki pehle se pata nahi chalta ki yeh wave election hai ya normal — result se pehle koi nahi jaanta. Dono par kharch inefficient hai lekin rational hai.

**3. Digital ne machine ke saath kya kiya — replace ya upgrade?**

> *Jawab:* Upgrade. Usne machine ka **nervous system** badla (raftaar, kharcha, apna channel, data, booth pramukh ko rozana line). Lekin vote abhi bhi ghar se nikalna padta hai aur bharosa abhi bhi aadmi se aata hai. Jinke paas machine hai unka digital kaam karta hai; jinke paas nahi, unke liye woh shor hai.

**4. Karyakarta system ke baare mein sabse imaandaar version kya hai?**

> *Jawab:* Woh khatam nahi ho raha — uska **fuel** badal raha hai. Purana fuel (umeed + pehchaan) naye ladke par kam kaam karta hai, usko seedha fayda chahiye. Isliye machine mehngi hoti ja rahi hai, gayab nahi ho rahi.

**5. Jab koi kahe "aaj kal elections X se jeete jaate hain" toh pehla sawaal kya?**

> *Jawab:* "X bechne wale kaun hain?" Data wala data ko decisive kahega, ground wala ground ko. Is sawaal par vendor ki baat ka weight kam rakho.

### What this means for you

Yeh chapter tumhe ek cheez de raha hai jo baaki kitab se ulti lagti hai: **yahan pakka mat bolna.**

Agar kisi neta ke saamne tum kaho "sir, aaj kal toh sab digital se hota hai" ya "sir, ground hi sab kuch hai" — dono mein se koi bhi line tumhe bahar ka aadmi bana degi, kyunki har tajurbewale aadmi ne dono ke exceptions dekhe hain.

Andar ka aadmi kya kehta hai? Woh kehta hai: **"depend karta hai — is baar hawa kaisi hai?"** Yeh ek line poore chapter ka summary hai, aur wahi line tumhe us kamre mein us aadmi jaisa banati hai jisne kaafi elections dekhe hain.

Aur agar tum kabhi is duniya ke liye kuch banao: Baban aur Sameer dono ki baat yaad rakhna. Data yeh bata sakta hai ki **kitne** log hain. Woh abhi tak yeh nahi batata ki **kaun kiska hai** — aur election usi doosri cheez par chalta hai. Jo us gap ko bhar dega, uske paas is duniya ki sabse keemti cheez hogi.

---

*Note: this chapter deliberately does not adjudicate. The relative weight of organisation, wave and performance in Indian elections is genuinely contested among scholars and practitioners, with real evidence cited on multiple sides; the same is true of the effect of digital campaigning and of the EVM debate. Descriptions of what digital changed (speed, cost, direct channels, data, booth-level coordination) are structural and widely observed. Baban and Sameer are fictional.*
