# Chapter 18
## Jaat ka ganit — system ka sabse gehra variable

---

### Pehle ek imaandaar baat

Yeh chapter mushkil hai — likhne mein bhi, padhne mein bhi.

Kyunki jaati ke baare mein do tarah ki baat hoti hai India mein. Ek — "jaati nahi honi chahiye" (yeh ek ichha hai). Do — "jaati se vote milta hai" (yeh ek ilzaam hai).

Yeh chapter dono nahi karega. Yeh chapter jaati ko **ek variable ki tarah** dekhega — kyunki agar tumhe machine samajhni hai, toh yeh us machine ka sabse gehra variable hai, aur ise chhodkar architecture adhoora rehta hai.

Main yahan yeh nahi keh raha ki yeh achha hai. Main yeh keh raha hoon ki **yeh hai.**

### Yeh block itna majboot kyun hai

Chapter 2 mein humne dekha: akela vote bekaar, block sab kuch. Chapter 4 mein humne rule banaya: **jahan zaroorat sabse gehri, wahan block sabse pakka.**

Ab jaati ko us rule se naapo:

```
BLOCK KA TYPE          KITNA PAKKA          KYUN

Mohalla                dheela                log aate-jaate rehte hain
Union/association      medium                membership badalti hai
Bhasha/region          theek                 pehchaan hai lekin
                                             rozana ka rishta nahi
Basti (ch.4)           pakka                 roz ki zaroorat
JAATI/SAMUDAY          SABSE PAKKA           janm se marann tak,
                                             shaadi, rishtedaari,
                                             mandir, sankat mein
                                             sahara, aur roz ka
                                             samajik daayra
```

Yehi jawab hai. **Jaati ka block isliye sabse pakka hai kyunki woh block sirf vote ke liye nahi bana hai.** Woh pehle se maujood hai — shaadi ka daayra, rishtedaari, biradari ki panchayat, sankat mein madad, samajik pehchaan.

Politics ne yeh block banaya nahi. **Politics ne is block ko istemaal kiya, kyunki woh pehle se wahan tha aur sabse sasta tha.**

Ek line mein:

> **Neta ko block chahiye. Jaati ek ready-made block hai jise banane mein usko ek rupaya bhi nahi lagta.**

### Ganit kaise chalta hai

Ab practical. Ek seat ka jaati-ganit aisa dikhta hai (numbers kalpanik, structure asli):

```
EK VIDHAN SABHA SEAT — 3,00,000 voters

Samuday A       75,000   (25%)  ← sabse bada
Samuday B       54,000   (18%)
Samuday C       45,000   (15%)
Samuday D       36,000   (12%)
Chhote samuday  90,000   (30%)  ← bikhre hue

Jeetne ke liye chahiye: ~70,000 (ch.2 ka hisaab)
```

Ab is table se poori strategy nikalti hai. Dekho kaise:

**Chaal 1 — Sabse bade ka candidate banao.**
A ka candidate khada karo. Agar A ka 75-80% mila, toh 55,000-60,000 ho gaya. Baaki 10,000-15,000 kahin se jodo. **Sabse seedhi chaal, aur sabse aam.**

**Chaal 2 — Gathbandhan (alliance) banao.**
B + D milakar 90,000. Agar dono ka 70% bhi mila toh 63,000. Isliye party B ka candidate degi lekin D ko kuch aur degi — koi pad, koi doosri seat, koi mantri pad.

**Yeh baat samajhna zaroori hai: "jaati ki rajneeti" ka matlab sirf apni jaati ko bulana nahi hai. Zyada baar iska matlab GATHBANDHAN hai — do-teen samudayon ko ek chhat ke neeche laana.**

**Chaal 3 — Saamne wale ka block todo.**
Agar saamne wala A ka hai, toh A ka ek aur candidate khada karwa do (ch.2 ka vote-katwa). A ka 75,000 do hisson mein bata, aur tumhara 60,000 jeet gaya.

**Chaal 4 — Bikhre huon ko jodo.**
Woh 90,000 "chhote samuday" — agar unhe ek saath laaya ja sake, toh woh sabse bada block hai. Yeh sabse mushkil chaal hai lekin sabse takatwar. Kai badi political success stories isi chaal par bani hain: **jo alag-alag chhote the, unko ek nayi badi pehchaan de do.**

### Reservation — rule jo ganit badalta hai

Kuch seats SC/ST ke liye reserved hoti hain — wahan sirf us varg ka candidate lad sakta hai.

Ab dekho system ka behaviour (aur ch.3 ka "sarpanch pati" wala pattern dobara):

```
Reserved seat par:
  - Candidate zaroor SC/ST hoga
  - Lekin us seat ka VOTER ganit nahi badla —
    wahan bhi doosre samuday bade ho sakte hain
  - Isliye jeetne wala candidate aksar woh hota hai
    jo apne varg ka hone ke saath-saath BADE samuday
    ko bhi sweekar ho

Nateeja (aur yeh contested hai):
  Kuch log kehte hain isse SC/ST ka asli
  pratinidhitva kam ho jaata hai — kyunki jeetne
  wala apne varg se zyada bade samuday par nirbhar hai
```

Phir wahi sabak: **rule ne candidate ka varg tay kiya, lekin voter ka ganit nahi badla. Aur system ne dono ke beech apna raasta nikaal liya.**

### Party aur jaati — kaun kiska istemaal kar raha hai

Ek aam sawaal: kya party jaati ka istemaal karti hai, ya jaati party ka?

**Dono.** Aur yeh do-tarfa rishta hi asli baat hai:

```
PARTY KA HISAAB:
  Usko har seat par 70,000 chahiye. Sabse sasta
  raasta = ready-made blocks jodna. Isliye woh
  ticket dete waqt jaati dekhti hai (ch.7)

SAMUDAY KA HISAAB:
  Unko chahiye ki sarkar mein unka aadmi ho —
  taaki naukri, admission, theke, aur police
  station mein unki sunwai ho (ch.10 ka favor
  economy)
  Isliye woh apne aadmi ko vote dete hain

Aur dono sahi hain apni jagah par.
```

Yeh line dhyan se padho, kyunki yeh is chapter ki sabse zaroori baat hai:

> **Voter jaati ko isliye vote nahi deta ki woh "jaatiwadi" hai. Woh isliye deta hai kyunki uska anubhav yeh hai ki uska kaam tab hota hai jab uska aadmi kursi par ho. Yeh vishwas galat ho sakta hai — lekin woh anubhav se bana hai, nafrat se nahi.**

Aur jis din kisi samuday ko lage ki "hamara aadmi bhi hamara kaam nahi karta", us din woh block hilne lagta hai. Yeh hota hai — aur yahi wajah hai ki jaati ka ganit har baar same nahi rehta.

### Jaati akeli kaafi nahi hai

Ab balance. Log jaati ko "poora jawab" samajh lete hain. Woh galat hai, aur uske teen saboot hain:

**1. Ek samuday hamesha ek taraf nahi jaata.** Uske andar ameer-gareeb, sheher-gaon, alag upjaatiyan — sab alag vote kar sakte hain.

**2. Lehar jaati ko kaat deti hai.** Kuch elections mein sab ganit bekaar ho jaate hain (ch.19).

**3. Aur sabse zaroori — jaati ganit ko MACHINE chahiye.** Tumhare paas 75,000 ka block ho, lekin agar election ke din unko ghar se nikaalne wala koi nahi (ch.9), toh woh 75,000 kaagaz par hain, EVM mein nahi.

> **Jaati batati hai ki kaun tumhara ho SAKTA hai. Machine tay karti hai ki woh sach mein vote DEGA ya nahi.**

### Contested corner

**Kya jaati ki rajneeti kam ho rahi hai?**

- **Camp A (kam ho rahi hai):** Naye voter, sheher, education, aur bade national mudde jaati ke upar chale gaye hain. Bade wave elections mein jaati ke ganit tootte dikhe hain.
- **Camp B (roop badla hai):** Jaati khatam nahi hui — woh ab khulke nahi boli jaati, lekin ticket ke faisle mein, gathbandhan mein aur booth-level strategy mein pehle jaisi hi hai. Bas uski bhasha badal gayi hai.

Yeh ek genuinely khula sawaal hai aur ispe achhe log alag raay rakhte hain. **Sabse imaandaar version shayad yeh hai: jaati ab akeli kaafi nahi hai, lekin abhi bhi zaroori hai. Jo sirf jaati par lade woh haar sakta hai; jo jaati ko poori tarah ignore kare woh aksar haarta hai.**

### Incentive equation

```
NETA:
  Chahiye:  70,000 ka ganit
  Milta hai: ready-made block, muft
  Darr:     apne hi samuday ka doosra neta khada ho
            jaye (ch.2 vote-katwa) — isliye woh apne
            samuday mein doosra ubharne nahi dena chahta
  Isliye:   samuday ke andar ki ladai aksar doosre
            samuday se ki ladai se tez hoti hai

SAMUDAY KA MUKHIYA:
  Deta hai: block
  Paata hai: apne logon ke kaam + apni izzat +
             "hum bhi ginti mein hain" ka ehsaas
  Isliye:   woh apne block ko jodkar rakhne mein
            zinda hai (ch.2 ka mukhiya, gehre roop mein)

VOTER:
  Uska tark: "mera aadmi kursi par hoga toh mera
             kaam hoga"
  Yeh anubhav se bana hai
  Isliye:   jab uska aadmi kaam nahi karta, toh
            woh block dheere-dheere hilta hai

PARTY:
  Uska tark: sabse sasta block wahi hai jo pehle
             se bana hua hai
  Isliye:   ticket ke faisle mein jaati hamesha
            table par hoti hai — chahe koi bole ya na bole
```

### Baban ki kahani — part 18

Baban 57 ka. Aur is saal usne apni zindagi ka sabse purana sawaal poocha — jo usne 40 saal se nahi poocha tha.

Uska samuday us zile mein na sabse bada tha na sabse chhota. Beech ka. Lagbhag 9-10%.

Aur ek din, apne ITI college ke office mein baithe hue, usne apne jeevan ka hisaab lagaya:

```
34 saal politics mein
11 booth se shuru, 3 wardon tak
90 parivaaron ki naukri
4,000+ ka credit society, ab 9,000
Do MLA campaign chalaye, teen jeete

Ticket: kabhi nahi
```

Aur usne pehli baar apne aap se poocha: **"agar main sabse bade samuday ka hota, toh kya mera ticket 20 saal pehle aa gaya hota?"**

Us raat usne register mein sabse mushkil entry likhi:

> **Jawab: shayad haan. Aur yeh soch ke mujhe gussa aata hai.**
>
> **Lekin main imaandaar rahunga apne saath. Meri jaati ne mera raasta chhota kiya — yeh sach hai. Lekin mere paas ek aur sach bhi hai: maine 26 saal kisi aur ke naam par kaam kiya (ch.14). Woh galti meri thi, meri jaati ki nahi.**
>
> **Aasan hota agar main sab kuch jaati ke sar daal deta. Lekin phir main woh galti kabhi na sudhaarta.**

Do mahine baad ek ghatna hui jisne usko chapter ka doosra aadha sikhaya.

Uske zile mein sabse bade samuday ka ek naujawan neta uth raha tha — 30 saal ka, tez, achha bolne wala. Sab kehte the "yeh agla MLA hai."

Aur phir usi samuday ka ek doosra aadmi khada ho gaya. Purana, kam padha-likha, lekin uske paas 25 saal ka network tha.

Do mahine mein us samuday ke andar aisi ladai hui jo Baban ne kisi doosre samuday ke khilaf kabhi nahi dekhi thi. Panchayat mein jhagda, shaadiyon mein alag-alag baithak, mandir committee mein toot.

Naujawan neta ka ticket kat gaya. Purane ko mila. Aur woh haar gaya — kyunki naujawan ke log ghar baith gaye.

Baban ne register mein likha:

> **Aaj samajh aaya ki sabse badi jaati hona bhi surakshit nahi hai. Bade block mein hamesha DO log hote hain jo usko apna maante hain — aur unki ladai baaki sab se tez hoti hai.**
>
> **Kyunki tumhara asli pratidwandi woh hai jo TUMHARE HI vote par daawa karta hai. Doosre samuday ka aadmi tumse vote nahi cheenta — woh tumhare paas tha hi nahi.**

Aur aakhri line, jo poore chapter ka sabse thanda sach thi:

> **Jaati ka ganit bahar se ek deewar dikhta hai — "yeh log ek hain." Andar se woh ek AKHADA hai. Aur is akhade mein jeetne wala woh nahi hota jiska khoon sabse shuddh hai. Jeetne wala woh hota hai jiske paas machine hai (ch.5), khaata hai (ch.10), aur sanstha hai (ch.11).**
>
> **Jaati tumhe darwaze tak le jaati hai. Andar wahi jaata hai jisne 20 saal kaam kiya ho.**

### Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

**1. Jaati ka block sabse pakka kyun hai?**

> *Jawab:* Kyunki woh vote ke liye bana hi nahi — woh pehle se maujood hai: shaadi ka daayra, rishtedaari, biradari panchayat, sankat mein sahara, samajik pehchaan. Neta ko ek ready-made block muft mein milta hai. Ch.4 ka rule yahan sabse gehra lagu hota hai: jahan zaroorat sabse gehri, wahan block sabse pakka.

**2. "Jaati ki rajneeti" ka sabse aam matlab kya hai — sirf apni jaati bulana?**

> *Jawab:* Nahi, zyada baar **gathbandhan** — do-teen samudayon ko ek chhat ke neeche laana, kyunki akela ek samuday aksar 70,000 tak nahi pahunchta. Aur chauthi chaal (bikhre chhote samudayon ko ek nayi badi pehchaan dena) sabse mushkil aur sabse takatwar hai.

**3. Voter apni jaati ko vote kyun deta hai — uska tark kya hai?**

> *Jawab:* Uska anubhav yeh hai ki uska kaam tab hota hai jab uska aadmi kursi par ho (ch.10 ka favor economy). Yeh nafrat se nahi, anubhav se bana vishwas hai. Aur jis din us samuday ko lage ki "hamara aadmi bhi hamara kaam nahi karta", block hilne lagta hai.

**4. Jaati ka ganit akela kaafi kyun nahi hai?**

> *Jawab:* Teen wajah: (a) ek samuday ke andar bhi ameer-gareeb, sheher-gaon, upjaatiyan alag vote karti hain, (b) lehar ganit kaat deti hai (ch.19), (c) aur sabse zaroori — 75,000 ka block bina machine ke sirf kaagaz par hai. **Jaati batati hai kaun tumhara ho sakta hai; machine tay karti hai woh vote dega ya nahi.**

**5. Sabse bade samuday ka hona bhi surakshit kyun nahi?**

> *Jawab:* Kyunki bade block par hamesha do log daawa karte hain, aur unki aapas ki ladai doosre samudayon se ki ladai se tez hoti hai — asli pratidwandi wahi hai jo tumhare hi vote par daawa karta hai. Andar jeetne wala shuddhta se nahi, machine + khaata + sanstha se jeetta hai.

### What this means for you

Do baatein, dono practical.

**Ek:** kisi seat ko samajhna ho toh uska jaati-ganit dekhna zaroori hai — lekin uske baad **doosra sawaal zyada important hai**: "us block ke andar kitne daavedaar hain?" Ek seat jahan sabse bada samuday ek neta ke peeche hai, aur ek seat jahan wahi samuday teen logon mein bata hai — dono bilkul alag seats hain, chahe table par numbers same dikhein.

**Do:** kisi neta se milte waqt uski jaati ka zikr karna zaroori nahi hai aur aksar galat bhi hai. Lekin **uske dimaag mein woh table hamesha khuli hoti hai.** Jab woh apne ilaake ki baat kare, woh blocks ki bhasha mein soch raha hoga — aur agar tum bhi blocks ki bhasha mein baat kar sako (bina jaati ka naam liye), toh woh samajh jaayega ki tum bahar ke aadmi nahi ho.
