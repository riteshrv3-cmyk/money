# Chapter 10
## Paanch saal — jab koi nahi dekh raha hota

---

### Sabse lambi cheez, sabse kam padhi gayi

Chunav 40 din ka hota hai. Kitaabein, news, bahas — sab usi 40 din par hoti hain.

Lekin MLA ki zindagi ka **97% hissa** woh 5 saal hai jab koi chunav nahi hai. Aur agle chunav ka result usi 97% mein decide ho jaata hai.

Yeh chapter us 97% ke baare mein hai — machine ki **steady state**.

### Subah — janta darbar

Har MLA ke ghar ke bahar subah ek queue hoti hai. Kahin usko "janta darbar" kehte hain, kahin bas "log aate hain."

```
KAUN AATA HAI (ek aam subah):

  8-10 log   sarkari kaagaz ke liye
             (ration card, pension, ghar ki scheme,
              certificate, naam list mein nahi aaya)

  5-8 log    police ka mamla
             (FIR likhwana, ya FIR rukwana, ya
              jamanat, ya jhagde ka settlement)

  4-6 log    transfer/posting
             (master ka transfer, clerk ka transfer,
              ya kisi ko rukwana)

  3-5 log    aspataal / admission
             (bed, operation ka paisa, college ki seat)

  2-4 log    apne karyakarta
             (unka apna kaam, ya kisi aur ke liye
              sifarish)

  1-2 log    thekedaar / vyapari
             (yeh queue mein nahi baithte — andar
              jaate hain, ch.4 wali baat)
```

Ab dekho MLA in sab ka kya karta hai:

```
Kaam ka type              MLA kya karta hai
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Chhota, aur uske
department mein hai       ek phone. Ho jaata hai.

Chhota, lekin doosre
ke ilaake mein            "dekhta hoon" — parchi par
                          likhkar de dega, aur us
                          parchi ka bhagya alag hai

Bada (transfer,
tender, bada kaam)        yeh sirf tab hota hai jab
                          MLA ruling party mein hai
                          (chapter 6 wali table)

Namumkin                  phir bhi SUNTA hai, poora
                          sunta hai, aur "koshish
                          karunga" bolta hai
```

Aur yahan is chapter ka pehla bada sach:

> **MLA sabka kaam nahi kar sakta. Woh 10 mein se shayad 3 ka kar paata hai. Lekin woh 10 ko SUNTA hai. Aur sunn-na hi asli product hai.**

Chapter 4 mein corporator ne Baban ko yehi bola tha. Ab uska ganit dekho:

- Kaam karna **mehnga** hai — usme uska asar kharch hota hai (har phone ka ek "credit" lagta hai officer ke saath).
- Sunn-na **free** hai — sirf 3 minute lagte hain.
- Aur jo aadmi suna gaya, woh apne mohalle mein jaakar kehta hai "saheb ne poora suna, mere haath pakde." Chahe kaam na hua ho.

Isliye janta darbar ek **manufacturing plant** hai — jahan roz 40-50 units "sune jaane ka ehsaas" banti hain, aur uski cost lagbhag zero hai.

### Parchi — MLA ka sabse chhota, sabse bada hathiyaar

Woh chhoti si parchi jispe MLA likh deta hai "kripya uchit karyavahi karein" (please do the needful) aur dastakhat kar deta hai.

Yeh parchi ek order nahi hai. Officer ise phenk bhi sakta hai. Toh iska matlab kya?

```
Parchi teen cheezein karti hai:

1. AADMI KE LIYE
   Usko lagta hai kaam shuru ho gaya. Uske haath mein
   ab kuch hai. Woh khush ghar jaata hai.
   (Yeh parchi ka 70% kaam hai.)

2. OFFICER KE LIYE
   Ek signal: "is aadmi ka MLA se sampark hai."
   Officer ko ab tay karna hai ki is MLA ki kitni
   chalti hai. Agar ruling party ka hai → kaam ho
   jayega. Agar nahi → file dheere chalegi.

3. MLA KE LIYE
   Record. Uske office mein parchi ki copy rehti hai.
   Chunav ke waqt woh nikal sakta hai: "maine tumhare
   gaon ke 60 logon ke kaam kiye."
```

Ek chhote kaagaz se teen alag log teen alag cheez samajh rahe hain. **Yeh system ki sabse elegant cheez hai.**

### Din bhar — maujoodgi ka kaam

Ab MLA ka din nikalta hai. Aur usme sabse zaroori kaam woh hai jo bahar se sabse bekaar lagta hai: **shaadi aur mayyat mein jaana.**

Ek baar main isko seedha likh doon:

> **Indian politics mein maujoodgi (attendance) ek kaam hai, sabse bada kaam. Aur mrityu par jaana sabse zaroori hai.**

Kyun? Sochke dekho:

- Shaadi mein 500 log hote hain. MLA aaya, 10 minute ruka, sabne dekha. Cost: 10 minute. Reach: 500 log.
- Mayyat mein 100 log hote hain. Lekin us parivaar ke liye **woh din zindagi bhar yaad rehta hai.** Jo us din aaya, woh bhoola nahi jaata.

Aur sabse bada point: **dukh mein aaya hua aadmi, sukh mein aaye hue aadmi se 10 guna zyada yaad rehta hai.** Isliye purane neta kehte hain: "shaadi chhoot sakti hai, mayyat nahi."

Ganit banao:

```
Ek seat: 2-4 lakh log, maano 60,000 parivaar
Har saal, har 100 parivaar mein kuch mrityu aur
kuch shaadi hoti hai — matlab saal bhar mein
sekdon aise mauke.

MLA sab par nahi ja sakta. Toh woh kis par jaata hai?
  → apne karyakartaon ke ghar (sabse pehle)
  → mukhiyaon ke ghar (block ka rishta)
  → bade blocks ke ghar
  → aur jahan uska aadmi keh de "yeh zaroori hai"
```

Dekho — woh apne **machine ke logon** ko sabse pehle rakhta hai, aam voter ko nahi. Kyunki machine 300 booth chalati hai, aur ek naraz booth pramukh 500 vote hila deta hai. Chapter 5 wapas aa gaya.

### Office aur PA — chhupa hua power centre

MLA ke office mein ek ya do log hote hain jo poora system chalate hain — PA (personal assistant), ya "saheb ka aadmi."

Yeh log kaagaz par kuch nahi hain. Practically woh **gate** hain:

```
PA decide karta hai (ya bahut asar daalta hai):
  - kaun andar jayega, kaun 3 ghante baithega
  - kaun sa phone MLA tak pahunchega
  - kaun si parchi par sach mein follow-up hoga
  - kaun sa aadmi "saheb ka aadmi" hai
```

Aur isliye politics mein ek purani baat hai: **MLA se milna aasan hai, uske PA se rishta banana mushkil hai — aur zyada zaroori.**

Yeh chhota sa gate poori kitab ka ek pattern hai: **jahan bhi demand supply se zyada hai, wahan ek gatekeeper paida ho jaata hai, aur us gatekeeper ki apni taakat ban jaati hai.**

### Favor economy — asli currency

Ab is chapter ka sabse zaroori section. MLA ki duniya mein roz jo cheez chalti hai woh paisa nahi hai. Woh **ehsaan** hai.

```
FAVOR KA CHAKKAR:

  MLA → officer   : "yeh kaam kar do"
  officer → MLA   : "meri posting dekh lena"

  MLA → aadmi     : kaam kar diya
  aadmi → MLA     : uska block, uska vote, uski
                    zubaan mein MLA ka naam

  MLA → karyakarta: uske bhai ki naukri ki sifarish
  karyakarta → MLA: 5 saal ki wafadaari, 300 vote

  vyapari → MLA   : chunav mein paisa
  MLA → vyapari   : file ka na rukna
```

In sab mein koi "transaction" nahi dikhti — koi receipt nahi, koi rate nahi. Lekin **har aadmi apna khaata mann mein rakhta hai.** Kisne kiske liye kya kiya, aur kiska udhaar baaki hai.

Aur yeh system paise se zyada takatwar hai, kyunki:

1. **Ehsaan par tax nahi lagta** aur koi record nahi banta.
2. **Ehsaan ka koi fix rate nahi** — isliye woh badta rehta hai. Ek chhota kaam bhi 10 saal ka rishta bana deta hai.
3. **Ehsaan lautaya nahi ja sakta poori tarah** — aur yehi uski taakat hai. Jo aadmi tumhara ehsaanmand hai, woh hamesha thoda sa tumhara rehta hai.

Isliye purana neta paisa lene se pehle **kaam karwata** hai. Paisa ek baar ka rishta hai. Ehsaan zindagi bhar ka.

### MLALADS — chhota fund, bada signal

Har MLA ko apne kshetra ke chhote kaamon ke liye ek fund milta hai (MLALADS / MLA local area development). Amount har state mein alag hai.

Iske teen kaam hain:

**1. Chhote kaam ka rasta.** Ek gaon mein community hall, ek school mein computer, ek mandir ke paas paving — yeh cheezein iss fund se hoti hain.

**2. Signal.** Jis gaon mein yeh kaam hua, wahan patthar lagta hai MLA ke naam ka. Woh patthar 5 saal bolega.

**3. Distribution ka hathiyaar.** Aur yeh sabse important hai. MLA ko decide karna hota hai — kis gaon mein, kis mohalle mein, kiski sifarish par. Matlab yeh fund uske **blocks ko reward karne ka** tareeka hai.

Ab dhyan do: paisa MLA ke haath mein nahi aata — kaam administration ke through hota hai, MLA sirf suggest karta hai. Lekin **suggest karna hi taakat hai**, kyunki suggest karne wala decide karta hai kiske gaon mein kaam jayega.

### Contested corner

**Kya "kaam" se vote milta hai?**

- **Camp A (performance):** Haan. Jo MLA sach mein sadak, paani, bijli laata hai, woh dobara jeetta hai. Voter ab performance dekhta hai.
- **Camp B (identity/machine):** Nahi. Bahut se kaam karne wale MLA haar jaate hain aur bahut se nikamme jeet jaate hain — kyunki asli cheez block, jaati, aur lehar hai.

Data dono taraf mila-jula hai (incumbents jeette bhi hain, harte bhi — states aur saalon ke hisaab se bahut variation hai). Sabse imaandaar version: **kaam ek zaroori base hai, lekin akela kaafi nahi. Kaam bina machine ke bekaar hai, aur machine bina kaam ke mehngi ho jaati hai.**

### Baban ki kahani — part 10

Baban 43 ka. Ab woh ek MLA ka "sab kuch" hai — official title nahi, lekin poora office usi ke through chalta hai.

Ek subah janta darbar mein ek budhi aurat aayi. Uski pension do saal se nahi aa rahi thi. Kaagaz sab theek the. Baban ne dekha aur samajh gaya — yeh mamla tehsil mein ek clerk ke paas atka hai, aur woh clerk aise 200 mamle roke baitha hai.

Usne MLA se kaha: "Sahab, ek phone karo."

MLA ne kaha: "Kar dunga. Lekin us tehsil mein mera aadmi nahi hai. Woh phone ka koi mol nahi."

Baban ne kuch aur socha. Usne kaha: "Phir yeh karo — aap ek phone mat karo. Aap **teen** phone karo. Ek clerk ko, ek tehsildar ko, aur ek us clerk ke gaon ke sarpanch ko."

MLA hansa: "Sarpanch ko kyun?"

"Kyunki clerk us gaon ka hai. Tehsildar ka phone usko dara nahi payega — woh jaanta hai tehsildar 2 saal mein chala jayega. Lekin uske gaon ka sarpanch usko zindagi bhar milega."

Pension 11 din mein aa gayi.

Us shaam Baban register mein likhta hai:

> **Taakat sirf upar se neeche nahi chalti. Har aadmi ke upar koi na koi hota hai — aur woh aksar uska boss nahi hota. Officer ka boss usse transfer kar sakta hai. Uska gaon usko sharminda kar sakta hai. Doosri cheez zyada asar karti hai.**

Do saal baad ek aur baat hui, jo usne kabhi nahi bhulai.

MLA ka sabse purana karyakarta — 22 saal se saath — uske bete ki mrityu ho gayi. MLA us din Mumbai mein tha, ek zaroori baithak mein, jahan uske constituency ke liye ek bada project pass hona tha.

MLA ne baithak chhod di aur 4 ghante gaadi chalakar mayyat mein pahuncha.

Baad mein Baban ne poocha: "Sahab, project ka kya?"

MLA ne kaha: "Project agli baithak mein bhi ho sakta hai. Aur agar nahi bhi hua, toh do saal baad ho jayega. **Lekin us aadmi ke bete ki mayyat dobara nahi hogi. Aur woh aadmi mere 40 booth chalata hai. Main us din nahi gaya hota, toh woh 22 saal ek din mein khatam ho jaate.**"

Baban ne us raat register mein likha, aur yeh line usne baad mein doosron ko sikhayi bhi:

> **Politics mein sabse mehngi cheez woh nahi hai jo tum kharch karte ho. Sabse mehngi cheez woh hai jahan tum MAUJOOD nahi the. Paisa wapas aa jaata hai. Project wapas aa jaata hai. Woh din wapas nahi aata.**

Aur uske neeche:

> **5 saal mein MLA jo kuch banata hai, woh sadak nahi hai. Woh EHSAAN KA KHAATA hai. Chunav ke din woh khaata bhunaya jaata hai. Jiska khaata khali hai, uske paas kitna bhi paisa ho, woh haar jaata hai.**

### Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

**1. MLA 10 mein se 3 ka hi kaam kar paata hai. Phir bhi 10 ko sunta kyun hai?**

> *Jawab:* Kaam karna mehnga hai (uska asar/credit kharch hota hai), sunn-na free hai (3 minute). Aur suna gaya aadmi apne mohalle mein jaakar uska naam leta hai chahe kaam na ho. Janta darbar "sune jaane ka ehsaas" banane ka zero-cost plant hai.

**2. Ek parchi ke teen alag matlab kya hain?**

> *Jawab:* Aadmi ke liye — "kaam shuru ho gaya" (uska 70% kaam yehi hai). Officer ke liye — signal ki is aadmi ka MLA se rishta hai, aur ab officer naapega ki us MLA ki chalti kitni hai. MLA ke liye — record, jo chunav mein dikhaya ja sakta hai.

**3. Mayyat shaadi se zyada zaroori kyun hai?**

> *Jawab:* Shaadi mein reach zyada hai (500 log dekhte hain) lekin yaad kam rehti hai. Mayyat mein reach kam hai lekin us parivaar ke liye woh din zindagi bhar ka hai. Dukh mein aaya aadmi sukh mein aaye se kai guna zyada yaad rehta hai — aur MLA sabse pehle apni machine ke logon ki mayyat par jaata hai, aam voter ki nahi.

**4. Ehsaan paise se zyada takatwar kyun hai?**

> *Jawab:* (a) Uspar tax nahi, record nahi. (b) Uska rate fix nahi, toh woh badhta rehta hai. (c) Woh poori tarah lautaya nahi ja sakta — isliye ehsaanmand aadmi hamesha thoda tumhara rehta hai. Paisa ek baar ka rishta hai, ehsaan zindagi bhar ka.

**5. MLALADS ka teesra (asli) kaam kya hai?**

> *Jawab:* Blocks ko reward karna. Paisa MLA ke haath mein aata hi nahi (administration kaam karti hai), lekin **suggest karne ka adhikaar** hi taakat hai — kyunki woh decide karta hai kis gaon, kis mohalle mein kaam jayega. Fund chhota hai, distribution ka signal bada hai.

### What this means for you

Kisi bhi MLA ke office mein jao toh do cheezein dekhna: **subah ki queue kitni lambi hai** (uski pahunch ka thermometer — lambi queue matlab log maante hain uski chalti hai), aur **PA kaun hai** (asli darwaza wahi hai). Aur agar tum us MLA ko kuch dena chahte ho jo uske liye sach mein keemti ho, toh yaad rakhna: uske paas paisa aa jaata hai, time nahi aata. Uska sabse bada roz ka dard yeh hai ki **usko yaad nahi rehta kisne kya maanga tha, kiska kaam hua, kiska nahi** — aur woh bhoolna hi uska khaata leak karta hai.
