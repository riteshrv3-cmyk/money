# Chapter 15
## MP — Delhi ka alag khel

---

### Sabse aam galatfehmi

Log samajhte hain MP "bada MLA" hai. Seedhi mein agla danda, wahi kaam, zyada area.

**Yeh galat hai.** MP aur MLA do alag naukriyaan hain, alag physics ke saath. Aur jab tak yeh farak samajh na aaye, tab tak Delhi ki aadhi khabrein samajh nahi aayengi.

Pehle numbers, phir wajah:

```
                       MLA                  MP (Lok Sabha)
Voters                 2-4 lakh             ~18 lakh (average)
Booths                 250-350              1,800-2,500
Andar kya hai          —                    6-8 vidhan sabha seats
                                            (matlab 6-8 MLAs uske
                                             area mein baithe hain)
Fund                   MLALADS (state ke    MPLADS: Rs 5 crore/saal
                       hisaab se alag)
Kaam kis sarkar se     STATE sarkar         CENTRE sarkar
```

Aakhri line par ruko. **Yeh poore chapter ka jawab hai.**

### MP ghar par kamzor kyun hota hai

Aam aadmi ki roz ki zaroorat kya hai? Chapter 10 ki list yaad karo: police, tehsil, ration card, school admission, bijli, paani, sadak, aspataal, transfer.

Ab poocho: **yeh sab kiske paas hai?**

```
Police             → state
Tehsil / patwari   → state
School, aspataal   → state (zyadatar)
Bijli, paani, sadak→ state (zyadatar)
Transfer-posting   → state
Ration card        → state implementation
```

**Lagbhag sab kuch state ke paas hai.** Aur MP ka rishta Centre se hai, state se nahi.

Ab dekho iska nateeja zameen par kya hota hai:

```
Ek aadmi apne gaon ka rasta banwane aata hai.

MLA ke paas jaata hai  → MLA ka phone chalta hai
                          (agar woh ruling party mein hai)
                          → kaam ho sakta hai

MP ke paas jaata hai   → MP kya karega? Woh state ke
                          officer ko order nahi de sakta.
                          Woh sirf "kehke dekh sakta hai."
```

Aur agar MP ki party state mein virodh mein hai, toh uski baat aur bhi kam chalti hai.

Isliye ek line jo har purana karyakarta jaanta hai:

> **MLA ke paas roz ka kaam hai. MP ke paas roz ka kaam nahi hai. Isliye aam aadmi apna kaam lekar MLA ke paas jaata hai, MP ke paas nahi.**

### Toh MP ke paas hai kya

Teen cheezein — aur teenon MLA se alag tarah ki hain:

**1. MPLADS — Rs 5 crore saalana**

Yeh MLA ke fund se aksar bada hota hai. Rules:
- Saal ke Rs 5 crore, do kishton mein (2.5 + 2.5), **seedha district authority ko** jaate hain
- MP paisa kharch nahi karta — woh District Collector ko kaam **suggest** karta hai
- Kam se kam 15% SC bahul ilaakon mein, 7.5% ST bahul ilaakon mein

Ab chapter 10 wala insight yahan bhi lagao: **paisa MP ke haath mein nahi hai, lekin "kis gaon mein" ka faisla uske haath mein hai.** Aur wahi faisla taakat hai.

Aur uska istemal? 6-8 vidhan sabha seats hain uske area mein. Toh MPLADS uska **apne MLAs ke saath rishta banane ya bigadne ka** tool ban jaata hai — kis MLA ke ilaake mein zyada kaam jaayega, kis mein kam.

**2. Delhi ki pahunch**

Yeh uski asli taakat hai:
- Central ministries mein baat pahunchana
- Central schemes apne ilaake mein khinchna (railway station, highway, university, central hospital)
- Central agencies aur PSU ke kaam

Matlab MP ka product **chhota rozana kaam nahi, bada project** hai. Ek MLA 500 logon ke chhote kaam karta hai. Ek MP ek railway line laata hai.

Farak yeh hai ki chhote kaam **roz vote banate hain**, aur bada project **5 saal mein ek baar dikhta hai** — aur uska credit aksar bat jaata hai.

**3. Party ke andar ka kad**

Ek MP apni party ka rashtriya chehra hota hai. Uski baat national leadership tak jaati hai. Yeh MLA se zyada hai.

### MP aur uske MLAs — ek chhupi hui ladai

Yeh section sabse practical hai, aur is chapter ka dil.

Ek Lok Sabha seat ke andar 6-8 vidhan sabha seats hain. Matlab **6-8 MLAs**, jo aksar usi party ke ho sakte hain.

Ab dekho tension kahan hai:

```
MP ko chahiye:   ki uske ilaake ke MLAs uske liye
                 kaam karein (unki machine = uski machine)

MLA ko chahiye:  ki uski seat par uska naam chale,
                 MP ka nahi

Aur donon ek hi voter ke saamne khade hain.
```

Aur ek gehri baat: **MP ke paas apni booth-level machine aksar nahi hoti.** 1,800-2,500 booth ek aadmi nahi sambhal sakta. Toh election ke waqt MP kiski machine par ladta hai?

**Apne MLAs ki machine par.**

Isliye MP election mein woh cheez hoti hai jo bahar se nahi dikhti: **MP ko apne hi party ke MLAs ko manana padta hai ki woh dil se kaam karein.** Aur agar kisi MLA ka MP se jhagda hai, toh woh MLA apne ilaake mein "kaam karta hua dikhta hai" lekin uska booth-level zor kam ho jaata hai — aur counting ke din woh vidhan sabha segment ka number bata deta hai (ch.9 ka report card, yahan bhi).

> **MP jeetta hai lehar se aur apne MLAs ki machine se. MLA jeetta hai apni machine se. Isliye MP zyada "hawa" par depend karta hai, aur MLA zyada "zameen" par.**

Aur yehi wajah hai ki **wave elections mein MP seats zyada palatti hain, MLA seats kam.**

### MP banna kaun chahta hai — aur kyun

Agar MP ghar par kamzor hai, toh log MP kyun banna chahte hain? Chaar wajah:

```
1. KAD (status)
   "Sansad" hona rajya se upar hai. Rashtriya pehchaan.

2. MANTRI KA RASTA
   Central mantri banne ka darwaza sirf yahan se
   khulta hai. Aur central mantri ki taakat kisi
   bhi MLA se kai guna hai.

3. BADE PROJECT
   Bade project = bade theke = bada paisa
   (ch.8 ka loop, bade size mein)

4. DELHI KA NETWORK
   Central ministries, PSU, industry — yeh network
   business ke liye MLA wale network se zyada
   keemti hai
```

Aur ek honest baat: **kai MLAs MP nahi banna chahte.** Kyunki MLA ke paas roz ki taakat hai (transfer, police, tehsil) aur MP ke paas nahi. Ek strong MLA jo apne ilaake ka raja hai, woh Delhi jaakar 543 mein se ek banne mein nuksaan dekh sakta hai.

### Rajya Sabha — teesra darwaza

Ek chhota section, kyunki iske bina picture adhoori hai.

Rajya Sabha ka member janta nahi chunti — **MLAs chunte hain** (state ke vidhayak vote dete hain).

Iska matlab kya hai:

```
- Rajya Sabha ki seat ke liye tumhe VOTER nahi,
  MLAs chahiye
- Matlab yeh seat party ke haath mein hai
- Isliye yahan aksar woh log jaate hain jo chunav
  nahi lad sakte/ladna nahi chahte: paisa dene wale,
  vishesh log, wakil, patrakar, aur woh neta jinko
  party "adjust" karna chahti hai
```

Yeh chapter 7 ka "pad" wala insight hai, sabse upar ke level par. **Rajya Sabha party ka sabse mehnga pad hai.**

### Contested corner

**Kya MP ka role badal raha hai?**

- **Camp A:** Haan. Ab MP bhi local kaam mein ghusta hai — MPLADS, Twitter par shikayat, central schemes ka credit lena. Voter ab MP se bhi rozana kaam maangta hai.
- **Camp B:** Nahi. Structure wahi hai — jo cheezein aam aadmi ko chahiye woh state ke paas hain. MP ka local kaam dikhawa zyada, asar kam. Isliye MP seats par national mudde chalte hain, aur MLA seats par local.

Sach shayad yeh hai ki **MP zyada "dikhne" laga hai, lekin uska structural power wahi hai** — kyunki subject list badli nahi hai.

### Incentive equation

```
MP:
  Paata hai:  kad, Delhi network, MPLADS 5 cr,
              central mantri ka rasta, bade project
  Nahi hai:   roz ka kaam (state ke paas), aur
              apni booth machine (bahut badi seat)
  Darr:       lehar palat gayi toh kuch nahi bachega —
              uska apna vote MLA jitna gehra nahi hota
  Isliye:     woh apne MLAs ko sambhalta hai, aur
              national narrative par depend karta hai

MLA (MP ke ilaake ka):
  Chahta hai: apni seat par apna naam
  Deta hai:   election mein machine (ya nahi deta)
  Isliye:     MP-MLA rishta hamesha thoda tana rehta hai

VOTER:
  Woh dono se alag cheez maangta hai:
    MLA se — mera kaam
    MP se  — mera desh/bada project/party ka chehra
  Isliye:     ek hi aadmi Lok Sabha mein ek party ko
              aur Vidhan Sabha mein doosri ko vote de
              deta hai — aur yeh irrational NAHI hai
```

Woh aakhri line bahut important hai. **Split voting** — Lok Sabha mein ek party, vidhan sabha mein doosri — India mein aam hai, aur log isko "confused voter" kehte hain. Voter confused nahi hai. Woh **do alag naukriyon ke liye do alag aadmi chun raha hai.**

### Baban ki kahani — part 15

Baban 55 ka. Uska chhota ITI college ek saal purana hai. Usko affiliation ke liye ek central body se NOC chahiye thi, aur woh file 8 mahine se Delhi mein atki thi.

Usne apne MLA se kaha. MLA ne saaf bola: "Yeh mera kaam nahi hai. Yeh Delhi ka hai. Tu MP saheb se mil."

Baban pehli baar MP ke office gaya. Aur wahan usne jo dekha, usne uski poori samajh mein ek naya kamra khol diya.

**Pehla farak:** MLA ke ghar ke bahar subah 40 log baithe hote the. MP ke office ke bahar **6 log** the. Aur unme se 4 thekedaar type dikhte the.

Usne MP ke PA se poocha: "Bheed kam hai?"

PA hansa: "Bheed MLA ke paas jaati hai. Yahan log tab aate hain jab kaam bada ho ya Delhi ka ho. Chhote kaam ke liye koi hum tak nahi aata — unko pata hai humse hoga nahi."

**Doosra farak:** MP ne Baban ka kaam 3 hafte mein karwa diya. Ek phone Delhi mein, ek chitthi. Jo kaam MLA 8 mahine mein nahi kar paya, MP ne 3 hafte mein kiya.

Baban ne register mein likha:

> **MLA aur MP mein chhota-bada ka farak nahi hai. DARWAAZE ka farak hai. Har aadmi ke paas kuch darwaaze hain aur baaki band hain. Galat aadmi ke paas sahi kaam le jaana — yehi sabse aam galti hai jo log karte hain, aur phir kehte hain "neta kaam nahi karta."**

**Teesra farak** usne 6 mahine baad dekha, aur woh sabse gehra tha.

Lok Sabha ka chunav aaya. Baban ke ilaake ke MLA — jiske bete ne ab seat sambhali thi — MP ke saath thande the. Purani ranjish.

Baban ne dekha ki MLA ne kya kiya: usne MP ke liye do rally karwayi, poster lagwaye, mancho par saath khada hua. Bahar se poora sath.

Lekin usne apne booth pramukhon ko woh phone nahi kiya jo woh apne khud ke chunav mein karta tha. Koi order nahi diya. Kuch mana bhi nahi kiya. Bas... zor nahi lagaya.

Counting ke din us vidhan sabha segment se MP ko baaki 6 segments se kaafi kam mila.

Baban ne register mein us raat sabse chalak line likhi:

> **MLA ne MP ko dhoka nahi diya. Usne bas apni poori taakat nahi lagayi. Aur politics mein yeh sabse surakshit hathiyaar hai — kyunki isko koi pakad nahi sakta. "Maine toh rally ki thi, poster lagaye the" — aur baat khatam.**
>
> **Machine chalane se nahi rukti. Machine dheere chalane se rukti hai. Aur dheemi machine ka koi saboot nahi hota.**

Aur neeche:

> **Isliye MP hamesha thoda ghabraya hua rehta hai. Uski kismat un 6-8 MLAs ke haath mein hai jo uske dost nahi, uske pratidwandi hain — kyunki dono ek hi zameen par khade hain.**

### Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

**1. MP ghar par MLA se kamzor kyun hai?**

> *Jawab:* Aam aadmi ki rozana zarooratein — police, tehsil, ration, school, bijli, sadak, transfer — lagbhag sab **state** ke paas hain. MP ka rishta Centre se hai. Isliye MP state ke officer ko order nahi de sakta, sirf keh sakta hai. Bheed MLA ke paas jaati hai, MP ke paas nahi.

**2. MPLADS ki asli taakat kya hai (paisa nahi)?**

> *Jawab:* Rs 5 crore/saal seedha district authority ko jaata hai, MP sirf **suggest** karta hai. Taakat "kis gaon mein" ke faisle mein hai. Aur kyunki uske andar 6-8 vidhan sabha seats hain, yeh fund uske apne MLAs ke saath rishta banane/bigadne ka tool ban jaata hai.

**3. MP kiski machine par chunav ladta hai, aur isse kya nateeja nikalta hai?**

> *Jawab:* Apne 6-8 MLAs ki machine par — kyunki 1,800-2,500 booth ek aadmi nahi sambhal sakta. Nateeja: MP lehar par zyada depend karta hai aur MLA zameen par. Isliye wave elections mein MP seats zyada palatti hain, MLA seats kam.

**4. Split voting (LS mein ek party, VS mein doosri) irrational kyun nahi hai?**

> *Jawab:* Voter do alag naukriyon ke liye do alag aadmi chun raha hai — MLA se woh apna rozana kaam maangta hai, MP se desh/bada project/party ka chehra. Alag job description, alag chunav.

**5. Rajya Sabha ka member kaun chunta hai, aur isse woh seat kya ban jaati hai?**

> *Jawab:* Janta nahi, **MLAs** chunte hain. Matlab woh seat party ke haath mein hai — isliye woh party ka sabse mehnga **pad** ban jaati hai, jo un logon ko diya jaata hai jo chunav nahi lad sakte ya jinko "adjust" karna hai.

### What this means for you

Ek practical rule jo tumhe seedha kaam aayega: **kaam le jaane se pehle poocho ki woh kaam kiske darwaze mein hai.** State ka kaam MLA ke paas, Centre ka kaam MP ke paas. Galat darwaze par sahi kaam le jaana sabse aam galti hai — aur uske baad log samajhte hain ki neta nikamma hai, jabki woh sirf galat aadmi tha.

Aur agar tum kabhi kisi MP se milo: yaad rakhna woh MLA se **zyada khula** hota hai naye logon ke liye — kyunki uske office mein bheed kam hai, aur uske paas woh rozana ka kaam nahi hai jisse woh apna naam banaye. Usko chahiye bade dikhne wale kaam, aur unke liye woh naye logon se milne ko tayyar rehta hai.
