# Chapter 12
## High command aur gut — har MLA do zindagi jeeta hai

---

### Ek aadmi, do chehre

Apne ilaake mein MLA raja hai. Log uske ghar ke bahar subah 7 baje se line lagate hain. Officer uska phone uthate hain. Uske aane par log khade ho jaate hain.

Wahi aadmi jab Mumbai ya Lucknow ya Delhi mein party ke daftar mein jaata hai, toh do ghante bahar baith sakta hai. Kisi PA ke phone ka intezaar kar sakta hai. Aur ek line se uska ticket kat sakta hai.

**Neeche raja, upar naukar.** Yeh do zindagi ek saath jeena har MLA ki roz ki reality hai, aur uske aadhe se zyada "ajeeb" faisle isi se explain hote hain.

Chapter 7 mein humne dekha tha ki uska pehla darr party hai, voter nahi. Yeh chapter us darr ke andar jaata hai.

### High command hai kya

"High command" ek shabd hai jiska matlab har party mein alag hai, lekin function ek hi hai: **woh kuch log jo ticket, pad, aur sarkar ke faisle karte hain.**

```
KIS TARAH KI PARTY          HIGH COMMAND ASAL MEIN KYA HAI

Rashtriya cadre-based       ek sangathan ka dhaancha —
                            adhyaksh, sangathan mantri,
                            sansadiya board. Vyakti se
                            zyada DHAANCHA faisla karta hai.

Parivaar-aadharit           ek parivaar, aur unke 3-5
                            bharosemand log. Faisla
                            aksar ek kamre mein.

Kshetriya (regional)        aksar ek hi neta — jo party
                            ka sansthapak hai. Party =
                            woh aadmi. Uske baad kya
                            hoga, yeh sabse bada sawaal
                            hota hai.
```

Lekin har case mein ek baat same hai:

> **High command ke paas do cheezein hain jo MLA ke paas nahi hain: TICKET aur PAD. Aur MLA ke paas do cheezein hain jo high command ke paas nahi hain: ZAMEEN KA RISHTA aur MACHINE.**

Yeh exchange hi poora rishta hai. Aur dono taraf ek doosre se darte hain — kyunki dono ek doosre ke bina adhoore hain.

### Gut (faction) kyun bante hain — inevitable ganit

Ab sabse zaroori baat. Log samajhte hain factions "khaandaani dushmani" ya "ego" se bante hain. Nahi. Woh **ganit** se bante hain.

Chapter 7 ka number wapas lao:

```
Ek party mein serious aspirants:  1,500-4,000
Tickets:                          288

Matlab: har ticket ke peeche 5-15 log line mein hain,
        aur unme se sirf 1 ko milega.
```

Ab socho — us 5-15 logon ka asli dushman kaun hai? Doosri party ka candidate? **Nahi.** Doosri party ka aadmi unse ticket nahi cheenta. Unka ticket unki apni party ka aadmi cheenta hai.

**Isliye har party ke andar competition doosri party se zyada tez hoti hai.** Aur jab competition tez ho, toh log group bana lete hain — kyunki akela aadmi high command ke saamne kuch nahi hai, lekin 20 MLA ka group kuch hai.

```
FACTION KA BANNA:

  akela MLA           →  ek line se ticket kat sakta hai
  20 MLA ka group     →  high command ko sochna padega

Isliye faction banana MLA ke liye BEEMA (insurance) hai,
gaddari nahi. Woh apne ticket ko safe kar raha hai.
```

Aur high command ko yeh pata hai. Isliye woh factions ko khatam nahi karta — **woh unhe balance karta hai**. Ek faction ko zyada taakat mili toh doosre ko thoda upar utha do. Yeh weakness nahi, technique hai:

> **Do kamzor gut ek majboot gut se behtar hain. Kyunki ek majboot gut kal tumhe replace kar sakta hai.**

### Faction bante kis cheez par hain

Alag-alag states mein alag-alag, lekin patterns yeh hain:

```
1. JAATI/SAMUDAY par
   Ek samuday ke MLA ek saath. Sabse aam aur
   sabse takatwar. (Chapter 18 poora ispe hai.)

2. ILAAKA (region) par
   State ke ek hisse ke log — kyunki unke mudde
   same hain (paani, industry, upeksha ka
   ehsaas).

3. KISI BADE NETA ke aas-paas
   Ek senior neta jiske paas paisa/pahunch hai,
   uske aas-paas juniors jud jaate hain. Woh
   unko ticket dilwata hai, woh uska number
   badhate hain.

4. PEEDHI (generation) par
   Purane vs naye. "Humne 30 saal diye" vs
   "ab naya khoon chahiye."
```

### Faction ka rozana ka kaam

Faction sirf ticket ke waqt active nahi hota. Uska roz ka kaam hota hai:

```
- apne logon ke transfer/posting karwana
  (isliye har faction ka apna "apna" mantri chahiye)

- apne logon ko board/corporation ke pad dilana

- apne ilaake mein fund/project khinchna

- aur sabse zaroori: high command tak apni AWAAZ
  pahunchana — "hamare bina yeh state nahi chalega"
```

Aur yehi wajah hai ki **mantri pad itna keemti hai** — mantri sirf ek aadmi nahi hota, woh apne poore faction ka transfer-posting aur fund ka darwaza hota hai (chapter 16).

### High command ka hathiyaar

High command MLA ko control kaise karta hai? Uske paas 5 cheezein hain:

```
1. TICKET
   Sabse bada. "Agli baar dekha jayega" — yeh ek
   line MLA ko 5 saal line mein rakhti hai.

2. PAD
   Mantri, board chairman, corporation adhyaksh,
   morcha adhyaksh. Unlimited supply (ch.5, ch.7).

3. TRANSFER-POSTING KA ACCESS
   MLA ka poora local asar isi par tika hai (ch.6).
   Agar CM ka office uska phone nahi uthata, toh
   uski seat mein uski keemat girne lagti hai.

4. PAISA / RESOURCE
   Chunav ke waqt kis seat ko kitna milega.

5. JAANCH KA DAR
   Yeh sabse kroor hathiyaar hai, aur sabse
   contested (neeche).
```

### Aur MLA ka hathiyaar

Doosri taraf khali nahi hai. MLA ke paas bhi hai:

```
1. MACHINE
   High command ke paas vote nahi hain. Vote
   MLA ki machine mein hain (ch.5). Party badal
   sakti hai candidate, lekin uska block usi ke
   saath jaata hai — aur wahi asli sawaal hai.

2. GINTI (numbers)
   Agar 20 MLA ek saath khade ho jaayein, toh
   sarkar hil sakti hai. Yeh chapter 13 ka
   poora subject hai.

3. SANSTHA
   Jiske paas college/cooperative hai (ch.11),
   woh party ke bina bhi zinda reh sakta hai.
   Aur high command ko yeh pata hota hai —
   isliye aise aadmi se woh alag tareeke se
   baat karta hai.
```

Ab poora balance samjho:

> **Jitni bhaari MLA ki apni machine aur sanstha, utni kam high command ki pakad. Aur jitna zyada MLA party ke naam par jeeta hua hai, utni zyada pakad.**

Isliye har MLA chupchaap ek hi kaam karta rehta hai: apne aap ko party ke naam se kam aur apne naam se zyada jeetne layak banana. Chapter 14 poora isi par hai.

### Contested corner

**Kya jaanch agencies political hathiyaar hain?**

- **Camp A:** Haan. Aksar dekha gaya hai ki jaanch tab tez hoti hai jab koi neta virodhi dal mein hai, aur party badalne ke baad dheemi ho jaati hai. Iske aankde aur timelines par bahut likha gaya hai.
- **Camp B:** Nahi. Jaanch kanoon ke hisaab se chalti hai, aur agar bhrashtachar hua hai toh party badalne se woh mit nahi jaata. Timing ka ilzaam har vipaksh lagata hai — jab woh khud satta mein tha tab bhi wahi hota tha.

**Is kitab ka position:** yeh sabse garam, sabse contested bahas hai aur main ise settle nahi karunga. Lekin architecture ke liye itna zaroor samjho: **jab kisi cheez par shak hota hai ki woh hathiyaar hai, toh woh hathiyaar ki tarah KAAM karne lagti hai — kyunki log uske dar se pehle hi jhukne lagte hain.** Asli istemal ho ya na ho, dar ka asar asli hota hai.

### Incentive equation

```
HIGH COMMAND:
  Chahiye:  seats, aur andar koi itna bada na ho
            jaye ki khatra ban jaye
  Deta hai: ticket, pad, access
  Darr:     bagawat, dal-badal, aur ek majboot gut
  Isliye:   woh factions ko todta nahi — BALANCE
            karta hai. Do kamzor > ek majboot.

MLA:
  Chahiye:  ticket (survival), aur apne logon ke kaam
  Deta hai: seat, aur assembly mein vote
  Darr:     akele pad jaana
  Isliye:   woh gut mein jaata hai — yeh beema hai,
            gaddari nahi

GUT KA LEADER (senior neta):
  Chahiye:  apni ginti badhaana — ginti = bhaav
  Deta hai: juniors ko ticket dilwana, unke kaam
  Darr:     uske log toot jayein
  Isliye:   woh apne logon ke liye ladta dikhta hai —
            yeh uski poonji hai
```

### Baban ki kahani — part 12

Baban 49 ka. Uski credit society ab 2,100 members ki hai. Uske 22 naukri wale parivaar ab 22 nahi, 60 hain (unke rishtedaar bhi jud gaye).

Us saal uska MLA — jiske saath woh 20 saal se tha — party ke andar ek badi ladai mein phans gaya. State mein do gut the: ek CM ke saath, ek ek senior mantri ke saath. Uske MLA senior mantri wale gut mein the.

Faisla hua CM ke haq mein. Aur teen mahine mein Baban ne woh dekha jo usne kitaabon mein kabhi nahi padha:

```
- MLA ka phone mantriyon ke office mein uthna kam ho gaya
- uske ilaake ke do bade project "review" mein chale gaye
- uske ilaake ka tehsildar badal gaya — aur naya aadmi
  doosre gut ka tha
- uske do purane karyakarta dheere-dheere doosre gut ke
  ek naye ladke ke saath dikhne lage
```

Chhe mahine mein, bina koi announcement ke, uske MLA ki taakat aadhi ho gayi.

Baban ne ek raat MLA se poocha: "Sahab, aapko kya lagta hai, aapka ticket katega?"

MLA ne bahut thake hue kaha: "Ticket ka faisla us kamre mein hoga jahan mera koi nahi hai. Maine 20 saal seat par kaam kiya. Lekin maine 20 saal us KAMRE mein kaam nahi kiya."

Baban ne us raat register mein sabse important line likhi:

> **Har neta do jagah kaam karta hai — apni seat par, aur party ke kamre mein. Zyadatar log sirf pehli jagah kaam karte hain, kyunki wahan mehnat dikhti hai. Lekin ticket doosri jagah tay hota hai. Aur jo doosri jagah kaam nahi karta, uska 20 saal ka kaam ek line se mit jaata hai.**

Aur neeche, ek aur baat jo usko us saal samajh aayi:

> **Gut ek galat cheez nahi hai. Akela MLA nanga hota hai. Lekin gut chunte waqt tum sirf aadmi nahi chunte — tum apna BHAVISHYA chunte ho. Aur agar tumhara gut haar gaya, toh tumhari seat par kiya hua kaam tumhe nahi bachayega.**

Us saal ke aakhir mein Baban ne pehli baar apne dam par ek cheez ki, jo usne pehle kabhi nahi ki thi: woh khud state capital gaya, apni credit society ka ek programme lekar, aur teen naye logon se mila — dono guton se.

Register mein us raat:

> **Aaj se main sirf ek aadmi ka aadmi nahi rahunga. Main apni ginti alag banaunga. Kyunki jo aadmi kisi EK ke saath bandha hai, uska bhavishya usi ke bhavishya ke saath doobta hai — aur main 20 saal se doob raha tha, mujhe pata hi nahi tha.**

### Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

**1. "Neeche raja, upar naukar" ka kya matlab, aur isse kya explain hota hai?**

> *Jawab:* Apne ilaake mein MLA ke paas asar aur izzat hai; party ke daftar mein woh line mein khada ek aadmi hai jiska ticket ek line se kat sakta hai. Isse explain hota hai ki uska pehla darr party ka hai (ch.7), aur woh apna itna time party ke andar politics mein kyun lagata hai.

**2. Faction ganit se kyun bante hain, ego se nahi?**

> *Jawab:* Ek ticket ke peeche 5-15 apne hi log hain. Matlab asli competition apni party ke andar hai. Akela MLA high command ke saamne kamzor hai, 20 ka group nahi. Isliye gut banana **beema** hai — apne ticket ko safe karne ki koshish, gaddari nahi.

**3. High command factions ko khatam kyun nahi karta?**

> *Jawab:* Kyunki do kamzor gut ek majboot gut se behtar hain. Ek majboot gut leadership ko replace kar sakta hai; do kamzor gut ek doosre ko rokte rehte hain aur dono ko high command ki zaroorat padti rehti hai. Balance karna technique hai, kamzori nahi.

**4. High command ke 5 hathiyaar aur MLA ke 3 hathiyaar kya hain?**

> *Jawab:* High command: ticket, pad, transfer-posting ka access, paisa/resource, aur (contested) jaanch ka dar. MLA: apni machine (vote uske paas hain), ginti (20 MLA milkar sarkar hila sakte hain), aur sanstha (party ke bina bhi zinda rehne ka base).

**5. Kaun sa MLA high command se sabse kam darta hai?**

> *Jawab:* Woh jiske paas apni machine aur apni sanstha hai, aur jo apne naam par jeetta hai — party ke naam par nahi. Uska block uske saath jaata hai, isliye party usse alag tareeke se baat karti hai. Isliye har MLA chupke se apne aap ko "party se bada" banane ki koshish karta rehta hai (ch.14).

### What this means for you

Kisi MLA ke saath baat karte waqt ek baat kabhi mat bhoolna: **uske do bosses hain — uska voter aur uska high command — aur woh high command se zyada darta hai.** Isliye jo cheez usko party ke andar mazboot dikhaye (bheed, data, "mere paas yeh hai jo doosron ke paas nahi"), woh uske liye seedha keemti hai.

Aur agar tum kabhi kisi neta ko samajhna chaho ki woh abhi kis haalat mein hai, toh yeh poocho: **"woh kis gut mein hai, aur woh gut abhi upar hai ya neeche?"** Uska poora saal bhar ka behavior — kitna khula hai, kitna ghabraaya hua hai, kitna kharch karne ko tayyar hai — usi ek jawab se bandha hota hai.
