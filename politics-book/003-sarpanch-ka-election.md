# Chapter 3
## Sarpanch ka election — poora system, sabse chhote size mein

---

### Gaon se shuru

Scientist ko cell samajhna ho toh woh poora sharir nahi kholta. Ek cell microscope ke neeche rakhta hai, kyunki cell mein wahi sab kuch hai jo sharir mein hai — bas itna chhota ki ek nazar mein dikh jaye.

Indian politics ka cell hai **sarpanch ka election**. 600-1000 voters. Har cheez jo aage Delhi tak milegi — block ka ganit, paisa, jaati, todne ki chaal, sarkari fund ka khel — sab yahan maujood hai, aur itne chhote size mein ki tum poora game ek saath dekh sakte ho.

Isliye yeh chapter dhyan se padho. Aage ke 18 chapters isi ka bada version hain.

### Pehla sawaal: is kursi mein hai kya?

Sarpanch ka honorarium mazaak jaisa hota hai — kai states mein kuch hazaar rupaye mahina. Lekin sarpanch ke elections mein log lakhs kharch karte hain, aur kuch jagah (jahan zameen mehngi hai) crores tak ki khabrein aati hain. *(Reported — aise numbers kaagaz par nahi hote.)*

Toh chapter 1 wala sawaal wapas: **yeh log kya khareed rahe hain?**

Kursi ke andar jo hai, uski list banao:

```
SARPANCH KE HAATH MEIN KYA HAI:

1. PAISA (upar se aata hua)
   Central/state government ab gaon ke development ka paisa
   SEEDHA gram panchayat ke account mein bhejti hai —
   sadak, naali, paani, shauchalay, sab ke fund.
   Kaam kaun karega, kis thekedar se, kis quality ka —
   panchayat decide karti hai. Matlab sarpanch decide karta hai.

2. LISTS (sabse underrated taakat)
   Garib kalyan ki har scheme ko ek LIST chahiye —
   kis parivar ko ghar milega, kise pension, kise ration
   ka labh. Yeh beneficiary lists banane mein panchayat
   ka haath hota hai. Jo list banata hai, woh decide
   karta hai kiska number pehle aayega.

3. KAAGAZ
   Rehne ka dakhla, character ka dakhla, chhoti NOCs —
   gaon ki zindagi ke choti-choti kaagazi zarooratein
   panchayat se hokar jaati hain.

4. IZZAT + LADDER
   Sarpanch gaon ka chehra hai. Tehsil aur zila ke neta
   usse baat karte hain. Yeh political career ki seedhi
   ka pehla danda hai — yahan se panchayat samiti, zila
   parishad, aur phir MLA ticket ka raasta dikhta hai.
```

Ab equation clear hai: honorarium chhota, control bada. **Chapter 1 ka formula, sabse chhote scale par: kamai kursi ki taakat se hogi, salary se nahi.**

### Campaign kaise ladta hai — 600 voters ka game

Yahan TV nahi hai, akhbaar nahi hai, speech ka stage nahi hai. Sab kuch chapter 2 ka ganit hai, seedha aur nanga:

**Step 1: Hisaab.** Har serious candidate ke dimaag mein (ya kisi Baban ki copy mein) gaon ka poora map hota hai: kaun sa khaandaan kitne vote, kaun sa mohalla kiske saath, pichhli baar kya hua tha. 600 vote = 12-15 blocks. Bas.

**Step 2: Mukhiya deals.** Har block ke mukhiya se alag baat. Kisi ko naali ka waada, kisi ko bete ke kaam ka, kisi ko sirf izzat — "tumhare aangan se hi parcha bharunga." Deal block se hoti hai, voter se nahi.

**Step 3: Bhandara.** Khaana politics ka sabse purana instrument hai. Community bhoj ka matlab sirf pet bharna nahi — yeh public commitment hai. Jo tumhara khaana kha gaya, woh gaon ke saamne tumhare aangan mein baitha. Ab uska palatna mehnga ho gaya.

**Step 4: Aakhri raat.** Jahan paisa ya sharaab chalti hai (aur bahut jagah chalti hai — reported), woh aakhri 48 ghante mein chalti hai, mukhiyaon ke through, list ke hisaab se. Chapter 2 ka contested corner yaad rakhna: yeh paisa vote pakka karta hai ya sirf "serious candidate" ka signal deta hai — iska settled jawab kisi ke paas nahi. Lekin itna settled hai ki jo candidate yeh nahi karta, usko log serious nahi maante.

**Step 5: Todna.** Agar saamne wale ka ganit tumse behtar hai, toh uska block todo. Uski jaati ka, uske mohalle ka ek aur candidate khada karwa do. Yeh vote-katwa chapter 2 mein mila tha — sarpanch election mein yeh sabse aam chaal hai.

### Reservation — ek rule jo poora game palat deta hai

Ek cheez sarpanch level par hai jo upar ke chapters mein alag roop mein milegi: **seat reservation ka rotation.** Sarpanch ki seat rotation se mahilaon ya SC/ST ke liye reserved hoti rehti hai.

Ab dekho system ka asli behavior. Jis saal seat mahila ke liye reserve hui, gaon ke purane players kya karte hain? Apni gharwali, bahu, ya behen ko khada kar dete hain. Gaon janta hai asli sarpanch kaun hoga. Iske liye ek khula naam bhi hai — **"sarpanch pati"** (sarpanch ka pati / asli operator).

Isko cynically mat padho, architecture ki tarah padho: **rule ne kursi ka NAAM badla, toh system ne kursi ke PEECHE ka aadmi same rakh liya.** Rule sudharne walon ne form badla; incentive same tha, toh function same raha. Yeh pattern yaad rakhna — aage har level par milega: jab bhi koi naya rule aata hai, system usko todta nahi, uske ird-gird naya rasta bana leta hai.

*(Doosra side bhi sach hai, aur contested hai: kai jagah reserved seat se aayi mahila sarpanch ne dheere-dheere asli control le liya. Rule kabhi-kabhi function bhi badal deta hai — bas hamesha nahi, aur dheere.)*

### Incentive equation

```
CANDIDATE ka hisaab:
  Lagata hai:  lakhs (bhandara, aakhri raat, mukhiya deals)
  Paata hai:   funds ka control + lists ka control + kaagaz
               + izzat + ladder ka pehla danda
  Darr:        haar gaya toh paisa gaya AUR izzat bhi —
               gaon mein haar chhupti nahi
  Isliye:      sarpanch election mein dushmani asli hoti hai;
               yeh career ka sabse personal election hai

MUKHIYA ka hisaab:
  Yeh uska quarterly results season hai. 5 saal mein ek baar
  uske block ki keemat khulke lagti hai. Dono taraf se offer
  aayenge. (Aur Tatya wali galti yaad hai? Dono ko haan
  nahi bolna hota.)

GAON ka hisaab:
  Gaon jaanta hai ki jeetne wala paisa nikalega (thekon se,
  lists se). Phir bhi vote deta hai. Kyun? Kyunki sawaal
  "imaandaar vs be-imaan" ka hai hi nahi. Sawaal hai:
  "MERE block ka kaam kaun karega?" Apna be-imaan paraye
  imaandaar se behtar hai — yeh voter ka rational sauda hai.
```

### Baban ki kahani — part 3

Baban ab 19 ka hai. Sarpanch ka agla election aa gaya, aur is baar Bhaskar thekedaar phir khada hai — lekin is baar Bhaskar ne ladke ko bulaya jiske baare mein sab kehte the: "Tatya ka beta, hisaab ka pakka."

Bhaskar ne kaha: "Pichhli baar teen vote se haara. Is baar tu mera hisaab rakh."

Baban ne teen mahine kaam kiya, aur teen cheezein ki.

**Pehli:** copy ka vote-map update kiya. 600 nahi, ab 640 vote the. Har block ke aage teen columns: kitne vote, mukhiya kaun, word ka pakka? Usne apne baap wali galti ulti karke istemaal ki — jin mukhiyaon ka record "dono taraf haan bolne ka" tha, unke blocks ko usne ginti se hi nikal diya. Unpe kharcha zero. "Jo bikta hai roz, woh khareedna bekaar."

**Doosri:** usne dekha ki purane sarpanch ka sabse bada block — 90 vote ka — do khaandaanon ka joint block hai, aur dono khaandaanon mein bahu ke lene-dene par andar hi andar khatas hai. Baban ne Bhaskar se kaha: "In dono ko todna paisa se nahi hoga. Chhote khaandaan ke ladke ko panchayat member ka ticket do — apne panel se khada karo." Woh ladka mana. 90 ka block toot ke 55-35 ho gaya, aur 35 Bhaskar ke panel mein aa gaye.

**Teesri:** aakhri hafte mein Bhaskar bola: "Ab bhandara bada karte hain, poore gaon ka." Baban ne roka: "Poora gaon nahi. Sirf woh 5 blocks jahan hum 250 tak pahunchte hain. Baaki blocks ka khaana kharcha hai, vote nahi." Bhaskar ne pehli baar kisi 19 saal ke ladke ki baat par 30,000 ka plan kaata.

Result: Bhaskar 31 vote se jeeta.

Us shaam Bhaskar ke aangan mein dhol baja. Baban peeche khada tha, copy haath mein. Panchayat ke naye members mithai baant rahe the, aur ek purana aadmi Baban ke paas aakar dheere se bola: "Ladke, tune kitna liya is kaam ka?"

Baban ne sach bola: "Kuch nahi."

Aadmi hansa: "Toh tu sabse mehnga aadmi hai is gaon ka. Jiska bhaav abhi laga nahi, uska bhaav sabse zyada hota hai."

Baban ko baat us waqt samajh nahi aayi. Chapter 5 mein aayegi — usko bhi, tumko bhi.

### Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

**1. Sarpanch ka honorarium hazaaron mein hai, election ka kharcha lakhs mein. Kursi ke andar woh 4 cheezein kya hain jo is gap ko explain karti hain?**

> *Jawab:* (1) Sarkari funds ka control (kaam, thekedar, quality decide karna), (2) beneficiary lists ka control (kiska number pehle), (3) kaagaz/dakhle/NOCs, (4) izzat + political ladder ka pehla danda. Yeh sab milakar kursi ki asli keemat hai.

**2. Bhandara sirf khaana kyun nahi hai?**

> *Jawab:* Woh public commitment hai. Gaon ke saamne tumhare aangan mein baithkar khaana = sabke saamne tumhare side dikhna. Iske baad palatna socially mehnga ho jaata hai. Politics mein khaana contract-signing ceremony hai.

**3. "Sarpanch pati" phenomenon architecture ke baare mein kya sikhata hai?**

> *Jawab:* Rule ne kursi ka naam badla (mahila reserved), lekin incentive same raha, toh system ne form ke peeche function same rakh liya — asli operator wahi purana aadmi. General sabak: naya rule system ko todta nahi, system uske ird-gird rasta bana leta hai. (Contested side: kabhi-kabhi, dheere-dheere, rule function bhi badal deta hai.)

**4. Voter jaanta hai ki jeetne wala paisa nikalega. Phir bhi vote deta hai — rational kaise?**

> *Jawab:* Voter ka sawaal "imaandaar vs be-imaan" nahi hai, "mere block ka kaam kaun karega" hai. Jo candidate uske block ka kaam karega, woh thoda kha bhi lega toh voter ka net sauda positive hai. Apna be-imaan > paraya imaandaar.

**5. Baban ne Bhaskar ka poore-gaon-ka-bhandara kyun kaata?**

> *Jawab:* Chapter 2 ka ganit: jeet 250 par hai, 640 par nahi. Jo blocks ginti mein nahi aate (pakke virodhi ya bikau), unpe kharcha vote nahi banata. Campaign ka paisa target blocks par lagta hai, poore gaon par nahi. Precision > volume.

### What this means for you

Jab bhi koi bada political phenomenon samajhna ho — MLA ki chaal, party ka faisla, defection — usko sarpanch size par chhota karke socho: "600 voters ke gaon mein yeh cheez kaisi dikhti?" 90% baar wahi structure niklega: blocks, mukhiya, funds, todna. Aur jab kisi neta se milo, yaad rakhna ki woh is cell-level game ko jeet kar aaya hai — usse blocks aur hisaab ki bhasha mein baat karoge toh woh tumhe apna aadmi samjhega, upar-upar ki "development" bhasha mein karoge toh bahar ka.
