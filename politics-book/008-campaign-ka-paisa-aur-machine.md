# Chapter 8
## Campaign ka paisa aur machine — sabse bada loop

---

### Woh sawaal jo poori kitab ka darwaza hai

Chapter 1 mein humne poocha tha, ab poora jawab denge:

```
MLA candidate ka LEGAL kharcha limit:  Rs 28-40 lakh
                                        (bade states mein 40)
MLA ki 5 saal ki total salary:          roughly Rs 60 lakh
                                        se 1.5 crore (state
                                        ke hisaab se)

Aur zameen par jo kharcha hota hai, uske baare mein
har journalist, har consultant, har candidate ek hi
baat kehta hai: woh limit se kai guna hota hai.
(Reported — is cheez ka koi audited number nahi ho sakta,
kyunki jo limit se upar hai woh kaagaz par aata hi nahi.)
```

Toh sawaal: **agar koi aadmi apni 5 saal ki salary se zyada paisa ek election par laga raha hai, toh woh paisa wapas kahan se aata hai?**

Is chapter mein hum woh poora loop kholenge. Aur main ise seedha likhunga, kyunki jhaadu se dhakne se architecture samajh nahi aata.

### Pehle: paisa jaata kahan hai

Campaign ka kharcha 4 buckets mein jaata hai. Public sirf pehla bucket dekhti hai.

**Bucket 1 — DIKHNE WALA (~10-20%)**
Poster, banner, hoarding, jhande, gaadi par speaker, office ka tent, pamphlet, social media. Yeh sab bill wala kharcha hai, aur yehi mostly official return mein dikhaya jaata hai.

**Bucket 2 — MACHINE KA KHARCHA (sabse bada, ~40-50%)**
Yeh chapter 5 ki pyramid ko 40 din chalane ka kharcha hai:

```
250-350 booth × 2-4 log per booth        = 600-1,200 log
+ gaadi wale, khaana banane wale,
  parchi baantne wale, bheed jutane wale = aur sekdon

Har aadmi ka roz ka: dihadi + khaana + chai + petrol
(reported rates: Rs 300-2,000/din, kaam aur jagah
ke hisaab se)
```

Chalo mota hisaab lagao: 800 log × Rs 700/din × 30 din = **Rs 1.68 crore**. Sirf logon ka. Sirf ek MLA seat ka. Aur limit Rs 40 lakh hai.

Ab tumhe apne aap samajh aa gaya hoga ki limit ka number aur zameen ka number kyun match nahi karte.

**Bucket 3 — GAADI AUR LOGISTICS (~15-25%)**
Election ke dinon mein 50-200 gaadiyaan. Petrol, driver, rent. Election ke din voter ko ghar se booth tak laane ka poora operation.

**Bucket 4 — SEEDHA VOTER TAK (contested, aur sabse chhupa)**
Yahan cash, sharaab, saree, bartan, "gaon ke mandir ko chanda", "bhandara" — sab aata hai. Yeh 100% undeclared hota hai. Iska size seat-to-seat itna alag hai ki koi average likhna imaandaari nahi hoga.

### Ab asli sawaal: paisa aata kahan se hai

Char raaste. Pehle do legal, teesra grey, chautha kaala.

**Rasta 1 — KHUD KA PAISA (legal)**

Isliye candidates aksar businessmen hote hain — thekedar, builder, shikshan sanstha wale, sugar/cooperative wale, transport wale, doctor. Politics mein aane se pehle paisa banana normal hai, kyunki entry fee hai.

Yeh baat bahut important hai: **India mein garib aadmi ke liye MLA banna ka darwaza lagbhag band hai — chunav ke kharche ki wajah se.** Isliye assembly mein karodpati MLAs ka anupaat itna zyada hota hai (ADR jaisi sansthaayein har election ke baad yeh numbers publish karti hain).

**Rasta 2 — PARTY KA PAISA (legal)**

Party fund se seat ko paisa milta hai. Lekin har seat ko poora nahi milta — party apne "A-grade" seats par zyada lagati hai. Isliye chapter 7 wali baat: jo candidate apna kharcha khud utha sakta hai, woh party ke liye sasta hai.

**Rasta 3 — SUPPORTERS AUR VYAPARI (grey)**

Yahan chapter 4 ka builder wapas aa gaya, bas bade size mein.

```
KAUN DETA HAI                 USKO KYA CHAHIYE
builder / developer           zameen, permission, zoning,
                              file ka na rukna
thekedar                      sarkari theke
sharab/mining/transport       licence, permit, route
                              (yeh sab sarkari permission
                               wale dhandhe hain)
shikshan sanstha / hospital   affiliation, land, approval
local vyapari                 rojmarra ki suraksha —
                              inspector, tax, licence
```

Dekho in sabme ek cheez common hai: **yeh sab woh dhandhe hain jinke andar sarkar ka haath hai.** Jis dhandhe ko sarkari permission chahiye, wahi dhandha politics ko funding karta hai. Yeh accident nahi hai — yeh system ka sabse seedha incentive hai.

Yeh paisa "chanda" ke roop mein bhi aata hai aur cash ke roop mein bhi. Legal route ke liye electoral bonds jaisi cheezein bani thi (Supreme Court ne 2024 mein unhe rad kar diya) — lekin bade paise ka ek hissa hamesha bina naam ke chalta raha hai.

**Rasta 4 — KAALA PAISA (illegal, aur openly discussed)**

Cash jo kisi kaagaz par nahi aata. Kahan se: upar wale rasta-3 walon se, ya candidate ke apne cash dhandhe se.

Main yahan kaise-karo nahi likh raha — sirf structure bata raha hoon, kyunki structure samjhe bina system samajh nahi aata:

- Election ke waqt cash ki movement badh jaati hai, aur election commission ki flying squads har election mein karodon rupay pakadti hain. Jo pakda jaata hai woh chal rahe paise ka ek chhota hissa hai.
- Kharcha aksar "third party" ke through hota hai — samarthak ka mandal, koi trust, koi mitra — taaki candidate ke official return mein na aaye.

### Ab loop band karo — return kahan se aata hai

Yeh chapter ka dil hai. Ek candidate Rs 2-5 crore lagata hai (reported range, seat ke hisaab se bahut alag). Woh wapas kaise nikalta hai?

```
INVESTMENT                      RETURN (5 saal mein)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
election ka kharcha    ──►      1. THEKE
                                   sarkari kaam — sadak, building,
                                   supply. Apne aadmi ko dilao,
                                   ya khud partner raho.

                                2. ZAMEEN
                                   Sabse bada. MLA ko pehle pata
                                   hota hai: road kahan aayegi,
                                   zone kab badlega, project kahan
                                   lagega. Us information ka
                                   asli mol yeh hai ki tum us
                                   zameen ko pehle le sakte ho.

                                3. TRANSFER-POSTING KA ASAR
                                   Jahan officer ki posting par
                                   asar ho, wahan us department
                                   ka rojmarra ka lena-dena
                                   asar mein aa jaata hai.
                                   (Reported — poora chapter 16.)

                                4. PERMISSION KA DHANDHA
                                   Licence, NOC, permit — jinko
                                   chahiye woh rasta dhoondhte hain.

                                5. INSTITUTION
                                   College, hospital, cooperative,
                                   sugar mill. Yeh sabse safed aur
                                   sabse takatwar return hai —
                                   poora chapter 11 ispe hai.
```

Ab poora loop ek nazar mein:

```
        ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
        │                                          │
        ▼                                          │
   PAISA (khud + supporter + kaala)                │
        │                                          │
        ▼                                          │
   CAMPAIGN (machine + gaadi + voter tak)          │
        │                                          │
        ▼                                          │
   VOTE  ──────►  KURSI                            │
                     │                             │
                     ▼                             │
              TAAKAT (theke, zameen, transfer,     │
                      permission, institution)     │
                     │                             │
                     └─────────────────────────────┘
                        agla election isi se funded
```

**Yeh loop poore Indian politics ka engine hai.** Aur ab woh baat samjho jo is kitab ka sabse bada point hai:

Is loop mein koi villain nahi hai. Har player rational hai. Builder ko permission chahiye. Karyakarta ko dihadi chahiye. Voter ko kaam chahiye. Candidate ko kursi chahiye. **Har aadmi apna sahi kaam kar raha hai, aur inka jod ek aisa system banata hai jo kisi ne design nahi kiya.**

Jab tak yeh loop chal raha hai, "imaandaar aadmi aage aaye" wali baat sirf ek ichha hai — kyunki imaandaar aadmi ke paas entry fee nahi hoti.

### Booth machine — 40 din ka operation

Paisa ek taraf. Ab machine ka actual kaam dekho, kyunki paisa isi par sabse zyada jaata hai.

```
CAMPAIGN KA CALENDAR (mota-mota)

2 saal pehle    aspirant apni machine banata hai,
  se           mayyat-shaadi ka attendance, chhote kaam

6 mahine       survey, ticket ki lobbying (chapter 7),
  pehle        apni bheed dikhana

45 din         ticket announce. Poora dhaancha activate:
  pehle        booth in-charge niyukt, voter list ka
               kaam, panna/page pramukh banana

30 din         daura shuru — gaon-gaon, mohalla-mohalla.
               Roz 8-15 gaon. Har jagah 15-20 minute.
               Kaam bolne ka nahi, DIKHNE ka.

15 din         bade neta ki sabha, bheed jutana
               (bheed ka apna kharcha hota hai — gaadi,
                khaana. Bheed vote nahi hai, bheed
                SIGNAL hai — "yeh jeet raha hai")

7 din          booth-wise micro-management. Kaunse ghar
               abhi tay nahi, kis mohalle mein jaana baaki

48 ghante      sabse important. Sab kuch band ho jaata hai
               officially. Zameen par sab kuch chalta hai.

Voting day     ek hi kaam: APNA VOTER GHAR SE NIKALO
               (chapter 9 poora ispe hai)
```

Dekho is calendar mein sabse lambi cheez kya hai — **2 saal pehle wali line.** Yeh chapter 6 ke Baban wala sabak hai: election ke din sirf ginti hoti hai us kaam ki jo pehle ho chuka hai.

### EC ke rules aur unke gaps

Election Commission ke paas asli taakat hai — woh candidate ko nakaam kar sakta hai, chunav rad kar sakta hai. Aur woh sach mein kaam karta hai: aachaar sanhita lagti hai, gaadi rukti hai, cash pakda jaata hai.

Lekin do structural gaps hain, aur inko samajhna zaroori hai:

**Gap 1 — Limit candidate par hai, PARTY par nahi.**
Candidate Rs 40 lakh se zyada nahi kharch kar sakta. Lekin **party apne prachar par jitna chahe kharch kar sakti hai** — bade neta ki rally, poore state ka vigyapan, helicopter. Woh candidate ke khaate mein nahi jaata.

Isliye ek badi party ka candidate aur ek chhoti party ka candidate — dono ki limit same hai, lekin unke peeche khada paisa bilkul alag hai.

**Gap 2 — Jo kaagaz par nahi aaya, woh naapa nahi ja sakta.**
Har candidate kharche ka hisaab jama karta hai, aur woh hisaab lagbhag hamesha limit ke andar hota hai. Kyunki jo cash mein hua, woh hisaab mein aaya hi nahi.

Yeh EC ki nakami nahi hai — yeh ek structural cheez hai. **Koi bhi limit us paise ko nahi rok sakti jo kaagaz par exist hi nahi karta.**

### Contested corner

**Paisa jeet dilata hai ya jeetne wala paisa khinchta hai?**

- **Camp A:** Paisa jeet dilata hai. Zyada paisa = zyada machine = zyada vote.
- **Camp B:** Ulta hai. Jo candidate jeetta hua dikhta hai, uske paas paisa apne aap aa jaata hai (supporter jeetne wale par lagate hain). Matlab paisa jeet ka **result** hai, kaaran nahi.

Sach mein dono chalte hain — ek doosre ko badhate hue. Lekin agar tumhe ek line chahiye: **paisa zaroori hai, kaafi nahi.** Bina paise ke ladna lagbhag namumkin hai; sirf paise se jeetna bhi lagbhag namumkin hai. Bahut saare bahut ameer candidate har election mein haarte hain.

### Baban ki kahani — part 8

Baban 37 ka. Yuva morcha ke pad ne usko zile bhar mein ghumaya. Ab teen vidhan sabha seats mein log usko naam se jaante hain.

Us saal party ne usko ek seat ka "chunav prabhari" banaya — matlab candidate nahi, lekin us seat ka poora operation uske haath mein.

Pehli baithak mein candidate ne (ek shikshan sanstha wala aadmi, paise wala) poocha: "Kitna lagega?"

Baban ne register khola aur poora hisaab rakh diya: itne booth, itne log, itne din, gaadi, khaana. Number bada tha.

Candidate ne kaha: "Kam karo."

Baban ne kaha: "Theek hai. Toh batao kaun se 60 booth chhod dein."

Candidate chup ho gaya. Phir bola: "Poora chahiye."

Us raat Baban ne register mein likha:

> **Har candidate pehle kharcha kam karna chahta hai. Phir usko dikhta hai ki kharcha kam karne ka matlab booth chhodna hai. Aur booth chhodne ka matlab haarna hai. Isliye har candidate aakhir mein poora paisa lagata hai — chahe woh kahin se bhi aaye.**

Do hafte baad usne woh cheez dekhi jiska usne sirf naam suna tha.

Ek raat 11 baje candidate ke ghar ek gaadi aayi. Ek aadmi utra, andar gaya, 20 minute baad chala gaya. Candidate bahar aaya aur bola: "Ab paise ki chinta mat karo."

Baban ne poocha nahi ki kaun tha. Usko zaroorat nahi thi — us aadmi ki gaadi par ek construction company ka naam tha, aur us seat mein ek nayi ring road proposed thi.

Election hua. Unka candidate 11,000 vote se jeeta.

Teen mahine baad Baban gaya candidate se milne — ab MLA sahab. Baithak mein woh hi construction wala aadmi baitha tha, chai pee raha tha, bilkul aaram se.

Baban ne us raat register mein woh line likhi jisko woh apni poori zindagi ka sabse imaandaar vaakya maanta raha:

> **Election chunav ke din khatam nahi hota. Us din to sirf hisaab shuru hota hai. Jo paisa aaya tha, uska bill 5 saal mein chukta hai — theke se, zameen se, file se. Isliye MLA banne wala aadmi azaad nahi hota. Woh us din se ek KARZDAAR hota hai.**

Aur uske neeche, chhota sa:

> **Aur karzdaar aadmi imaandaar nahi reh sakta. Yeh uski niyat ka sawaal nahi hai. Yeh uske khaate ka sawaal hai.**

### Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

**1. Legal limit Rs 40 lakh, machine ka hisaab crores mein. Yeh gap structurally kaise chalta hai?**

> *Jawab:* (a) Limit **candidate** par hai, **party** ke prachar par nahi — party unlimited kharch kar sakti hai. (b) Jo paisa cash mein chala, woh official return mein aata hi nahi, aur jo kaagaz par nahi hai use naapa nahi ja sakta. Isliye har return limit ke andar hota hai.

**2. Campaign ka sabse bada kharcha kaunsa bucket hai, aur kyun?**

> *Jawab:* Machine — 600-1,200 logon ko 30-45 din chalana (dihadi + khaana + gaadi). Kyunki 250-350 booth par ek hi din, ek hi waqt logon ki zaroorat hai, aur booth chhodne ka matlab seedha vote chhodna hai.

**3. Loop poora bolo: paisa kahan se aata hai aur wapas kaise nikalta hai?**

> *Jawab:* Aata hai: khud ka paisa + party + permission-wale dhandhon se (builder, thekedar, sharab/mining/transport, shikshan) + kaala paisa. Nikalta hai: theke, zameen ki advance information, transfer-posting ka asar, permission ka dhandha, aur institution. Kursi se taakat, taakat se paisa, paise se agla chunav.

**4. Wahi dhandhe politics ko funding kyun karte hain — builder, mining, sharab, shikshan?**

> *Jawab:* Kyunki in sabme sarkar ka haath hai — inhe permission, licence, zameen, approval chahiye. Jis dhandhe ko sarkari permission chahiye, usko politics se rishta chahiye. Yeh system ka sabse seedha incentive hai, koi sazish nahi.

**5. "Paisa jeet dilata hai" — dono camps aur imaandaar nateeja?**

> *Jawab:* Camp A: paisa → machine → vote. Camp B: jo jeetta dikhta hai uske paas paisa khud aa jaata hai, matlab paisa jeet ka result hai. Imaandaar nateeja: **paisa zaroori hai, kaafi nahi** — bina paise ladna namumkin, sirf paise se jeetna bhi namumkin (bahut ameer candidates har baar haarte hain).

### What this means for you

Jab bhi kisi MLA se milo, ek sawaal apne dimaag mein rakhna (poochhna nahi): **"iska chunav kisne funded kiya?"** Uska jawab uske 5 saal ke behavior ka 50% explain kar dega — kis department mein woh dilchaspi leta hai, kis file ke peeche padta hai, kaun uske ghar bina register ke andar jaata hai. Aur doosri baat: MLA ka sabse bada dabav "public ki seva" nahi hai — woh apna **khaata** hai. Jo cheez uske khaate ko halka kare (kharcha bachaye, machine sasti chalaye, agli baar ka risk kam kare), woh uske liye seedha keemti hai.
