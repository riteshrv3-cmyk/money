# Chapter 9
## Election day — jab machine discharge hoti hai

---

### Ek din, jisme 5 saal ki ginti hoti hai

Ab tak humne banaya: blocks (ch.2), machine (ch.5), ticket (ch.7), paisa (ch.8). Aaj woh din hai jab yeh sab ek saath, ek hi din, 12 ghante mein kaam mein aata hai.

Aur pehle ek galatfehmi todo. Election ke din **koi mind nahi badalta.**

```
Election ke din ka kaam:
  ❌  logon ko convince karna
  ✅  jo pehle se apna hai, usko GHAR SE NIKALNA
```

Yeh farak samajh lo toh poora din samajh aa jayega. Voting day persuasion ka din nahi, **logistics** ka din hai. Chapter 2 mein turnout ko hathiyar kaha tha — aaj woh hathiyar chalega.

Kyun? Simple ganit:

```
Maano tumhare pakke samarthak 80,000 hain.
Agar unme se 60% nikle    → 48,000 vote
Agar unme se 75% nikle    → 60,000 vote

Antar: 12,000 vote. Bina ek bhi aadmi ka mann badle.
```

Bahut saari seats 12,000 se kam ke antar par tay hoti hain. **Isliye election ka din turnout ka operation hai, opinion ka nahi.**

### Din se pehle: 48 ghante ka silence

Voting se 48 ghante pehle prachar band ho jaata hai — officially. Sabha nahi, jaloos nahi, TV/radio par prachar nahi.

Aur zameen par ulta hota hai: **yeh 48 ghante sabse zyada kaam ke hote hain.**

```
Kya hota hai (reported):
  - Ghar-ghar chupchaap sampark — bade jamawade nahi,
    chhoti tolis
  - Parchi banti hai (voter slip) aur ghar-ghar pahunchti hai
  - Mukhiyaon ke saath aakhri baithak — "kitne pakke?"
  - Jahan cash/sharaab chalti hai, woh isi window mein chalti hai
  - Baahar se aaye karyakarta seat chhodte hain (rule hai
    ki jo voter nahi hai woh seat mein nahi ruk sakta) —
    lekin sab nahi jaate
  - Doosri taraf ke naraz logon se aakhri baat
```

Yeh prachar-band ka rule bekaar nahi hai — usne shor rok diya. Lekin usne **rishta** nahi roka, aur asli kaam rishte se hi hota hai.

### Parchi — sabse chhoti, sabse taakatwar cheez

Voter slip. Ek kaagaz ka tukda jisme voter ka naam, booth number, aur serial number hota hai.

Yeh cheez do kaam karti hai, aur doosra wala non-obvious hai:

**1. Practical:** Voter ko pata chal jaata hai kis booth par jaana hai, uska number kya hai. Line mein waqt bachta hai.

**2. Asli kaam — TRACKING:** Parchi baantne wala aadmi janta hai kis ghar mein parchi gayi. Aur voting ke din woh check kar sakta hai ki us ghar se log nikle ya nahi.

Sarkar (EC) bhi apni official slip deti hai. Lekin party ki apni parchi alag hoti hai — party ke rang aur nishaan ke saath (rules ke andar rehkar). Aur uska asli maqsad voter ki madad nahi, **apne voter ki pehchaan** hai.

### Voting ka din — ghante ke hisaab se

```
05:30  Booth par party ka table lagta hai (booth se
       nirdharit doori par, rule ke hisaab se).
       Har booth par 2-4 log: ek parchi ke liye, ek
       list ke liye, ek ghar bhejne ke liye.

06:00  Polling agent booth ke ANDAR baithta hai.
       Yeh alag aadmi hai aur yeh sabse zaroori aadmi
       hai (neeche poora section).

07:00  Voting shuru. Pehle do ghante mein aksar bujurg
       aur pakke samarthak aate hain.

09:00  Pehla hisaab. Har booth se number aata hai:
       "abhi tak kitne percent."

11:00  DOOSRA hisaab, aur yahan asli kaam shuru hota hai.
       Har booth ka aadmi apni list dekhta hai: kaunse
       PAKKE ghar se abhi tak koi nahi aaya.
       → gaadi bhejo, aadmi bhejo, phone karwao

13:00  Sabse dheema time. Dopahar mein log nahi nikalte.
       Machine ka poora zor isi waqt lagta hai.

15:00  Aakhri push. Jo ghar bache hain, unpar personally
       koi jaata hai — aksar wahi aadmi jiska us ghar
       se rishta hai.

17:00  Voting band. Booth ke bahar ka hisaab: "hamare
       kitne gaye."

18:00  Poore seat ka number jodna. Yahan se andaaza
       lagta hai — kaunse booth par turnout kam raha,
       kaunse mein zyada, aur kiska matlab kya hai.

Raat   Karyakartaon ka khaana. Ginti abhi baaki hai,
       lekin kaam khatam.
```

Dekho is poore din mein kya nahi hai: **koi bhashan nahi, koi convincing nahi, koi rally nahi.** Sirf list, gaadi, phone, aur ghar.

### Polling agent — sabse underrated aadmi

Har booth ke andar har candidate ka ek agent baith sakta hai. Woh vote nahi daal sakta, prachar nahi kar sakta. Toh woh karta kya hai?

```
1. PEHCHAAN
   Woh dekhta hai ki jo aadmi vote dene aaya hai, woh
   sach mein wahi hai. Farzi vote (kisi aur ke naam par
   vote dena) ko rokne ka pehla pehra yehi aadmi hai.

2. HISAAB
   Woh andar baithkar note karta hai kaun-kaun aaya.
   Bahar wale ko live number milta rehta hai — is se
   pata chalta hai kaunse ghar bache hain.

3. GAWAAH
   Agar kuch galat hota hai (machine, process, dhamki),
   woh likhit shikayat kar sakta hai. Uske bina koi
   record nahi banta.

4. MAUJOODGI KA DABAAV
   Sabse bada kaam yehi hai. Jis booth par saamne wale
   ka koi aadmi nahi hai, wahan galat kaam ka darwaza
   khulta hai. Aur jis booth par dono taraf ke log
   baithe hain, wahan sab theek chalta hai.
```

Ab Baban ka chapter 6 wala sabak wapas dekho — 312 mein se 97 booth khali the. Iska matlab sirf itna nahi tha ki "vote kam padenge." Iska matlab yeh bhi tha ki **97 booth par unka koi aankh nahi thi.**

Aur ab ek line jo poori kitab mein sabse practical hai:

> **Booth ke andar ek aadmi baithane ke liye tumhe ek aisa aadmi chahiye jo (a) us gaon ka voter ho, (b) poora din free ho, (c) padha-likha ho ki likh sake, aur (d) tumhara itna apna ho ki bik na jaye. Aise 300 aadmi 40 din mein nahi milte. Woh 5 saal mein bante hain.**

Yeh ek line hai jo poori machine ki zaroorat ko justify karti hai.

### Counting day — alag jaanwar

Ginti alag din hoti hai, aur uska apna operation hota hai.

```
- Counting centre par har candidate ke counting agent
  baithte hain, har table par.
- Ginti ROUND mein hoti hai — har round mein kuch booths
  ki EVM. Isliye result dheere-dheere banta hai,
  aur har round ke baad pata chalta hai kaunse ilaake
  se kya mila.
- Yahan hi candidate ko booth-wise sach dikhta hai:
  kis mukhiya ne kaam kiya, kisne dhoka diya.
```

Aur yeh aakhri baat bahut important hai. **Counting sirf result nahi deti — woh 5 saal ke rishton ka report card deti hai.**

Jis mukhiya ne kaha tha "mere 400 vote pakke hain," uske booth ka number aa jaata hai. Agar wahan se sirf 150 mile, toh us aadmi ka bhaav agli baar gir jaata hai. Chapter 2 mein Tatya ke saath yehi hua tha, bas gaon ke level par.

Isliye counting ke baad jeet ya haar se pehle, har candidate ke office mein ek aur kaam hota hai: **booth-wise list nikal kar dekhna kaun sach bol raha tha.**

### EVM aur VVPAT — sirf itna

Yeh sawaal har baar aata hai, toh chhota aur imaandaar jawab:

- Machine se voting hoti hai, aur VVPAT ek parchi dikhata hai jise voter dekh sakta hai; woh parchi ek box mein girti hai.
- Ilzaam lage hain, aur adaalat aur EC dono ne bar-bar review kiya hai; kuch VVPAT parchiyon ki ginti ka milaan bhi hota hai.
- **Is kitab ka position:** yeh ek contested public bahas hai, aur is kitab ka kaam machine samjhana hai, is bahas ko settle karna nahi.

Lekin ek architectural baat zaroor samjho, jo bahas se alag hai: **jo party booth par apne log nahi baitha sakti, woh kisi bhi system mein — kaagaz ya machine — kamzor rahegi.** Technology gawaah ki zaroorat khatam nahi karti.

### Contested corner

**48 ghante ka cash/sharaab kitna asar karta hai?**

- **Camp A:** Decisive hai close seats mein. Aakhri raat mein jo baant deta hai, woh 2-5% ghuma leta hai.
- **Camp B:** Bahut kam. Log sabse le lete hain (dono taraf se), aur vote apne block ke hisaab se hi dete hain. Baantne wale ko lagta hai usne khareeda, asal mein usne sirf apna signal diya.

Chapter 2 mein bhi yeh aaya tha, aur jawab abhi bhi settle nahi hai. Practitioner log aksar beech ka kehte hain: **paisa apne pakke logon ko nikalne mein madad karta hai, doosre ke logon ko todne mein nahi.** Matlab woh turnout ka tool hai, persuasion ka nahi — jo is poore chapter ki theory se match karta hai.

### Incentive equation

```
CANDIDATE:
  Aaj woh kuch nahi kar sakta. Uska poora control
  khatam ho chuka hai. Isliye yeh uske liye sabse
  ghabrahat wala din hota hai — 2 saal ka kaam,
  crores ka paisa, aur aaj sirf DEKH sakta hai.

BOOTH PRAMUKH:
  Aaj uska exam hai. Uska number counting ke din
  khulega. Agar uske booth se kam nikla, uski keemat
  gir gayi — chahe wajah kuch bhi ho.
  Isliye: woh sach mein zor lagata hai, kisi ke
  kehne se nahi, apni izzat ke liye.

VOTER:
  Aaj uski keemat sabse zyada hai — 5 saal mein ek din.
  Isliye aaj uske ghar sab aayenge.
  Kal se koi nahi aayega.

MUKHIYA:
  Aaj uska daawa test ho raha hai. Usne jitne vote
  ka waada kiya, utne nikalne chahiye — warna uska
  bhaav gir jayega.
```

Woh aakhri box sabse zyada revealing hai. **Election ke din machine isliye nahi chalti ki candidate ne paisa diya. Woh isliye chalti hai ki har aadmi ka apna number us din publicly test ho raha hai.**

### Baban ki kahani — part 9

Baban 40 ka. Ab woh do zile mein "chunav ka aadmi" hai — jise party mushkil seat par bhejti hai.

Us saal usko ek seat mili jo party 3 baar se haar rahi thi. Candidate naya tha, paisa theek tha, machine kamzor.

Baban ne 45 din pehle poori seat ka naksha banaya: 289 booth. Har booth ke aage teen column — pichhli baar hamara vote, is baar ka andaaza, aur wahi purana column: **"aaj kaun sambhal raha hai."**

68 khaane khali the.

Candidate ne kaha: "Paise se bhar denge."

Baban ne is baar woh nahi kiya jo usne 29 ki umar mein kiya tha — chup nahi raha. Usne kaha: "Nahi. In 68 mein se 40 hum chhod denge. Baaki 28 par main apne purane log doosri seat se laoonga — woh un gaon ke rishtedaar hain."

Candidate bhadka: "40 booth chhod doge? Woh 20,000 vote hain!"

Baban ne kaha: "Woh 20,000 vote nahi hain. Wahan hamare pichhli baar ke vote 3,000 the. Agar main wahan kiraye ke log baithaunga toh woh log paisa lekar din bhar chai peeyenge aur shaam ko doosri taraf ko batayenge ki hamari haalat kya hai. **Khali booth se bura ek beimaan booth hai.** Woh 40 chhodo. Us paisa se main baaki 249 booth par gaadi badha dunga."

Candidate ne maan liya — mann se nahi, par maan liya.

Voting ke din 11 baje wala hisaab aaya. Baban ne apne 249 booth ki list dekhi aur 61 aise ghar-group nikale jahan se ek bhi vote nahi pada tha. Har ek par aadmi bheja. Do gaon mein gaadi kam padi toh usne apni khud ki gaadi bhej di aur khud 4 ghante ek chai ki tapri par baitha raha.

Result: unka candidate 2,300 vote se jeeta. Teen haar ke baad.

Counting ke din raat ko, jab sab jashn mana rahe the, Baban ne booth-wise list nikali aur do ghante akela baitha raha.

Usne dekha: jin 40 booth ko unhone chhoda tha, wahan unko 2,900 vote mile the — matlab pichhli baar jitne hi. **Chhodne se kuch nahi gaya.**

Aur woh 28 booth jahan usne doosri seat se apne purane log laaye the — wahan turnout aur unka vote dono baaki seat se zyada tha.

Register mein us raat ki line:

> **Chunav ke din tum vote nahi banate. Tum sirf apne bane hue vote ko GHAR SE BAHAR NIKALTE ho. Jo aadmi yeh samajh gaya, woh aadhe paise mein jeetta hai. Jo nahi samjha, woh dugna paisa lagakar bhi haarta hai — kyunki woh un logon par kharch karta hai jo kabhi uske the hi nahi.**

Aur neeche, chhoti si:

> **Khali booth se bura ek beimaan booth hai. Yeh baat maine 20 saal mein seekhi, aur ab main isse kabhi nahi bhoolunga.**

### Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

**1. Election ke din ka asli kaam kya hai, aur uska ganit kya hai?**

> *Jawab:* Turnout — apne pakke voter ko ghar se nikalna, kisi ka mind badalna nahi. Ganit: 80,000 pakke samarthak par 60% vs 75% turnout ka antar 12,000 vote hai, bina ek bhi aadmi ka mann badle. Bahut si seats isse kam antar par tay hoti hain.

**2. Parchi ka asli (non-obvious) kaam kya hai?**

> *Jawab:* Tracking. Jo aadmi parchi baantta hai woh jaanta hai kis ghar mein gayi, aur voting ke din check kar sakta hai ki us ghar se log nikle ya nahi. Voter ki suvidha upar ka kaam hai; **apne voter ki pehchaan aur ginti** asli kaam hai.

**3. Polling agent ke 4 kaam, aur sabse bada kaunsa?**

> *Jawab:* Pehchaan (farzi vote rokna), hisaab (live turnout), gawaah (likhit shikayat), aur maujoodgi ka dabaav. Sabse bada chautha — jis booth par saamne wale ka koi nahi baitha, wahan galat kaam ka darwaza khulta hai.

**4. Counting sirf result kyun nahi deti?**

> *Jawab:* Woh booth-wise report card deti hai. Jis mukhiya ne 400 vote ka daawa kiya tha, uske booth ka number khul jaata hai. Isse har rishte ka sach saamne aata hai aur agli baar ke liye har aadmi ka bhaav tay hota hai.

**5. "Khali booth se bura ek beimaan booth hai" — kyun?**

> *Jawab:* Khali booth par tumhara vote utna hi aayega jitna waise bhi aata. Kiraye ka beimaan aadmi paisa bhi khata hai, kaam bhi nahi karta, aur upar se tumhari asli haalat (kitne pakke, kahan kamzor) doosri taraf pahuncha deta hai. Matlab nuksaan double — paisa gaya aur information bhi gayi.

### What this means for you

Kisi bhi candidate ki asli taakat naapni ho toh ek hi sawaal poocho: **"tumhare kitne booth par apna aadmi hai?"** Yeh sawaal uski poori 5 saal ki mehnat ko ek number mein compress kar deta hai — aur is sawaal ka jawab dete hi uske chehre se pata chal jayega ki woh kis haalat mein hai. Aur yaad rakhna, election ke aakhri hafte mein har candidate ki sabse badi tension ek hi hoti hai: **usko pata nahi hota ki uske apne log sach bol rahe hain ya nahi.** Woh andhera hi is machine ka sabse purana, sabse mehnga dard hai.
