# Chapter 6
## Seat ki anatomy — MLA asal mein hai kya

---

### Pehle ek galatfehmi todte hain

School mein padhaya jaata hai: **"MLA vidhan sabha ka sadasya hota hai. Uska kaam kanoon banana hai."**

Yeh technically sach hai aur practically lagbhag jhoot hai.

Sochne ki cheez: ek state assembly saal mein kitne din baithti hai? Aksar 20-40 din ke aas-paas — kai states mein isse bhi kam. Matlab saal ke 365 dinon mein se lagbhag **330-345 din MLA vidhan sabha mein hai hi nahi.**

Aur jin 30 dinon mein woh hai, wahan bhi asli faisle kaun karta hai? Bill sarkar (cabinet) laati hai, party whip jaari karti hai, aur MLA ko party line ke hisaab se vote dena hota hai — nahi toh dal-badal kanoon ke tahat sadasyata ja sakti hai (chapter 13). Matlab uska **vote bhi zyadatar uska apna nahi hota.**

Toh phir — 330 din woh karta kya hai? Aur log usse chunte kyun hain?

**Iska jawab is chapter ka poora kaam hai, aur yeh jawab poori kitab ka sabse zaroori correction hai.**

### Do job descriptions

```
KAAGAZ WALI JOB (jo constitution mein likhi hai)
  - Vidhan sabha mein baitho
  - Bill par bahas karo, vote do
  - Sawaal poochho, committee mein raho
  - Apne kshetra ki awaaz uthao
  Time: saal ke ~30 din

ASLI JOB (jo voter expect karta hai)
  - Har mayyat, har shaadi, har satyanarayan pooja mein aao
  - Roz subah logon ki shikayat suno
  - Police station, tehsil, hospital, school mein
    "phone karo" — logon ke kaam karwao
  - Sadak, naali, paani, transformer — kaam dilao
  - Apne 1,000-3,000 karyakarta sambhalo
  - Party ke andar apni jagah bachao
  Time: saal ke 365 din
```

Voter MLA se kanoon nahi maangta. Voter MLA se **pahunch** maangta hai — sarkar tak pahunchne ka rasta. Uske liye MLA vidhayak nahi, **sifarish ka darwaza** hai.

Aur yehi wajah hai ki ek MLA jo assembly mein 5 saal mein ek baar nahi bola, phir bhi bade margin se jeet jaata hai — jabki ek MLA jo assembly mein shandar bhashan deta hai, gaon mein mayyat par nahi pahuncha, woh haar jaata hai.

**System bura nahi hai. System ne bas ek doosra kaam maang liya hai.** Voter ko roz ki zindagi mein jo chahiye, MLA usko wahi de raha hai.

### Seat ka size — aur usse jo sab kuch nikalta hai

```
Ek assembly seat (approx):  2-4 lakh voters
                            250-350 polling booths
                            50-150 gaon, ya sheher ke
                            kuch wards
```

Ab is size se teen cheezein apne aap nikalti hain, aur teenon MLA ka behavior samjha deti hain:

**1. Woh sabko nahi jaan sakta.** 3 lakh log. Agar woh roz 100 logon se mile toh poori seat se milne mein 8 saal lagenge — aur term 5 saal ki hai. Isliye woh **majboori se** blocks aur mukhiyaon ke through kaam karta hai. Chapter 2 ka ganit yahan physical reality ban jaata hai.

**2. Woh akela kuch nahi kar sakta.** 250-350 booth. Ek din mein sab par apna aadmi baithana hai. Isliye machine (chapter 5) uski life-line hai, shauk nahi.

**3. Uska time hi uska sabse scarce resource hai.** Paisa woh jugaad kar lega. Time nahi kar sakta. Isliye MLA ki poori zindagi ek **time-allocation problem** hai: aaj kis gaon jaunga, kiski mayyat par jaunga, kiska phone uthaunga.

Yeh teesri baat sabse zyada kaam ki hai. Agar tumhe kabhi kisi MLA ke behavior ka reason samajh na aaye, toh yeh poochho: **"iske time ke hisaab se yeh rational hai kya?"** Aksar jawab mil jayega.

### MLA ke haath mein sach mein kya hai

Log samajhte hain MLA ke paas bahut taakat hai. Sach isse zyada interesting hai — uske paas **thodi si asli taakat aur bahut sara asar** hai:

```
JO SEEDHA USKE HAATH MEIN HAI:
  - MLA fund (MLALADS): apne kshetra mein chhote kaam
    suggest karne ka fund. Amount state ke hisaab se
    alag hota hai, aur MLA ise seedha kharch nahi karta —
    woh kaam SUGGEST karta hai, paisa administration
    ke through jaata hai.
  - Vidhan sabha mein sawaal aur bahas ka adhikaar
  - Kuch committees mein jagah

JO SEEDHA NAHI HAI, LEKIN ASAR HAI (yeh bada hissa hai):
  - Transfer-posting: officer ko hatana/lagwana — MLA
    order nahi de sakta, lekin mantri/CM tak uski baat
    jaati hai. Ruling party ka MLA ho toh asar bahut,
    virodhi dal ka ho toh bahut kam.
  - Police: FIR, jamanat, case ki "dekhbhal"
  - Sarkari kaam ke tender aur thekedar
  - Scheme ki beneficiary lists — kiska ghar, kiski
    pension, kiska connection pehle
  - College/hospital admission ki sifarish
  - Local officers ka rojmarra ka behavior
```

Ab dhyan do — is list ka 80% "asar" wala hissa hai, "adhikaar" wala nahi. Aur yehi baat MLA ki poori zindagi ka darr banati hai:

> **MLA ki taakat kanoon se nahi aati. Uski taakat is baat se aati hai ki log maante hain uski baat chal jaati hai.**

Aur is tarah ki taakat bahut nazuk hoti hai. Jis din ruling party se woh bahar hua, ya jis din uska mantri se rishta bigda, us din uska phone uthna kam ho jaata hai — aur jis din phone uthna kam hua, us din uska block hilna shuru ho jaata hai.

**Isliye ruling party ka MLA hona aur virodhi dal ka MLA hona do bilkul alag naukriyaan hain.** Ek ke paas asar hai, doosre ke paas sirf awaaz. Yeh baat chapter 12 aur 13 mein bahut kaam aayegi — dal-badal ki aadhi wajah yehi hai.

### Ek din — MLA ki zindagi

Kitaab mein numbers se zyada yeh cheez samjhati hai. Ek aam din:

```
06:30  Ghar par log aana shuru — 20-40 log, ghar ke
       bahar hi baith jaate hain
07:00  Chai, akhbaar, PA ke saath aaj ki list
08:00  Janta darbar — ek-ek karke sunna, parchi par
       likhna, phone karna (chapter 10 ka poora subject)
10:30  Gaadi — do gaon ka daura
11:30  Ek mayyat (mrityu par jaana sabse zaroori kaam
       hai — is par chapter 10 mein poora section hai)
13:00  Ek shaadi mein 10 minute, khaana nahi, sirf
       dikhna aur photo
14:30  Tehsil office — do kaam ke liye officer se milna
16:00  Apne karyakartaon ke saath baithak — kaun naraz
       hai, kiska kaam atka hai
18:00  Ek programme ka udghatan — 4 minute ka bhashan
20:00  Ghar — phir se 15-20 log
22:00  Phone: mantri ke PA ko, zila adhyaksh ko,
       do naraz karyakarta ko
23:30  Din khatam
```

Ab is din mein dhoondho: kanoon banane ka kaam kahan hai? **Kahin nahi.** Aur phir bhi yeh aadmi apni job theek kar raha hai — kyunki uski asli job yeh hi hai.

Ek cheez aur note karo: is poore din mein woh **kisi ek aadmi ke liye 3-4 minute se zyada** nahi de raha. Yeh kanjoosi nahi hai. 3 lakh voters aur 24 ghante ke beech ka ganit yehi allow karta hai.

### Ruling party vs virodhi — do alag zindagi

```
                     RULING PARTY MLA      VIRODHI DAL MLA
Phone uthta hai      haan                  aksar nahi
Transfer mein baat   chalti hai            nahi chalti
Fund ka kaam         theek chalta hai      atakta hai
Karyakarta           saath hain            dheere-dheere
                                           khiskte hain
Agli baar jeetne     achha                 mushkil
ka chance
```

Yeh table poore Indian politics ka ek chhupa hua engine hai. Kyunki iska matlab yeh hai ki **haarne ka nuksaan sirf 5 saal ka nahi hota — woh compounding hota hai.** Virodhi dal mein baithe MLA ke log dheere-dheere ruling party mein chale jaate hain, kyunki karyakarta ko suraksha aur kaam chahiye (chapter 5), aur woh virodhi dal ke paas nahi hai.

Yehi wajah hai ki dal-badal itna common hai (chapter 13), aur yehi wajah hai ki MLA ke liye "sarkar mein hona" bhashan ka mudda nahi, **survival** ka mudda hai.

### Contested corner

**Kya MLA ka kaam badal raha hai?**

- **Camp A:** Haan. Ab log direct portal, helpline, aur online se kaam karwa lete hain; MLA ka bicholiya wala role kam ho raha hai. Naya voter sadak-naali se zyada bade mudde par vote deta hai.
- **Camp B:** Nahi. Portal ne form badla, kaam nahi. Aaj bhi log portal par form bharkar MLA ke office aate hain ki "iska follow-up karwa do." Bicholiya ki zaroorat khatam nahi hui, bas ab woh digital form ke saath aata hai.

Dono mein sach hai. Sabse imaandaar version shayad yeh hai: **bade sheher mein Camp A zyada sach hai, gaon aur chhote sheher mein Camp B.**

### Incentive equation

```
MLA:
  Deta hai:  pahunch (sifarish), maujoodgi (har sukh-dukh
             mein dikhna), kaam
  Paata hai: kursi + uska poora conversion (chapter 1),
             machine, aur agli baar ticket ka dawa
  Darr:      TEEN darr, is order mein:
             1. ticket kat jaye (party se) — chapter 7
             2. seat haar jaye (voter se)
             3. sarkar chali jaye (asar khatam) — upar wali table
  Isliye:    uska sabse zyada time party ke andar aur apne
             karyakartaon par jaata hai, aam voter par nahi

VOTER:
  Deta hai:  block ka vote
  Paata hai: sarkar tak pahunchne ka ek rasta
  Darr:      kaam ke liye koi phone karne wala na ho
  Isliye:    woh us MLA ko chunta hai jo "milta hai",
             us MLA ko nahi jo "sahi bolta hai"
```

Woh "TEEN darr" wali line dobara padho, aur unka **order** dekho. MLA ka pehla darr voter nahi hai — party hai. Kyunki agar ticket hi kat gaya, toh voter ke paas usko chunne ka option hi nahi bachega. Yeh order poori kitab ka sabse counter-intuitive sach hai, aur chapter 7 poora isi par hai.

### Baban ki kahani — part 6

Baban 29 ka hai. Corporator ke paas 7 saal ho gaye. Uske paas ab 3 nahi, 11 booth hain — lagbhag 1,800 vote uske naam par hilte hain. Log usse "Baban bhau" bulate hain.

Us saal MLA ka election aaya. Corporator ne Baban ko MLA candidate ke campaign office mein bhej diya — "seekh ke aa."

Pehle hafte Baban ka bharam toota.

Woh sochta tha MLA ban-na corporator ban-ne ka bada version hai. Andar jaake dekha: **ganit alag hai.**

Corporator ke ward mein 22,000 vote the. Baban un 22,000 mein se lagbhag har mohalle ka naam jaanta tha. MLA ki seat mein **2 lakh 90 hazaar** vote the aur **312 booth**.

Pehle din campaign office mein ek badi deewar par booth ka naksha laga tha. 312 khaane. Har khaane mein teen number: pichhli baar kitne vote pade, hamein kitne mile, aur ek column jo khali tha — "**aaj kaun sambhal raha hai**".

Baban ne gina. **97 khaane khali the.** Matlab 312 mein se 97 booth par candidate ka koi aadmi nahi tha.

Usne candidate ke bhai se poocha: "Yeh 97 kaise bharenge?"

Bhai ne kandha uchkaya: "Paisa dedenge, log mil jayenge."

Baban ne kuch nahi kaha. Lekin us raat usne apni copy — ab woh purani copy nahi, ek register tha — mein likha:

> **Paise se aadmi milta hai. Aadmi se BOOTH nahi milta. Booth par baithne wale ko us gaon ke logon ka naam pata hona chahiye. Woh 40 din mein nahi banta.**

Election hua. Candidate 4,000 vote se haara.

Baban ne baad mein hisaab lagaya — jin 97 booths par unka koi nahi tha, wahan unka average vote baaki booths se kaafi kam tha. Poora antar nikala toh woh 4,000 se zyada tha.

Usne register mein woh line likhi jo aage 20 saal uske kaam aayi:

> **Election ke din koi election nahi jeeta jaata. Us din sirf ginti hoti hai us kaam ki jo 5 saal mein hua ya nahi hua.**

Aur ek chhoti si baat aur — jo usne kisi ko batayi nahi. Jab candidate haara, toh haarne ke teen mahine ke andar us candidate ke paas aane wale logon ki bheed lagbhag khatam ho gayi. Baban ne dekha ki jo aadmi 40 din pehle "saheb saheb" karta tha, woh ab phone nahi uthata.

Usne register mein likha:

> **Log neta se nahi judte. Log us RASTE se judte hain jo neta ke paas hota hai. Rasta gaya, log gaye.**

### Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

**1. MLA saal ke ~30 din assembly mein hota hai. Baaki 330 din woh kya karta hai, aur uski "asli job description" kya hai?**

> *Jawab:* Asli job = **pahunch ka darwaza** banna. Janta darbar, mayyat/shaadi mein maujoodgi, police-tehsil-hospital mein phone karwana, kaam dilana, aur apni machine (karyakarta) sambhalna. Voter usse kanoon nahi maangta, sarkar tak rasta maangta hai.

**2. "MLA ki taakat 20% adhikaar aur 80% asar hai" — iska practical matlab kya hai?**

> *Jawab:* Uske paas seedha adhikaar bahut kam hai (MLALADS suggest karna, assembly mein bolna). Baaki sab — transfer, police, tender, lists — asar se hota hai, matlab tabhi jab log maanein ki uski baat chalti hai. Aur asar nazuk hai: sarkar badli ya mantri se rishta bigda toh asar khatam.

**3. Ruling party MLA aur virodhi dal MLA — asli farak kya, aur uska compounding effect kya?**

> *Jawab:* Ruling party mein phone uthta hai, kaam hota hai, karyakarta saath rehte hain. Virodhi dal mein kaam atakta hai, aur isliye karyakarta dheere-dheere doosri taraf khiskte hain. Matlab haar sirf 5 saal ka nuksaan nahi — machine bhi kamzor hoti jaati hai, jisse agli baar aur mushkil ho jaati hai. Yehi dal-badal ka bada karan hai.

**4. MLA ke teen darr kaunse, aur unka order kya hai — aur order counter-intuitive kyun hai?**

> *Jawab:* (1) Ticket katna, (2) seat haarna, (3) sarkar jaana. Pehla darr party ka hai, voter ka nahi — kyunki ticket hi nahi mila toh voter ke paas usse chunne ka option hi nahi. Bahar se lagta hai neta voter se darta hai; asal mein woh pehle party se darta hai.

**5. Baban ne kyun kaha ki "paise se booth nahi milta"?**

> *Jawab:* Booth par baithne wale ko us ilaake ke logon ke naam, rishte aur history pata honi chahiye — taaki woh pehchaan sake kaun apna hai, kaun nahi, aur kise ghar se nikalwana hai. Yeh rishta 5 saal mein banta hai; 40 din ka kiraye ka aadmi sirf kursi bhar sakta hai, kaam nahi kar sakta.

### What this means for you

Kisi bhi MLA se milne se pehle do cheezein pata karo: **(1) woh ruling party mein hai ya nahi**, aur **(2) uski seat kitne booth ki hai aur pichhli baar margin kitna tha.** Pehli baat batayegi ki uske paas asar hai ya nahi — matlab woh kuch karwa sakta hai ya sirf sun sakta hai. Doosri batayegi ki woh kitna surakshit hai — aur jo MLA jitna kam surakshit hai, woh utna hi zyada khula hai us cheez ke liye jo usko agli baar jeetne mein madad kare. Aur yaad rakhna: uska sabse scarce resource paisa nahi, **time** hai. Jo cheez uska time bachaye, uski keemat uske liye khud-ba-khud badi hai.
