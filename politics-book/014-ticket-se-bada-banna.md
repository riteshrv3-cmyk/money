# Chapter 14
## Ticket se bada banna — aur dynasty kyun banti hai

---

### Har neta ka chhupa hua lakshya

Ab tak humne dekha ki MLA phansa hua aadmi hai:

- Uska ticket party ke haath mein hai (ch.7)
- Uski taakat sarkar mein hone par tiki hai (ch.6)
- Uske gut ka bhavishya uske haath mein nahi (ch.12)
- Aur agar sab bigad jaye toh todna padta hai (ch.13)

Toh har samajhdar neta ek hi cheez ki taraf bhaagta hai, chahe woh kabhi khulke na bole:

> **"Main aisa ban jaun ki party mujhe zaroorat samjhe — main party ko zaroorat na samjhun."**

Iska matlab hai: **apne naam par jeetna, party ke naam par nahi.**

Yeh chapter us safar ke baare mein hai. Aur uske aakhir mein jo cheez banti hai, use log "dynasty" kehte hain — aur samajhte hain ki woh ek bimari hai. **Yeh chapter dikhayega ki woh bimari nahi, is machine ka natural nateeja hai.**

### Do tarah ke MLA

Sabse pehle yeh farak samjho, kyunki poori kitab mein yeh sabse kaam ka classification hai:

```
PARTY KE NAAM PAR JEETNE WALA
  - lehar aayi toh jeeta
  - party ka nishaan hi uski taakat hai
  - ticket kata toh khatam
  - doosri party mein jaake haar jaata hai
  - party usse "replaceable" maanti hai

APNE NAAM PAR JEETNE WALA
  - uska apna vote hai, party se alag
  - ticket kata toh nirdaleeya jeet sakta hai
  - doosri party mein jaake bhi jeetta hai
  - party usse DARTI hai
```

Ab poori kitab ka sabse practical test:

> **Ek neta ki asli taakat naapni ho? Yeh poocho: "agar iska ticket kat jaye aur yeh nirdaleeya lad le, toh kitne vote layega?"**
>
> Agar jawab "10,000" hai — woh party ka aadmi hai.
> Agar jawab "60,000" hai — party uska aadmi hai.

### Apna vote kaise banta hai

Party ke nishaan se alag, apna khud ka vote — yeh 4 cheezon se banta hai. Aur teeno hum pehle dekh chuke hain, ab jodenge:

**1. EHSAAN KA KHAATA (ch.10)**

Woh log jinke kaam usne khud karwaye. Yeh vote party ka nahi hai — yeh **uska** hai, kyunki us aadmi ne kaam hote hue dekha aur usne uska naam liya.

20 saal mein agar kisi ne 20,000 parivaaron ke chhote-mote kaam karwaye, toh uske paas ek aisa vote-bank hai jo kisi jhande se nahi bandha.

**2. SANSTHA (ch.11)**

College ke staff, cooperative ke members, mill ke supplier. Yeh log party dekhkar vote nahi karte — **woh apni rozi dekhkar karte hain.**

Yeh sabse pakka apna vote hai. Isliye ch.11 mein humne kaha tha ki sanstha "insurance" hai.

**3. JAATI/SAMUDAY KA NETRITVA (ch.18)**

Agar woh apne samuday ka sabse bada neta ban gaya, toh us samuday ka vote uska hai — chahe woh kisi bhi party mein ho. Party badalne par bhi block saath jaata hai.

**4. MACHINE JO USKE NAAM SE JUDI HO (ch.5)**

Aur yeh sabse nazuk cheez hai. Karyakarta ka rishta party se hai ya aadmi se? Chapter 13 mein humne dekha — **aadmi se.** Lekin yeh apne aap nahi hota. Yeh tab hota hai jab neta apne logon ke kaam **apne naam par** karwata hai.

Baban ne ch.11 mein yehi likha tha: *"naukri MLA ne di, lekin sifarish maine ki. Log usko yaad rakhte hain jisne unka naam liya."*

### Ab dynasty — aur kyun woh natural hai

Ab sabse important part. Ek neta ne 20-25 saal mein yeh sab bana liya:

```
- 20,000 parivaaron ka ehsaan ka khaata
- ek college / cooperative (500 naukri, har saal admission)
- apne samuday mein sabse bada naam
- 300 booth par apne log
- thekedaaron aur vyapariyon ka network
- officers ke saath 20 saal ke rishte
```

Ab woh 65 saal ka hai. Ek sawaal uske saamne khada hai:

> **"Yeh sab kiske paas jayega?"**

Aur ab har raaste ko soch kar dekho:

```
RASTA 1: Party ko de do
  → party kisi aur ko ticket degi. Uska poora
    network us naye aadmi ka ho jayega. Uska
    parivaar 2 saal mein bhool diya jayega.
    Aur uska college? Naya MLA usko tang karega.

RASTA 2: Apne sabse achhe karyakarta ko de do
  → woh aadmi 5 saal mein khud bada ho jayega
    aur uske parivaar ko nahi poochega. (Yeh
    hazaar baar hua hai.)

RASTA 3: Apne bete/beti ko de do
  → network bhi bacha, sanstha bhi bachi,
    parivaar bhi bacha
```

**Rasta 3 hi bachta hai.** Aur woh isliye bachta hai kyunki system mein koi aur mechanism hai hi nahi jo uski 25 saal ki poonji ko transfer kar sake.

Ab yeh line dhyan se padho, kyunki yeh is chapter ka dil hai:

> **Dynasty isliye nahi banti ki neta apne bachche se pyaar karta hai. Dynasty isliye banti hai ki Indian politics mein RETIREMENT ka koi rasta nahi hai. Jo aadmi kursi chhodta hai, uska sab kuch chala jaata hai — sanstha, network, suraksha, sab. Isliye woh kursi chhodta nahi, TRANSFER karta hai. Aur transfer sirf khoon ke rishte mein bharosemand hota hai.**

Aur party ko iss se dikkat kyun nahi hoti? Kyunki **party ke liye bhi yeh sabse aasan sauda hai:**

```
Party ka hisaab:
  Purana MLA hataao        → naya banane mein 10 saal
                             aur seat ka risk
  Uske bete ko ticket do   → machine ready, sanstha
                             ready, vote ready,
                             aur woh bhi ehsaanmand
```

**Dono taraf ka ganit dynasty ki taraf le jaata hai.** Isliye har party mein — har ek mein, exception nahi — dynasty milti hai.

### Dynasty ke 3 roop

```
1. SEEDHI (beta/beti)
   Sabse aam. Aksar pehle koi chhoti kursi
   (zila parishad, yuva morcha), phir ticket.

2. GHUMAKAR (patni/bhai/bahu)
   Jab beta chhota hai ya seat reserve ho gayi
   (ch.3 wala "sarpanch pati" ka bada version).

3. SANSTHA KE RASTE
   Beta pehle college/cooperative ka chairman
   banta hai. Wahan se apna base banata hai,
   phir ticket maangta hai. Yeh sabse "safed"
   raasta hai — woh neta ka beta nahi, "shiksha
   ka kaam karne wala" dikhta hai.
```

Teesra raasta sabse chalak hai, aur isliye sabse common ho raha hai.

### Contested corner

**Kya dynasty buri hai?**

- **Camp A:** Haan. Yogyata (merit) khatam ho jaati hai. Ek karyakarta 30 saal kaam kare, aur ticket ek 26 saal ke ladke ko mile jiska sirf baap bada tha. Isse system ke andar ki umeed (ch.5 ka fuel) mar jaati hai.
- **Camp B:** Utna nahi jitna lagta hai. Voter ko option hai aur woh dynasty ke logon ko harata bhi hai. Aur dynasty ka aadmi bachpan se politics dekhta hai — usko kaam ki samajh zyada hoti hai. Aur ek practical baat: har dhandhe mein parivaar chalta hai (business, doctor, film) — politics mein hi kyun aitraaz?

**Is kitab ka position:** yeh moral bahas hai aur main ise settle nahi karunga. Lekin **architecture** ke liye ek baat pakki hai: jab tak retirement ka koi rasta nahi hai aur jab tak neta ki poori poonji uske apne rishton mein bandhi hai, tab tak dynasty ka incentive rahega — chahe log usse pasand karein ya na karein.

Aur ek data-jaisi baat bhi honestly bolni chahiye: **dynasty ke log har baar nahi jeette.** Bahut baar haarte hain. Matlab voter poori tarah bebas nahi hai. Lekin unko ladne ka **mauka** aam aadmi se kai guna zyada aasan milta hai — aur asli asamanta wahan hai, result mein nahi.

### Incentive equation

```
PURANA NETA (60+):
  Uske paas:  25 saal ki poonji (network, sanstha,
              ehsaan, machine)
  Uska darr:  retirement = sab kuch khatam.
              Uski sanstha par naya MLA kabza karega,
              uske log 6 mahine mein bhool jayenge
  Isliye:     woh kursi chhodta nahi — TRANSFER
              karta hai. Aur transfer ke liye
              khoon sabse safe hai

PARTY:
  Uska hisaab: naya aadmi banane mein 10 saal +
               seat ka risk; beta lene mein ready
               machine + ehsaanmand aadmi
  Isliye:      party dynasty ko rokti nahi

KARYAKARTA (ch.5):
  Uska nuksaan: uski "umeed" wali seedhi ka aakhri
                danda kat gaya
  Lekin woh phir bhi rukta hai — kyunki uske paas
  suraksha aur pehchaan abhi bhi wahin hai
  (Aur yeh system ka sabse kroor hissa hai)

VOTER:
  Usko farak kam padta hai — kyunki uska sawaal
  "kaun" nahi, "mera kaam hoga ya nahi" hai.
  Aur beta aksar wahi network chala raha hota hai
```

### Baban ki kahani — part 14

Baban 54 ka. Uski credit society ab 4,000 members ki hai. Uske through lagbhag 90 parivaaron ko naukri mili hai. Teen wardon mein log usse "Baban dada" bulate hain.

Aur us saal jo hua, usne uski poori zindagi ka hisaab saamne rakh diya.

Uske MLA — 26 saal ka saath — bimaar pade. Do mahine aspataal mein. Party ne agle chunav ki taiyari shuru ki.

Baban ne pehli baar apne liye ticket maanga.

Woh state capital gaya. Teen din rukа. Sabse mila. Aur uski daleel simple thi: "26 saal. Meri society ke 4,000 member. 90 parivaar ki naukri meri sifarish se. Main is seat ko jaanta hoon."

Faisla aaya: ticket **MLA ke bete** ko mila. 31 saal ka ladka, jo teen saal pehle tak Pune mein naukri kar raha tha.

Baban zila adhyaksh se milne gaya — wahi purana aadmi, ab budha. Is baar Baban gussa mein nahi tha. Usne sirf ek sawaal poocha: "Sach batao. Ganit kya tha?"

Adhyaksh ne poori imaandaari se jawab diya, aur wahi jawab is chapter ka nichod hai:

"Baban, tumhare 4,000 member hain. Uske baap ke college mein 400 staff hain, 2,000 students, aur unke parivaar. Tumhare 90 parivaar naukri wale hain. Uske baap ke 500 hain. Tumhara ehsaan ka khaata 15 saal ka hai. Uske baap ka 26 saal ka hai — aur woh khaata **uske bete ke naam par transfer ho jayega**, kyunki log kehte hain 'sahab ka ladka hai.'

Tum 26 saal se us khaate ko CHALA rahe the. Lekin woh khaata kabhi tumhare naam par nahi tha."

Baban ghar aaya. Us raat usne register mein sabse lambi entry likhi:

> **Aaj mujhe apni zindagi ki sabse badi galti dikhi, aur woh 26 saal purani hai.**
>
> **Maine 26 saal machine chalayi. Main sabse behtar chalata tha. Lekin main hamesha KISI AUR ke naam par chalata raha.**
>
> **Jab maine kisi ka kaam karwaya, log kehte the "sahab ne karwa diya." Kabhi-kabhi kehte the "Baban dada ne karwaya" — lekin unke dimaag mein sahab ka chehra tha.**
>
> **Ehsaan ka khaata ek hi cheez par bharosa karta hai: log kiska NAAM lete hain. Aur naam hamesha uska hota hai jiske paas KURSI hai — chalane wale ka nahi.**

Aur neeche, teen aur line — jo poore chapter ka theory hai, ek aadmi ki zindagi se nikla hua:

> **1. Party mein 26 saal dene se ticket nahi milta. Ticket us aadmi ko milta hai jiske paas woh cheez hai jo party ke paas NAHI hai. Mere paas jo tha, woh party ka hi tha.**

> **2. Beta jeetega. Aur woh 31 saal ka hai, usne kuch nahi kiya. Lekin uske paas 26 saal ka khaata transfer ho gaya. Yeh anyay nahi hai — yeh ganit hai. Aur ganit se ladne ka koi faayda nahi.**

> **3. Meri society chhoti hai. Lekin woh MERI hai. Aur agar main 8 saal pehle shuru kar deta — jab MLA ne pehli baar mujhse kaha tha "tu samajhdar hai isliye khatarnak hai" — toh aaj 4,000 nahi, 15,000 member hote.**

Aur sabse aakhir mein, ek line jo usne bade akshar mein likhi:

> **KISI AUR KI MACHINE CHALANA EK NAUKRI HAI. APNI CHHOTI SI MACHINE BANANA EK POONJI HAI. Main 26 saal naukri karta raha aur samajhta raha ki poonji bana raha hoon.**

### Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

**1. Neta ki asli taakat naapne ka ek-line test kya hai?**

> *Jawab:* "Agar iska ticket kat jaye aur yeh nirdaleeya lade, toh kitne vote layega?" Kam vote = woh party ka aadmi. Bahut vote = party uska aadmi. Yeh test party-ke-naam-par-jeetne wale aur apne-naam-par-jeetne wale ko alag kar deta hai.

**2. "Apna vote" kis chaar cheezon se banta hai?**

> *Jawab:* (1) Ehsaan ka khaata — jinke kaam usne khud karwaye, (2) sanstha — jinki rozi usse judi hai, (3) apne samuday ka netritva, (4) machine jo uske apne naam se judi ho (party ke jhande se nahi).

**3. Dynasty ka asli structural kaaran kya hai?**

> *Jawab:* Indian politics mein **retirement ka koi rasta nahi hai**. Kursi chhodne par neta ki 25 saal ki poonji (network, sanstha, ehsaan, machine) bikhar jaati hai — koi mechanism nahi jo usse transfer kare. Isliye woh kursi chhodta nahi, transfer karta hai, aur transfer sirf khoon ke rishte mein bharosemand hai.

**4. Party dynasty ko rokti kyun nahi?**

> *Jawab:* Party ka apna ganit bhi wahi kehta hai. Naya aadmi banane mein 10 saal aur seat ka risk; bete ko ticket dene mein ready machine, ready sanstha, ready vote — aur upar se woh ehsaanmand rehta hai. Dono taraf ka incentive dynasty ki taraf le jaata hai.

**5. Baban ki 26 saal purani galti kya thi?**

> *Jawab:* Usne machine chalayi lekin hamesha **kisi aur ke naam par**. Ehsaan ka khaata us aadmi ke naam par banta hai jiske paas kursi hai, chalane wale ke naam par nahi. Isliye 26 saal ki mehnat transfer nahi hui — woh MLA ke bete ke paas chali gayi. Kisi aur ki machine chalana naukri hai; apni chhoti machine banana poonji hai.

### What this means for you

Kisi bhi neta ko dekho toh yeh poocho: **"iska apna vote kitna hai, aur uska source kya hai?"** Aur agar woh dynasty ka aadmi hai, toh usse kam mat aanko — usko transfer mein jo mila hai woh asli hai (machine, sanstha, khaata), aur woh usse chalana bhi jaanta hai.

Aur ek baat jo tumhare apne kaam ki hai, seedhe shabdon mein: **Baban ki galti sirf politics ki galti nahi hai.** Kisi aur ke platform par, kisi aur ke naam par, kisi aur ke customer ke saath 26 saal lagana — woh naukri hai, chahe woh kitna bhi kaabil kaam ho. Poonji tab banti hai jab log **tumhara naam lete hain**. Yeh baat is kitab se bahar bhi utni hi sach hai jitni andar.
