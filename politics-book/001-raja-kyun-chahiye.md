# Chapter 1
## Raja kyun chahiye — why the decider job exists

---

### Gaon se shuru

Sau parivaar ek valley mein baste hain. Kheti hai, kuaan hai, mandir hai. Abhi tak koi sarkar nahi, koi neta nahi — kyunki kisi ne abhi tak yeh cheez invent hi nahi ki.

Pehle mahine sab theek chalta hai. Phir zindagi apna kaam karti hai:

- Do bhai zameen ke boundary par lad pade. Dono kehte hain "patthar wahan nahi, yahan tha."
- Upar wale khet ka aadmi poora paani apni taraf mod leta hai. Neeche ke teen khet sookh rahe hain.
- Raat mein kisi ke dhor chori hue. Sab ek doosre par shak kar rahe hain.
- Kuaan sabka hai, lekin uski safai koi nahi karta. Jo cheez sabki hai, woh kisi ki nahi hoti.

Ab dhyan se dekho: yeh char problems kheti se solve nahi hoti, taakat se solve nahi hoti, paise se bhi solve nahi hoti. Inko solve karne ke liye chahiye ek **faisla** — aur faisla tabhi chalta hai jab sab usse maanein.

Yeh hai governance. Tumhari 10 needs ki list mein jo "governance" likha hai, uska asli matlab yeh hai: **jhagde ka faisla, sabki cheezon ki dekhbhal, aur bahar ke khatre se raksha.** Roti jitni real need hai, yeh bhi utni hi real hai. Bas dikhti kam hai, kyunki jab yeh kaam ho raha hota hai, tab kuch hota hua dikhta nahi. Governance sirf tab dikhti hai jab woh fail hoti hai.

### Ab village ka sawaal

Gaon ne maan liya ki ek decider chahiye. Ab do naye sawaal khade hote hain, aur poori kitab in dono ke andar hai:

**Sawaal 1: Decider ko denge kya?**

Farmer anaaj ugata hai, toh anaaj bechke kamata hai. Decider kya "ugata" hai? Faisle. Lekin faisla bikta nahi. Jhagde ka faisla karne ke baad decider haath mein kya lekar ghar jayega?

Gaon kehta hai: theek hai, sab thoda thoda anaaj denge, decider ka pet chal jayega. Yeh hai **salary** — aur dhyan rakhna, yeh hamesha chhoti hoti hai. Kyun? Kyunki gaon kabhi nahi chahega ki decider ki salary badi ho. "Woh toh sirf baithke faisle karta hai" — yeh feeling har gaon mein, har desh mein, har zamane mein same hai.

**Sawaal 2: Decider ki baat log maanenge kyun?**

Faisla tabhi faisla hai jab dono party maane — haarne wala bhi. Iske liye decider ke paas chahiye **taakat**: gaon ke jawan uske kehne par khade hon, galat karne wale ko saza de sake, sahi karne wale ko inaam.

Aur yahin par woh cheez janam leti hai jisko samjhe bina Indian politics kabhi samajh nahi aayegi:

### The strange job — duniya ki sabse ajeeb naukri

Decider ki job description likho toh aisi dikhti hai:

```
Salary:  chhoti (gaon kanjoosi karega, hamesha)
Taakat:  bahut badi (faisle, saza, inaam, sab uske haath)
```

Yeh combination duniya ki kisi aur naukri mein nahi milta. Aur yehi combination poore system ka engine hai.

Kyunki jab **salary chhoti ho aur taakat badi ho**, toh us taakat ki apni ek keemat ban jaati hai:

- Jiska jhagda chal raha hai, woh chahega faisla uske haq mein ho. Woh decider ko khush rakhega.
- Jisko kuaan ke paas ki zameen chahiye, woh decider ke ghar anaaj bhijwayega.
- Jisko raat ki chori mein apna naam saaf karana hai, woh decider ka aadmi ban jayega.

Decider ne kisi se kuch manga nahi. **Taakat ke aas-paas log khud line laga lete hain.** Power apne aap convert hoti hai — favors mein, izzat mein, anaaj mein, aur aage jaake paise mein.

Ab wapas dekho salary wali baat. Gaon ne salary chhoti rakhi thi taaki decider par kharcha kam ho. Result kya nikla? Decider ne apni kamai ka rasta salary se hatakar taakat mein dhoondh liya. **Gaon ne kanjoosi ki, aur usi kanjoosi ne woh cheez paida ki jisko aaj hum corruption kehte hain.** Yeh koi accident nahi hai. Yeh design ka natural result hai. Isko yaad rakhna — aage har chapter mein yeh wapas aayega.

### Aaj ke real numbers

Ab gaon se seedha 2026 ke India mein aao. Wahi structure, bade numbers:

```
MLA ki salary (state ke hisaab se):
  Bihar          ~Rs 45,000 / mahina (basic)
  Uttar Pradesh  ~Rs 65,000 / mahina
  Maharashtra    ~Rs 1.8 lakh basic, ~Rs 2.3-2.5 lakh sab milakar
  Telangana      ~Rs 2.75 lakh / mahina (sabse zyada)

MLA banne ka legal kharcha limit: Rs 28-40 lakh (ek election)
Asli kharcha jo campaigns mein hota hai: iska kai guna
                                  (reported — exact number koi
                                   kaagaz par nahi likhta)
```

Ab ek minute ruko aur khud se poocho — yeh chapter ka sabse important sawaal hai:

> **Ek aadmi 5 saal ki naukri ke liye, jisme total salary ~Rs 60 lakh se 1.5 crore banegi, election par crores kyun kharch karta hai?**

Agar yeh naukri sirf salary hoti, toh yeh sauda duniya ka sabse ghatiya investment hai. Koi bania yeh deal kabhi nahi karega.

Lekin log jaan lagakar yeh deal karte hain. Iska matlab sirf ek hai: **jo cheez woh khareed rahe hain, woh salary nahi hai.** Woh khareed rahe hain kursi — matlab taakat — matlab woh sab kuch jo taakat ke aas-paas apne aap line lagata hai: thekede, zameen ke faisle, transfer-posting, institutions, izzat, aur agli election jeetne ki machine.

Village wale decider aur aaj ke MLA mein sirf scale ka fark hai. Structure bilkul same hai: **chhoti salary + badi taakat = kamai taakat se hogi, salary se nahi.**

### Incentive equation (yeh har chapter mein hoga)

```
DECIDER/NETA ka hisaab:
  Deta hai:   faisle, suraksha, kaam karwana
  Paata hai:  salary (chhoti) + power-conversion (asli kamai)
  Darr:       kursi jaana = sab conversion band
  Isliye:     uska har kaam ka asli maqsad ek hai — kursi bachana

GAON/VOTER ka hisaab:
  Deta hai:   vote, support
  Paata hai:  faisle apne haq mein, kaam, suraksha
  Darr:       galat aadmi ko taakat de di toh 5 saal bhugtna
  Isliye:     voter bhi apna sauda kar raha hai, bhakti nahi
```

Dono taraf sauda hai. Yeh samajh lo toh politics mein "achha aadmi / bura aadmi" wali kahani khatam ho jaati hai, aur uski jagah ek system dikhne lagta hai jisme har player apna rational game khel raha hai.

### Baban ki kahani — part 1

Nimgaon, ek chhota sa gaon. Baban 12 saal ka hai. Uska baap Tatya — chaar ekad ka kisaan, mandi mein jhukkar milne wala aadmi.

Us saal Baban ke chacha aur padosi Ganpat mein zameen ka jhagda hua. Boundary ka patthar raat mein hil gaya tha — kisne hilaya, yeh dono ko "pakka pata" tha, aur dono ka pakka alag tha. Do mahine dono ghar ek doosre se nahi bole. Chacha ne darwaza band kar liya, Ganpat ne kuein ka rasta rok diya. Baban ne pehli baar dekha ki ek patthar do parivaron ko kaise kha jaata hai.

Baap log police ke paas kyun nahi gaye? Baban ne poocha tha. Tatya hansa: "Police? Police ko patthar dikhega? Police ko kaagaz chahiye, aur kaagaz dono ke paas nahi."

Phir ek shaam sarpanch aaya — safed kurta, dheemi chaal, peeche do aadmi. Aangan mein khatiya bichhi. Sarpanch aadha ghanta baitha. Pehle chacha ko poora bolne diya, beech mein ek baar bhi nahi roka. Phir Ganpat ko. Phir chai pi, aur chai ke baad ek line boli: "Patthar wahan lagega jahan tumhare baap ke waqt tha — Ganpat ke khet mein do haath andar. Aur Ganpat, kuein ka rasta kal se khula."

Dono maan gaye. Dono ko aadha-aadha mila tha, isliye dono haar ke bhi jeet gaye. Jo kaam do mahine, do parivaar, aur ek band darwaza nahi kar paya, woh aadhe ghante mein ho gaya.

Lekin Baban ne woh cheez dekhi jo baaki sab ne miss kar di. Jaate waqt Ganpat sarpanch ke paas gaya aur dheere se bola: "Bhau, mera chhota ladka... school mein naam likhwana hai, master maanta nahi." Sarpanch ne bina ruke kaha: "Somvaar ko bhej dena."

Faisla free tha. Lekin faisle ke saath ek queue shuru ho gayi thi.

Us raat Baban ne apne baap se poocha: "Sarpanch kheti kyun nahi karta?"

Tatya ne kaha: "Uska kaam faisle karna hai."

"Toh usko anaaj kaun deta hai?"

"Gaon deta hai. Panchayat se milta hai thoda."

Baban ne teesra sawaal poocha, jo asli sawaal tha: "Itna thoda milta hai toh uske ghar mein sabse pehle bijli kaise aayi? Pakka ghar kaise bana?"

Tatya chup ho gaya. Phir bola: "So ja."

Jab baap "so ja" bole, tab samajh lo sawaal sahi jagah laga hai. Baban ko poora jawab 30 saal baad milega. Humein agle 14 chapters mein milega.

### Self-test (pehle apne dimaag se jawab do, phir neeche check karo)

**1. Governance ek "need" kyun hai — sirf ek sarkari system kyun nahi?**

> *Jawab:* Kyunki kuch problems sirf faisle se solve hoti hain — jhagde, saajhi cheezein (kuaan), chori/suraksha. Yeh problems har basti mein khud paida hoti hain, koi impose nahi karta. Jahan 100 log saath rahenge, wahan decider ki demand apne aap banegi — isliye yeh need hai, system baad mein aata hai.

**2. Decider ki salary hamesha chhoti kyun, aur us kanjoosi ka side-effect kya?**

> *Jawab:* Gaon ko lagta hai "yeh sirf baithke faisle karta hai," isliye koi community decider ko zyada dena nahi chahti. Side-effect: decider apni kamai salary ke bahar dhoondta hai — taakat ke conversion mein. Kam salary corruption ko rokta nahi, paida karta hai.

**3. MLA candidate crores kharch karke ~Rs 1 crore ki naukri kyun leta hai?**

> *Jawab:* Kyunki woh naukri nahi khareed raha, kursi khareed raha hai. Kursi = faisle + saza + inaam ki taakat, aur us taakat ke aas-paas favors, theke, zameen, transfer, institutions ki queue khud lag jaati hai. Salary is deal ka sabse chhota hissa hai.

**4. "Power apne aap convert hoti hai" — teen raste jo village mein dikhe?**

> *Jawab:* (1) Jiska jhagda pending hai, woh decider ko khush rakhta hai. (2) Jisko saajhi cheez (kuaan ke paas ki zameen) chahiye, woh bhent bhejta hai. (3) Jisko protection/naam saaf karana hai, woh decider ka aadmi ban jaata hai. Teenon mein decider ne kuch manga nahi — line khud lagi.

Chaaron sahi the? Chapter 2 kholo. Koi galat tha? Us section par wapas jao.

### What this means for you

Jab bhi kisi neta se milo — MLA ho ya sarpanch — uski salary ko poori tarah bhool jao. Uska asli P&L yeh hai: **kursi bachane ka kharcha vs taakat se hone wali conversion.** Uske har decision ko is lens se dekho, aur uska behavior predict karna shuru ho jayega. Aur jab tum usse kuch bechne jaoge, toh yaad rakhna — jo cheez uski kursi bachaane mein madad kare, wahi cheez uske liye keemti hai. Baaki sab kharcha hai.
