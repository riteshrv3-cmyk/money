# Chapter 16
## Mantri aur CM — jeetne ka MATLAB kya hai

---

### Ab tak ka sabse bada sawaal

13 chapters se hum yeh dekh rahe hain ki log kursi ke liye crores kharch karte hain, 20 saal lagate hain, party badalte hain, parivaar daav par lagate hain.

Ab sawaal: **jeet kar milta kya hai?**

Chapter 1 mein humne kaha tha "taakat". Chapter 8 mein humne kaha tha "theke, zameen, permission". Lekin woh sab **nateeje** hain. Unka **source** kya hai?

Is chapter ka jawab do shabdon mein hai:

> **FILE aur POSTING.**

Sarkar chalti kaise hai? Do cheezon se. Ek — kaagaz par faisla likha jaata hai (file). Do — us kaagaz par likhne wala aadmi kaun hai, yeh tay hota hai (posting).

Jo in dono ko hila sakta hai, uske paas asli taakat hai. Aur woh aadmi hai **mantri**.

### MLA se mantri — kya badalta hai

Chapter 6 mein humne kaha tha: MLA ki taakat 20% adhikaar + 80% asar hai. Woh officer ko order nahi de sakta, sirf keh sakta hai.

Mantri banne par yeh ulta ho jaata hai:

```
                    MLA                  MANTRI
Officer se          keh sakta hai        ORDER de sakta hai
                                         (apne vibhag mein)
File par            request              DASTAKHAT
Transfer            sifarish             FAISLA
                                         (apne vibhag mein)
Budget              apna MLALADS         poore vibhag ka
Officers            unse milna padta     woh uske NEECHE
                    hai                  kaam karte hain
```

Yeh chhota upgrade nahi hai. **Yeh alag duniya hai.**

Aur isliye mantri pad Indian politics ki sabse zyada ladi jaane wali cheez hai — ticket se bhi zyada. Ticket sirf line mein khada karta hai. Mantri pad taakat deta hai.

### Vibhag (portfolio) — sab barabar nahi hote

Yeh section sabse practical hai. Har mantri pad ek jaisa nahi hota. Unme farak hota hai, aur farak teen cheezon se aata hai:

```
KYA CHAHIYE                    KAUNSE VIBHAG MEIN HAI

1. PAISA (bada budget, theke)
   → PWD (sadak/building), Irrigation, Urban
     Development, Housing, Health (bade tender)

2. POSTING KI TAAKAT (kitne officers)
   → Home (police), Revenue (patwari/tehsildar),
     Education (hazaaron master), Health

3. PERMISSION KA CONTROL
   → Excise (sharab licence), Mining, Transport
     (permit), Industries (zameen/approval),
     Urban Development (building)

KAM KEEMTI (bahar se izzatdaar, andar se khali)
   → Culture, Sports, Statistics, Protocol
```

Ab dhyan do — **jo vibhag "achhe" lagte hain woh aksar kamzor hote hain, aur jo boring lagte hain woh sabse takatwar.**

Culture mantri newspaper mein zyada dikhta hai. Revenue mantri poore state ke patwari-tehsildar ki posting par baitha hai — matlab har zameen ke kaagaz par uska asar hai.

Isliye jab bhi khabar aaye ki "mantrimandal ka batwara hua", asli khabar naam nahi hoti. **Asli khabar yeh hoti hai ki kis gut ko kaunsa vibhag mila.** Jiske gut ko Home, Revenue aur PWD mile, woh sarkar mein bhaari hai — chahe uske paas kitne bhi MLA hon.

### Transfer-posting ka arthashastra

Ab is kitab ka sabse honest section. Yeh mechanism samjhe bina Indian sarkar samajh nahi aati.

Sabse pehle structure:

```
Ek state mein hazaaron sarkari post hain. Aur woh
posts BARABAR nahi hain:

"MALDAAR" POST                    "KHALI" POST
jahan roz lena-dena hai:          jahan kuch nahi hota:
- bade sheher ka police station   - koi sudoor training centre
- highway wala RTO                - record room
- bade zile ka excise             - archives, library
- mining wala ilaaka              - koi board jisme kaam hi nahi
- bade project wala PWD sub-div
- bade ilaake ka tehsil
```

Ab ek officer ke liye do posts ka farak sirf "kaam" ka nahi hai — **uski poori zindagi ka farak hai.**

Aur yahan se poora chakkar shuru hota hai:

```
1. Officer ko maldaar post chahiye
2. Post ka faisla mantri (aur uske through, uske
   gut) ke haath mein hai
3. Isliye officer mantri se rishta banata hai
4. Aur mantri jaanta hai ki us post ki keemat kya hai

Nateeja (yeh reported hai, aur ispe ilzaam,
jaanch aur adaalat ke faisle hote rahe hain):
kuch jagah posting ki apni ek "keemat" ban jaati hai
```

Aur ab woh baat jo sabse zaroori hai — **isko sirf paisa mat samjho.** Yeh usse zyada gehra hai:

> **Transfer-posting ki taakat ka asli istemaal paisa nahi hai. Asli istemaal yeh hai ki har officer jaanta hai ki uski agli posting kis par depend karti hai. Aur jo aadmi yeh jaanta hai, woh apne aap "sahi" faisle lene lagta hai — bina kisi order ke.**

Yehi wajah hai ki mantri ko roz order dene ki zaroorat nahi padti. Uska hona hi kaafi hai. Officer khud samajh jaata hai ki file kis taraf jhukni chahiye.

Aur yeh chapter 12 se judta hai: **mantri sirf ek aadmi nahi hai — woh apne poore gut ke logon ke liye transfer, theke aur kaam ka darwaza hai.** Isliye har gut apna mantri chahta hai. Bina mantri ke gut bhookha reh jaata hai, aur bhookha gut toot jaata hai (ch.13).

### Mantri vs officer — asli rishta

Ab ek galatfehmi todte hain. Log samajhte hain mantri sab kuch karwa leta hai aur IAS uska naukar hai. **Yeh aadha sach hai.**

Officer ke paas apne hathiyaar hain:

```
OFFICER KE HATHIYAAR:

1. NIYAM (rules)
   "Sir, yeh niyam ke khilaf hai." Isko todna
   mushkil hai, kyunki galat file par DASTAKHAT
   officer ke hote hain — mantri ke nahi.
   Jab jaanch hoti hai, phansta officer hai.

2. DER (delay)
   File ko technically zinda rakho aur chalne mat do.
   "Clarification maanga hai." Yeh sabse takatwar
   aur sabse surakshit hathiyaar hai.
   (Ch.15 ka Baban wala insight: dheema chalana
    kabhi pakda nahi jaata.)

3. LIKHIT ORDER MAANGNA
   "Sir, likhkar de dijiye." Yeh sabse bada
   hathiyaar hai, aur isiliye sabse kam istemaal
   hota hai — kyunki iske baad rishta khatam.

4. SERVICE KI SURAKSHA
   Officer ko nikalna bahut mushkil hai. Zyada
   se zyada transfer ho sakta hai. Aur uska career
   30 saal ka hai; mantri ka 5 saal ka.
```

Aur yeh aakhri point sabse gehra hai:

> **Mantri 5 saal ka hai. Officer 30 saal ka hai. Dono ko yeh pata hai. Isliye ek samajhdar officer mantri se ladta nahi — woh WAQT KAAT deta hai.**

Isliye asli rishta yeh hai: **dono ko ek doosre ki zaroorat hai aur dono ek doosre se thoda darte hain.** Mantri ko kaam karwana hai, officer ko posting chahiye aur bachna bhi hai. Aur beech mein ek line hoti hai jise dono jaante hain lekin kabhi likhte nahi.

### CM — sabse upar

CM ke paas woh sab hai jo mantri ke paas hai, aur teen cheezein aur:

```
1. MANTRI KA FAISLA
   Kaun mantri banega, aur kaunsa vibhag milega.
   Matlab har mantri uske haath mein hai.
   (Isliye mantri CM se utna hi darta hai jitna
    MLA high command se — ch.12 ka pattern dobara)

2. TOP POSTING
   Chief Secretary, DGP, collectors, bade officers —
   sab CM ke haath mein

3. POORA BUDGET aur ANNOUNCEMENT KA HAQ
   Kaun sa project kahan jayega. Aur yeh sabse
   bada political hathiyaar hai — kyunki har
   MLA usse apne ilaake ke liye kuch chahta hai
```

Aur isliye CM ke saamne har MLA chhota ho jaata hai. Ch.12 mein humne kaha tha "neeche raja, upar naukar." CM woh aadmi hai jiske saamne poora state naukar hai.

Lekin CM ki apni kamzori bhi hai, aur woh bilkul wahi hai jo humne poori kitab mein dekhi hai:

> **CM ki kursi MLAs ki ginti par tiki hai. Isliye CM ko har waqt apni ginti sambhalni padti hai — matlab mantri pad baantne padte hain, gut balance karne padte hain, aur naraz logon ko rokna padta hai. CM sabse takatwar aadmi hai aur sabse zyada blackmail-able bhi.**

Chapter 13 (dal-badal) is line ke bina samajh nahi aata.

### Contested corner

**Kaun zyada takatwar hai — neta ya babu (officer)?**

- **Camp A (neta):** Officer transfer ho sakta hai, neta nahi. Aur aakhir mein policy ka faisla chuni hui sarkar ka hai. Officer sirf implement karta hai.
- **Camp B (babu):** Neta 5 saal ka hai, officer 30 saal ka. Officer file likhta hai, aur jo likhta hai wahi record ban jaata hai. Mantri chala jayega, officer rahega — aur system officer ke hisaab se chalta rahega.

Dono sahi hain, alag-alag paimane par. **Ek faisle mein neta bhaari hai. Poore system mein babu bhaari hai** — kyunki system rozana chalta hai aur rozana officer chalata hai.

### Incentive equation

```
MANTRI:
  Paata hai: file par dastakhat + posting ka faisla
             + vibhag ka budget
  Deta hai:  apne gut ke logon ko kaam, apne ilaake
             ko project
  Darr:      vibhag chhin jaye / CM naraz ho jaye
             (kyunki uski kursi CM ke haath mein hai)
  Isliye:    woh CM ke saamne wahi hota hai jo MLA
             high command ke saamne hota hai

OFFICER:
  Chahta hai: achhi posting + bachav (jaanch se)
  Deta hai:   kaam, ya der
  Darr:       galat file par uske dastakhat hain,
              mantri ke nahi
  Isliye:     woh ladta nahi, WAQT KAATTA hai —
              kyunki uska career 30 saal ka hai

CM:
  Paata hai:  poore state ka control
  Darr:       ginti. Har waqt.
  Isliye:     woh poori taakat ke bawajood roz
              gut-balancing karta rehta hai

MLA (jiske paas kuch nahi):
  Uske liye mantri ka darwaza hi sab kuch hai.
  Isliye woh apne gut ke mantri se judta hai —
  aur agar uske gut ka koi mantri nahi, toh
  woh 5 saal bhookha rehta hai (→ ch.13)
```

### Baban ki kahani — part 16

Baban 56 ka. Uske ITI college ko ek sarkari scheme ke tahat equipment grant mil sakti thi. File district se state chali gayi thi. 5 mahine se wahin thi.

Baban ne pehle purana tareeka aazmaya — MLA se kehlwaya. Kuch nahi hua.

Phir usne woh kiya jo 30 saal ke tajurbe ne sikhaya tha: **usne pata lagaya ki file kis TABLE par hai.**

File ek Deputy Secretary ke paas thi. Baban us aadmi se mila. Aur us mulakat ne usko woh sikhaya jo woh 30 saal se aadha-adhoora jaanta tha.

Officer ne file khol kar dikhayi. Bola: "Dekho. Isme ek document ki copy nahi hai. Main isko aage bhej doon toh Audit mein yeh mere naam par aayega. Tumhara mantri mujhse kahega 'bhej do', aur do saal baad jab jaanch hogi toh mantri kahenge 'mujhe kya pata tha, officer ne bheji thi.' **Dastakhat mere hain, mantri ke nahi.**"

Baban ne poocha: "Toh chahiye kya?"

"Woh document. Bas."

Woh document 11 din mein ban gaya. Grant 6 hafte mein aa gayi.

Baban ne us raat register mein likha:

> **Maine 30 saal socha ki file "sifarish" se chalti hai. Aaj pata chala ki file DAR se rukti hai. Officer ko sifarish nahi chahiye thi — usko apni suraksha chahiye thi.**
>
> **Jab tak main sifarish bhej raha tha, woh dar raha tha. Jis din maine uska dar hataya, file apne aap chal gayi. Aur mujhe kisi mantri ki zaroorat hi nahi padi.**

Do mahine baad usne doosri cheez dekhi, aur woh sabse bada sabak thi.

Uske ilaake ka tehsildar badal gaya. Naya aadmi aaya, aur 3 hafte mein har chhoti file ka rukna shuru ho gaya. Log pareshan.

Baban ne pata lagaya ki naya tehsildar kis mantri ke gut se aaya hai. Aur phir usne kuch nahi kiya — bas intezaar kiya.

7 mahine baad sarkar mein gut badla. Woh tehsildar apne aap transfer ho gaya.

Register mein:

> **Officer ka behaviour uski niyat nahi hai. Uski POSTING hai. Woh us aadmi ke hisaab se chalta hai jisne usko wahan bithaya, aur us aadmi ke hisaab se jo usko agli jagah bithayega.**
>
> **Isliye kisi officer ko samajhna ho toh yeh mat poocho ki woh kaisa aadmi hai. Yeh poocho: yeh kiska aadmi hai, aur uski agli posting kiske haath mein hai?**

Aur us saal ka aakhri note, jo poore chapter ka nichod tha:

> **Aaj samajh aaya ki neta kursi ke liye itna kyun ladta hai. Kursi se paisa nahi milta. Kursi se FILE aur POSTING milti hai. Aur poori sarkar sirf yeh do cheezein hain. Baaki sab — scheme, budget, bhashan — un do cheezon ke upar ka kapda hai.**

### Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

**1. Sarkar ki asli taakat kis do cheez mein hai?**

> *Jawab:* **File** (kaagaz par faisla) aur **posting** (kaagaz par likhne wala aadmi kaun hoga). Jo in dono ko hila sakta hai, uske paas asli taakat hai. Scheme, budget aur bhashan in dono ke upar ka kapda hai.

**2. MLA se mantri banne par kya badalta hai?**

> *Jawab:* MLA officer se **keh** sakta hai, mantri apne vibhag mein **order** de sakta hai. MLA sifarish karta hai, mantri dastakhat karta hai. MLA ka MLALADS hai, mantri ke paas poore vibhag ka budget hai. 20% adhikaar + 80% asar wala samikaran ulta ho jaata hai.

**3. "Boring vibhag zyada takatwar hote hain" — kyun?**

> *Jawab:* Kyunki taakat budget, posting ki sankhya, aur permission ke control se aati hai — glamour se nahi. Revenue (har zameen ka kaagaz) aur Home (police) Culture ya Sports se kai guna bhaari hain. Isliye mantrimandal ki asli khabar naam nahi, **vibhag ka batwara** hoti hai.

**4. Transfer-posting ki taakat ka asli istemaal kya hai (paisa nahi)?**

> *Jawab:* Har officer jaanta hai uski agli posting kis par depend karti hai — isliye woh **bina order ke** apne aap "sahi" faisle lene lagta hai. Mantri ko roz order dene ki zaroorat nahi padti; uska hona hi kaafi hai.

**5. Officer mantri se ladta kyun nahi, aur uska sabse surakshit hathiyaar kya hai?**

> *Jawab:* Kyunki mantri 5 saal ka hai aur officer 30 saal ka — samajhdar officer waqt kaat deta hai. Sabse surakshit hathiyaar **der** hai (file ko technically zinda rakho, chalne mat do), kyunki dheema chalana kabhi pakda nahi ja sakta. Aur galat file par dastakhat officer ke hote hain, mantri ke nahi — isliye uska asli dar jaanch ka hai.

### What this means for you

Do practical cheezein.

**Ek:** jab bhi koi sarkari kaam atka ho, yeh mat poocho "kaunse neta se kehlwayein." Yeh poocho: **"file kis table par hai, aur us aadmi ka dar kya hai?"** Aksar file sifarish se nahi rukti — woh officer ke dar se rukti hai. Uska dar hatao, file apne aap chalegi, aur kisi neta ki zaroorat hi nahi padegi.

**Do:** kisi bhi mantri ya officer ko samajhna ho toh position mat dekho, **vibhag aur posting** dekho. Mantri ka naam kam batata hai, uska vibhag sab batata hai. Aur officer ki niyat kuch nahi batati — yeh batata hai ki uski agli posting kiske haath mein hai.
