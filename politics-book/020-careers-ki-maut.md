# Chapter 20
## Careers ki maut — jo galtiyan system ke niyam dikhati hain

---

### Ulta padhne ka faayda

Ab tak humne dekha ki careers kaise **bante** hain. Aaj dekhenge ki woh kaise **khatam** hote hain.

Wajah sirf yeh nahi ki failure dilchasp hai. Wajah yeh hai:

> **Har system ka asli niyam tab dikhta hai jab koi usse todta hai. Jab tak sab theek chal raha hai, niyam invisible rehte hain.**

Yeh chapter 6 maut dikhayega. Har ek ke peeche ek niyam hai jo humne pehle padha — aur ab woh niyam saabit hoga.

---

### Maut 1: Machine ke bina jeetna

**Kahani ka aakar:** Lehar aayi. Ek aadmi jeet gaya jiske paas na machine thi, na khaata, na sanstha. Party ka nishaan tha aur hawa thi.

**Phir 5 saal mein kya hua:**

```
Saal 1:  Woh khush hai. Log aa rahe hain.
Saal 2:  Log kaam maang rahe hain. Uske paas
         karyakarta nahi hain jo unke kaam
         dekhein. Woh khud sab nahi kar sakta.
Saal 3:  Log kehne lage "yeh milta nahi hai."
         Uske ilaake ke chhote neta doosri taraf
         dekhne lage.
Saal 4:  Party ke survey mein uska naam neeche.
Saal 5:  Ticket kata — ya mila aur haar gaya.
```

**Niyam jo saabit hua (ch.5, ch.9):**

> **Lehar tumhe kursi de sakti hai. Lehar tumhe machine nahi de sakti. Aur bina machine ke kursi ek udhaar hai jiska bill 5 saal baad aata hai.**

Yeh sabse aam maut hai. Har wave ke baad agli baar mein aise bahut se log gayab ho jaate hain.

---

### Maut 2: Ticket kata, aur bagawat galat ki

**Kahani ka aakar:** 20 saal ka wafadaar. Ticket kat gaya (ch.7). Gusse mein nirdaleeya khada ho gaya.

**Do anjaam hote hain, aur farak ek hi cheez se aata hai:**

```
AGAR uske paas APNA vote tha (ch.14):
  → woh 40-60,000 le gaya
  → apni hi party ka candidate hara diya
  → party ko dikha diya ki "mere bina yeh seat nahi"
  → 5 saal baad party usko wapas le aayi,
    aur is baar ticket diya

AGAR uske paas party ka vote tha:
  → woh 8,000 par simat gaya
  → party ne dekh liya ki iski keemat kya hai
  → aur ab woh party ke liye bhi bekaar hai
    (kyunki usne bagawat ki thi)
  → khatam. Dono taraf se.
```

**Niyam (ch.14):**

> **Bagawat ek TEST hai jo tum apne upar khud lete ho, aur uska result public hota hai. Agar tumhare paas apna vote hai toh bagawat bargaining hai. Agar nahi hai toh bagawat aatmahatya hai.**
>
> **Aur sabse kroor baat: bagawat se pehle tumhe khud nahi pata hota ki tumhare paas kya hai. Isliye log galat andaza lagate hain — aur ek baar lagane ke baad wapas nahi le sakte.**

---

### Maut 3: Galat gut chuna

**Kahani ka aakar:** Do gut the. Usne ek chuna — soch samajh kar, wafadaari se. Uska gut party ke andar haar gaya (ch.12).

**Phir:**

```
- Uska mantri se rishta khatam
- Uske ilaake ke transfer doosre gut ke hisaab se
- Uske project "review" mein
- Uske karyakarta dheere-dheere doosri taraf
  (kyunki unko kaam chahiye — ch.5)
- 5 saal mein machine aadhi
```

Aur yahan sabse kroor cheez: **usne kuch galat nahi kiya tha.** Usne sirf ek taraf chuna, aur woh taraf haar gayi.

**Niyam (ch.12):**

> **Gut chunna zaroori hai (akela aadmi nanga hai), lekin gut chunte waqt tum apna bhavishya kisi aur ke haath mein de dete ho. Aur uske haarne par tumhari seat par kiya hua kaam tumhe nahi bachayega.**

Isliye tajurbewale log kya karte hain? Woh gut mein rehte hain lekin **doosri taraf ka darwaza band nahi karte.** Bahar se yeh do-mooha lagta hai. Andar se yeh survival hai. (Aur haan — Tatya wali galti bhi yaad rakhna, ch.2: dono taraf khulke haan bol dena alag baat hai aur usme bhaav girta hai. Farak "rishta rakhna" aur "waada karna" ka hai.)

---

### Maut 4: Kursi chali gayi, aur peeche kuch nahi tha

**Kahani ka aakar:** Do term ka MLA. Achha kaam kiya. Paisa bhi banaya. Lekin sab kuch **kursi ke saath juda** tha — koi sanstha nahi, koi permanent base nahi (ch.11).

**Haarne ke baad:**

```
Mahina 1:   log abhi bhi aate hain
Mahina 3:   bheed aadhi
Mahina 6:   phone uthna kam. Uske apne PA ne
            doosri naukri dhoondh li
Saal 1:     uske purane karyakarta naye MLA ke
            saath dikhte hain
Saal 2:     woh shaadi-mayyat mein jaata hai aur
            usko aage nahi bithate
```

Aur woh aadmi ab kya kar sakta hai? Uske paas na naukri hai, na dhandha, na sanstha. Sirf ek pehchaan hai jo dheere-dheere mit rahi hai.

**Niyam (ch.11):**

> **Taakat udhaar ki cheez hai. Sanstha apni cheez hai. Jisne taakat ko sanstha mein nahi badla, uski poori zindagi ek 5-saal ke lease par thi.**

Aur yeh maut sabse dukh bhari isliye hai kyunki **uska waqt tha aur usne dhyan nahi diya.** Woh 10 saal kursi par tha. Woh kar sakta tha.

---

### Maut 5: Beta nahi sambhaal paya

**Kahani ka aakar:** Bade neta ne 30 saal ka saamraajya banaya — sanstha, machine, khaata, sab. Beta ko diya (ch.14).

**Aur beta doob gaya. Kyun?**

```
Baap ne 30 saal mein network banaya — matlab
usne 30 saal LOGON KE KAAM kiye. Har rishta
usne khud banaya tha.

Beta ko network MILA. Usne banaya nahi.

Aur network ek cheez nahi hai — woh RISHTON KA
JOD hai. Rishta transfer nahi hota; sirf uski
LIST transfer hoti hai.

Beta list ke logon ko phone karta hai. Woh log
aate hain — pehli baar baap ki wajah se.
Doosri baar? Ab beta ko khud kuch karna hoga.

Aur agar woh nahi kar paya — kyunki usne kabhi
subah 7 baje darbar mein nahi baitha, kabhi
mayyat mein 4 ghante gaadi nahi chalayi — toh
list 3 saal mein khali ho jaati hai.
```

**Niyam (ch.10, ch.14):**

> **Ehsaan ka khaata transfer hota hai. Ehsaan banane ki AADAT transfer nahi hoti. Aur khaata kharch hota rehta hai — agar naya jama nahi hua toh woh khali ho jaata hai.**

Isliye kuch dynasty chalti hain aur kuch teen saal mein baith jaati hain. Farak paisa nahi hai — farak yeh hai ki bete ne kaam seekha ya sirf virasat li.

---

### Maut 6: Zamana badal gaya, aadmi nahi

**Kahani ka aakar:** 25 saal ka mazboot neta. Machine solid, khaata bhara, sanstha chalti hui. Aur phir uske ilaake ka ganit hi badal gaya:

```
- Naya industrial area aaya, bahar se log aa base
  (uske purane blocks ka anupaat gir gaya)
- Naye ladke naukri ke liye sheher gaye — unka
  gaon ke mukhiya se rishta khatam
- Uske samuday ka ek naya, tez neta uth gaya
- Aur mudde badal gaye: uska "sadak-paani" wala
  pitch naye voter ke liye purana ho gaya
```

Usne wahi kiya jo 25 saal se kar raha tha. Aur woh ab kaam nahi kiya.

**Niyam (ch.19):**

> **Har machine ek KHAAS zamane ke liye tune ki jaati hai. Jo cheez tumhe 25 saal chalati hai, wahi cheez tumhe ek din marti hai — kyunki tum usse chhodne ko tayyar nahi hote jisne tumhe banaya.**

---

### Aur ek maut jo dikhti nahi: dheere-dheere wali

Upar ki 6 mein se har ek dramatic hai. Lekin sabse aam maut dramatic nahi hoti:

```
Aadmi jeetta rehta hai, lekin har baar margin kam
hota jaata hai.
Woh dekh raha hai lekin maan nahi raha.
Uske do-teen achhe log chale gaye aur usne naye
nahi banaye.
Uski sanstha chal rahi hai lekin badh nahi rahi.
Aur ek din woh 4,000 vote se haar jaata hai —
aur sab kehte hain "achanak kaise?"

Achanak kuch nahi hua. 10 saal se ho raha tha.
```

**Yeh sabse aam maut hai, aur sabse kam likhi jaati hai.**

### Contested corner

**Kya political career bachaya ja sakta hai?**

- **Camp A:** Haan. Jo aadmi waqt par pehchaan le — sanstha bana le, beta tayyar kar le, naye logon ko jagah de de — woh generations chal jaata hai. Bahut se parivaar chal rahe hain.
- **Camp B:** Nahi, aakhir mein sab girta hai. Har base kabhi na kabhi purana ho jaata hai, aur nayi peedhi purane ko nahi maanti. Bas kuch der zyada chal lete hain.

**Beech ka sach:** koi permanent nahi hai, lekin **umar bahut alag ho sakti hai** — 5 saal se 50 saal tak. Aur woh farak in chapters mein likhi cheezon se banta hai, kismat se nahi.

### Incentive equation — maut ke waqt

```
NETA (girta hua):
  Uska sabse bada dushman: INKAAR (denial)
  Kyunki maanne ka matlab hai ki uski poori
  pehchaan hil rahi hai
  Isliye: woh aksar aakhri tak yeh kehta hai
          "is baar ganit alag tha"

USKE LOG:
  Woh pehle jaante hain — kyunki zameen par
  woh hain
  Lekin woh usse bata nahi sakte, kyunki bure
  samachar lane wala aadmi pehle nikala jaata hai
  Isliye: girta hua neta hamesha SABSE AAKHIR
          mein jaanta hai

PARTY:
  Uske paas survey hai — usko pehle pata chal
  jaata hai
  Isliye: ticket ka faisla aksar neta ke
          apne andaze se pehle ho jaata hai

NAYA AADMI:
  Yeh uska mauka hai. Har girti hui machine
  ke tukde kisi na kisi ke paas jaate hain
```

Woh doosra box sabse gehra hai. **Girta hua aadmi sabse aakhir mein jaanta hai** — kyunki uske aas-paas ke log usse sach nahi bata sakte. Yeh sirf politics mein nahi hota; yeh har sangathan mein hota hai.

### Baban ki kahani — part 20

Baban 58 ka. Aur us saal uske MLA — 30 saal ka saath — chale gaye.

Bimari lambi thi. Aakhri do mahine woh ghar par the.

Baban roz jaata tha. Aur usne wahan woh dekha jo poore chapter ka sabse gehra sabak tha.

**Pehle mahine:** ghar ke bahar 30-40 log roz. Neta log, karyakarta, thekedaar. Gaadiyaan.

**Doosre mahine:** 8-10 log.

**Aakhri hafte:** ghar par sirf parivaar tha, do purane karyakarta, aur Baban.

Ek shaam MLA ne — bahut kamzor awaaz mein — Baban se kaha:

"Baban, main sochta tha mere 5,000 aadmi hain."

Baban chup raha.

"Aaj ginta hoon toh 6 nikalte hain."

Baban ne kaha: "Sahab, woh 5,000 aapke aadmi the. Woh aapki KURSI ke aadmi the. Kursi chali gayi toh woh chale gaye. Aur yeh unki galti nahi hai — unko bhi apna kaam karwana hai, aur ab woh kisi aur ke paas hoga."

MLA hansa — dheere se. Bola: "Tu hamesha sach bolta tha. Isliye maine tujhe ticket kabhi nahi diya."

Baban chaunk gaya. 30 saal mein pehli baar yeh baat khulke aayi.

MLA ne aage kaha: "Tu jaanta tha ki asli ginti kya hai. Tu 14 ki jagah 6 bolta. Aur is dhandhe mein jo aadmi sach bolta hai, usse log kursi nahi dete — usse log kaam karwate hain. **Sach bolne wala aadmi zaroori hota hai, lekin usko upar nahi jaane dete. Kyunki upar wale ko jhooth chahiye — apne baare mein.**"

Do hafte baad woh chale gaye.

Antim yatra mein 4,000 log the. Do mantri aaye. Akhbaar mein photo aaya.

Baban peeche khada tha. Aur usne dekha ki woh 4,000 log — jinme se zyadatar 6 mahine se ghar nahi aaye the — sab wapas aa gaye the. Ek din ke liye.

Us raat register mein, sabse lambi entry:

> **Aaj sab wapas aaye the. Aur kal koi nahi aayega.**
>
> **Maine 30 saal is aadmi ka kaam kiya. Woh achha aadmi tha. Aur woh isliye nahi doobа ki usne kuch galat kiya — woh isliye dooba ki uske paas kursi ke alawa kuch nahi tha. Uska college tha, haan — lekin woh bhi usne bete ke naam par rakha, apne network ko usse joda nahi. Do cheezein alag-alag chalti rahi.**
>
> **Aur maine aaj 6 tarah ki maut ek saath dekhi:**
> **- uske paas machine thi, lekin machine uski kursi se judi thi, uske naam se nahi**
> **- usne gut chuna aur woh gut haara**
> **- usne sanstha banayi lekin usko apne network se nahi joda**
> **- uska beta list leke aaya, aadat nahi**
> **- zamana badal gaya aur woh wahi pitch dete rahe**
> **- aur sabse badi baat: 10 saal se sab gir raha tha aur kisi ne unko nahi bataya. Main bhi nahi bataya. Kyunki bura samachar laane wala aadmi pehle nikala jaata hai.**

Aur aakhri do line, jo uski poori zindagi ka faisla thi:

> **Main 58 saal ka hoon. Meri society 9,000 ki hai, mera college chhota hai, mera koi ticket nahi hai. Kisi kitaab mein mera naam nahi aayega.**
>
> **Lekin agar kal main chala jaun, toh mere 90 parivaar ki naukri nahi jaayegi, meri society chalti rahegi, aur mera college khula rahega. Maine kursi kabhi nahi paayi. Lekin maine woh cheez bana li jo kursi ke baad bhi rehti hai. Aur ab mujhe lagta hai ki maine hisaab jeet liya — bas woh hisaab nahi jo main 25 saal ki umar mein jeetna chahta tha.**

### Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

**1. Lehar se jeetne wale ki maut kaise hoti hai?**

> *Jawab:* Lehar kursi deti hai, machine nahi. Bina machine ke woh logon ke kaam nahi kar paata (uske paas karyakarta nahi), 3 saal mein log kehne lagte hain "yeh milta nahi", uske chhote neta doosri taraf jaate hain, aur agli baar ticket kat jaata hai ya woh haar jaata hai. **Bina machine ki kursi 5 saal ka udhaar hai.**

**2. Bagawat kab bargaining hai aur kab aatmahatya?**

> *Jawab:* Agar uske paas **apna vote** hai (ch.14) toh woh 40-60,000 le jaakar apni party ko harata hai, apni keemat saabit karta hai, aur wapas aa jaata hai. Agar uske paas party ka vote tha toh woh 8,000 par simat jaata hai aur dono taraf se khatam. Kroor baat: bagawat se pehle usko khud nahi pata hota ki uske paas kya hai.

**3. "Beta list le paya, aadat nahi" ka matlab?**

> *Jawab:* Ehsaan ka **khaata** transfer hota hai, lekin ehsaan **banane ki aadat** transfer nahi hoti. List ke log pehli baar baap ki wajah se aate hain; doosri baar bete ko khud kaam karna padta hai. Agar usne kabhi darbar mein nahi baitha, mayyat mein nahi gaya, toh khaata 3 saal mein khali ho jaata hai — kyunki woh kharch hota rehta hai aur jama nahi hota.

**4. Sabse aam maut kaunsi hai, aur woh kyun dikhti nahi?**

> *Jawab:* **Dheere-dheere wali** — margin har baar thoda girta hai, achhe log jaate hain aur naye nahi bante, sanstha badhti nahi. Ek din woh haar jaata hai aur sab kehte hain "achanak kaise?" Achanak kuch nahi hota, 10 saal se ho raha hota hai.

**5. Girta hua neta sabse aakhir mein kyun jaanta hai?**

> *Jawab:* Uske log zameen par hain, unko pehle pata chal jaata hai — lekin woh bata nahi sakte, kyunki **bura samachar laane wala aadmi pehle nikala jaata hai**. Aur neta ka apna sabse bada dushman inkaar hai, kyunki maanne ka matlab hai apni poori pehchaan ka hilna. Party ko survey se pehle pata chal jaata hai — isliye ticket ka faisla aksar neta ke apne andaze se pehle ho jaata hai.

### What this means for you

Yeh chapter tumhe do cheezein deta hai.

**Ek, padhne ke liye:** jab bhi koi neta girta hua dikhe, ganit kar lo ki woh kaunsi maut mar raha hai. Aur yaad rakhna ki **woh khud sabse aakhir mein jaanega.** Isliye girte hue aadmi ke saamne "aap toh mazboot hain" bolna aasan hai aur bekaar hai — aur uske aas-paas har aadmi wahi bol raha hoga.

**Do, apne liye — aur yeh is kitab ki sabse zaroori baat hai:** in 6 mauton mein se 5 ka ilaaj ek hi hai, aur woh ilaaj kursi milne se pehle karna padta hai. **Kuch aisa bana lo jo tumhari kursi ke bina bhi chale, aur jo tumhare NAAM par ho.** Baban ko ticket kabhi nahi mila. Lekin 58 saal ki umar mein uske paas woh cheez thi jo 30 saal ke us MLA ke paas nahi thi jiske paas sab kuch tha.

Yeh baat politics se bahar bhi utni hi sach hai.

---

*Note: the six failure modes here are analytical patterns assembled from widely observed features of Indian political careers — wave-elected first-timers losing re-election, rebel candidates splitting votes, factional losers being starved of postings and funds, defeated leaders losing their networks, dynastic successors failing to sustain inherited bases, and long-serving leaders being overtaken by demographic and issue change. They are presented as mechanisms, not as accounts of any specific individual. Baban and his MLA are fictional.*
