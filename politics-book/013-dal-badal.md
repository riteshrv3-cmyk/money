# Chapter 13
## Dal-badal — jab system ka bhaav khulke lagta hai

---

### Sabse imaandaar din

Politics mein sab kuch chhupa hua hota hai. Ticket ka faisla kamre mein, paisa cash mein, ehsaan bina receipt ke.

Lekin ek cheez aisi hai jahan sab kuch **khulke saamne aa jaata hai** — jab koi neta party badalta hai.

Us din pata chalta hai:
- us aadmi ki asli keemat kya thi
- uske saath kitne log the (aur kitne sirf naam ke the)
- party ne usko kitna zaroori samjha
- aur poore system mein wafadaari ka bhaav kya hai

**Isliye dal-badal is kitab ka sabse important chapter hai. Yeh system ka X-ray hai.**

### Pehle: kanoon kya kehta hai

1985 mein anti-defection law aaya (52nd amendment, Tenth Schedule). Wajah: us waqt itna dal-badal ho raha tha ki sarkarein mahinon mein girti thi. Ek mashhoor kissa hai Haryana ke ek vidhayak ka jo ek hi din mein kai baar paala badal gaya — usi se "Aaya Ram Gaya Ram" muhavra chala.

Kanoon ne kya kiya:

```
AGAR EK MLA:
  - apni party chhod deta hai, YA
  - party ke whip ke khilaf vote deta hai

  → uski SADASYATA ja sakti hai
     (faisla Speaker/Chairman karta hai)

CHHOOT (exception):
  Agar party ke do-tihai (2/3) sadasya ek saath
  doosri party mein MERGE ho jaayein, toh
  sadasyata nahi jaati.
```

Ab dhyan se dekho. Kanoon ne dal-badal rok diya? **Nahi.** Kanoon ne dal-badal ka **format badal diya.**

```
PEHLE:  ek-ek aadmi tootta tha (retail)
AB:     poora group tootta hai (wholesale)
```

Yeh chapter 3 ka "sarpanch pati" wala sabak hai, bade level par: **rule ne behaviour ko rokа nahi, usne behaviour ko ek naya raasta pakadne par majboor kar diya.**

Aur is ek baat se 2/3 wali ginti poore Indian politics ka sabse important number ban gayi. Har toot-phoot ki khabar mein tum yeh ginti dekhoge — "itne MLA saath hain, do-tihai poore hue ya nahi."

### Doosra raasta: istifa aur dobara chunav

Ek aur tareeka hai jo kanoon ke andar hai: **MLA istifa de de.**

Sadasyata khud chhod di, toh ayogya (disqualify) hone ka sawaal hi nahi. Phir woh doosri party se **upchunav (by-election)** lad leta hai.

Iska matlab kya hai:
- Woh apni seat par apna daawa test kar raha hai
- Nayi party usko ticket deti hai (yehi deal ka hissa hai)
- Aur aksar mantri pad bhi

Karnataka 2019 mein aisa hi hua tha — kai MLAs ne istifa diya, sarkar gir gayi, phir upchunav hue. Yeh raasta mehnga hai (dobara chunav ladna padta hai) lekin legally saaf hai.

### Ab asli sawaal: neta party badalta kyun hai

Log samajhte hain "lalach". Yeh aadha sach hai aur poora bekaar explanation hai. Asli wajah **chapter 6 ki table** hai. Wapas dekho:

```
                     RULING PARTY MLA      VIRODHI DAL MLA
Phone uthta hai      haan                  aksar nahi
Transfer mein baat   chalti hai            nahi chalti
Fund ka kaam         theek chalta hai      atakta hai
Karyakarta           saath hain            khiskte hain
Agli baar jeetne     achha                 mushkil
```

Ab is table ko 5 saal chalao. Virodhi dal ke MLA ke saath kya hota hai:

```
Saal 1:  "koi baat nahi, 5 saal baad wapas aayenge"
Saal 2:  uske do kaam atak gaye. Log kehne lage
         "in se kaam nahi hota"
Saal 3:  uska ek booth pramukh ruling party ke
         ladke ke saath dikha. Kyun? Kyunki us
         ladke se uska kaam ho gaya (ch.5 — karyakarta
         ko suraksha chahiye)
Saal 4:  uski sanstha (agar hai) par jaanch/audit
         ka dabav. Uske thekedaar doosri taraf.
Saal 5:  ticket toh mil jayega, lekin machine aadhi
         reh gayi hai
```

**Matlab virodhi dal mein rehna sirf 5 saal ka nuksaan nahi hai — woh compounding decay hai.** Har saal uski machine thodi aur ghisti hai.

Toh ab dal-badal ko dobara dekho:

> **Neta party isliye nahi badalta ki usko paisa mil gaya. Woh isliye badalta hai ki usko dikh raha hai ki agar woh nahi badla, toh 5 saal mein uske paas machine hi nahi bachegi — aur machine ke bina woh phir kabhi wapas nahi aa payega.**

Yeh **survival** ka faisla hai, lalach ka nahi. (Lalach bhi hota hai. Lekin lalach akela itne logon ko ek saath nahi hilata.)

### Dal-badal ke 5 trigger

Zameen par yeh cheezein toot ka switch dabati hain:

```
1. TICKET KATNA (sabse bada)
   Chapter 7 yaad hai — list aane ke 24-48 ghante
   mein sabse zyada toot hoti hai. Aadmi ke paas
   time nahi hota, aur uski poori machine bekar
   ho jayegi agar woh nahi lada.

2. SARKAR BANNE KA GANIT
   Jab result hung ho — kisi ko poora bahumat na
   mile — tab har MLA ki keemat achanak 10x ho
   jaati hai. Kyunki 5-10 log sarkar bana ya
   gira sakte hain.

3. GUT KA HAARNA (ch.12)
   Uska senior neta party ke andar haar gaya.
   Ab uska koi bolne wala nahi. Woh phansa hua hai.

4. LAMBI VIRODH KI HAALAT
   Upar wali decay. Do-teen term virodhi dal mein
   nikal gaye, ab machine bachani hai.

5. JAANCH KA DABAV (contested — ch.12)
   Ilzaam yeh hai ki jaanch ka dar aadmi ko todta
   hai. Yeh sabse contested wajah hai aur ise
   proof ki tarah mat lo.
```

### Resort politics — ek ajeeb, aur bilkul logical cheez

Jab sarkar hung ho ya toot ka khatra ho, toh MLAs ko bus mein bithakar kisi resort ya hotel mein le jaaya jaata hai — dusre state mein, phone band, media door.

Bahar se yeh comedy lagti hai. Andar se yeh **bilkul rational** hai:

```
KYUN LE JAATE HAIN:

1. Doosri taraf ka offer MLA tak pahunch na paye
2. MLA apne hi logon (aur parivaar) ke dabav se
   door rahe — kyunki uske ilaake ke thekedaar
   aur karyakarta bhi usse kehte hain "udhar
   chalo, kaam hoga"
3. Ginti pakki rahe — roz subah ginti hoti hai
4. Aur sabse zaroori: DIKHANA ki hamare paas
   ginti hai. Media ko photo chahiye — 45 MLA
   ek saath. Woh photo hi bargaining power hai
```

Yeh chapter 2 ka block-ganit hai, sabse upar ke level par. **MLA khud ab ek "vote" ban gaya hai, aur unhe ek block ki tarah sambhala ja raha hai.** Wahi mukhiya-wali baat, bas ab mukhiya ek senior neta hai aur block MLAs ka hai.

### Deal mein kya milta hai

Jab koi group tootta hai, toh negotiation mein yeh cheezein hoti hain (yeh khulke bhi hoti hain aur chhupke bhi):

```
KHULA (announce hota hai):
  - mantri pad
  - board/corporation ka chairman-ship
  - agle chunav ka ticket (likhit nahi, lekin
    yeh sabse zaroori shart hai)
  - unke gut ke logon ke liye bhi tickets

CHHUPA (reported, aur aksar ilzaam):
  - paisa
  - jaanch ka "settle" hona
  - unke ilaake ke theke aur transfer par control
```

Aur ek cheez jo sabse kam samjhi jaati hai lekin sabse zaroori hai: **naye aane wale ko woh cheez chahiye jo uski machine ko zinda rakhe.** Matlab uske apne logon ke kaam. Agar uske 300 karyakartaon ke kaam nahi hue, toh 6 mahine mein uske log usse chhod denge — aur phir woh nayi party ke liye bhi bekaar hai.

### Purani party ka nuksaan — do tarah ka

Jab koi bada neta tootta hai:

```
1. TURANT: ginti kam ho gayi. Sarkar ka khatra.

2. DHEERE (zyada bada): uske saath uski MACHINE
   jaati hai. 300 booth ke log, 40 mukhiya,
   uski sanstha ka poora network.

   Party ke paas us seat par ab kuch nahi bacha —
   sirf jhanda bacha. Aur jhanda vote nahi deta.
```

Isliye party ka pehla kaam toot ke baad yeh hota hai: **uske gut ke doosre-teesre level ke logon ko rok lo.** Unko pad do, ticket ka waada do. Kyunki agar machine ruk gayi, toh neta akela chala gaya — aur akela neta doosri party mein utna keemti nahi hai.

Yahan poori kitab ka ek important sach hai:

> **Neta ki keemat uske naam mein nahi, uske SAATH AANE WALON mein hoti hai. Jo neta akela tootta hai, uska bhaav aadha ho jaata hai — aur nayi party usko do saal mein bhool jaati hai.**

### Contested corner

**Kya anti-defection law kaam kar raha hai?**

- **Camp A (kaam kar raha hai):** Roz-roz ki toot band ho gayi. Ab ek aadmi apni marzi se nahi tod sakta — usko 2/3 chahiye, ya istifa dena padega. Sarkarein pehle se zyada sthir hain.
- **Camp B (fail ho gaya):** Isne dal-badal ko rokа nahi, sirf mehnga aur bada kar diya — ab poora group tootta hai. Aur upar se isne MLA ko party ka gulaam bana diya: ab woh apne kshetra ke hisaab se vote nahi kar sakta, whip ke hisaab se karna padta hai. Matlab isne loktantra ko party ke andar khatam kar diya.

**Ek aur contested cheez: Speaker ka role.** Ayogya ghoshit karne ka faisla Speaker karta hai — jo khud ek party ka aadmi hota hai. Ilzaam lagta hai ki Speaker faisla mahinon-saalon tak latka dete hain, jab tak sarkar ka ganit set na ho jaye. Adalaton ne bhi kai baar is der par tippani ki hai. Iska koi saaf structural samadhan abhi tak nahi nikla.

### Incentive equation

```
TOOTNE WALA MLA:
  Deta hai:  apni party, apni "wafadaar" wali image
  Paata hai: mantri pad / ticket / apne logon ke kaam
             / machine ka bachav
  Darr:      (a) machine ka dheere-dheere marna
             (b) toota lekin akela — toh bhaav aadha
  Isliye:    woh AKELA nahi todta. Woh pehle apne
             logon ko ginta hai. Ginti hi uska bhaav hai.

LENE WALI PARTY:
  Deti hai:  pad, ticket, kaam
  Paati hai: ginti (sarkar), aur ek ready-made machine
  Darr:      aane wala 6 mahine mein bekar ho jaye
             (agar uske log saath nahi aaye)
  Isliye:    woh sirf aadmi nahi kharidti — woh uske
             SAATH AANE WALON ko ginti hai

CHHODI GAYI PARTY:
  Turant nuksaan: ginti
  Asli nuksaan:   machine
  Isliye:    woh turant uske gut ke junior logon ko
             pad baantna shuru kar deti hai

KARYAKARTA (ch.5):
  Uske liye yeh sabse mushkil din hai. Usko chunna
  hai: 20 saal ka aadmi, ya woh party jisme uske
  kaam honge?
  Aur aksar woh AADMI ko chunta hai — kyunki uska
  rishta party se nahi, us aadmi se tha
```

Woh aakhri box sabse zaroori hai. **Yehi wajah hai ki machine neta ke saath jaati hai, party ke paas nahi rehti.**

### Baban ki kahani — part 13

Baban 51 ka. Uske MLA ka gut do saal se haara hua tha (ch.12).

Us saal state mein chunav hua aur result hung aaya. Kisi ko poora bahumat nahi. Aur do din mein poora state ek ginti ka khel ban gaya.

Teesre din raat 1 baje Baban ke ghar ek gaadi rukhi. Doosre gut ka ek aadmi — jise Baban 15 saal se jaanta tha — andar aaya, chai bhi nahi pi, aur seedha bola:

"Tumhare saheb ke saath kitne hain?"

Baban ne kaha: "Woh saheb se poocho."

Aadmi hansa: "Saheb se poocha toh woh 14 bolenge. Isliye main tumse pooch raha hoon. **Tumhe pata hai asli ginti kya hai.**"

Baban chup raha. Kyunki aadmi sahi tha — usko pata tha ki 14 mein se 6 pakke the, 4 hilne wale the, aur 4 sirf naam ke the.

Agle 4 din Baban ne apni zindagi ka sabse ajeeb kaam kiya: woh un 14 MLAs ke **karyakartaon** se phone par baat karta raha. MLA se nahi — unke neeche wale logon se.

Aur usne jo dekha, usne uski poori samajh badal di.

```
6 MLA ke log bole:      "saheb jahan jaayein, hum wahan"
4 MLA ke log bole:      "hum saheb ke saath hain, lekin
                         agar udhar gaye toh hamara kaam
                         hoga na? do saal se kuch nahi hua"
4 MLA ke log bole:      "hum toh party ke saath hain,
                         saheb ka pata nahi"
```

Baban ne register mein us raat likha:

> **MLA ki ginti do baar hoti hai. Ek baar uske apne muh se, jo hamesha bade number bolta hai. Aur ek baar uske logon ke muh se, jo asli hoti hai. Jo aadmi doosri ginti kar leta hai, woh pehli ginti wale se hamesha aage rehta hai.**

Aakhir mein unka gut nahi toota — sarkar doosre tareeke se ban gayi. Lekin us hafte mein Baban ne teen cheezein seekhin jo usne likh kar rakhi:

> **1. Jab ginti ka khel shuru hota hai, tab har aadmi ka asli bhaav 48 ghante mein khul jaata hai. Saal bhar jo chhupa rehta hai, woh us raat pata chal jaata hai.**

> **2. Koi neta akela nahi tootta. Woh todne se pehle apne logon se poochta hai — aur agar log mana kar dein, toh woh ruk jaata hai. Matlab asli faisla neta nahi, uske log karte hain. Bas yeh baat kabhi likhi nahi jaati.**

> **3. Aur sabse badi baat: wafadaari kabhi party se nahi hoti. Wafadaari hamesha AADMI se hoti hai — aur aadmi se bhi tab tak jab tak uske through kaam hote hain. Jis din kaam rukte hain, wafadaari ki ginti ulti chalne lagti hai.**

Aur us hafte ke aakhir mein, ek chhoti si line — jo uski apni zindagi ke baare mein thi:

> **Aaj mujhe pehli baar dikha ki poore state mein sirf 5-6 log the jo asli ginti jaante the. Aur main unme se ek tha. Maine 25 saal kisi aur ke liye ginti ki hai. Ab bhi mera naam kisi list mein nahi hai.**

### Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

**1. Anti-defection law ne dal-badal ko rokа ya badla — kaise?**

> *Jawab:* Badla. Pehle ek-ek aadmi tootta tha (retail), ab 2/3 wali chhoot ki wajah se **poora group** tootta hai (wholesale). Rule ne behaviour band nahi kiya, usse naya raasta pakadne par majboor kiya — wahi "sarpanch pati" wala pattern (ch.3), bade level par.

**2. Neta party kyun badalta hai — lalach se aage wala asli jawab?**

> *Jawab:* Survival. Virodhi dal mein rehna compounding decay hai — kaam atakte hain, karyakarta khiskte hain, thekedaar door jaate hain, aur 5 saal mein machine hi nahi bachti. Machine ke bina woh phir kabhi wapas nahi aa sakta. Isliye woh machine bachane ke liye todta hai, paise ke liye nahi (paisa bonus hai).

**3. Resort politics logical kyun hai — 4 wajah?**

> *Jawab:* (1) Doosri taraf ka offer MLA tak na pahunche, (2) uske apne log/parivaar ka dabav door rahe, (3) roz ginti pakki rahe, (4) media ko photo mile — "45 MLA ek saath" wali photo hi bargaining power hai.

**4. "Akela tootne wale ka bhaav aadha" kyun?**

> *Jawab:* Kyunki neta ki keemat uske naam mein nahi, uske saath aane walon (machine) mein hai. Nayi party usko ginti aur ready-made machine ke liye leti hai. Agar uske 300 karyakarta saath nahi aaye, toh 6 mahine mein woh nayi party ke liye bhi bekaar hai.

**5. Karyakarta toot ke waqt kise chunta hai, aur kyun?**

> *Jawab:* Aksar **aadmi** ko, party ko nahi — kyunki uska rishta party ke jhande se nahi, us aadmi se tha jisne uske kaam karwaye (ch.5). Aur isliye machine neta ke saath jaati hai, purani party ke paas sirf jhanda bachta hai.

### What this means for you

Jab bhi khabar aaye ki koi neta party badal raha hai, us khabar ko is tarah mat padho ki "isne dhoka diya." Yeh do sawaal poocho: **"iski machine ka kya haal tha?"** aur **"iske saath kitne log ja rahe hain?"** Pehla batayega usne kyun toda, doosra batayega uska asli bhaav kya hai.

Aur agar kabhi kisi neta se milo jab uski party virodh mein hai — yaad rakhna ki woh sabse zyada dabav mein hai aur sabse zyada khula bhi. Uske paas paisa kam hoga, lekin uski zaroorat sabse tez hogi: **usko woh cheez chahiye jo uski machine ko zinda rakh sake jab tak uske paas taakat nahi hai.**
