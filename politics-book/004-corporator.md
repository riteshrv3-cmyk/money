# Chapter 4
## Corporator — wahi cell, sheher mein, zyada paise ke saath

---

### Gaon se sheher

Ab Nimgaon chhodo. Sheher chalo.

Sheher mein bhi wahi need hai jo gaon mein thi — jhagde, saajhi cheezein, suraksha. Lekin teen cheezein badal jaati hain, aur teenon system ka behavior badal deti hain:

```
                    GAON                    SHEHER (ward)
Voters              600-1,000               15,000-60,000
Log ek doosre ko    sab sabko jaante        koi kisi ko nahi jaanta
Sabse badi cheez    zameen (kheti)          zameen (construction)
Paisa               thoda, sarkari          bahut, sarkari + private
```

Teesri line sabse important hai. Gaon mein zameen se anaaj nikalta hai. Sheher mein zameen se **building** nikalti hai, aur building se paisa nikalta hai — gaon ke kisi bhi hisaab se hazaar guna zyada.

Isliye sheher ka corporator gaon ke sarpanch ka bada bhai nahi hai. Woh **ek alag jaanwar** hai, jo ek alag cheez ke ird-gird bana hai: **construction**.

### Ward mein kya hota hai

Corporator ek ward se jeetta hai — sheher ka ek tukda. Municipal corporation (mahanagar palika) mein woh ek member hota hai, sau-do sau members mein se ek.

Uske haath mein kya hai:

```
1. WARD KA KAAM
   Sadak, naali, paani ki line, street light, kachra,
   garden. Chhota kaam, lekin ROZ ka kaam — aur har kaam
   ka ek thekedar hota hai.

2. PERMISSIONS KA RASTA
   Building plan pass hona, water connection, drainage
   connection, trade licence, shop ka NOC. Corporator
   khud sign nahi karta — officer karta hai — lekin
   corporator ke bina file "chalti" nahi. Iska matlab
   samajhna is chapter ka asli kaam hai (neeche).

3. ATIKRAMAN (encroachment) KA SWITCH
   Footpath par thela, awaidh floor, awaidh shed —
   yeh sab technically illegal hai. Toh chalta kyun hai?
   Kyunki hatana bhi ek DECISION hai. Jiske haath mein
   "hatao ya rehne do" ka switch hai, uske paas roz ki
   taakat hai — poore sheher ke chhote dhandhon par.

4. SLUM KA VOTE-BANK
   Sheher ka sabse solid block. Wahan har cheez —
   paani, bijli ka connection, ration card, ghar ka
   proof — kisi na kisi permission par tiki hai.
   Aur permission ka rasta corporator se hokar jaata hai.
```

### Sheher ka block gaon se alag kyun hota hai

Gaon mein block khaandaan ka tha — khoon ka rishta. Sheher mein khoon nahi chalta, kyunki padosi ka naam bhi nahi pata.

Toh sheher mein block kis cheez se banta hai? **Zaroorat se.**

```
GAON KA BLOCK          SHEHER KA BLOCK
khaandaan (40 vote)    slum/basti (2,000 vote)
jaati (500 vote)       society/apartment (300 vote)
mohalla (200 vote)     bhasha/region ke log (5,000 vote)
                       hawker/vyapari association (800 vote)
```

Dhyan do: sheher ke blocks **institutions** hain, parivaar nahi. Basti ka ek leader, society ka secretary, hawker union ka pramukh, mandir/masjid committee — yeh sheher ke mukhiya hain.

Aur inme sabse solid kaun hai? **Basti.** Kyunki wahan zaroorat sabse gehri hai. Apartment wale ko corporator ki zaroorat saal mein shayad ek baar padti hai. Basti wale ko har mahine padti hai — paani, kaagaz, hatane ki dhamki, bachche ka school. Jahan zaroorat roz ki hai, wahan block sabse pakka hai.

Yeh ek line yaad rakhna, kyunki yeh poori kitab mein sach rahegi:

> **Vote-bank wahan sabse majboot hota hai jahan zaroorat sabse gehri hai — jahan log sabse zyada padhe-likhe ya sabse zyada paisewale hain wahan nahi.**

Isliye sheher ka corporator apna zyada waqt basti mein deta hai, un colonies mein nahi jahan bade log rehte hain. Bade log shikayat karte hain ki "hamare yahan koi neta aata hi nahi." Woh sahi keh rahe hain — aur wajah simple hai: unka block loose hai, unki turnout kam hai, aur unko corporator ki roz zaroorat nahi. Woh ganit mein bhaari nahi padte.

### Builder ka rishta — sheher ka asli engine

Ab is chapter ka core. Dhyan se, kyunki yeh mechanism aage har level par milega, bas naam badal ke.

Ek builder ko project banane ke liye kya chahiye? Zameen, paisa, aur **kaagaz ka pahaad** — plan approval, height clearance, water/drainage connection, occupancy certificate, aur beech mein aane wale local virodh ka settlement.

Kaagaz technically **officer** deta hai — corporator nahi. Toh builder corporator ke paas kyun jaata hai?

Teen wajah:

**1. Speed.** Har file mein delay ka option hota hai. "Objection" lagana, "clarification" maangna, file ko table par rakh dena — yeh sab legal hai. Ek project mein 6 mahine ka delay builder ke liye interest aur cost ka pahaad hai. **Corporator file ko haan nahi karwata, woh file ko RUKNE se bachata hai.** Yeh farak samajhna zaroori hai — taakat mostly "haan" karwane mein nahi hoti, "na" ko rokne mein hoti hai.

**2. Zameeni virodh.** Project ke saamne wale log dhool, rasta band, paani ki shikayat leke khade ho jaate hain. Corporator un logon ka hi leader hai. Woh chahe toh virodh khada karwa sakta hai, chahe toh baitha sakta hai.

**3. Corporation ke andar ki awaaz.** Standing committee, general body — jahan tenders aur proposals pass hote hain, wahan corporator baitha hai.

Iske badle builder kya deta hai? Yahan honest raho:

```
KHULA (declared, legal):
  - Ward ke kaam ke liye "CSR" type contribution
  - Ganesh utsav / navratri / cricket tournament ka sponsorship
  - Society ke liye garden, gym, ambulance donate karna

CHHUPA (undeclared — reported, kaagaz par nahi):
  - Cash percentage, project size par
  - Flats: project mein ek-do flat "book" ho jaate hain
    naam par ya kisi rishtedaar ke naam par, kaagaz par
    kam daam mein
  - Partnership: corporator ya uska rishtedaar seedha
    project mein hissedaar
  - Election ka poora kharcha builder uthata hai
```

Ab poora circle dekho, kyunki yehi is chapter ka architecture hai:

```
   BUILDER ────paisa────► CORPORATOR ────kaam/utsav────► BASTI/WARD
      ▲                        │                            │
      │                        │                            │ vote
      │                        ▼                            │
      └──── file chalti ── TAAKAT (kursi) ◄─────────────────┘
```

Yeh loop khud ko chalata hai. Builder ka paisa corporator ke ward ke kaam aur utsav mein lagta hai. Us kaam se vote aate hain. Vote se kursi rehti hai. Kursi se builder ki file chalti hai. Aur phir se.

**Is loop mein koi villain nahi hai — sirf players hain jinke incentives fit ho jaate hain.** Builder ko speed chahiye, basti ko kaam chahiye, corporator ko vote chahiye. Teenon ko jo chahiye woh mil raha hai. Jo nahi mil raha, woh sheher ki planning hai — kyunki us loop mein "acchi planning" ki koi party hai hi nahi.

Yehi wajah hai ki sheher badhte hain lekin plan ke hisaab se nahi badhte. Galat log nahi hain — loop mein planning ka koi customer nahi hai.

### Contested corner

**Kya corporator sach mein itna taakatwar hai?**

- **Camp A:** Haan. Ward level par woh gatekeeper hai; officer usse ladta nahi kyunki officer ko roz kaam karna hai.
- **Camp B:** Nahi — asli taakat commissioner (IAS) aur bade neta ke paas hai. Corporator sirf messenger hai, aur badle mein choti-moti cheezein paata hai. Bada paisa upar jaata hai, corporator ko sirf hissa milta hai.

Sach shayad sheher ke size par depend karta hai: chhote sheher mein corporator asli player, bade metro mein woh chain ka ek hissa. Chapter 16 mein jab officer-neta ka rishta khulega, tab yeh saaf hoga.

### Incentive equation

```
CORPORATOR:
  Deta hai:  file ka na-rukna, virodh ka settlement,
             basti ka kaam
  Paata hai: builder ka paisa + election ka kharcha
             + ward par roz ka control
  Darr:      ward chhota hai — 15-60 hazaar vote. Ek basti
             naraz hui toh seat gayi. Isliye corporator
             ka phone 24 ghante khula rehta hai.
  Isliye:    woh sabse ZYADA available neta hota hai
             (MLA se bhi zyada) — yeh uski majboori hai,
             achhai nahi

BUILDER:
  Deta hai:  paisa (khula + chhupa)
  Paata hai: time — aur real estate mein time hi paisa hai
  Darr:      project atka toh interest kha jayega
  Isliye:    woh corporator ko kharcha nahi, INSURANCE
             maanta hai

BASTI ka voter:
  Deta hai:  block vote (sabse solid)
  Paata hai: paani, kaagaz, bulldozer se bachav
  Darr:      ghar par nishaan lag jaye
  Isliye:    uska vote sabse loyal hai, kyunki uska
             risk sabse bada hai
```

### Baban ki kahani — part 4

Bhaskar sarpanch ban gaya. Baban ka gaon mein kaam khatam.

22 saal ki umar mein Baban sheher aaya — ek rishtedaar ke through, ek corporator ke office mein "ladka" banke. Tankha 4,000 rupaye mahina. Kaam: register likhna, phone uthana, aur logon ko baithana.

Pehle hafte mein usko ek jhatka laga. Gaon mein sarpanch ke paas din mein 10 log aate the. Yahan corporator ke office mein subah 8 baje se 40-50 log baithe hote the — aur corporator sabse milta tha. Har ek se do minute, teen minute. Baban ko lagta tha yeh aadmi pagal hai, itna time kyun de raha hai.

Ek din usne poocha: "Saheb, aap sabse kyun milte ho? Aadha to kaam bhi nahi hota."

Corporator ne jawab diya jo Baban ne 30 saal yaad rakha: "**Kaam nahi hota, lekin milna hota hai. Log kaam ke liye nahi aate, sune jaane ke liye aate hain. Jo unko sunta hai, unka vote uska hai — kaam ho ya na ho.**"

Doosra jhatka do mahine baad laga. Ek shaam office khali tha, aur ek aadmi aaya — mehanga kurta, gaadi bahar. Woh baithne wali kursi par nahi baitha; seedha andar chala gaya. Baban ne register nikala uska naam likhne ke liye. Corporator ne haath se ishara kiya — mat likh.

Woh builder tha.

Us raat Baban ne apni copy mein — wahi purani aadat — do column banaye:

```
JO REGISTER MEIN LIKHE JAATE HAIN  |  JO NAHI LIKHE JAATE
40-50 log roz                       |  hafte mein 2-3
paani, kaagaz, shikayat             |  file, plan, zameen
dete hain: VOTE                     |  dete hain: PAISA
```

Aur neeche ek line likhi, jo is kitab ka aadha hissa hai:

> **Neta do log se milta hai. Ek se vote milta hai, doosre se paisa. Dono zaroori hain. Ek ke bina kursi nahi, doosre ke bina chunav nahi.**

Teesri cheez Baban ne dheere-dheere samjhi, aur woh sabse zaroori thi. Corporator basti mein har Sunday jaata tha — bina kaam ke, bina camera ke. Baban ko lagta tha yeh waqt ki barbadi hai. Ek Sunday corporator ne usse kaha: "Basti ke 2,000 vote hain. Society waalon ke 400. Society waale mujhe gaali dete hain ki main sirf basti jaata hoon. Sahi kehte hain. Lekin unko meri zaroorat saal mein ek baar padti hai. Basti waalon ko har mahine. **Jiski zaroorat roz ki hai, uska vote pakka hai.** Main achha aadmi nahi hoon, main ginti ka aadmi hoon."

Baban ne pehli baar samjha ki gaon aur sheher mein farak sirf size ka nahi hai. Gaon mein block khoon se banta tha. Sheher mein block **zaroorat** se banta hai — aur zaroorat khoon se zyada wafadaar hoti hai.

### Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

**1. Sheher ka block gaon ke block se kis cheez par bana hota hai, aur sabse solid block kaunsa hota hai?**

> *Jawab:* Gaon mein khoon/khaandaan par; sheher mein zaroorat par (basti, society, union, bhasha-group). Sabse solid = basti, kyunki wahan zaroorat roz ki hai — paani, kaagaz, bulldozer se bachav. Rule: jahan zaroorat sabse gehri, wahan vote sabse pakka.

**2. Builder corporator ke paas kyun jaata hai jab kaagaz officer deta hai?**

> *Jawab:* Kyunki corporator file ko "haan" nahi karwata — usko **rukne se bachata** hai. Delay legal hai aur builder ke liye sabse mehngi cheez hai (interest + cost). Saath mein zameeni virodh baithana aur corporation ki committee mein awaaz. Taakat "na" ko rokne mein hai, "haan" karwane mein nahi.

**3. Sheher plan ke hisaab se kyun nahi badhte — kaun galat hai?**

> *Jawab:* Koi nahi. Builder-corporator-basti ka loop teenon ko woh de raha hai jo unhe chahiye (speed, vote, kaam). Us loop mein "acchi planning" ka koi customer hi nahi hai — jo cheez kisi ka incentive nahi hoti, woh apne aap nahi hoti.

**4. Corporator MLA se zyada available kyun hota hai?**

> *Jawab:* Uska ward chhota hai (15-60 hazaar vote). Ek basti naraz = seat gayi. Chhoti seat = zyada nazuk = zyada availability. Yeh uski achhai nahi, ganit ki majboori hai.

**5. "Kaam nahi hota, lekin milna hota hai" — iska ganit kya hai?**

> *Jawab:* Har aadmi ka kaam karwana namumkin hai (resources limited). Lekin sunna free hai, aur sune jaane wala aadmi apne block mein tumhara naam leta hai. Milna ek scalable product hai, kaam nahi. Chapter 10 mein yeh janta darbar ke roop mein poora khulega.

### What this means for you

Sheher ka koi bhi neta samajhna ho toh do sawaal poocho: **"iske blocks kahan hain?"** aur **"iske ward mein construction chal raha hai kya?"** Pehla batayega uski kursi kis par tiki hai, doosra batayega uska paisa kahan se aata hai. Aur agar tum kabhi kisi corporator ya MLA ko kuch bechne jao — yaad rakhna uske paas do alag queue hain, aur woh tumhe pehle 5 minute mein decide kar lega ki tum kis queue ke ho: register wali, ya jo register mein nahi likhi jaati.

*Structure verified: corporators sit in municipal corporations, wards vary roughly 15,000-60,000 voters by city size; building permissions are issued by corporation officers, not corporators.
