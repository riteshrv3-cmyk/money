Part 0
The Operating System

1. Raja kyun chahiye — why the decider job exists

Gaon se shuru

Sau parivaar ek valley mein baste hain. Kheti hai, kuaan hai, mandir hai. Abhi tak koi sarkar nahi, koi neta nahi — kyunki kisi ne abhi tak yeh cheez invent hi nahi ki.

Pehle mahine sab theek chalta hai. Phir zindagi apna kaam karti hai:

  • Do bhai zameen ke boundary par lad pade. Dono kehte hain "patthar wahan nahi, yahan tha."
  • Upar wale khet ka aadmi poora paani apni taraf mod leta hai. Neeche ke teen khet sookh rahe hain.
  • Raat mein kisi ke dhor chori hue. Sab ek doosre par shak kar rahe hain.
  • Kuaan sabka hai, lekin uski safai koi nahi karta. Jo cheez sabki hai, woh kisi ki nahi hoti.

Ab dhyan se dekho: yeh char problems kheti se solve nahi hoti, taakat se solve nahi hoti, paise se bhi solve nahi hoti. Inko solve karne ke liye chahiye ek faisla — aur faisla tabhi chalta hai jab sab usse maanein.

Yeh hai governance. Tumhari 10 needs ki list mein jo "governance" likha hai, uska asli matlab yeh hai: jhagde ka faisla, sabki cheezon ki dekhbhal, aur bahar ke khatre se raksha. Roti jitni real need hai, yeh bhi utni hi real hai. Bas dikhti kam hai, kyunki jab yeh kaam ho raha hota hai, tab kuch hota hua dikhta nahi. Governance sirf tab dikhti hai jab woh fail hoti hai.

Ab village ka sawaal

Gaon ne maan liya ki ek decider chahiye. Ab do naye sawaal khade hote hain, aur poori kitab in dono ke andar hai:

Sawaal 1: Decider ko denge kya?

Farmer anaaj ugata hai, toh anaaj bechke kamata hai. Decider kya "ugata" hai? Faisle. Lekin faisla bikta nahi. Jhagde ka faisla karne ke baad decider haath mein kya lekar ghar jayega?

Gaon kehta hai: theek hai, sab thoda thoda anaaj denge, decider ka pet chal jayega. Yeh hai salary — aur dhyan rakhna, yeh hamesha chhoti hoti hai. Kyun? Kyunki gaon kabhi nahi chahega ki decider ki salary badi ho. "Woh toh sirf baithke faisle karta hai" — yeh feeling har gaon mein, har desh mein, har zamane mein same hai.

Sawaal 2: Decider ki baat log maanenge kyun?

Faisla tabhi faisla hai jab dono party maane — haarne wala bhi. Iske liye decider ke paas chahiye taakat: gaon ke jawan uske kehne par khade hon, galat karne wale ko saza de sake, sahi karne wale ko inaam.

Aur yahin par woh cheez janam leti hai jisko samjhe bina Indian politics kabhi samajh nahi aayegi:

The strange job — duniya ki sabse ajeeb naukri

Decider ki job description likho toh aisi dikhti hai:

Salary:  chhoti (gaon kanjoosi karega, hamesha)
Taakat:  bahut badi (faisle, saza, inaam, sab uske haath)

Yeh combination duniya ki kisi aur naukri mein nahi milta. Aur yehi combination poore system ka engine hai.

Kyunki jab salary chhoti ho aur taakat badi ho, toh us taakat ki apni ek keemat ban jaati hai:

  • Jiska jhagda chal raha hai, woh chahega faisla uske haq mein ho. Woh decider ko khush rakhega.
  • Jisko kuaan ke paas ki zameen chahiye, woh decider ke ghar anaaj bhijwayega.
  • Jisko raat ki chori mein apna naam saaf karana hai, woh decider ka aadmi ban jayega.

Decider ne kisi se kuch manga nahi. Taakat ke aas-paas log khud line laga lete hain. Power apne aap convert hoti hai — favors mein, izzat mein, anaaj mein, aur aage jaake paise mein.

Ab wapas dekho salary wali baat. Gaon ne salary chhoti rakhi thi taaki decider par kharcha kam ho. Result kya nikla? Decider ne apni kamai ka rasta salary se hatakar taakat mein dhoondh liya. Gaon ne kanjoosi ki, aur usi kanjoosi ne woh cheez paida ki jisko aaj hum corruption kehte hain. Yeh koi accident nahi hai. Yeh design ka natural result hai. Isko yaad rakhna — aage har chapter mein yeh wapas aayega.

Ab ek minute ruko aur khud se poocho — yeh chapter ka sabse important sawaal hai:

Ek aadmi 5 saal ki naukri ke liye, jisme total salary ~Rs 60 lakh se 1.5 crore banegi, election par crores kyun kharch karta hai?

Agar yeh naukri sirf salary hoti, toh yeh sauda duniya ka sabse ghatiya investment hai. Koi bania yeh deal kabhi nahi karega.

Lekin log jaan lagakar yeh deal karte hain. Iska matlab sirf ek hai: jo cheez woh khareed rahe hain, woh salary nahi hai. Woh khareed rahe hain kursi — matlab taakat — matlab woh sab kuch jo taakat ke aas-paas apne aap line lagata hai: thekede, zameen ke faisle, transfer-posting, institutions, izzat, aur agli election jeetne ki machine.

Village wale decider aur aaj ke MLA mein sirf scale ka fark hai. Structure bilkul same hai: chhoti salary + badi taakat = kamai taakat se hogi, salary se nahi.

Baban ki kahani — part 1

Nimgaon, ek chhota sa gaon. Baban 12 saal ka hai. Uska baap Tatya — chaar ekad ka kisaan, mandi mein jhukkar milne wala aadmi.

Us saal Baban ke chacha aur padosi Ganpat mein zameen ka jhagda hua. Boundary ka patthar raat mein hil gaya tha — kisne hilaya, yeh dono ko "pakka pata" tha, aur dono ka pakka alag tha. Do mahine dono ghar ek doosre se nahi bole. Chacha ne darwaza band kar liya, Ganpat ne kuein ka rasta rok diya. Baban ne pehli baar dekha ki ek patthar do parivaron ko kaise kha jaata hai.

Baap log police ke paas kyun nahi gaye? Baban ne poocha tha. Tatya hansa: "Police? Police ko patthar dikhega? Police ko kaagaz chahiye, aur kaagaz dono ke paas nahi."

Phir ek shaam sarpanch aaya — safed kurta, dheemi chaal, peeche do aadmi. Aangan mein khatiya bichhi. Sarpanch aadha ghanta baitha. Pehle chacha ko poora bolne diya, beech mein ek baar bhi nahi roka. Phir Ganpat ko. Phir chai pi, aur chai ke baad ek line boli: "Patthar wahan lagega jahan tumhare baap ke waqt tha — Ganpat ke khet mein do haath andar. Aur Ganpat, kuein ka rasta kal se khula."

Dono maan gaye. Dono ko aadha-aadha mila tha, isliye dono haar ke bhi jeet gaye. Jo kaam do mahine, do parivaar, aur ek band darwaza nahi kar paya, woh aadhe ghante mein ho gaya.

Lekin Baban ne woh cheez dekhi jo baaki sab ne miss kar di. Jaate waqt Ganpat sarpanch ke paas gaya aur dheere se bola: "Bhau, mera chhota ladka... school mein naam likhwana hai, master maanta nahi." Sarpanch ne bina ruke kaha: "Somvaar ko bhej dena."

Faisla free tha. Lekin faisle ke saath ek queue shuru ho gayi thi.

Us raat Baban ne apne baap se poocha: "Sarpanch kheti kyun nahi karta?"

Tatya ne kaha: "Uska kaam faisle karna hai."

"Toh usko anaaj kaun deta hai?"

"Gaon deta hai. Panchayat se milta hai thoda."

Baban ne teesra sawaal poocha, jo asli sawaal tha: "Itna thoda milta hai toh uske ghar mein sabse pehle bijli kaise aayi? Pakka ghar kaise bana?"

Tatya chup ho gaya. Phir bola: "So ja."

Jab baap "so ja" bole, tab samajh lo sawaal sahi jagah laga hai. Baban ko poora jawab 30 saal baad milega. Humein agle 14 chapters mein milega.

Self-test (pehle apne dimaag se jawab do, phir neeche check karo)

1. Governance ek "need" kyun hai — sirf ek sarkari system kyun nahi?

Jawab: Kyunki kuch problems sirf faisle se solve hoti hain — jhagde, saajhi cheezein (kuaan), chori/suraksha. Yeh problems har basti mein khud paida hoti hain, koi impose nahi karta. Jahan 100 log saath rahenge, wahan decider ki demand apne aap banegi — isliye yeh need hai, system baad mein aata hai.

2. Decider ki salary hamesha chhoti kyun, aur us kanjoosi ka side-effect kya?

Jawab: Gaon ko lagta hai "yeh sirf baithke faisle karta hai," isliye koi community decider ko zyada dena nahi chahti. Side-effect: decider apni kamai salary ke bahar dhoondta hai — taakat ke conversion mein. Kam salary corruption ko rokta nahi, paida karta hai.

3. MLA candidate crores kharch karke ~Rs 1 crore ki naukri kyun leta hai?

Jawab: Kyunki woh naukri nahi khareed raha, kursi khareed raha hai. Kursi = faisle + saza + inaam ki taakat, aur us taakat ke aas-paas favors, theke, zameen, transfer, institutions ki queue khud lag jaati hai. Salary is deal ka sabse chhota hissa hai.

4. "Power apne aap convert hoti hai" — teen raste jo village mein dikhe?

Jawab: (1) Jiska jhagda pending hai, woh decider ko khush rakhta hai. (2) Jisko saajhi cheez (kuaan ke paas ki zameen) chahiye, woh bhent bhejta hai. (3) Jisko protection/naam saaf karana hai, woh decider ka aadmi ban jaata hai. Teenon mein decider ne kuch manga nahi — line khud lagi.

Chaaron sahi the? Chapter 2 kholo. Koi galat tha? Us section par wapas jao.

What this means for you

Jab bhi kisi neta se milo — MLA ho ya sarpanch — uski salary ko poori tarah bhool jao. Uska asli P&L yeh hai: kursi bachane ka kharcha vs taakat se hone wali conversion. Uske har decision ko is lens se dekho, aur uska behavior predict karna shuru ho jayega. Aur jab tum usse kuch bechne jaoge, toh yaad rakhna — jo cheez uski kursi bachaane mein madad kare, wahi cheez uske liye keemti hai. Baaki sab kharcha hai.

Numbers: MLA salaries state-wise (Bihar ~45k, UP ~65k, Maharashtra ~2.3-2.5L all-in, Telangana ~2.75L) — salaryinsight.in, collegesearch.in, tribuneindia.com, prsindia.org. Election spend caps Rs 28-40 lakh — drishtiias.com. "Asli kharcha kai guna" is reported/estimate, not an official figure.

2. Vote ki keemat — ek vote bekaar hai, vote-bank sab kuch hai

Gaon se shuru

Nimgaon mein ab decider chunna hai. Gaon ne tay kiya: har aadmi-aurat ka ek vote, jiske zyada vote uski kursi.

Pehli nazar mein yeh barabari ka system lagta hai. Sau log, sau vote, sabki keemat same.

Ab ek kisaan ki nazar se dekho — naam rakho Tatya. Tatya sochta hai: "Mera ek vote hai. Sau mein se ek. Agar main vote dun ya na dun, result shayad hi badle. Toh meri keemat kya hai?"

Tatya bilkul sahi soch raha hai. Akela vote sach mein bekaar hai. Yeh koi cynical baat nahi, seedha ganit hai: 100 vote mein 1 vote result 1% badalta hai. 3 lakh mein 1 vote kuch nahi badalta.

Lekin ab dekho kya hota hai. Tatya ke ghar mein 8 vote hain. Uske bhai, chachere bhai, unki gharwaliyan — milakar 40 vote. Aur Tatya ke kehne par poora khaandaan ek taraf jaata hai.

Ab Tatya ki keemat kya hai? 40. Ab decider banne ka sapna dekhne wala har aadmi Tatya ke ghar chai peene aayega.

Yeh hai is chapter ka poora sach, ek line mein: vote ki keemat tab paida hoti hai jab vote ek saath BLOCK mein chalta hai. Akela vote paisa nahi hai. Block ek bank account hai. Isliye naam pada — vote-bank.

Neta ki nazar se wahi gaon

Ab kursi ke candidate ki nazar se dekho. Uske paas time limited hai, paisa limited hai. Woh 100 logon se alag-alag nahi mil sakta.

Uske saamne gaon aise dikhta hai:

Tatya ka khaandaan        40 vote   (Tatya se baat karo)
Mandir ka pujari group    15 vote   (pujari se baat karo)
Kuaan ke paas ke ghar     20 vote   (unka mukhiya — Ganpat)
Naye base huye log        10 vote   (koi mukhiya nahi — mehnga sauda)
Idhar-udhar ke            15 vote   (kisi ki nahi sunte — chhod do)

Dekha kya hua? Candidate ne 100 logon ko 5 line mein compress kar diya. Ab usse sirf 3 aadmiyon se baat karni hai — Tatya, pujari, Ganpat — aur 75 vote ka intezam ho jaata hai.

Yeh compression hi politics hai. Neta log individuals ko ignore nahi karte kyunki woh bure hain. Ignore karte hain kyunki ganit yehi kehta hai. Ek-ek vote ke liye baat karna 100 meetings hai; block ke liye baat karna 3 meetings. Jo candidate individuals se milta rahega, woh haar jayega us candidate se jo mukhiyaon se milta hai.

Aur is ganit ka ulta side bhi samajh lo: jo aadmi apne peeche block khada kar sakta hai, uski political keemat hai. Jo nahi kar sakta, woh kitna bhi padha-likha ho, uski keemat zero hai. Yehi wajah hai ki gaon ka anpadh Tatya MLA ke saath stage par baithta hai aur sheher ka professor ghar par TV dekhta hai.

Ab asli India ke numbers

Structure wahi, scale badha do:

Ek assembly seat (MLA):     ~2-4 lakh voters (approx, state par depend)
Turnout (kitne vote dete):  ~60-66% aam taur par
                            (2024 Lok Sabha: 66%; Delhi 2025: ~61%)

Matlab ~3 lakh ki seat par asli pade vote: ~1.8-2 lakh

Aur ab woh number jo sab kuch samjha deta hai:

Jeetne wale ko kitne % vote milte hain?

2024 Lok Sabha:  jeetne walon ka average 50.58%
                 → 49% winners ne AADHE se KAM vote par jeeta
Delhi 2025:      winners ka average 51%

Iska matlab dhyan se samjho. India mein First Past The Post system hai — jiske sabse zyada vote, woh jeeta. 50% ki zaroorat nahi. Agar 4 candidate hain, toh 30-35% vote par bhi seat jeet jaati hai.

Ab poora hisaab jodo:

3,00,000 voters ki seat
   x 62% turnout        = ~1,86,000 vote padenge
   x ~35-40% (jeet)     = ~65,000-75,000 vote chahiye

Matlab: 3 LAKH log jinke naam par seat hai, unme se sirf
        ~70,000 ko apna banana hai. Baaki 2.3 lakh ka
        kuch bhi socho, farak nahi padta.

Yeh number — "jeetne ke liye asli kitne chahiye" — har campaign ka starting point hai. Aage chapter 8 mein jab hum booth system kholenge, toh dekhoge ki poori machine isi 70,000 ke ird-gird bani hai: kaun se mohalle, kaun se khaandaan, kaun se block milakar 70,000 banta hai.

Teen cheezein jo is ganit se apne aap nikalti hain

1. Vote todna bhi utna hi keemti hai jitna vote jodna. Agar mere paas 70,000 nahi ban rahe, toh doosra rasta hai: saamne wale ke block ko do hisson mein todo. Uske jaati ke doosre neta ko khada karwa do, uska vote batega, mera 60,000 bhi jeet jayega. Jab bhi koi "chhota candidate" khada dikhe jo jeet nahi sakta, yeh mat poochho "yeh kyun lada?" — yeh poochho "iske ladne se kiska ganit sudhra?"

2. Turnout ek hathiyar hai. Mere block ke log vote dene niklein, saamne wale ke log ghar baithe rahein — result wahi hai jo vote badalne se aata. Isliye election ke din ki asli ladai persuasion nahi, transportation hai: gaadi, parchi, "chalo chalo" karne wale ladke. Yeh chapter 9 ka poora subject hai.

3. Numbers pehle se pata hote hain. Voter list public hoti hai. Har candidate ko mahino pehle pata hota hai kaun se booth par kitne vote hain, pichhli baar kya hua tha. Election ke din surprise kam hota hai, hisaab zyada. Jo cheez TV par "lehar" dikhti hai, woh zameen par spreadsheet hai.

Contested corner (yahan map fuzzy hai)

Ek sawaal jispe khud experts ek raay nahi rakhte: kya paisa dekar vote sach mein khareed sakte ho?

  • Camp A kehta hai: haan — sharaab, cash, saree bat-ti hai, aur close seats mein yehi farak banati hai.
  • Camp B kehta hai: nahi — log paisa sabse le lete hain aur vote apne block ke hisaab se hi dete hain. Paisa vote nahi khareedta, sirf "race mein serious hoon" ka signal deta hai.

Zameen ka sach shayad beech mein hai, aur seat-by-seat alag hai. Is kitab mein jab bhi aisi jagah aayegi jahan ek settled answer nahi hai, main tumhe dono camp dikhaunga. Clarity ka matlab yeh bhi hai ki pata ho kahan par certainty fake hoti hai.

Incentive equation

NETA ka hisaab:
  70,000 ka target → blocks se banta hai → mukhiyaon se banta hai
  Isliye: neta ka asli customer VOTER nahi, MUKHIYA hai
  Voter ke liye kaam = retail (mehnga, dheema)
  Mukhiya ke liye kaam = wholesale (sasta, tez)

MUKHIYA (Tatya) ka hisaab:
  Deta hai: apna block (40-500 vote)
  Paata hai: neta ka direct access, apne logon ke kaam,
             gaon mein izzat, "MLA mera aadmi hai" ki taakat
  Darr: block toot gaya toh keemat zero
  Isliye: mukhiya apna block jodkar rakhne mein hi zinda hai

VOTER ka hisaab:
  Akele vote ki keemat ~zero, yeh usse pata hai
  Block ke saath rehne par: kaam hote hain, suraksha milti hai
  Isliye: block ke saath vote dena voter ke liye RATIONAL hai,
          bhed-chaal nahi

Yeh aakhri line dobara padho. Bahar se dekhne par "yeh log jhund mein vote kyun dete hain, apna dimaag kyun nahi lagate" wali feeling aati hai. Andar se dekho toh block ke saath rehna hi voter ka sabse samajhdar sauda hai — akela voter kuch nahi manwa sakta, block sab kuch manwa leta hai.

Baban ki kahani — part 2

Baban ab 15 saal ka hai. Sarpanch ka election aaya — 20 saal mein pehli baar purane sarpanch ko koi serious challenger mila hai: Bhaskar, jo sheher mein thekedaari karke wapas aaya hai, jeep ke saath.

Ek shaam purana sarpanch khud Baban ke ghar aaya. Baithak mein baitha, Tatya se kheti ki baat ki — paani ki, mandi ke bhaav ki, sab ki. Vote ka naam tak nahi liya. Chai pi, aur jaate waqt darwaze par ruk kar sirf itna bola: "Tatya bhau, ghar ke sab vote yaad rakhna."

Baban ne pehli baar dekha ki uska baap — jo mandi mein sabse jhukkar milta hai — apne ghar ki baithak mein sarpanch se barabar ki kursi par baitha tha. Aur sarpanch chai peene aaya tha, bulaya nahi gaya tha.

Do din baad Bhaskar ki jeep bhi aayi. Bhaskar seedha bola: "Kaka, chaalis vote tumhare haath mein hain. Main sarpanch bana toh tumhara chhota ladka panchayat office mein kaam karega."

Ab Tatya ke paas do offer the. Aur yahan Baban ne apni zindagi ki pehli political galti hote dekhi — apne hi baap se.

Tatya ne dono ko haan bol di. Sochta tha dono taraf se fayda lega. Baban ne dheere se kaha bhi: "Baba, dono ko haan bologe toh dono ko pata chal jayega." Tatya ne daant diya: "Tu abhi bachcha hai."

Result aaya. Purana sarpanch teen vote se jeeta. Aur agle hafte se dono taraf ke aadmi Tatya ko mandi mein dekh kar muskurana band ho gaye. Dono ko pata chal chuka tha ki Tatya ne dono ko haan boli thi — gaon mein baat chhupti nahi. Tatya ka block abhi bhi 40 vote ka tha, lekin uski keemat gir gayi thi, kyunki keemat vote mein nahi, BHAROSE mein hoti hai. Jo mukhiya apna word nahi rakhta, uska block agli baar ginti mein nahi aata.

Raat ko Baban ne apni copy ke aakhri page par gaon ka hisaab likha: 600 vote. Jeet ~250 par. Khaandaan ke 40, mama-side ke 30, kuaan wale mohalle ke 25... aur har block ke aage ek naya column banaya jo uske baap ne kabhi nahi banaya tha: "word ka pakka?"

Woh 15 saal ka tha, aur usne is chapter ka sabak apne ghar ki izzat girte hue seekha tha: block currency hai, lekin us currency ki backing bharosa hai. Jis din bharosa gaya, us din 40 vote sirf 40 log reh jaate hain.

Self-test (pehle apne dimaag se, phir check)

1. Akela vote bekaar, lekin block ke saath jaana rational — dono ek saath sach kaise?

Jawab: Akela vote result nahi badal sakta (1 in 3 lakh), yeh ganit hai. Lekin block ke member ke roop mein voter ko kaam, suraksha aur access milta hai — block ki taakat ka hissa milta hai. Matlab vote ki keemat individual level par zero hai aur block level par poori — voter block join karke apne zero ko non-zero bana leta hai.

2. 3 lakh ki seat par asli kitne vote chahiye?

Jawab: 3,00,000 x ~62% turnout = ~1,86,000 pade vote. FPTP mein 4-5 candidates ke beech ~35-40% par jeet = ~65,000-75,000 vote. Seat 3 lakh ki dikhti hai, ladai ~70 hazaar ki hoti hai.

3. Na-jeetne wala candidate kyun ladta hai?

Jawab: Kyunki uske ladne se kisi aur ka ganit sudhrta hai — woh kisi block ko todta hai (vote-katwa). Ya khud ko agli baar ke liye register kar raha hai: "mere paas 15,000 vote hain" dikha kar woh agli deal mein seat ya post maang sakta hai. Haarna bhi ek business model hai.

4. Neta ka asli customer kaun?

Jawab: Mukhiya/block-leader, voter nahi. Ek voter = 1 vote (retail). Ek mukhiya = 40-500 vote (wholesale). Limited time aur paise mein wholesale hi rational hai. Isliye neta ka network voters ka nahi, mukhiyaon ka hota hai.

5. "Vote kharide ja sakte hain?" — dono camps.

Jawab: Camp A: haan — cash/sharaab/saree close seats mein margin bana deti hai. Camp B: nahi — log sabse le lete hain, vote apne block ke hisaab se dete hain; paisa sirf "main serious candidate hoon" ka signal hai. Settled answer nahi hai — seat-by-seat alag, aur yehi is sawaal ka sahi jawab hai.

What this means for you

Jab bhi koi seat ya koi neta samajhna ho, sabse pehle yeh mat poochho "log kise pasand karte hain." Yeh poochho: "is seat ka 70,000 kaise banta hai — kaun se blocks, kaun se mukhiya?" Popularity TV ka subject hai. Blocks zameen ka subject hai. Aur jo aadmi tumhare saamne baitha hai (MLA ho ya uska PA), uski har raat ki tension yehi spreadsheet hai — kaun sa block pakka hai, kaun sa hil raha hai. Uski duniya mein sabse keemti cheez woh hai jo usse yeh spreadsheet zyada saaf dikha de.

3. Sarpanch ka election — poora system, sabse chhote size mein

Gaon se shuru

Scientist ko cell samajhna ho toh woh poora sharir nahi kholta. Ek cell microscope ke neeche rakhta hai, kyunki cell mein wahi sab kuch hai jo sharir mein hai — bas itna chhota ki ek nazar mein dikh jaye.

Indian politics ka cell hai sarpanch ka election. 600-1000 voters. Har cheez jo aage Delhi tak milegi — block ka ganit, paisa, jaati, todne ki chaal, sarkari fund ka khel — sab yahan maujood hai, aur itne chhote size mein ki tum poora game ek saath dekh sakte ho.

Isliye yeh chapter dhyan se padho. Aage ke 18 chapters isi ka bada version hain.

Pehla sawaal: is kursi mein hai kya?

Sarpanch ka honorarium mazaak jaisa hota hai — kai states mein kuch hazaar rupaye mahina. Lekin sarpanch ke elections mein log lakhs kharch karte hain, aur kuch jagah (jahan zameen mehngi hai) crores tak ki khabrein aati hain. (Reported — aise numbers kaagaz par nahi hote.)

Toh chapter 1 wala sawaal wapas: yeh log kya khareed rahe hain?

Kursi ke andar jo hai, uski list banao:

SARPANCH KE HAATH MEIN KYA HAI:

1. PAISA (upar se aata hua)
   Central/state government ab gaon ke development ka paisa
   SEEDHA gram panchayat ke account mein bhejti hai —
   sadak, naali, paani, shauchalay, sab ke fund.
   Kaam kaun karega, kis thekedar se, kis quality ka —
   panchayat decide karti hai. Matlab sarpanch decide karta hai.

2. LISTS (sabse underrated taakat)
   Garib kalyan ki har scheme ko ek LIST chahiye —
   kis parivar ko ghar milega, kise pension, kise ration
   ka labh. Yeh beneficiary lists banane mein panchayat
   ka haath hota hai. Jo list banata hai, woh decide
   karta hai kiska number pehle aayega.

3. KAAGAZ
   Rehne ka dakhla, character ka dakhla, chhoti NOCs —
   gaon ki zindagi ke choti-choti kaagazi zarooratein
   panchayat se hokar jaati hain.

4. IZZAT + LADDER
   Sarpanch gaon ka chehra hai. Tehsil aur zila ke neta
   usse baat karte hain. Yeh political career ki seedhi
   ka pehla danda hai — yahan se panchayat samiti, zila
   parishad, aur phir MLA ticket ka raasta dikhta hai.

Ab equation clear hai: honorarium chhota, control bada. Chapter 1 ka formula, sabse chhote scale par: kamai kursi ki taakat se hogi, salary se nahi.

Campaign kaise ladta hai — 600 voters ka game

Yahan TV nahi hai, akhbaar nahi hai, speech ka stage nahi hai. Sab kuch chapter 2 ka ganit hai, seedha aur nanga:

Step 1: Hisaab. Har serious candidate ke dimaag mein (ya kisi Baban ki copy mein) gaon ka poora map hota hai: kaun sa khaandaan kitne vote, kaun sa mohalla kiske saath, pichhli baar kya hua tha. 600 vote = 12-15 blocks. Bas.

Step 2: Mukhiya deals. Har block ke mukhiya se alag baat. Kisi ko naali ka waada, kisi ko bete ke kaam ka, kisi ko sirf izzat — "tumhare aangan se hi parcha bharunga." Deal block se hoti hai, voter se nahi.

Step 3: Bhandara. Khaana politics ka sabse purana instrument hai. Community bhoj ka matlab sirf pet bharna nahi — yeh public commitment hai. Jo tumhara khaana kha gaya, woh gaon ke saamne tumhare aangan mein baitha. Ab uska palatna mehnga ho gaya.

Step 4: Aakhri raat. Jahan paisa ya sharaab chalti hai (aur bahut jagah chalti hai — reported), woh aakhri 48 ghante mein chalti hai, mukhiyaon ke through, list ke hisaab se. Chapter 2 ka contested corner yaad rakhna: yeh paisa vote pakka karta hai ya sirf "serious candidate" ka signal deta hai — iska settled jawab kisi ke paas nahi. Lekin itna settled hai ki jo candidate yeh nahi karta, usko log serious nahi maante.

Step 5: Todna. Agar saamne wale ka ganit tumse behtar hai, toh uska block todo. Uski jaati ka, uske mohalle ka ek aur candidate khada karwa do. Yeh vote-katwa chapter 2 mein mila tha — sarpanch election mein yeh sabse aam chaal hai.

Reservation — ek rule jo poora game palat deta hai

Ek cheez sarpanch level par hai jo upar ke chapters mein alag roop mein milegi: seat reservation ka rotation. Sarpanch ki seat rotation se mahilaon ya SC/ST ke liye reserved hoti rehti hai.

Ab dekho system ka asli behavior. Jis saal seat mahila ke liye reserve hui, gaon ke purane players kya karte hain? Apni gharwali, bahu, ya behen ko khada kar dete hain. Gaon janta hai asli sarpanch kaun hoga. Iske liye ek khula naam bhi hai — "sarpanch pati" (sarpanch ka pati / asli operator).

Isko cynically mat padho, architecture ki tarah padho: rule ne kursi ka NAAM badla, toh system ne kursi ke PEECHE ka aadmi same rakh liya. Rule sudharne walon ne form badla; incentive same tha, toh function same raha. Yeh pattern yaad rakhna — aage har level par milega: jab bhi koi naya rule aata hai, system usko todta nahi, uske ird-gird naya rasta bana leta hai.

(Doosra side bhi sach hai, aur contested hai: kai jagah reserved seat se aayi mahila sarpanch ne dheere-dheere asli control le liya. Rule kabhi-kabhi function bhi badal deta hai — bas hamesha nahi, aur dheere.)

Baban ab 19 ka hai. Sarpanch ka agla election aa gaya, aur is baar Bhaskar thekedaar phir khada hai — lekin is baar Bhaskar ne ladke ko bulaya jiske baare mein sab kehte the: "Tatya ka beta, hisaab ka pakka."

Baban ne teen mahine kaam kiya, aur teen cheezein ki.

Usne apne baap wali galti ulti karke istemaal ki — jin mukhiyaon ka record "dono taraf haan bolne ka" tha, unke blocks ko usne ginti se hi nikal diya. Unpe kharcha zero. "Jo bikta hai roz, woh khareedna bekaar."

Doosri: usne dekha ki purane sarpanch ka sabse bada block — 90 vote ka — do khaandaanon ka joint block hai, aur dono khaandaanon mein bahu ke lene-dene par andar hi andar khatas hai. Aadmi hansa: "Toh tu sabse mehnga aadmi hai is gaon ka. Jiska bhaav abhi laga nahi, uska bhaav sabse zyada hota hai."

Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

1. Sarpanch ka honorarium hazaaron mein hai, election ka kharcha lakhs mein. Kursi ke andar woh 4 cheezein kya hain jo is gap ko explain karti hain?

Jawab: (1) Sarkari funds ka control (kaam, thekedar, quality decide karna), (2) beneficiary lists ka control (kiska number pehle), (3) kaagaz/dakhle/NOCs, (4) izzat + political ladder ka pehla danda. Yeh sab milakar kursi ki asli keemat hai.

2. Bhandara sirf khaana kyun nahi hai?

Jawab: Woh public commitment hai. Gaon ke saamne tumhare aangan mein baithkar khaana = sabke saamne tumhare side dikhna. Iske baad palatna socially mehnga ho jaata hai. Politics mein khaana contract-signing ceremony hai.

3. "Sarpanch pati" phenomenon architecture ke baare mein kya sikhata hai?

Jawab: Rule ne kursi ka naam badla (mahila reserved), lekin incentive same raha, toh system ne form ke peeche function same rakh liya — asli operator wahi purana aadmi. General sabak: naya rule system ko todta nahi, system uske ird-gird rasta bana leta hai. (Contested side: kabhi-kabhi, dheere-dheere, rule function bhi badal deta hai.)

4. Voter jaanta hai ki jeetne wala paisa nikalega. Phir bhi vote deta hai — rational kaise?

Jawab: Voter ka sawaal "imaandaar vs be-imaan" nahi hai, "mere block ka kaam kaun karega" hai. Jo candidate uske block ka kaam karega, woh thoda kha bhi lega toh voter ka net sauda positive hai. Apna be-imaan > paraya imaandaar.

5. Baban ne Bhaskar ka poore-gaon-ka-bhandara kyun kaata?

Jawab: Chapter 2 ka ganit: jeet 250 par hai, 640 par nahi. Jo blocks ginti mein nahi aate (pakke virodhi ya bikau), unpe kharcha vote nahi banata. Campaign ka paisa target blocks par lagta hai, poore gaon par nahi. Precision > volume.

What this means for you

Jab bhi koi bada political phenomenon samajhna ho — MLA ki chaal, party ka faisla, defection — usko sarpanch size par chhota karke socho: "600 voters ke gaon mein yeh cheez kaisi dikhti?" 90% baar wahi structure niklega: blocks, mukhiya, funds, todna. Aur jab kisi neta se milo, yaad rakhna ki woh is cell-level game ko jeet kar aaya hai — usse blocks aur hisaab ki bhasha mein baat karoge toh woh tumhe apna aadmi samjhega, upar-upar ki "development" bhasha mein karoge toh bahar ka.

Numbers note: sarpanch honorarium (kuch hazaar/mahina, state-wise alag) aur "lakhs/crores ka kharcha" reported hai — is level par audited data nahi hota. Central funds ka gram panchayat ke account mein seedha aana (Finance Commission grants) verified structure hai. Nimgaon/Baban/Bhaskar fictional.

4. Corporator — wahi cell, sheher mein, zyada paise ke saath

Gaon se sheher

Ab Nimgaon chhodo. Sheher chalo.

Sheher mein bhi wahi need hai jo gaon mein thi — jhagde, saajhi cheezein, suraksha. Lekin teen cheezein badal jaati hain, aur teenon system ka behavior badal deti hain:

                    GAON                    SHEHER (ward)
Voters              600-1,000               15,000-60,000
Log ek doosre ko    sab sabko jaante        koi kisi ko nahi jaanta
Sabse badi cheez    zameen (kheti)          zameen (construction)
Paisa               thoda, sarkari          bahut, sarkari + private

Teesri line sabse important hai. Gaon mein zameen se anaaj nikalta hai. Sheher mein zameen se building nikalti hai, aur building se paisa nikalta hai — gaon ke kisi bhi hisaab se hazaar guna zyada.

Isliye sheher ka corporator gaon ke sarpanch ka bada bhai nahi hai. Woh ek alag jaanwar hai, jo ek alag cheez ke ird-gird bana hai: construction.

Ward mein kya hota hai

Corporator ek ward se jeetta hai — sheher ka ek tukda. Municipal corporation (mahanagar palika) mein woh ek member hota hai, sau-do sau members mein se ek.

Uske haath mein kya hai:

1. WARD KA KAAM
   Sadak, naali, paani ki line, street light, kachra,
   garden. Chhota kaam, lekin ROZ ka kaam — aur har kaam
   ka ek thekedar hota hai.

2. PERMISSIONS KA RASTA
   Building plan pass hona, water connection, drainage
   connection, trade licence, shop ka NOC. Corporator
   khud sign nahi karta — officer karta hai — lekin
   corporator ke bina file "chalti" nahi. Iska matlab
   samajhna is chapter ka asli kaam hai (neeche).

3. ATIKRAMAN (encroachment) KA SWITCH
   Footpath par thela, awaidh floor, awaidh shed —
   yeh sab technically illegal hai. Toh chalta kyun hai?
   Kyunki hatana bhi ek DECISION hai. Jiske haath mein
   "hatao ya rehne do" ka switch hai, uske paas roz ki
   taakat hai — poore sheher ke chhote dhandhon par.

4. SLUM KA VOTE-BANK
   Sheher ka sabse solid block. Wahan har cheez —
   paani, bijli ka connection, ration card, ghar ka
   proof — kisi na kisi permission par tiki hai.
   Aur permission ka rasta corporator se hokar jaata hai.

Sheher ka block gaon se alag kyun hota hai

Gaon mein block khaandaan ka tha — khoon ka rishta. Sheher mein khoon nahi chalta, kyunki padosi ka naam bhi nahi pata.

Toh sheher mein block kis cheez se banta hai? Zaroorat se.

GAON KA BLOCK          SHEHER KA BLOCK
khaandaan (40 vote)    slum/basti (2,000 vote)
jaati (500 vote)       society/apartment (300 vote)
mohalla (200 vote)     bhasha/region ke log (5,000 vote)
                       hawker/vyapari association (800 vote)

Dhyan do: sheher ke blocks institutions hain, parivaar nahi. Basti ka ek leader, society ka secretary, hawker union ka pramukh, mandir/masjid committee — yeh sheher ke mukhiya hain.

Aur inme sabse solid kaun hai? Basti. Kyunki wahan zaroorat sabse gehri hai. Apartment wale ko corporator ki zaroorat saal mein shayad ek baar padti hai. Basti wale ko har mahine padti hai — paani, kaagaz, hatane ki dhamki, bachche ka school. Jahan zaroorat roz ki hai, wahan block sabse pakka hai.

Yeh ek line yaad rakhna, kyunki yeh poori kitab mein sach rahegi:

Vote-bank wahan sabse majboot hota hai jahan zaroorat sabse gehri hai — jahan log sabse zyada padhe-likhe ya sabse zyada paisewale hain wahan nahi.

Isliye sheher ka corporator apna zyada waqt basti mein deta hai, un colonies mein nahi jahan bade log rehte hain. Bade log shikayat karte hain ki "hamare yahan koi neta aata hi nahi." Woh sahi keh rahe hain — aur wajah simple hai: unka block loose hai, unki turnout kam hai, aur unko corporator ki roz zaroorat nahi. Woh ganit mein bhaari nahi padte.

Builder ka rishta — sheher ka asli engine

Ab is chapter ka core. Dhyan se, kyunki yeh mechanism aage har level par milega, bas naam badal ke.

Ek builder ko project banane ke liye kya chahiye? Zameen, paisa, aur kaagaz ka pahaad — plan approval, height clearance, water/drainage connection, occupancy certificate, aur beech mein aane wale local virodh ka settlement.

Kaagaz technically officer deta hai — corporator nahi. Toh builder corporator ke paas kyun jaata hai?

Teen wajah:

1. Speed. Har file mein delay ka option hota hai. "Objection" lagana, "clarification" maangna, file ko table par rakh dena — yeh sab legal hai. Ek project mein 6 mahine ka delay builder ke liye interest aur cost ka pahaad hai. Corporator file ko haan nahi karwata, woh file ko RUKNE se bachata hai. Yeh farak samajhna zaroori hai — taakat mostly "haan" karwane mein nahi hoti, "na" ko rokne mein hoti hai.

2. Zameeni virodh. Project ke saamne wale log dhool, rasta band, paani ki shikayat leke khade ho jaate hain. Corporator un logon ka hi leader hai. Woh chahe toh virodh khada karwa sakta hai, chahe toh baitha sakta hai.

3. Corporation ke andar ki awaaz. Standing committee, general body — jahan tenders aur proposals pass hote hain, wahan corporator baitha hai.

Iske badle builder kya deta hai? Yahan honest raho:

KHULA (declared, legal):
  - Ward ke kaam ke liye "CSR" type contribution
  - Ganesh utsav / navratri / cricket tournament ka sponsorship
  - Society ke liye garden, gym, ambulance donate karna

CHHUPA (undeclared — reported, kaagaz par nahi):
  - Cash percentage, project size par
  - Flats: project mein ek-do flat "book" ho jaate hain
    naam par ya kisi rishtedaar ke naam par, kaagaz par
    kam daam mein
  - Partnership: corporator ya uska rishtedaar seedha
    project mein hissedaar
  - Election ka poora kharcha builder uthata hai

Ab poora circle dekho, kyunki yehi is chapter ka architecture hai:

   BUILDER ────paisa────► CORPORATOR ────kaam/utsav────► BASTI/WARD
      ▲                        │                            │
      │                        │                            │ vote
      │                        ▼                            │
      └──── file chalti ── TAAKAT (kursi) ◄─────────────────┘

Yeh loop khud ko chalata hai. Builder ka paisa corporator ke ward ke kaam aur utsav mein lagta hai. Us kaam se vote aate hain. Vote se kursi rehti hai. Kursi se builder ki file chalti hai. Aur phir se.

Is loop mein koi villain nahi hai — sirf players hain jinke incentives fit ho jaate hain. Builder ko speed chahiye, basti ko kaam chahiye, corporator ko vote chahiye. Teenon ko jo chahiye woh mil raha hai. Jo nahi mil raha, woh sheher ki planning hai — kyunki us loop mein "acchi planning" ki koi party hai hi nahi.

Yehi wajah hai ki sheher badhte hain lekin plan ke hisaab se nahi badhte. Galat log nahi hain — loop mein planning ka koi customer nahi hai.

Contested corner

Kya corporator sach mein itna taakatwar hai?

  • Camp A: Haan. Ward level par woh gatekeeper hai; officer usse ladta nahi kyunki officer ko roz kaam karna hai.
  • Camp B: Nahi — asli taakat commissioner (IAS) aur bade neta ke paas hai. Corporator sirf messenger hai, aur badle mein choti-moti cheezein paata hai. Bada paisa upar jaata hai, corporator ko sirf hissa milta hai.

Sach shayad sheher ke size par depend karta hai: chhote sheher mein corporator asli player, bade metro mein woh chain ka ek hissa. Chapter 16 mein jab officer-neta ka rishta khulega, tab yeh saaf hoga.

CORPORATOR:
  Deta hai:  file ka na-rukna, virodh ka settlement,
             basti ka kaam
  Paata hai: builder ka paisa + election ka kharcha

Bhaskar sarpanch ban gaya. Baban ka gaon mein kaam khatam.

Doosra jhatka do mahine baad laga. Ek shaam office khali tha, aur ek aadmi aaya — mehanga kurta, gaadi bahar. Woh baithne wali kursi par nahi baitha; seedha andar chala gaya. Baban ne register nikala uska naam likhne ke liye. Corporator ne haath se ishara kiya — mat likh.

Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

1. Sheher ka block gaon ke block se kis cheez par bana hota hai, aur sabse solid block kaunsa hota hai?

Jawab: Gaon mein khoon/khaandaan par; sheher mein zaroorat par (basti, society, union, bhasha-group). Sabse solid = basti, kyunki wahan zaroorat roz ki hai — paani, kaagaz, bulldozer se bachav. Rule: jahan zaroorat sabse gehri, wahan vote sabse pakka.

2. Builder corporator ke paas kyun jaata hai jab kaagaz officer deta hai?

Jawab: Kyunki corporator file ko "haan" nahi karwata — usko rukne se bachata hai. Delay legal hai aur builder ke liye sabse mehngi cheez hai (interest + cost). Saath mein zameeni virodh baithana aur corporation ki committee mein awaaz. Taakat "na" ko rokne mein hai, "haan" karwane mein nahi.

3. Sheher plan ke hisaab se kyun nahi badhte — kaun galat hai?

Jawab: Koi nahi. Builder-corporator-basti ka loop teenon ko woh de raha hai jo unhe chahiye (speed, vote, kaam). Us loop mein "acchi planning" ka koi customer hi nahi hai — jo cheez kisi ka incentive nahi hoti, woh apne aap nahi hoti.

4. Corporator MLA se zyada available kyun hota hai?

Jawab: Uska ward chhota hai (15-60 hazaar vote). Ek basti naraz = seat gayi. Chhoti seat = zyada nazuk = zyada availability. Yeh uski achhai nahi, ganit ki majboori hai.

5. "Kaam nahi hota, lekin milna hota hai" — iska ganit kya hai?

Jawab: Har aadmi ka kaam karwana namumkin hai (resources limited). Lekin sunna free hai, aur sune jaane wala aadmi apne block mein tumhara naam leta hai. Milna ek scalable product hai, kaam nahi. Chapter 10 mein yeh janta darbar ke roop mein poora khulega.

What this means for you

Sheher ka koi bhi neta samajhna ho toh do sawaal poocho: "iske blocks kahan hain?" aur "iske ward mein construction chal raha hai kya?" Pehla batayega uski kursi kis par tiki hai, doosra batayega uska paisa kahan se aata hai. Aur agar tum kabhi kisi corporator ya MLA ko kuch bechne jao — yaad rakhna uske paas do alag queue hain, aur woh tumhe pehle 5 minute mein decide kar lega ki tum kis queue ke ho: register wali, ya jo register mein nahi likhi jaati.

Structure verified: corporators sit in municipal corporations, wards vary roughly 15,000-60,000 voters by city size; building permissions are issued by corporation officers, not corporators. The undeclared side of builder-corporator exchange (cash %, flats, partnerships) is reported/journalistic, not documented — treat as pattern, not proof. Nimgaon/Baban/Bhaskar fictional.

5. Karyakarta — hazaaron log muft mein kaam kyun karte hain

Sabse bada sawaal jo koi nahi poochta

Election ke din ek MLA candidate ke liye zameen par kitne log kaam karte hain? Ek 3 lakh voter wali seat par, theek se ladne wale candidate ke paas 1,000 se 3,000 log active hote hain — booth par baithne wale, gaadi chalane wale, parchi baantne wale, bheed jutane wale, jhande lagane wale.

Ab ek bania ki tarah sawaal poocho: 1,000 logon ko 40 din kaam par rakhne ka kharcha kitna hoga?

Agar sabko dihadi dena pade, toh yeh number itna bada ho jaata hai ki koi bhi campaign namumkin ho jaye. Aur phir bhi har seat par, har party ke paas, yeh log hote hain.

Toh sach kya hai? Sach yeh hai:

Yeh chapter us aadmi ke baare mein hai. Aur maano ya na maano — poore Indian political system ka sabse zaroori aur sabse kam samjha gaya hissa yehi hai. Agar tumhe karyakarta samajh aa gaya, toh tumhe samajh aa jayega ki yeh poori machine chalti kaise hai, kyunki uska poora physical kaam yehi aadmi karta hai.

Toh woh aata kyun hai?

Chapter 1 ka sabak yaad karo: har player apna rational sauda kar raha hai. Toh karyakarta ka sauda kya hai? Woh apna time, apni gaadi, apni jawani, kabhi-kabhi apna paisa lagata hai. Badle mein kya paata hai?

Char cheezein. Aur inko ratna nahi, samajhna:

1. UMEED (hope) — sabse bada khichne wala

Karyakarta ke saamne ek seedhi hai jo usse dikhti hai:

karyakarta → booth pramukh → mandal/ward ka pad
    → panchayat/nagar ka ticket → zila ka pad
        → MLA ka ticket → ...

Yeh seedhi asli hai — log sach mein chadhte hain. Lekin ganit dekho: hazaaron karyakarta, aur ticket ek. Yeh lottery jaisa hai — jeetne ka chance kam hai, lekin sirf khelne walon ko milta hai. Aur khelne ki fees "5 saal wafadaari" hai.

Isliye karyakarta ka sabse bada dushman doosri party nahi hoti. Uska sabse bada dushman apni hi party ka woh aadmi hota hai jo usse aage nikal raha hai. Yaad rakhna — chapter 12 (factions) ka poora beej yahan hai.

2. SURAKSHA (protection) — sabse turant fayda

Yeh sabse practical hai, aur isko bahar wale sabse kam samajhte hain.

Ek aam aadmi ki zindagi mein sarkar ek deewar hai — police, tehsil, bijli office, hospital, school admission. Har jagah line hai, aur line mein uska number aakhri hai.

Karyakarta ke paas ek cheez hoti hai jo aam aadmi ke paas nahi: ek phone number jo uthaya jaata hai. Uska thaana mein kaam ho jaata hai. Uske bhatije ka admission ho jaata hai. Uske chacha ka bijli connection aa jaata hai.

Aur yeh sirf uske liye nahi hai — yahi uski gaon/mohalle mein keemat hai. Log uske paas aate hain kyunki uska number lagta hai. Woh khud ek chhota mukhiya ban jaata hai. Chapter 2 wala block? Karyakarta ke haath mein ek chhota block aa jaata hai.

Toh dekho kya hua: usne party ko time diya, party ne usko access diya, access se usko apna block mila, aur block se usko party mein aur keemat mili. Yeh loop hai — aur loop khud ko majboot karta jaata hai.

3. PEHCHAAN (identity) — sabse gehri, sabse kam dikhne wali

Ek aam aadmi 30 saal ka hai, naukri sadharan hai, gaon mein koi khaas naam nahi. Party ka jhanda uthate hi kuch badal jaata hai. Ab woh "party ka aadmi" hai. Shaadi mein usko aage bithate hain. Uske mobile mein MLA ka number hai. Rally mein woh stage ke paas khada hota hai.

Yeh cheez paise mein nahi milti, aur isko chhodna bahut mushkil hota hai — kyunki chhodne ka matlab hai wapas "koi nahi" ban jaana.

Isliye jab tum kisi 45 saal ke karyakarta ko dekho jisko 20 saal mein kuch nahi mila, aur phir bhi woh subah 7 baje jhanda lagane pahunch jaata hai — usko bewakoof mat samajhna. Woh apni pehchaan bacha raha hai. Uske paas jo hai, woh usne 20 saal mein banaya hai, aur woh party ke andar hi exist karta hai.

4. DHANDHA (business) — sabse honest wajah

Kuch log seedha isliye aate hain kyunki party ka aadmi hona kaam aata hai:

  • Thekedar ko theka chahiye — sarkari kaam party ke aadmi ko milna aasan hai
  • Transporter ko permit chahiye
  • Chhote dukandaar ko licence/atikraman se bachav chahiye
  • Zameen ke dhandhe wale ko information chahiye — kahan road aayegi, kahan zone badlega

Yeh log "karyakarta" hain kaagaz par, lekin unka sauda seedha business hai. Aur system ko inki sabse zyada zaroorat hoti hai, kyunki paisa aur gaadi yahi log dete hain.

Sochne ki cheez: ek state assembly saal mein kitne din baithti hai? Aksar 20-40 din ke aas-paas — kai states mein isse bhi kam. Matlab saal ke 365 dinon mein se lagbhag 330-345 din MLA vidhan sabha mein hai hi nahi.

Aur jin 30 dinon mein woh hai, wahan bhi asli faisle kaun karta hai? Toh phir — 330 din woh karta kya hai? Aur log usse chunte kyun hain?

Iska jawab is chapter ka poora kaam hai, aur yeh jawab poori kitab ka sabse zaroori correction hai.

Voter MLA se kanoon nahi maangta. Voter MLA se pahunch maangta hai — sarkar tak pahunchne ka rasta. Uske liye MLA vidhayak nahi, sifarish ka darwaza hai.

Aur yehi wajah hai ki ek MLA jo assembly mein 5 saal mein ek baar nahi bola, phir bhi bade margin se jeet jaata hai — jabki ek MLA jo assembly mein shandar bhashan deta hai, gaon mein mayyat par nahi pahuncha, woh haar jaata hai.

System bura nahi hai. System ne bas ek doosra kaam maang liya hai. Voter ko roz ki zindagi mein jo chahiye, MLA usko wahi de raha hai.

Ab is size se teen cheezein apne aap nikalti hain, aur teenon MLA ka behavior samjha deti hain:

1. Woh sabko nahi jaan sakta. 3 lakh log. Agar woh roz 100 logon se mile toh poori seat se milne mein 8 saal lagenge — aur term 5 saal ki hai. Isliye woh majboori se blocks aur mukhiyaon ke through kaam karta hai. Chapter 2 ka ganit yahan physical reality ban jaata hai.

2. Woh akela kuch nahi kar sakta. 250-350 booth. Ek din mein sab par apna aadmi baithana hai. Isliye machine (chapter 5) uski life-line hai, shauk nahi.

3. Uska time hi uska sabse scarce resource hai. Paisa woh jugaad kar lega. Time nahi kar sakta. Isliye MLA ki poori zindagi ek time-allocation problem hai: aaj kis gaon jaunga, kiski mayyat par jaunga, kiska phone uthaunga.

Yeh teesri baat sabse zyada kaam ki hai. Agar tumhe kabhi kisi MLA ke behavior ka reason samajh na aaye, toh yeh poochho: "iske time ke hisaab se yeh rational hai kya?" Aksar jawab mil jayega.

Aur is tarah ki taakat bahut nazuk hoti hai. Jis din ruling party se woh bahar hua, ya jis din uska mantri se rishta bigda, us din uska phone uthna kam ho jaata hai — aur jis din phone uthna kam hua, us din uska block hilna shuru ho jaata hai.

Contested corner

Kya MLA ka kaam badal raha hai?

  • Camp A: Haan. Ab log direct portal, helpline, aur online se kaam karwa lete hain; MLA ka bicholiya wala role kam ho raha hai. Naya voter sadak-naali se zyada bade mudde par vote deta hai.
  • Camp B: Nahi. Portal ne form badla, kaam nahi. Aaj bhi log portal par form bharkar MLA ke office aate hain ki "iska follow-up karwa do." Bicholiya ki zaroorat khatam nahi hui, bas ab woh digital form ke saath aata hai.

Dono mein sach hai. Sabse imaandaar version shayad yeh hai: bade sheher mein Camp A zyada sach hai, gaon aur chhote sheher mein Camp B.

Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

1. MLA saal ke ~30 din assembly mein hota hai. Baaki 330 din woh kya karta hai, aur uski "asli job description" kya hai?

Jawab: Asli job = pahunch ka darwaza banna. Janta darbar, mayyat/shaadi mein maujoodgi, police-tehsil-hospital mein phone karwana, kaam dilana, aur apni machine (karyakarta) sambhalna. Voter usse kanoon nahi maangta, sarkar tak rasta maangta hai.

2. "MLA ki taakat 20% adhikaar aur 80% asar hai" — iska practical matlab kya hai?

Jawab: Uske paas seedha adhikaar bahut kam hai (MLALADS suggest karna, assembly mein bolna). Baaki sab — transfer, police, tender, lists — asar se hota hai, matlab tabhi jab log maanein ki uski baat chalti hai. Aur asar nazuk hai: sarkar badli ya mantri se rishta bigda toh asar khatam.

3. Ruling party MLA aur virodhi dal MLA — asli farak kya, aur uska compounding effect kya?

Jawab: Ruling party mein phone uthta hai, kaam hota hai, karyakarta saath rehte hain. Virodhi dal mein kaam atakta hai, aur isliye karyakarta dheere-dheere doosri taraf khiskte hain. Matlab haar sirf 5 saal ka nuksaan nahi — machine bhi kamzor hoti jaati hai, jisse agli baar aur mushkil ho jaati hai. Yehi dal-badal ka bada karan hai.

4. MLA ke teen darr kaunse, aur unka order kya hai — aur order counter-intuitive kyun hai?

Jawab: (1) Ticket katna, (2) seat haarna, (3) sarkar jaana. Pehla darr party ka hai, voter ka nahi — kyunki ticket hi nahi mila toh voter ke paas usse chunne ka option hi nahi. Bahar se lagta hai neta voter se darta hai; asal mein woh pehle party se darta hai.

5. Baban ne kyun kaha ki "paise se booth nahi milta"?

Jawab: Booth par baithne wale ko us ilaake ke logon ke naam, rishte aur history pata honi chahiye — taaki woh pehchaan sake kaun apna hai, kaun nahi, aur kise ghar se nikalwana hai. Yeh rishta 5 saal mein banta hai; 40 din ka kiraye ka aadmi sirf kursi bhar sakta hai, kaam nahi kar sakta.

What this means for you

Kisi bhi MLA se milne se pehle do cheezein pata karo: (1) woh ruling party mein hai ya nahi, aur (2) uski seat kitne booth ki hai aur pichhli baar margin kitna tha. Pehli baat batayegi ki uske paas asar hai ya nahi — matlab woh kuch karwa sakta hai ya sirf sun sakta hai. Doosri batayegi ki woh kitna surakshit hai — aur jo MLA jitna kam surakshit hai, woh utna hi zyada khula hai us cheez ke liye jo usko agli baar jeetne mein madad kare. Aur yaad rakhna: uska sabse scarce resource paisa nahi, time hai. Jo cheez uska time bachaye, uski keemat uske liye khud-ba-khud badi hai.

Structure verified: state assemblies typically sit for a few dozen days a year (varies widely by state and year); MLALADS exists in most states with state-specific amounts and is administered through the district administration on MLA recommendation, not spent directly by the MLA; anti-defection provisions bind MLAs to party whips. Assembly seat size (~2-4 lakh voters, ~250-350 booths) is approximate and varies by state and delimitation. The daily schedule is illustrative, not a documented case. Baban fictional.

Public ko ek election dikhta hai: candidate vs candidate, do mahine ka shor, phir result.

Us se pehle ek aur election hota hai. Usme koi voting nahi hoti, koi date nahi hoti, koi result declare nahi hota. Usme kaun jeeta yeh sirf ek line se pata chalta hai — party ki list mein tumhara naam hai ya nahi.

Ticket kis cheez par milta hai

Log samajhte hain ticket "wafadaari" par milta hai, ya "kaam" par. Sach zyada thanda hai. Party ek hi cheez maximize karti hai:

JEETNE KA CHANCE.

Party ko tumse pyaar nahi hai. Party ko seat chahiye. Har baaki cheez — wafadaari, kaam, jaati, paisa — sirf isliye matter karti hai kyunki woh jeetne ke chance ko badhati ya ghatati hai.

Ab wo saari cheezein dekho jo us "chance" ko banati hain:

1. WINNABILITY KA SABOOT (sabse bada)

Party ke paas survey hote hain — apne bhi, kiraye ke bhi. Woh dekhte hain: is seat par kaun sa naam sabse zyada vote khinch sakta hai. Yeh number aksar aakhri faisla karta hai.

Aur is wajah se ek cheez hoti hai jo bahar se bahut ajeeb lagti hai: party apne 20 saal purane wafadaar ka ticket kaat kar doosri party se aaye hue aadmi ko de deti hai. Wafadaar ko lagta hai dhoka hua. Party ke hisaab se yeh simple ganit hai — naya aadmi 30,000 vote laata hai, purana 12,000.

Yaad rakhna: party ke liye ticket inaam nahi hai, investment hai.

2. JAATI/SAMUDAY KA FIT

Har seat ka apna ganit hota hai (chapter 18 poora ispe hai). Agar seat mein ek samuday 60,000 vote ka hai, toh party us samuday ka candidate dhoondhegi — chahe woh kam kabil ho.

Yeh "bura" nahi hai party ke hisaab se — yeh chapter 2 ka block-ganit hai. Ek candidate jo apne saath ek 60,000 ka block leke aata hai, woh us candidate se behtar investment hai jo akela aata hai.

3. MACHINE (chapter 5 ka connection)

Party poochti hai: "iske peeche kitne log hain?" Jiske paas 300 booth par apne aadmi hain, uska daawa mazboot hai. Jiske paas sirf naam hai, uska nahi.

Isliye ticket ke aspirants saal bhar bheed dikhate rehte hain — janm-din par hoarding, rally mein gaadiyaan, "samarthak" ka pradarshan. Woh voter ko impress nahi kar rahe. Woh party ko apni machine ka size dikha rahe hain.

4. PAISA

Do tarah se:

  • Campaign ka kharcha khud utha sakta hai kya? Party ke paas har seat ka poora paisa nahi hota. Jo candidate apna kharcha khud utha le, woh party ke liye sasta hai.
  • Aur — reported, aur khulke maana nahi jaata — kuch jagah ticket ke liye party fund mein bada "yogdaan" expected hota hai. Iske ilzaam har party par lage hain, sabne inkaar kiya hai. Isko is kitab mein pattern ki tarah rakho, proof ki tarah nahi.

5. WAFADAARI AUR SANGATHAN

Yeh sabse kam weight wali cheez hai — lekin zero nahi. Party ko dikhana hota hai ki wafadaari ka bhi mol hai, warna machine ka fuel (chapter 5 ka "umeed") khatam ho jayega.

Isliye har party har baar kuch tickets purane wafadaaron ko deti hai — bhale seat kamzor ho. Yeh signal hai, poore system ko: "line mein lage raho, number aata hai."

6. HIGH COMMAND KA RISHTA

Faisla aakhir mein kuch logon ke kamre mein hota hai. Jiska us kamre mein koi bolne wala hai, uska chance badh jaata hai. Chapter 12 poora is kamre ke baare mein hai.

Ticket kat-ne ka din

Yeh Indian politics ka sabse kroor din hota hai, aur sabse zyada revealing bhi.

List aati hai. Aksar aakhri waqt par — nomination ki aakhri tareekh se ek-do din pehle. Yeh timing accident nahi hai: jitna kam waqt bachega, naraz aadmi ke paas utne kam options bachenge.

Ab jiska ticket kat gaya, uske paas kya raaste hain?

1. CHUP RAHO
   "Party ka faisla sar-aankhon par." Umeed: agli baar
   ya koi pad/corporation ka chairman-ship.

2. BAGAWAT — khud khade ho jao
   Nirdaleeya (independent) lado. Aksar haarte hain,
   lekin apni party ke official candidate ko HARA dete hain.
   Yeh badla bhi hai aur bargaining bhi: "dekh lo, mere
   bina yeh seat nahi jeetti."

3. DOOSRI PARTY MEIN JAO
   Agar tumhare paas block hai, toh doosri party ka
   darwaza usi raat khulta hai. Isliye ticket katne ke
   24-48 ghante mein sabse zyada dal-badal hota hai.

4. SABOTAGE
   Rehna party mein, lekin andar se apne hi candidate ko
   harana. Sabse aam, sabse mushkil pakadna, aur andar
   walon ko hamesha pata hota hai.

Aur ab poore system ki ek chalak baat samjho. Party jaanti hai ki 4-5 log naraz honge. Toh party unko sambhalne ke liye kya deti hai? Aksar pad — corporation ka chairman, board ka member, morcha ka adhyaksh.

Yaad hai chapter 5 ka insight? Party ka unlimited product izzat hai. Ticket 288 hain, lekin pad hazaaron banaye ja sakte hain. Isliye har ticket ke saath 4-5 pad bant-te hain — naraz logon ko line mein rokne ke liye.

Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

1. "Asli election ticket ka hota hai" — iska ganit kya hai?

Jawab: Ek party mein hazaaron serious aspirants, tickets sirf seats ke barabar (jaise 288), aur unme se aadhe se zyada pehle se bhare hue. Matlab candidate ka pehla aur sabse mushkil muqabla apni hi party ke logon se hota hai, virodhi party se nahi.

2. Party ticket dete waqt kya maximize karti hai, aur isse kaun sa "ajeeb" behavior explain hota hai?

3. Aspirant saal bhar bheed aur hoarding kyun dikhata hai — kiske liye?

Jawab: Voter ke liye kam, party ke liye zyada. Woh apni machine ka size dikha raha hai, kyunki machine ticket ke faisle ka ek bada factor hai. Uska har kaam do audiences ke liye counted hota hai.

4. Ticket katne par 4 raaste kaunse, aur party naraz aadmi ko kaise sambhalti hai?

5. Adhyaksh ne Baban ko ticket kyun nahi diya, uske 1,800 vote hone ke bawajood?

Jawab: Kyunki woh 1,800 already party ke the — Baban party ka aadmi tha, uska block party ke saath hi rehta. Ticket dekar party woh vote khareedti hai jo abhi uske paas NAHI hain (thekedaar ke 900). Wafadaar ka vote party ke liye "already booked" hota hai, isliye uski ginti nahi hoti.

1. Legal limit Rs 40 lakh, machine ka hisaab crores mein. Yeh gap structurally kaise chalta hai?

Jawab: (a) Limit candidate par hai, party ke prachar par nahi — party unlimited kharch kar sakti hai. (b) Jo paisa cash mein chala, woh official return mein aata hi nahi, aur jo kaagaz par nahi hai use naapa nahi ja sakta. Isliye har return limit ke andar hota hai.

2. Campaign ka sabse bada kharcha kaunsa bucket hai, aur kyun?

Jawab: Machine — 600-1,200 logon ko 30-45 din chalana (dihadi + khaana + gaadi). Kyunki 250-350 booth par ek hi din, ek hi waqt logon ki zaroorat hai, aur booth chhodne ka matlab seedha vote chhodna hai.

3. Loop poora bolo: paisa kahan se aata hai aur wapas kaise nikalta hai?

Jawab: Aata hai: khud ka paisa + party + permission-wale dhandhon se (builder, thekedar, sharab/mining/transport, shikshan) + kaala paisa. Nikalta hai: theke, zameen ki advance information, transfer-posting ka asar, permission ka dhandha, aur institution. Kursi se taakat, taakat se paisa, paise se agla chunav.

4. Wahi dhandhe politics ko funding kyun karte hain — builder, mining, sharab, shikshan?

Jawab: Kyunki in sabme sarkar ka haath hai — inhe permission, licence, zameen, approval chahiye. Jis dhandhe ko sarkari permission chahiye, usko politics se rishta chahiye. Yeh system ka sabse seedha incentive hai, koi sazish nahi.

Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

1. MLA 10 mein se 3 ka hi kaam kar paata hai. Phir bhi 10 ko sunta kyun hai?

Jawab: Kaam karna mehnga hai (uska asar/credit kharch hota hai), sunn-na free hai (3 minute). Aur suna gaya aadmi apne mohalle mein jaakar uska naam leta hai chahe kaam na ho. Janta darbar "sune jaane ka ehsaas" banane ka zero-cost plant hai.

2. Ek parchi ke teen alag matlab kya hain?

Jawab: Aadmi ke liye — "kaam shuru ho gaya" (uska 70% kaam yehi hai). Officer ke liye — signal ki is aadmi ka MLA se rishta hai, aur ab officer naapega ki us MLA ki chalti kitni hai. MLA ke liye — record, jo chunav mein dikhaya ja sakta hai.

3. Mayyat shaadi se zyada zaroori kyun hai?

Jawab: Shaadi mein reach zyada hai (500 log dekhte hain) lekin yaad kam rehti hai. Mayyat mein reach kam hai lekin us parivaar ke liye woh din zindagi bhar ka hai. Dukh mein aaya aadmi sukh mein aaye se kai guna zyada yaad rehta hai — aur MLA sabse pehle apni machine ke logon ki mayyat par jaata hai, aam voter ki nahi.

4. Ehsaan paise se zyada takatwar kyun hai?

Jawab: (a) Uspar tax nahi, record nahi. (b) Uska rate fix nahi, toh woh badhta rehta hai. (c) Woh poori tarah lautaya nahi ja sakta — isliye ehsaanmand aadmi hamesha thoda tumhara rehta hai. Paisa ek baar ka rishta hai, ehsaan zindagi bhar ka.

5. MLALADS ka teesra (asli) kaam kya hai?

Jawab: Blocks ko reward karna. Paisa MLA ke haath mein aata hi nahi (administration kaam karti hai), lekin suggest karne ka adhikaar hi taakat hai — kyunki woh decide karta hai kis gaon, kis mohalle mein kaam jayega. Fund chhota hai, distribution ka signal bada hai.

Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

1. Neta ki asli taakat naapne ka ek-line test kya hai?

Jawab: "Agar iska ticket kat jaye aur yeh nirdaleeya lade, toh kitne vote layega?" Kam vote = woh party ka aadmi. Bahut vote = party uska aadmi. Yeh test party-ke-naam-par-jeetne wale aur apne-naam-par-jeetne wale ko alag kar deta hai.

2. "Apna vote" kis chaar cheezon se banta hai?

Jawab: (1) Ehsaan ka khaata — jinke kaam usne khud karwaye, (2) sanstha — jinki rozi usse judi hai, (3) apne samuday ka netritva, (4) machine jo uske apne naam se judi ho (party ke jhande se nahi).

3. Dynasty ka asli structural kaaran kya hai?

Jawab: Indian politics mein retirement ka koi rasta nahi hai. Kursi chhodne par neta ki 25 saal ki poonji (network, sanstha, ehsaan, machine) bikhar jaati hai — koi mechanism nahi jo usse transfer kare. Isliye woh kursi chhodta nahi, transfer karta hai, aur transfer sirf khoon ke rishte mein bharosemand hai.

4. Party dynasty ko rokti kyun nahi?

Jawab: Party ka apna ganit bhi wahi kehta hai. Naya aadmi banane mein 10 saal aur seat ka risk; bete ko ticket dene mein ready machine, ready sanstha, ready vote — aur upar se woh ehsaanmand rehta hai. Dono taraf ka incentive dynasty ki taraf le jaata hai.

5. Baban ki 26 saal purani galti kya thi?

Jawab: Usne machine chalayi lekin hamesha kisi aur ke naam par. Ehsaan ka khaata us aadmi ke naam par banta hai jiske paas kursi hai, chalane wale ke naam par nahi. Isliye 26 saal ki mehnat transfer nahi hui — woh MLA ke bete ke paas chali gayi. Kisi aur ki machine chalana naukri hai; apni chhoti machine banana poonji hai.

What this means for you

Kisi bhi neta ko dekho toh yeh poocho: "iska apna vote kitna hai, aur uska source kya hai?" Aur agar woh dynasty ka aadmi hai, toh usse kam mat aanko — usko transfer mein jo mila hai woh asli hai (machine, sanstha, khaata), aur woh usse chalana bhi jaanta hai.

Aur ek baat jo tumhare apne kaam ki hai, seedhe shabdon mein: Baban ki galti sirf politics ki galti nahi hai. Kisi aur ke platform par, kisi aur ke naam par, kisi aur ke customer ke saath 26 saal lagana — woh naukri hai, chahe woh kitna bhi kaabil kaam ho. Poonji tab banti hai jab log tumhara naam lete hain. Yeh baat is kitab se bahar bhi utni hi sach hai jitni andar.

Do rules jo hamesha kaam aayenge

Rule 1 — Har behaviour ke peeche incentive dhoondho, niyat nahi.

Jab bhi koi political cheez ajeeb lage — koi wafadaar ka ticket kat gaya, koi neta ne party badal di, koi bade neta ne chhoti si baat par itna zor lagaya — yeh mat poochho "yeh aisa kyun kar raha hai, kitna galat hai." Yeh poocho:

"Is aadmi ki jagah par khade hokar, iske teen darr aur iske resources ko dekhte hue — yeh faisla rational hai kya?"

95% baar jawab haan hoga. Aur jab jawab haan hota hai, tab tumhe system samajh aata hai, sirf ghatna nahi.

Rule 2 — Yaad rakho ki naksha kahan dhundhla hai.

Kuch cheezein settle nahi hain, aur unpe pakka bolna hi outsider ki nishaani hai:

- Kya paisa/sharab sach mein vote khareedta hai?
  (Shayad turnout ka tool hai, persuasion ka nahi)
- Kya "kaam" se vote milta hai?
  (Zaroori base hai, akela kaafi nahi)
- Kya jaanch agencies hathiyaar hain?
  (Sabse garam bahas — lekin DAR ka asar asli hai,
   chahe istemal ho ya na ho)
- Kya karyakarta system kamzor ho raha hai?
  (Dhaancha abhi bhi decisive, lekin uska tareeka badla)
- Kya anti-defection law kaam kar raha hai?
  (Stability aayi, lekin MLA party ka gulaam bhi ban gaya)

Veteran ki nishaani yeh nahi hai ki uske paas har sawaal ka jawab hai. Veteran ki nishaani yeh hai ki usko pata hai kaunse sawaal ka jawab kisi ke paas nahi hai.

Aakhri self-test — poori kitab ka

Yeh 8 sawaal poori kitab ka test hain. Bina peeche dekhe jawab do.

1. Neta ki job ki shakal kya hai, aur usse kya nikalta hai?

Chhoti salary + badi taakat → kamai taakat se hogi, salary se nahi. Yeh corruption ka design hai, niyat nahi.

2. Neta ka asli customer kaun hai aur kyun?

Mukhiya/block-leader. Ek voter = 1 vote (retail), ek mukhiya = 40-500 vote (wholesale). Limited time mein wholesale hi rational hai.

3. Karyakarta muft kyun kaam karta hai?

Umeed, suraksha, pehchaan, dhandha. Aur party ka unlimited zero-cost product — pad/izzat — yehi kharidta hai.

4. Ticket kis cheez par milta hai?

Sirf jeetne ke chance par. Wafadaari ginti mein nahi aati kyunki wafadaar ka vote already party ka hai. Party woh khareedti hai jo abhi uska nahi.

5. Election ke din ka kaam kya hai?

Turnout, persuasion nahi. Apne bane hue vote ko ghar se nikalna. Parchi = tracking, polling agent = aankh, counting = report card.

6. Neta institution kyun banata hai?

Kursi nazuk hai (80% asar, jo sarkar badalte hi khatam). Sanstha taakat ka permanent version hai — naukri, admission, bheed, izzat, zameen — jo haarne par bhi chalti rehti hai.

7. Dal-badal ka asli kaaran kya hai?

Virodhi dal mein machine ka compounding decay. Machine ke bina wapasi namumkin hai, isliye toot survival hai. Aur akela tootne wale ka bhaav aadha — keemat machine mein hai, naam mein nahi.

8. Dynasty kyun banti hai?

Retirement ka koi rasta nahi. 25 saal ki poonji transfer karne ka sirf ek bharosemand raasta hai — khoon. Aur party ko bhi ready machine sasti padti hai.

Agar aathon ka jawab bina peeche dekhe aa gaya — naksha tumhare dimaag mein hai. Ab koi bhi political khabar tumhare liye shor nahi rahegi; har baat ka ek khaana hoga.

What this means for you — aakhri baar

Is kitab ne tumhe do cheezein di hain.

Pehli: ek naksha, jisme har nayi political jaankari ka ek khaana hai. Ab jab koi khabar aaye — koi neta toota, koi ticket kata, koi sarkar bani — tumhe pata hoga kahan dekhna hai: uska power source kya tha, uski machine ka kya haal tha, uska gut kahan khada tha.

Doosri, aur zyada zaroori: ek nazariya. Yeh machine kisi ne design nahi ki. Woh sirf incentives ka jod hai — har aadmi apna rational kaam kar raha hai, aur unka total ek aisa system banata hai jo kisi ek aadmi ki niyat se nahi chalta.

Aur isi wajah se, jab tum kisi neta ke saamne baithoge, tum us kamre mein ek alag aadmi hoge. Tum us aadmi ko "neta" ki tarah nahi dekhoge. Tum dekhoge: iska level, iska power source, iska abhi ka khatra, aur isliye iski abhi ki zaroorat.

Aur jab tum kisi ki zaroorat theek se dekh lete ho, tab tumhe usse kuch bechne ki zaroorat nahi padti. Tum sirf usse woh dete ho jo usko waise bhi chahiye tha.
