# Chapter 5
## Karyakarta — hazaaron log muft mein kaam kyun karte hain

---

### Sabse bada sawaal jo koi nahi poochta

Election ke din ek MLA candidate ke liye zameen par kitne log kaam karte hain? Ek 3 lakh voter wali seat par, theek se ladne wale candidate ke paas 1,000 se 3,000 log active hote hain — booth par baithne wale, gaadi chalane wale, parchi baantne wale, bheed jutane wale, jhande lagane wale.

Ab ek bania ki tarah sawaal poocho: **1,000 logon ko 40 din kaam par rakhne ka kharcha kitna hoga?**

Agar sabko dihadi dena pade, toh yeh number itna bada ho jaata hai ki koi bhi campaign namumkin ho jaye. Aur phir bhi har seat par, har party ke paas, yeh log hote hain.

Toh sach kya hai? Sach yeh hai:

```
Kuch log paise par hote hain (election ke aakhri dino mein
mostly, ya specific kaam ke liye — reported rates aksar
Rs 300-2,000/din ki range mein bataye jaate hain, jagah
aur kaam ke hisaab se, plus khaana-chai-petrol)

Lekin machine ka DHAANCHA un logon par khada hai jo
paise ke liye nahi aate — karyakarta.
```

Yeh chapter us aadmi ke baare mein hai. Aur maano ya na maano — **poore Indian political system ka sabse zaroori aur sabse kam samjha gaya hissa yehi hai.** Agar tumhe karyakarta samajh aa gaya, toh tumhe samajh aa jayega ki yeh poori machine chalti kaise hai, kyunki uska poora physical kaam yehi aadmi karta hai.

### Toh woh aata kyun hai?

Chapter 1 ka sabak yaad karo: har player apna rational sauda kar raha hai. Toh karyakarta ka sauda kya hai? Woh apna time, apni gaadi, apni jawani, kabhi-kabhi apna paisa lagata hai. Badle mein kya paata hai?

Char cheezein. Aur inko ratna nahi, samajhna:

**1. UMEED (hope) — sabse bada khichne wala**

Karyakarta ke saamne ek seedhi hai jo usse dikhti hai:

```
karyakarta → booth pramukh → mandal/ward ka pad
    → panchayat/nagar ka ticket → zila ka pad
        → MLA ka ticket → ...
```

Yeh seedhi asli hai — log sach mein chadhte hain. Lekin ganit dekho: hazaaron karyakarta, aur ticket ek. Yeh lottery jaisa hai — **jeetne ka chance kam hai, lekin sirf khelne walon ko milta hai.** Aur khelne ki fees "5 saal wafadaari" hai.

Isliye karyakarta ka sabse bada dushman doosri party nahi hoti. **Uska sabse bada dushman apni hi party ka woh aadmi hota hai jo usse aage nikal raha hai.** Yaad rakhna — chapter 12 (factions) ka poora beej yahan hai.

**2. SURAKSHA (protection) — sabse turant fayda**

Yeh sabse practical hai, aur isko bahar wale sabse kam samajhte hain.

Ek aam aadmi ki zindagi mein sarkar ek deewar hai — police, tehsil, bijli office, hospital, school admission. Har jagah line hai, aur line mein uska number aakhri hai.

Karyakarta ke paas ek cheez hoti hai jo aam aadmi ke paas nahi: **ek phone number jo uthaya jaata hai.** Uska thaana mein kaam ho jaata hai. Uske bhatije ka admission ho jaata hai. Uske chacha ka bijli connection aa jaata hai.

Aur yeh sirf uske liye nahi hai — **yahi uski gaon/mohalle mein keemat hai.** Log uske paas aate hain kyunki uska number lagta hai. Woh khud ek chhota mukhiya ban jaata hai. Chapter 2 wala block? Karyakarta ke haath mein ek chhota block aa jaata hai.

Toh dekho kya hua: usne party ko time diya, party ne usko access diya, access se usko apna block mila, aur block se usko party mein aur keemat mili. **Yeh loop hai — aur loop khud ko majboot karta jaata hai.**

**3. PEHCHAAN (identity) — sabse gehri, sabse kam dikhne wali**

Ek aam aadmi 30 saal ka hai, naukri sadharan hai, gaon mein koi khaas naam nahi. Party ka jhanda uthate hi kuch badal jaata hai. Ab woh "party ka aadmi" hai. Shaadi mein usko aage bithate hain. Uske mobile mein MLA ka number hai. Rally mein woh stage ke paas khada hota hai.

Yeh cheez paise mein nahi milti, aur isko chhodna bahut mushkil hota hai — kyunki chhodne ka matlab hai wapas "koi nahi" ban jaana.

Isliye jab tum kisi 45 saal ke karyakarta ko dekho jisko 20 saal mein kuch nahi mila, aur phir bhi woh subah 7 baje jhanda lagane pahunch jaata hai — usko bewakoof mat samajhna. Woh apni pehchaan bacha raha hai. **Uske paas jo hai, woh usne 20 saal mein banaya hai, aur woh party ke andar hi exist karta hai.**

**4. DHANDHA (business) — sabse honest wajah**

Kuch log seedha isliye aate hain kyunki party ka aadmi hona kaam aata hai:

- Thekedar ko theka chahiye — sarkari kaam party ke aadmi ko milna aasan hai
- Transporter ko permit chahiye
- Chhote dukandaar ko licence/atikraman se bachav chahiye
- Zameen ke dhandhe wale ko information chahiye — kahan road aayegi, kahan zone badlega

Yeh log "karyakarta" hain kaagaz par, lekin unka sauda seedha business hai. **Aur system ko inki sabse zyada zaroorat hoti hai, kyunki paisa aur gaadi yahi log dete hain.**

### Ab machine ki asli shakal

In char logon ko milakar dekho toh party ka dhaancha samajh aata hai — jo actually ek **pyramid** hai:

```
                 MLA / candidate
                        │
            ┌───────────┴───────────┐
        mandal/ward ke pad-adhikari   (10-30 log)
            │
    ┌───────┴────────┐
  booth pramukh × 250-300 booths      (250-300 log)
        │
   ┌────┴─────┐
 page pramukh / 10-ghar wala aadmi    (1,000-3,000 log)
        │
      VOTER
```

Neeche wala aadmi 20-50 ghar sambhalta hai. Woh un gharon ko naam se jaanta hai. Election ke din uska ek hi kaam hai: **un 20-50 gharon ko booth tak le aana.**

Aur ab poori kitab ka ek bada connection banao:

- Chapter 2 mein humne kaha: neta ka customer voter nahi, **mukhiya** hai.
- Yeh pyramid us baat ka **physical dhaancha** hai. Party ka kaam yeh hai ki har block ke mukhiya ko pyramid mein ek pad de do. Mukhiya ko izzat mil gayi, party ko block mil gaya.

**Party ek machine nahi hai jo bahar se blocks ko kharidti hai. Party ek dhaancha hai jisme blocks ke mukhiyaon ko pad dekar andar rakh liya jaata hai.** Yeh ek line 20 chapters mein kaam aayegi.

### Karyakarta ki keemat kab pata chalti hai

Saal bhar yeh log invisible hote hain. Unki keemat teen mauko par khulti hai:

**1. Ticket ke waqt (chapter 7).** Party survey karti hai — kis aspirant ke peeche kitne log hain. Jiske peeche machine nahi, uska ticket kat sakta hai chahe woh kitna bhi popular ho.

**2. Election ke din (chapter 9).** Booth par baithne ke liye har booth par 2-4 log chahiye. 300 booth = 600-1,200 log, ek hi din, ek hi waqt. Yeh log paise se nahi aate — yeh 5 saal ke rishte se aate hain. **Jis candidate ke paas booth par baithne wale nahi hain, woh election se pehle hi haar chuka hai.**

**3. Dal-badal ke waqt (chapter 13).** Jab neta party badalta hai, asli sawaal yeh hota hai: uske saath uske log aayenge ya nahi? Neta ke bina machine chal jaati hai. Machine ke bina neta kuch nahi hai.

### Contested corner

**Karyakarta ka system majboot ho raha hai ya kamzor?**

- **Camp A:** Kamzor. Ab paisa aur WhatsApp zyada important hain; log wafadaari se nahi, dihadi se aate hain; naya ladka 5 saal free kaam karne ko tayyar nahi jab uske paas Instagram aur naukri dono hain.
- **Camp B:** Aur majboot. Jo party sabse ache se karyakarta ka dhaancha banati hai (booth-level tak), wahi jeetti hai — aur yeh 2014 ke baad aur zyada sach hua hai, kam nahi.

Dono ke paas evidence hai. Shayad sach yeh hai ki **dhaancha ab bhi decisive hai, lekin usko chalane ka tarika badal gaya hai** — pehle sirf rishta tha, ab rishta + WhatsApp group + data. Chapter 19 mein isko poora kholenge.

### Incentive equation

```
KARYAKARTA:
  Deta hai:  time, gaadi, jawani, kabhi paisa, aur
             sabse keemti — apna chhota block
  Paata hai: umeed (ticket ki lottery) + suraksha (phone
             uthta hai) + pehchaan (main kuch hoon) +
             dhandha (kuch logon ke liye)
  Darr:      party mein uska number peeche chala jaye;
             ya usse bada koi aur uske ilaake mein aa jaye
  Isliye:    uska asli dushman apni party ka pratidwandi hai

NETA:
  Deta hai:  access, pad, chhote kaam, izzat
  Paata hai: 1,000-3,000 logon ki free labour + unke blocks
  Darr:      machine doosre ke paas chali jaye
  Isliye:    neta ka aadha waqt VOTER ke liye nahi, apne
             karyakartaon ko sambhalne mein jaata hai
             (shaadi, mayyat, aspataal — sabse pehle
              apne logon ke)

PARTY:
  Deta hai:  dhaancha (pad, naam, jhanda)
  Paata hai: hazaaron blocks, ek chhat ke neeche
  Isliye:    party ka asli product IZZAT hai — woh cheez
             jo woh unlimited chhaap sakti hai aur jiski
             cost zero hai
```

Woh aakhri line dobara padho. **Party ke paas paisa limited hai, ticket limited hain, lekin PAD aur IZZAT unlimited hain.** "Yuva morcha ka mandal upadhyaksh" — is naam ki cost zero hai, aur yeh ek aadmi ko 5 saal kaam par laga deta hai. Yeh system ka sabse chalak hissa hai.

### Master map — paanchwa box

```
        ┌─────────────────────────┐
        │   NEED: GOVERNANCE      │
        └───────────┬─────────────┘
                    ▼
   ┌────────────────────────────┐      ┌──────────────────────┐
   │   DECIDER (neta)           │◄─────│ SARKARI PAISA        │
   │   salary ↓  taakat ↑       │      │ + LISTS + KAAGAZ     │
   └───────────┬────────────────┘      └──────────────────────┘
               │                       ┌──────────────────────┐
               │                 ◄─────│ PRIVATE PAISA        │
               │                       │ (chahiye: SPEED)     │
               ▼                       └──────────────────────┘
   ┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │   MACHINE (karyakarta pyramid)   ← NAYA    │
   │   mandal → booth pramukh → page pramukh    │
   │   fuel: umeed + suraksha + pehchaan +dhandha│
   └───────────┬────────────────────────────────┘

```

Ab machine ka bicholiya (middle layer) juda. Dekho neta seedha blocks se nahi juda — **beech mein machine hai.** Yeh insight aage bahut kaam aayega: jo aadmi machine control karta hai, woh neta ko bhi control karta hai.

### Baban ki kahani — part 5

Baban 24 ka hai. Corporator ke office mein do saal ho gaye. Tankha ab 6,000.

Ek shaam corporator ne usse kaha: "Tu register likhta rehta hai. Ab ek kaam kar — Shivaji Nagar ka booth number 114, 118, 121 — teen booth tera. Voter list le, ghar-ghar ja, aur mujhe batana kaun kiske saath hai."

Baban ne poocha: "Iske kitne paise?"

Corporator hansa nahi. Bola: "Paise nahi. **Tere naam ki parchi lagegi.** Us ilaake mein log kahenge 'Baban saheb ka aadmi hai.' Baaki tu khud dekh lena."

Baban ko us waqt yeh dhokha laga.

Teen mahine baad usko samajh aaya. Booth 118 ki ek aurat ka bijli connection do saal se atka tha. Baban ne ek phone karwa diya — corporator ke office se. Connection 8 din mein aa gaya.

Us hafte, us basti mein, Baban ka naam badal gaya. Ab woh "office ka ladka" nahi tha. Ab woh **"woh ladka jiska kaam ho jaata hai"** tha.

Agle do mahine mein 14 log usko dhoondhte hue aaye. Usne 9 ke kaam karwa diye, 5 ke nahi hue. Jo 9 the, unhone apne ghar ke, apne rishtedaaron ke naam batane shuru kar diye.

Ek saal baad Baban ke paas teen booth the aur usko pata tha ki us ilaake mein lagbhag 400 vote uske kehne par hil sakte hain.

Us saal ki Diwali par corporator ne usko bulaya aur kaha: "Ab samajh aaya tankha kya thi?"

Baban ne kaha: "Haan. Aap paise nahi de rahe the. Aap **rasta** de rahe the."

Corporator ne sar hilaya: "Aur ek baat samajh le. Main tujhe kabhi itna paisa nahi de sakta tha ki tu 5 saal ruk jaata. Lekin 400 vote — woh tere hain, mere nahi. **Isliye tu ab mujhse nahi jaayega, kyunki jaayega toh 400 vote yahin chhod ke jaana padega.**"

Us raat Baban ne copy nikali. Bahut dino baad. Aur usne ek line likhi jo aage uski poori zindagi ka formula ban gayi:

> **Party paisa nahi deti. Party rasta deti hai. Aur jo aadmi rasta le leta hai, woh phir apne rastey se bandha jaata hai.**

Neeche usne ek aur line likhi, chhoti si, jo us purane aadmi ki baat ka jawab thi jo usne 19 saal ki umar mein suni thi — *"jiska bhaav abhi laga nahi, uska bhaav sabse zyada hota hai"*:

> **Ab mera bhaav lag gaya hai. Aur bhaav lagne ka matlab hai — ab main bik sakta hoon. Yehi taakat hai, yehi phansna hai.**

### Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

**1. Karyakarta muft mein kaam kyun karta hai — chaaron wajah batao.**

> *Jawab:* (1) Umeed — pad se ticket tak ki seedhi, lottery jaisi lekin sirf khelne walon ke liye. (2) Suraksha — uska phone uthaya jaata hai, uske kaam hote hain, aur isse uski apni keemat banti hai. (3) Pehchaan — "party ka aadmi" hona, jo paise se nahi milta. (4) Dhandha — thekedar/transporter/dukandaar ke liye seedha business fayda.

**2. Party ka "unlimited product" kya hai aur woh itna powerful kyun hai?**

> *Jawab:* Pad aur izzat. Ticket limited hai, paisa limited hai, lekin "mandal upadhyaksh" jaise pad ki cost zero hai aur woh unlimited banaye ja sakte hain. Zero-cost cheez se 5 saal ki labour milti hai — system ka sabse chalak hissa.

**3. Karyakarta ka sabse bada dushman kaun, aur kyun?**

> *Jawab:* Apni hi party ka pratidwandi. Kyunki competition ticket/pad ke liye hai, aur ticket apni party se milta hai — doosri party se nahi. Yehi factions (chapter 12) ka beej hai.

**4. Neta seedha blocks se kyun nahi judta — beech mein kya hai?**

> *Jawab:* Machine (karyakarta pyramid). Party har block ke mukhiya ko ek pad dekar andar rakh leti hai. Isliye jo machine control karta hai woh neta ko bhi control karta hai — aur isliye dal-badal mein asli sawaal "log saath aayenge ya nahi" hota hai.

**5. Corporator ne Baban ko paise ki jagah "rasta" kyun diya — aur usme uska apna fayda kya tha?**

> *Jawab:* Paisa dene se Baban 5 saal nahi rukta (paisa kahin bhi mil jaata hai). Rasta dene se Baban ne apna khud ka 400-vote ka block bana liya — aur woh block usi ilaake se juda hai, corporator ke through banaa hai. Ab Baban jaayega toh apna banaya hua sab chhodkar jaana padega. Sabse sasti loyalty woh hai jo aadmi khud apne haathon se banata hai.

### What this means for you

Kisi bhi neta ki asli taakat naapni ho toh uski rally ki bheed mat gino — **uske karyakarta gino.** Bheed kiraye par milti hai, karyakarta 5 saal mein banta hai. Aur agar kabhi kisi political operation ko samajhna ho ya usme kuch bechna ho, toh yaad rakhna: is system ka sabse bada dard-bhara point yehi pyramid hai — kaun kiske saath hai, kaun hil raha hai, kis booth par apna aadmi nahi hai. Jo cheez yeh pyramid saaf dikha de, woh neta ke liye software nahi, **aaina** hai.

---

*Worker payment rates (Rs 300-2,000/day plus food/travel, for 30-45 days) are widely reported by campaign-services vendors and journalism, not officially documented — treat as reported range. Pyramid structure (mandal → booth → page/panna pramukh) is a documented feature of major Indian party organisations. Nimgaon/Baban fictional.*
