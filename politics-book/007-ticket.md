# Chapter 7
## Ticket — asli election, jo public kabhi nahi dekhti

---

### Do elections, aur pehla wala mushkil hai

Public ko ek election dikhta hai: candidate vs candidate, do mahine ka shor, phir result.

Us se pehle ek aur election hota hai. Usme koi voting nahi hoti, koi date nahi hoti, koi result declare nahi hota. Usme kaun jeeta yeh sirf ek line se pata chalta hai — party ki list mein tumhara naam hai ya nahi.

Yeh hai **ticket** ka election. Aur yeh pehle wale se **kahin zyada mushkil** hai.

Ganit dekho:

```
Ek state, maano 288 seats.
Har seat par har badi party mein serious aspirants: 5-15

Matlab ek party ke andar:  ~1,500-4,000 log
Ticket:                     288

Aur us 288 mein se aadhi se zyada seats par pehle se
maujooda MLA ya pakka daavedar baitha hai.
```

Toh naye aadmi ke liye asli competition **"main vs saamne wali party"** nahi hai. Asli competition **"main vs apni hi party ke 8 log"** hai.

Yeh baat chapter 6 ke "teen darr" wale order ko explain karti hai. MLA ka pehla darr party hai, voter nahi — kyunki party ka darwaza pehle aata hai. Voter ka number baad mein aata hai.

### Ticket kis cheez par milta hai

Log samajhte hain ticket "wafadaari" par milta hai, ya "kaam" par. Sach zyada thanda hai. Party ek hi cheez maximize karti hai:

> **JEETNE KA CHANCE.**

Party ko tumse pyaar nahi hai. Party ko seat chahiye. Har baaki cheez — wafadaari, kaam, jaati, paisa — sirf isliye matter karti hai kyunki woh jeetne ke chance ko badhati ya ghatati hai.

Ab wo saari cheezein dekho jo us "chance" ko banati hain:

**1. WINNABILITY KA SABOOT (sabse bada)**

Party ke paas survey hote hain — apne bhi, kiraye ke bhi. Woh dekhte hain: is seat par kaun sa naam sabse zyada vote khinch sakta hai. Yeh number aksar aakhri faisla karta hai.

Aur is wajah se ek cheez hoti hai jo bahar se bahut ajeeb lagti hai: **party apne 20 saal purane wafadaar ka ticket kaat kar doosri party se aaye hue aadmi ko de deti hai.** Wafadaar ko lagta hai dhoka hua. Party ke hisaab se yeh simple ganit hai — naya aadmi 30,000 vote laata hai, purana 12,000.

Yaad rakhna: **party ke liye ticket inaam nahi hai, investment hai.**

**2. JAATI/SAMUDAY KA FIT**

Har seat ka apna ganit hota hai (chapter 18 poora ispe hai). Agar seat mein ek samuday 60,000 vote ka hai, toh party us samuday ka candidate dhoondhegi — chahe woh kam kabil ho.

Yeh "bura" nahi hai party ke hisaab se — yeh chapter 2 ka block-ganit hai. Ek candidate jo apne saath ek 60,000 ka block leke aata hai, woh us candidate se behtar investment hai jo akela aata hai.

**3. MACHINE (chapter 5 ka connection)**

Party poochti hai: "iske peeche kitne log hain?" Jiske paas 300 booth par apne aadmi hain, uska daawa mazboot hai. Jiske paas sirf naam hai, uska nahi.

Isliye ticket ke aspirants saal bhar bheed dikhate rehte hain — janm-din par hoarding, rally mein gaadiyaan, "samarthak" ka pradarshan. Woh voter ko impress nahi kar rahe. **Woh party ko apni machine ka size dikha rahe hain.**

**4. PAISA**

Do tarah se:

- *Campaign ka kharcha khud utha sakta hai kya?* Party ke paas har seat ka poora paisa nahi hota. Jo candidate apna kharcha khud utha le, woh party ke liye sasta hai.
- *Aur — reported, aur khulke maana nahi jaata — kuch jagah ticket ke liye party fund mein bada "yogdaan" expected hota hai.* Iske ilzaam har party par lage hain, sabne inkaar kiya hai. Isko is kitab mein pattern ki tarah rakho, proof ki tarah nahi.

**5. WAFADAARI AUR SANGATHAN**

Yeh sabse kam weight wali cheez hai — lekin zero nahi. Party ko dikhana hota hai ki wafadaari ka bhi mol hai, warna machine ka fuel (chapter 5 ka "umeed") khatam ho jayega.

Isliye har party har baar kuch tickets purane wafadaaron ko deti hai — bhale seat kamzor ho. **Yeh signal hai, poore system ko: "line mein lage raho, number aata hai."**

**6. HIGH COMMAND KA RISHTA**

Faisla aakhir mein kuch logon ke kamre mein hota hai. Jiska us kamre mein koi bolne wala hai, uska chance badh jaata hai. Chapter 12 poora is kamre ke baare mein hai.

### Aspirant ki zindagi — 5 saal ka interview

Ab is sabko jodo, aur samjho ki ek ticket-aspirant ki zindagi kaisi hoti hai.

Woh 5 saal tak ek **interview** de raha hai, jiska result ek baar aata hai. Uska har kaam do audiences ke liye hota hai:

```
KAAM                        VOTER KE LIYE      PARTY KE LIYE
mayyat mein jaana           "hamara aadmi"     "iska sampark hai"
rally mein bheed lana       "iske peeche log"  "iski machine hai"
apne kharche se ambulance   "yeh madad karta"  "iske paas paisa hai"
apni jaati ka sammelan      "yeh hamara"       "iske paas block hai"
neta ke saath photo         "iski pahunch"     "yeh line mein hai"
```

Har cheez do baar count hoti hai. Aur aspirant jaanta hai ki **party wali column zyada important hai** — kyunki party ka darwaza pehle aata hai.

### Ticket kat-ne ka din

Yeh Indian politics ka sabse kroor din hota hai, aur sabse zyada revealing bhi.

List aati hai. Aksar aakhri waqt par — nomination ki aakhri tareekh se ek-do din pehle. Yeh timing accident nahi hai: **jitna kam waqt bachega, naraz aadmi ke paas utne kam options bachenge.**

Ab jiska ticket kat gaya, uske paas kya raaste hain?

```
1. CHUP RAHO
   "Party ka faisla sar-aankhon par." Umeed: agli baar
   ya koi pad/corporation ka chairman-ship.

2. BAGAWAT — khud khade ho jao
   Nirdaleeya (independent) lado. Aksar haarte hain,
   lekin apni party ke official candidate ko HARA dete hain.
   Yeh badla bhi hai aur bargaining bhi: "dekh lo, mere
   bina yeh seat nahi jeetti."

3. DOOSRI PARTY MEIN JAO
   Agar tumhare paas block hai, toh doosri party ka
   darwaza usi raat khulta hai. Isliye ticket katne ke
   24-48 ghante mein sabse zyada dal-badal hota hai.

4. SABOTAGE
   Rehna party mein, lekin andar se apne hi candidate ko
   harana. Sabse aam, sabse mushkil pakadna, aur andar
   walon ko hamesha pata hota hai.
```

Aur ab poore system ki ek chalak baat samjho. Party jaanti hai ki 4-5 log naraz honge. Toh party unko sambhalne ke liye kya deti hai? Aksar **pad** — corporation ka chairman, board ka member, morcha ka adhyaksh.

Yaad hai chapter 5 ka insight? **Party ka unlimited product izzat hai.** Ticket 288 hain, lekin pad hazaaron banaye ja sakte hain. Isliye har ticket ke saath 4-5 pad bant-te hain — naraz logon ko line mein rokne ke liye.

### Ek chalak detail: "survey"

Har party kehti hai "hum survey ke aadhar par ticket dete hain." Yeh aadha sach hai, aur uska doosra aadha zyada dilchasp hai.

Survey do kaam karta hai:

1. **Sach mein winnability naapna** — yeh asli use hai.
2. **Faisle ko bahar ka roop dena** — "maine nahi kaata, survey ne kaata." Yeh leadership ko naraz aadmi ke gusse se bachata hai.

Isliye survey ka number kabhi public nahi hota. Jo cheez public nahi hoti, uspe bahas nahi ho sakti — aur yeh bilkul bhi accident nahi hai.

### Contested corner

**Ticket ke liye paisa kitna chalta hai?**

- **Camp A:** Bahut. Kuch seats par ticket ki ek "rate" hoti hai, aur bina us paise ke naam list mein nahi aata.
- **Camp B:** Zyada bada factor winnability hai. Paisa isliye maayne rakhta hai kyunki paisewala candidate campaign khud chala leta hai — na ki isliye ki usne ticket "khareeda".

Sach: har party mein, har state mein alag. Aur is cheez ka koi audited data nahi hai, sirf ilzaam aur inkaar hain. **Is kitab ka rule: jahan sirf ilzaam hain, wahan hum pattern likhenge, faisla nahi.**

### Incentive equation

```
PARTY (high command):
  Chahiye:  seat, aur poore state mein zyada se zyada seats
  Deti hai: ticket (288) + pad (unlimited)
  Darr:     galat candidate = seat gayi; aur naraz aadmi
            = bagawat = seat gayi
  Isliye:   party har ticket ke saath naraz logon ka
            "management plan" bhi banati hai

ASPIRANT:
  Deta hai:  5 saal, apna paisa, apni machine banana
  Chahta hai: ek naam ek list mein
  Darr:      5 saal ki mehnat ek line se khatam ho jaye
  Isliye:    woh sirf kaam nahi karta — woh apna kaam
             DIKHATA hai, kyunki dikhna hi uska
             application form hai

MAUJOODA MLA (sitting):
  Uska darr sabse alag hai: usko naye aspirants se
  khatra hai jo uske hi ilaake mein bheed dikha rahe hain.
  Isliye sitting MLA aksar apne hi party ke uparte hue
  ladke ko rokne mein utni hi energy lagata hai jitni
  virodhi dal par.
```

### Master map — ticket ka darwaza juda

```
        ┌─────────────────────────┐
        │   NEED: GOVERNANCE      │
        └───────────┬─────────────┘
                    ▼
   ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
   │  PARTY / HIGH COMMAND      ← NAYA    │
   │  maximize: JEETNE KA CHANCE          │
   │  deti hai: TICKET (limited)          │
   │            PAD (unlimited)           │
   └───────────┬──────────────────────────┘
               │ ticket = pehla darwaza
               ▼
   ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐   ┌──────────────────┐
   │  MLA / CANDIDATE                     │◄──│ SARKARI + PRIVATE│
   │  taakat = 20% adhikaar + 80% asar    │   │ PAISA            │
   └───────────┬──────────────────────────┘   └──────────────────┘
               ▼
   ┌────────────────────────────┐
   │  MACHINE (karyakarta)      │ ← ticket ka saboot bhi yehi

               ▼

   └────────────────────────────┘
```

Ab machine dikhne lagi: **voter se pehle party aati hai.** Public ko lagta hai chain neeche se upar chalti hai (voter → MLA). Asal mein ek darwaza upar se pehle khulta hai.

### Baban ki kahani — part 7

Baban 34 ka hai. Ab woh "office ka ladka" nahi — corporator ke poore ward ka manager hai, aur do aur wardon mein uska naam chalta hai.

Us saal party ne nagar palika ke chunav se pehle naye chehre dhoondhne shuru kiye. Baban ka naam chala. Zila adhyaksh ne khud bulaya. Baban ne raat bhar neend nahi li.

List aayi. Uska naam nahi tha.

Ticket us aadmi ko mila jo 8 mahine pehle doosri party se aaya tha — ek thekedar, jiske paas do trucks, ek marriage hall, aur us ward mein 900 vote ka apna samuday tha.

Baban seedha zila adhyaksh ke paas gaya. Gussa nahi — sawaal.

"Mere paas 11 booth hain. Mere naam par 1,800 vote hilte hain. Usne toh 8 mahine pehle party join ki hai. Kyun?"

Adhyaksh ne panne palte aur bola: "Tere 1,800 vote **hamare hi** vote hain. Tu hamara aadmi hai, teri machine hamari machine hai — tu ticket na bhi le toh woh 1,800 hamein hi milenge. Uske 900 vote hamare nahi hain. Ticket dekar hum 900 KHARIDTE hain. Tere ko dekar hum 1,800 dobara nahi kharid sakte — woh already hamare hain."

Baban stunned reh gaya. Yeh sabse kroor, sabse saaf ganit tha jo usne kabhi suna tha.

Adhyaksh ne aage kaha: "Aur ek baat. Tu naraz hoke bagawat karega toh mujhe pata hai tu 1,800 le jayega aur hamara candidate haar jayega. Isliye main tujhe abhi ek pad de raha hoon — yuva morcha, nagar adhyaksh. Kaam kam, naam bada."

Baban ghar aaya. Register khola. Do line likhi:

> **Party wafadaari ka inaam nahi deti. Party wafadaari ko GINTI mein hi nahi leti — kyunki wafadaar aadmi ka vote already uska hai. Party woh cheez khareedti hai jo abhi uski nahi hai.**

> **Aur naraz aadmi ko party ticket se nahi, PAD se sambhalti hai. Ticket 288 hain. Pad hazaar. Isliye har ticket ke saath 4 pad bant-te hain.**

Phir usne teesri line likhi, jo sabse zaroori thi:

> **Iska matlab yeh nahi ki wafadaari bekaar hai. Iska matlab yeh hai ki wafadaari KAAFI nahi hai. Jab tak main sirf "hamara aadmi" rahunga, mera ticket kabhi nahi aayega. Ticket us din aayega jis din mere paas kuch aisa hoga jo party ka NAHI hai — apna block, jo mere jaane se mere saath jaayega.**

Us raat Baban ne yuva morcha ka pad le liya.

Aur usi raat se usne ek naya kaam shuru kiya, jo usne kisi ko nahi bataya: apne 11 booth ke logon se woh ab **party ke naam par** nahi, **apne naam par** milna shuru ho gaya. Chhoti si baat hai. Poori zindagi ka farak hai.

### Self-test (pehle dimaag, phir check)

**1. "Asli election ticket ka hota hai" — iska ganit kya hai?**

> *Jawab:* Ek party mein hazaaron serious aspirants, tickets sirf seats ke barabar (jaise 288), aur unme se aadhe se zyada pehle se bhare hue. Matlab candidate ka pehla aur sabse mushkil muqabla apni hi party ke logon se hota hai, virodhi party se nahi.

**2. Party ticket dete waqt kya maximize karti hai, aur isse kaun sa "ajeeb" behavior explain hota hai?**

> *Jawab:* Sirf **jeetne ka chance**. Isliye party 20 saal ke wafadaar ka ticket kaat kar naye aaye hue aadmi ko de sakti hai — kyunki naya aadmi apne saath naya block laata hai. Ticket inaam nahi, investment hai.

**3. Aspirant saal bhar bheed aur hoarding kyun dikhata hai — kiske liye?**

> *Jawab:* Voter ke liye kam, **party ke liye zyada**. Woh apni machine ka size dikha raha hai, kyunki machine ticket ke faisle ka ek bada factor hai. Uska har kaam do audiences ke liye counted hota hai.

**4. Ticket katne par 4 raaste kaunse, aur party naraz aadmi ko kaise sambhalti hai?**

> *Jawab:* Chup rehna, bagawat (independent lad kar apni hi party ko harana), doosri party mein jaana, ya andar se sabotage. Party sambhalti hai **pad** dekar — kyunki ticket limited hai lekin pad unlimited aur zero-cost hain.

**5. Adhyaksh ne Baban ko ticket kyun nahi diya, uske 1,800 vote hone ke bawajood?**

> *Jawab:* Kyunki woh 1,800 already party ke the — Baban party ka aadmi tha, uska block party ke saath hi rehta. Ticket dekar party woh vote khareedti hai jo abhi uske paas NAHI hain (thekedaar ke 900). Wafadaar ka vote party ke liye "already booked" hota hai, isliye uski ginti nahi hoti.

### What this means for you

Kisi bhi neta ko samajhna ho toh yeh mat poochho "yeh kitna popular hai." Yeh poochho: **"iska ticket kitna surakshit hai?"** Jo MLA ticket ko lekar nishchint hai, woh alag aadmi hai. Jo har saal apni hi party ke teen ladkon ko apne ilaake mein bheed jutate dekhta hai, woh dooba hua aadmi hai — aur woh us cheez ke liye sabse zyada khula hai jo usko party ke saamne mazboot dikhaye. Aur agar tum kabhi kisi aspirant se milo (jo abhi MLA nahi hai), toh yaad rakhna: uska asli customer voter nahi hai, **party ka woh kamra hai** — aur woh us kamre mein apna number badhane ke liye kuch bhi karega.
