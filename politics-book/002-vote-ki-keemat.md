# Chapter 2
## Vote ki keemat — ek vote bekaar hai, vote-bank sab kuch hai

---

### Gaon se shuru

Nimgaon mein ab decider chunna hai. Gaon ne tay kiya: har aadmi-aurat ka ek vote, jiske zyada vote uski kursi.

Pehli nazar mein yeh barabari ka system lagta hai. Sau log, sau vote, sabki keemat same.

Ab ek kisaan ki nazar se dekho — naam rakho Tatya. Tatya sochta hai: "Mera ek vote hai. Sau mein se ek. Agar main vote dun ya na dun, result shayad hi badle. Toh meri keemat kya hai?"

Tatya bilkul sahi soch raha hai. **Akela vote sach mein bekaar hai.** Yeh koi cynical baat nahi, seedha ganit hai: 100 vote mein 1 vote result 1% badalta hai. 3 lakh mein 1 vote kuch nahi badalta.

Lekin ab dekho kya hota hai. Tatya ke ghar mein 8 vote hain. Uske bhai, chachere bhai, unki gharwaliyan — milakar 40 vote. Aur Tatya ke kehne par poora khaandaan ek taraf jaata hai.

Ab Tatya ki keemat kya hai? **40.** Ab decider banne ka sapna dekhne wala har aadmi Tatya ke ghar chai peene aayega.

Yeh hai is chapter ka poora sach, ek line mein: **vote ki keemat tab paida hoti hai jab vote ek saath BLOCK mein chalta hai.** Akela vote paisa nahi hai. Block ek bank account hai. Isliye naam pada — **vote-bank**.

### Neta ki nazar se wahi gaon

Ab kursi ke candidate ki nazar se dekho. Uske paas time limited hai, paisa limited hai. Woh 100 logon se alag-alag nahi mil sakta.

Uske saamne gaon aise dikhta hai:

```
Tatya ka khaandaan        40 vote   (Tatya se baat karo)
Mandir ka pujari group    15 vote   (pujari se baat karo)
Kuaan ke paas ke ghar     20 vote   (unka mukhiya — Ganpat)
Naye base huye log        10 vote   (koi mukhiya nahi — mehnga sauda)
Idhar-udhar ke            15 vote   (kisi ki nahi sunte — chhod do)
```

Dekha kya hua? Candidate ne 100 logon ko 5 line mein compress kar diya. Ab usse sirf 3 aadmiyon se baat karni hai — Tatya, pujari, Ganpat — aur 75 vote ka intezam ho jaata hai.

Yeh compression hi politics hai. **Neta log individuals ko ignore nahi karte kyunki woh bure hain. Ignore karte hain kyunki ganit yehi kehta hai.** Ek-ek vote ke liye baat karna 100 meetings hai; block ke liye baat karna 3 meetings. Jo candidate individuals se milta rahega, woh haar jayega us candidate se jo mukhiyaon se milta hai.

Aur is ganit ka ulta side bhi samajh lo: **jo aadmi apne peeche block khada kar sakta hai, uski political keemat hai. Jo nahi kar sakta, woh kitna bhi padha-likha ho, uski keemat zero hai.** Yehi wajah hai ki gaon ka anpadh Tatya MLA ke saath stage par baithta hai aur sheher ka professor ghar par TV dekhta hai.

### Ab asli India ke numbers

Structure wahi, scale badha do:

```
Ek assembly seat (MLA):     ~2-4 lakh voters (approx, state par depend)
Turnout (kitne vote dete):  ~60-66% aam taur par
                            (2024 Lok Sabha: 66%; Delhi 2025: ~61%)

Matlab ~3 lakh ki seat par asli pade vote: ~1.8-2 lakh
```

Aur ab woh number jo sab kuch samjha deta hai:

```
Jeetne wale ko kitne % vote milte hain?

2024 Lok Sabha:  jeetne walon ka average 50.58%
                 → 49% winners ne AADHE se KAM vote par jeeta
Delhi 2025:      winners ka average 51%
```

Iska matlab dhyan se samjho. India mein **First Past The Post** system hai — jiske sabse zyada vote, woh jeeta. 50% ki zaroorat nahi. Agar 4 candidate hain, toh 30-35% vote par bhi seat jeet jaati hai.

Ab poora hisaab jodo:

```
3,00,000 voters ki seat
   x 62% turnout        = ~1,86,000 vote padenge
   x ~35-40% (jeet)     = ~65,000-75,000 vote chahiye

Matlab: 3 LAKH log jinke naam par seat hai, unme se sirf
        ~70,000 ko apna banana hai. Baaki 2.3 lakh ka
        kuch bhi socho, farak nahi padta.
```

Yeh number — "jeetne ke liye asli kitne chahiye" — har campaign ka starting point hai. Aage chapter 8 mein jab hum booth system kholenge, toh dekhoge ki poori machine isi 70,000 ke ird-gird bani hai: kaun se mohalle, kaun se khaandaan, kaun se block milakar 70,000 banta hai.

### Teen cheezein jo is ganit se apne aap nikalti hain

**1. Vote todna bhi utna hi keemti hai jitna vote jodna.**
Agar mere paas 70,000 nahi ban rahe, toh doosra rasta hai: saamne wale ke block ko do hisson mein todo. Uske jaati ke doosre neta ko khada karwa do, uska vote batega, mera 60,000 bhi jeet jayega. Jab bhi koi "chhota candidate" khada dikhe jo jeet nahi sakta, yeh mat poochho "yeh kyun lada?" — yeh poochho "**iske ladne se kiska ganit sudhra?**"

**2. Turnout ek hathiyar hai.**
Mere block ke log vote dene niklein, saamne wale ke log ghar baithe rahein — result wahi hai jo vote badalne se aata. Isliye election ke din ki asli ladai persuasion nahi, **transportation** hai: gaadi, parchi, "chalo chalo" karne wale ladke. Yeh chapter 9 ka poora subject hai.

**3. Numbers pehle se pata hote hain.**
Voter list public hoti hai. Har candidate ko mahino pehle pata hota hai kaun se booth par kitne vote hain, pichhli baar kya hua tha. Election ke din surprise kam hota hai, hisaab zyada. Jo cheez TV par "lehar" dikhti hai, woh zameen par spreadsheet hai.

### Contested corner (yahan map fuzzy hai)

Ek sawaal jispe khud experts ek raay nahi rakhte: **kya paisa dekar vote sach mein khareed sakte ho?**

- **Camp A kehta hai:** haan — sharaab, cash, saree bat-ti hai, aur close seats mein yehi farak banati hai.
- **Camp B kehta hai:** nahi — log paisa sabse le lete hain aur vote apne block ke hisaab se hi dete hain. Paisa vote nahi khareedta, sirf "race mein serious hoon" ka signal deta hai.

Zameen ka sach shayad beech mein hai, aur seat-by-seat alag hai. Is kitab mein jab bhi aisi jagah aayegi jahan ek settled answer nahi hai, main tumhe dono camp dikhaunga. Clarity ka matlab yeh bhi hai ki pata ho **kahan par certainty fake hoti hai.**

### Incentive equation

```
NETA ka hisaab:
  70,000 ka target → blocks se banta hai → mukhiyaon se banta hai
  Isliye: neta ka asli customer VOTER nahi, MUKHIYA hai
  Voter ke liye kaam = retail (mehnga, dheema)
  Mukhiya ke liye kaam = wholesale (sasta, tez)

MUKHIYA (Tatya) ka hisaab:
  Deta hai: apna block (40-500 vote)
  Paata hai: neta ka direct access, apne logon ke kaam,
             gaon mein izzat, "MLA mera aadmi hai" ki taakat
  Darr: block toot gaya toh keemat zero
  Isliye: mukhiya apna block jodkar rakhne mein hi zinda hai

VOTER ka hisaab:
  Akele vote ki keemat ~zero, yeh usse pata hai
  Block ke saath rehne par: kaam hote hain, suraksha milti hai
  Isliye: block ke saath vote dena voter ke liye RATIONAL hai,
          bhed-chaal nahi
```

Yeh aakhri line dobara padho. Bahar se dekhne par "yeh log jhund mein vote kyun dete hain, apna dimaag kyun nahi lagate" wali feeling aati hai. Andar se dekho toh block ke saath rehna hi voter ka sabse samajhdar sauda hai — akela voter kuch nahi manwa sakta, block sab kuch manwa leta hai.

### Baban ki kahani — part 2

Baban ab 15 saal ka hai. Sarpanch ka election aaya — 20 saal mein pehli baar purane sarpanch ko koi serious challenger mila hai: Bhaskar, jo sheher mein thekedaari karke wapas aaya hai, jeep ke saath.

Ek shaam purana sarpanch khud Baban ke ghar aaya. Baithak mein baitha, Tatya se kheti ki baat ki — paani ki, mandi ke bhaav ki, sab ki. Vote ka naam tak nahi liya. Chai pi, aur jaate waqt darwaze par ruk kar sirf itna bola: "Tatya bhau, ghar ke sab vote yaad rakhna."

Baban ne pehli baar dekha ki uska baap — jo mandi mein sabse jhukkar milta hai — apne ghar ki baithak mein sarpanch se **barabar ki kursi par** baitha tha. Aur sarpanch chai peene *aaya* tha, bulaya nahi gaya tha.

Do din baad Bhaskar ki jeep bhi aayi. Bhaskar seedha bola: "Kaka, chaalis vote tumhare haath mein hain. Main sarpanch bana toh tumhara chhota ladka panchayat office mein kaam karega."

Ab Tatya ke paas do offer the. Aur yahan Baban ne apni zindagi ki pehli political galti hote dekhi — apne hi baap se.

Tatya ne dono ko haan bol di. Sochta tha dono taraf se fayda lega. Baban ne dheere se kaha bhi: "Baba, dono ko haan bologe toh dono ko pata chal jayega." Tatya ne daant diya: "Tu abhi bachcha hai."

Result aaya. Purana sarpanch teen vote se jeeta. Aur agle hafte se dono taraf ke aadmi Tatya ko mandi mein dekh kar muskurana band ho gaye. Dono ko pata chal chuka tha ki Tatya ne dono ko haan boli thi — gaon mein baat chhupti nahi. Tatya ka block abhi bhi 40 vote ka tha, lekin uski **keemat gir gayi thi**, kyunki keemat vote mein nahi, BHAROSE mein hoti hai. Jo mukhiya apna word nahi rakhta, uska block agli baar ginti mein nahi aata.

Raat ko Baban ne apni copy ke aakhri page par gaon ka hisaab likha: 600 vote. Jeet ~250 par. Khaandaan ke 40, mama-side ke 30, kuaan wale mohalle ke 25... aur har block ke aage ek naya column banaya jo uske baap ne kabhi nahi banaya tha: **"word ka pakka?"**

Woh 15 saal ka tha, aur usne is chapter ka sabak apne ghar ki izzat girte hue seekha tha: block currency hai, lekin us currency ki backing bharosa hai. Jis din bharosa gaya, us din 40 vote sirf 40 log reh jaate hain.

### Self-test (pehle apne dimaag se, phir check)

**1. Akela vote bekaar, lekin block ke saath jaana rational — dono ek saath sach kaise?**

> *Jawab:* Akela vote result nahi badal sakta (1 in 3 lakh), yeh ganit hai. Lekin block ke member ke roop mein voter ko kaam, suraksha aur access milta hai — block ki taakat ka hissa milta hai. Matlab vote ki keemat individual level par zero hai aur block level par poori — voter block join karke apne zero ko non-zero bana leta hai.

**2. 3 lakh ki seat par asli kitne vote chahiye?**

> *Jawab:* 3,00,000 x ~62% turnout = ~1,86,000 pade vote. FPTP mein 4-5 candidates ke beech ~35-40% par jeet = **~65,000-75,000 vote**. Seat 3 lakh ki dikhti hai, ladai ~70 hazaar ki hoti hai.

**3. Na-jeetne wala candidate kyun ladta hai?**

> *Jawab:* Kyunki uske ladne se kisi aur ka ganit sudhrta hai — woh kisi block ko todta hai (vote-katwa). Ya khud ko agli baar ke liye register kar raha hai: "mere paas 15,000 vote hain" dikha kar woh agli deal mein seat ya post maang sakta hai. Haarna bhi ek business model hai.

**4. Neta ka asli customer kaun?**

> *Jawab:* Mukhiya/block-leader, voter nahi. Ek voter = 1 vote (retail). Ek mukhiya = 40-500 vote (wholesale). Limited time aur paise mein wholesale hi rational hai. Isliye neta ka network voters ka nahi, mukhiyaon ka hota hai.

**5. "Vote kharide ja sakte hain?" — dono camps.**

> *Jawab:* Camp A: haan — cash/sharaab/saree close seats mein margin bana deti hai. Camp B: nahi — log sabse le lete hain, vote apne block ke hisaab se dete hain; paisa sirf "main serious candidate hoon" ka signal hai. Settled answer nahi hai — seat-by-seat alag, aur yehi is sawaal ka sahi jawab hai.

### What this means for you

Jab bhi koi seat ya koi neta samajhna ho, sabse pehle yeh mat poochho "log kise pasand karte hain." Yeh poochho: **"is seat ka 70,000 kaise banta hai — kaun se blocks, kaun se mukhiya?"** Popularity TV ka subject hai. Blocks zameen ka subject hai. Aur jo aadmi tumhare saamne baitha hai (MLA ho ya uska PA), uski har raat ki tension yehi spreadsheet hai — kaun sa block pakka hai, kaun sa hil raha hai. Uski duniya mein sabse keemti cheez woh hai jo usse yeh spreadsheet zyada saaf dikha de.
