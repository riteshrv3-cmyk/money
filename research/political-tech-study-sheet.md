# Political-Tech Study Sheet (for MLA meeting)

Scope: citizen grievance / constituent CRM products, US + UK + India, plus AI tools.
Ranked by usefulness in YOUR pitch, not by company size.
Deep-study products: #1 to #4. Learn these to speak 2 minutes each.
One-line products: #5 to #10. Only recognise the names.
India section: #11 to #14. Your ammo when he says "this exists in India."

## DEEP STUDY (memorise these 4 stories)

### 1. Intranet Quorum (IQ) - USA - the "everyone uses it" proof
- What it does: One system in a Congressman's office that logs EVERY citizen letter, email, call, and social media message. Tracks who asked what, ensures every message gets a reply, and gives the leader reports on what citizens are angry about (by area and by issue).
- Problem solved: No citizen request is ever forgotten. The leader is never blind about public mood.
- Who uses it + scale: About 60-65% of the US Congress, 50% of US Governors, 100+ government agencies. Running for 30+ years (Leidos company).
- Business model: Software + support contracts paid by government office budgets.
- Founding story: Built over 30 years ago inside big defence/IT company Leidos, became the standard tool of the US Congress.
- SPOKEN LINES:
  - "Sir, in America, 60% of all Congressmen run their office on one software called IQ. Every citizen letter is logged and answered. It has worked for 30 years."
  - "The Congressman opens a report and sees: this area, this issue, this many angry people."
  - Marathi hint: "प्रत्येक तक्रारीला नंबर, प्रत्येक नंबरला उत्तर."

### 2. Indigov - USA - the "new startup, big money" proof
- What it does: Modern citizen-CRM for elected representatives. Automatically sorts incoming citizen messages and drafts replies. Cut reply time from 45 days to under 8 hours, automating 70% of all incoming mail.
- Problem solved: Office drowning in citizen messages; slow replies make the leader look inactive.
- Who uses it + scale: Thousands of US officials (Congress, state legislatures, governors, mayors) representing 150+ million Americans. Example: Michigan House of Representatives.
- Business model: Software subscription. Raised $38M+ from investors ($25M Series B in 2022).
- Founding story: Startup founded around 2019 to replace old tools like IQ with modern tech.
- SPOKEN LINES:
  - "Sir, a new American company Indigov raised 38 million dollars only to solve this one problem: replying to citizens fast."
  - "Their result: work that took 45 days now happens in 8 hours."
  - Marathi hint: "अमेरिकेत ४५ दिवसांचं काम आता ८ तासांत होतं."

### 3. DearMP - UK - the "how it works" story
- What it does: Casework software for UK MP offices. Every citizen complaint automatically becomes a numbered case with an owner, a deadline, and a file. Cases can be filtered by urgency or inactivity, so nothing falls through the cracks. Every constituent has one page: all their emails, calls, cases, visits.
- Problem solved: Follow-up. The MP can prove: "I solved 4,000 citizen cases this year."
- Who uses it + scale: Constituency MP offices across the UK (newer product; older rivals eCasework and CaseworkerMP serve MPs and councillors too).
- Business model: Monthly software subscription per office.
- Founding story: Built by a UK product designer (Ben Dunn Flores) specifically for MP offices.
- SPOKEN LINES:
  - "In England, when a citizen complains to his MP, the complaint gets a case number and a deadline. The citizen gets updates until it is solved."
  - "The MP shows in elections: I personally solved 4,000 cases. With proof."
  - Marathi hint: "इंग्लंडमध्ये खासदाराकडची प्रत्येक तक्रार म्हणजे एक केस नंबर, एक डेडलाईन."

### 4. Polimorphic - USA - the AI story (fits your agency intro)
- What it does: AI "front desk" for government: AI answers citizen phone calls, chats, SMS, and emails 24/7 in 75+ languages, files their requests, and updates them on status.
- Problem solved: Citizens want answers at night, in their language, instantly. Staff cannot scale; AI can.
- Who uses it + scale: Local governments across the US.
- Business model: Software subscription. Raised $18.6M Series A in July 2025 from General Catalyst ($28M total).
- Founding story: Young AI startup; investors bet big money that AI will run citizen service.
- SPOKEN LINES:
  - "Sir, the newest wave: American investors gave 18 million dollars in 2025 to a company whose AI receptionist answers citizens 24 hours, in 75 languages."
  - "This is exactly my agency's work: bringing AI into daily operations. I want to bring this to your office first in Maharashtra."
  - Marathi hint: "AI आता सरकारी कामातही आलं आहे - २४ तास, ७५ भाषांमध्ये."

## RECOGNISE THE NAMES (one line each)

5. Fireside (USA): The other big US Congress CRM - citizen database + grievance tracking + newsletters + leader's website in one; founded 2001 by former political staffers, now part of FiscalNote.
6. eCasework (UK): Cloud casework tool for councillors, MPs, MSPs and unions - collect, track, and resolve citizen cases from phone or computer; used by councils like Lambeth and Rotherham.
7. CaseworkerMP (UK): Casework system widely used in UK MP offices; has official training courses - shows how standard this work is there.
8. SeeClickFix 311 (USA, by CivicPlus): App where citizens photo-report potholes, garbage, streetlights; city staff track every report to completion on a map.
9. Granicus govDelivery (USA/UK): Government mass-communication platform - 4,200+ agencies, 300M+ subscribers; how governments send updates to citizens at scale.
10. Citibot (USA): AI chatbot on SMS/web - citizens text their city a question or complaint, AI answers instantly from official information (since 2016).

## INDIA SECTION - ammo for "this exists in India too"

11. Saral app (BJP internal, by Jarvis Consulting): Database of ~60 lakh booth workers, AI calls daily, booths classified safe/battleground/difficult. WEAKNESS: party property - an individual MLA cannot buy it, and it manages workers, not citizen grievances.
12. Leadtech (Delhi): Sells constituency management software to MLAs since 2008, claims 1500+ political clients. WEAKNESS: software-only sold from Delhi; the MLA's own staff must run it, so it mostly dies unused. No done-for-you service, no Marathi-first WhatsApp workflow.
13. I-PAC type consultancies: Full campaign war-rooms. WEAKNESS: come only at election time, charge in crores, leave after results. Nothing year-round for one MLA office.
14. Aaple Sarkar / CPGRAMS (government portals): Maharashtra's own grievance portal - citizen gets a token number, 21-working-day deadline, escalation if dissatisfied. WEAKNESS/OPPORTUNITY: this exists for the ADMINISTRATION. The MLA's office has nothing like it. Citizens who come to the MLA get no token, no tracking, no deadline.

### Your killer line (memorise):
"Sir, Maharashtra government already gives citizens a token number and a 21-day deadline on Aaple Sarkar. But when a citizen comes to YOUR office, he gets nothing - no number, no tracking, no proof of your work. In America and England, the leader's own office has this system. I will build it for you: in Marathi, on WhatsApp, and my team runs it - your staff does not need to learn anything."

### Your honest agency line (since the US client claim is only partly true):
"I run an agency that helps businesses adopt AI in their daily operations. The same class of work is done in the US for politicians by companies like Indigov and Polimorphic - I have studied their systems deeply, and I build the same class of solutions here."
(Do NOT name a US political client you don't have. One counter-question destroys trust.)

## COMEBACK-MACHINE REFERENCES (products already in the market - use for trust)

These prove to him: "this kind of system already exists, serious people pay for it."

### India - karyakarta / campaign products (proof the market is real)
15. Smart Neta (Bengaluru): Election management platform, claims 100+ elections managed across Karnataka, Goa, AP, Telangana, Odisha, TN. Karyakarta mobile app with geo-tagged tasks, voter surveys, real-time worker tracking - for MLA/MP/mayor/local body candidates. Closest India proof that individual candidates buy worker-tracking software.
16. Ran-Neeti: Web+app campaign module with a dedicated karyakarta management feature - two-way communication party office <-> karyakarta <-> voter.
17. Nagrik Political Suite: "Political back office management" - same back-office concept.
18. Voter Software Pro / Prasaar: Voter list + booth agent + karyakarta tracking apps sold to campaign teams.
19. Saral (BJP internal, repeat from #11): THE best trust reference: "Sir, BJP already runs exactly this - 60 lakh workers tracked daily, AI calls, every booth classified. That machine is why they win. You cannot buy Saral. I am building your own version of it."

### International - worker/supporter machines
20. NationBuilder (USA, global): Supporter + volunteer + voter database used by thousands of campaigns in many countries.
21. Ecanvasser (Ireland, global): Door-to-door canvassing + volunteer tracking, mobile apps in 13 languages, used worldwide. Proof this is a global product category.

### WARNING - direct rival (do NOT name him this in the meeting)
- Pollity (pollity.in): "Digital Chief of Staff" for MPs/MLAs - grievance tracking, MPLADS/MLALADS fund dashboards, staff coordination, auto social content. Was stated to launch Q3 2026 (this quarter). BUT: it targets SITTING MPs/MLAs (fund dashboards are useless to a lost candidate). Your window: move now, and own the out-of-power segment they ignore.

### The white-space line (your strongest trust argument):
"Sir, look at the whole world: America's IQ and Indigov serve SITTING leaders. India's Smart Neta and Leadtech sell ELECTION-SEASON tools. BJP's Saral is party property. Not one product in the world is built for the leader who LOST and is fighting to come back. That leader needs it most - 5 years of keeping workers together and citizens remembering him. That is what I am building, and you will be the first in India to have it."
