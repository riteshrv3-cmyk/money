# Idea 3: AI interview software for Indian IT/startups (B2B)

Full sourced raw research: [research/raw/ai-interview.md](raw/ai-interview.md). Day 2 adversarial refutation applied below.

## Scores (frozen rubric)

**Ranking A — speed to first ₹1: 2.25 / 5**
Lowest of the four. No personal connection to any buyer, cold B2B outreach required, and even "founder-led" startups often route hiring decisions through a small buying committee, not one person who can say yes on the spot.

**Ranking B — ceiling: 4.25 / 5**
Highest ceiling score of the four — but the ceiling is highest precisely *because* the market is already large and proven, which is also what makes it hardest to enter.

## What survived adversarial refutation

- **Funded, revenue-proven incumbents already occupy every price tier.** Confirmed on all 3 adversarial lenses — this is the strongest, cleanest "survived" verdict in the entire research round. BarRaiser (~$9.4M ARR, bootstrapped), InCruiter ($1M→$2.5M FY26), Karat ($73.6M ARR, $1.1B valuation), Mercor (~$2B ARR run-rate, in talks for $20B). One refutation lens noted this hits a zero-capital solo entrant *hardest*, not softest — no budget for the sales motion, brand trust, or enterprise distribution needed to unseat any of them.
- **Candidates dislike AI-first-round interviews and some abandon applications over it (31.4% in a 1,066-person US survey).** Confirmed on source-quality and logical-consistency, but flagged as *demand-side friction relevant to a different buyer* — a solo founder's actual customer is the employer buying the tool, not the candidate being screened, so this doesn't validate demand for a new entrant.

## What got knocked down

- **"70% of AI startups build in saturated categories, interview tools among them"** — refuted on all 3 lenses. The source is a generic, uncited startup-advice blog; it names *resume builders* as an example category, not interview tools, and the 70% figure has no traceable methodology. Drop this claim entirely.
- **"India's screening burden is rising (18→24 interviews/hire, +30% experience bar)"** — refuted on all 3 lenses. The cited HireDoor report does not actually contain these figures when checked directly, and HireDoor is a vendor selling into this exact market — a direct conflict of interest with no independent corroboration found.

Net effect: removing the two weak "there's growing pain" claims leaves the idea resting almost entirely on the one claim that survived cleanly — and that claim cuts against the idea, not for it.

## Fatal flaw

**This is the most saturated of the four ideas, and the saturation claim is the best-evidenced fact in the whole research round.** A zero-capital solo founder would be entering a category where the cheapest credible competitor already has $9.4M ARR and the most expensive has revenue 1,000x that. There is no visible wedge — no under-served price tier, no under-served geography, no under-served company size — that the research surfaced.
