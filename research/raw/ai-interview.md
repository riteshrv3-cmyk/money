# Raw research: AI interview software for Indian IT/startups (B2B)

Day 1 discovery, Aug 2026. Source: Workflow `four-ideas-discovery`, agents `ai-interview:market-numbers`, `ai-interview:complaints`, `ai-interview:founder-stories`.

## Lens A — Market numbers

- **BarRaiser** (India's leading "interview-as-a-service"): 400,000+ interviews, 500+ companies, bootstrapped, 2025 revenue ~$9.4M ARR, ~$28.1M valuation, 85 employees, founded 2020. — [getlatka.com](https://getlatka.com/companies/barraiser.com#funding) (medium, revenue-tracker DB not company-disclosed)
- InCruiter: no public company-side pricing (sales call required); pays ~₹600/interview to its freelance interviewer network. — [g2.com](https://www.g2.com/products/incruiter/pricing) (medium)
- Intervue.io: live-engineer (not fully autonomous AI) technical interviews, ₹2,000-3,000/interview, annual contracts with volume discounts, 2 free trial credits. — [intervue.io](https://www.intervue.io/pricing) (high)
- Interviewer.AI: ₹1,000-2,900/interview. — [thehirehub.ai](https://www.thehirehub.ai/blog/ai-interview-platforms-india) (medium)
- HireVue: no public pricing; India estimates ₹1,650-4,150+/interview; Essentials tier ~$35,000/year for 2,500-7,500 employee orgs, realistic all-in first-year cost $50,000-120,000; limited Indian regional-language support, struggles below 4 Mbps. — [thehirehub.ai](https://www.thehirehub.ai/blog/ai-interview-platforms-india-2026) (medium)
- **Mercor** (US, not India-first-round-specific): $100M Series B Feb 2025 at $2B; $350M Series C Oct 2025 at $10B; in talks for $20B valuation Jul 2026; ARR run-rate ~$2B, doubled in 4 months; reportedly FCF-profitable in early 2026; also faced a 2025-26 data breach and contractor lawsuits. — [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/mercor-is-in-talks-for-a-20b-valuation/) (high)
- New 2025-vintage AI-native first-round entrants: uRecruits, Peoplebox.ai "Nova," Cutshort, TheHireHub (Hindi/regional focus), Micro1. — [prnewswire.com](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/urecruits-takes-scheduling-and-first-round-interviews-off-recruiters-plates-as-interview-scheduling-and-ai-pre-screening-agents-go-live-302758777.html) (medium, vendor PR)
- Strella ($18M raised, 2024 entrant) cited alongside Outset/Discuss.io as evidence the space has become a "crowded field" by 2026. — [userintuition.ai](https://www.userintuition.ai/posts/best-ai-interview-platforms-2026-comparison/) (low, competitor-comparison blog)
- 70%+ of 2026 AI-startup founders build in already-saturated categories (resume builders cited as one example); saturation still leaves room in narrow vertical niches. — [preuve.ai](https://preuve.ai/blog/what-is-a-saturated-market) (low, generic advice blog)
- India IT job market grew 16% in 2025, 1.8M total roles. — [angelone.in](https://www.angelone.in/news/economy/india-s-it-job-market-grows-by-16-in-2025-quess-corp) (medium)
- India hiring rose 15% in 2025 per FOUNDIT tracker. — [news.careers360.com](https://news.careers360.com/india-job-market-2025-ai-hiring-growth-foundit-insights-tracker-report) (medium)
- Interviews-per-hire in India rose 18→24 over 2025; experience bar in postings up ~30% — screening burden is rising, not falling. — [hiredoor.in](https://www.hiredoor.in/blog/state-of-tech-hiring-india-july-2026) (medium, vendor report)
- AI-linked job postings: 2.9 lakh in 2025, projected 3.8 lakh (+32%) in 2026. — [taggd.in](https://taggd.in/blogs/it-hiring-trends/) (low)
- 65% of startups have no dedicated HR department — founders are often both buyer and user of hiring tools, meaning the buyer may be a time-poor founder, not a TA/HR head. — [althire.ai](https://althire.ai/feeds/blog/top-recruiting-software-startups) (low, no methodology shown)
- Vendor-documented buyer personas split into HR leaders (growth startups), TA heads (enterprise), campus-placement managers — enterprise sales requires navigating a buying committee. — [zavnia.com](https://zavnia.com/blog/best-ai-interview-software-for-startups) (low)

## Lens B — Complaints

- Major Indian campus drives (TCS, Infosys) pull 50,000-80,000 applications per role. — [thepeoplesboard.com](https://www.thepeoplesboard.com/hr-technology/ai-resume-screening-india-audit-guide/) (medium)
- A single Flipkart category-manager opening drew 4,000 resumes in a week. — same source (medium)
- ~70% of Indian HR/business leaders use generative AI in some capacity (reporting, learning, recruiting). — same source (medium)
- 37% of orgs "actively integrating" or "experimenting" with GenAI in recruiting (LinkedIn Future of Recruiting 2025), up from 27% a year earlier. — [hr-brew.com](https://www.hr-brew.com/stories/2026/04/07/overwhelmed-by-applications-recruiters-turn-to-ai-pre-screening-tools-to-winnow-down-applications) (medium)
- Recruiters overwhelmed by hundreds/thousands of applications within a day or two of posting — driving the shift to AI pre-screening. — same source (medium)
- Enhancv survey (1,066 US job seekers): 50.5% received a silent (no-feedback) rejection in the past year; of those, 63.8% believed AI made the decision vs 13.9% human. — [enhancv.com](https://enhancv.com/blog/ai-hiring-statistics/) (high)
- Only 9.7% of candidates were clearly told AI was involved in evaluating them; 68.5% got no disclosure at all. — same source (high)
- **31.4% of job seekers abandoned applications or declined interviews specifically to avoid AI video/chatbot screening**; 79.1% of those abandoned roles paid under $100k. — same source (high)
- 47.7% agreed AI hiring tools are biased against their demographic group; neurodivergent respondents agreed more (53.4% vs 45.8%). — same source (high)
- 49.6% of job seekers used AI themselves to help with AI-screened interviews; 5.6% used AI live during the screening. — same source (high)
- Named candidate (Debra Borchardt) abandoned an AI interview after 3 questions, calling it "added indignity." Another (Allen Rausch) called AI interviews impersonal and "wasting my time." — [techspot.com](https://www.techspot.com/news/108920-ai-interview-bots-spark-backlash-frustrated-job-applicants.html) (high, named quotes)
- A candidate was rejected after the AI bot interviewing him glitched, then sent him someone else's rejection letter. — [yourtango.com](https://www.yourtango.com/self/mans-ai-interviewer-crashes-then-sends-wrong-rejection-letter) (medium)
- A viral incident: an Apriora-powered AI interviewer at Stretch Lab repeated "vertical bar pilates" 14 times in 25 seconds — went viral (6.8M views). — [aol.com](https://www.aol.com/sports/ai-job-interview-spirals-dystopian-214904008.html) (medium)
- ACLU filed a complaint against Intuit/HireVue alleging their AI hiring tech performs worse for deaf and non-white applicants. — [danielphilipjohnson.com](https://danielphilipjohnson.com/blog/my-hirevue-nightmare-a-developers-critique-of-ai-interviews-bias-black-boxes) (medium)

## Lens C — Founder stories

- Mercor founded 2023 by 3 Thiel Fellows (Foody, Hiremath, Midha) who dropped out of Harvard/Georgetown. Crossed $2B annualized revenue June 2026 (doubled from $1B Feb 2026); 400,000+ candidates screened via 20-min AI interview. — [forbes.com](https://www.forbes.com/sites/richardnieva/2026/07/09/mercor-fundraise/), [thefoundernation.com](https://www.thefoundernation.com/mercor-founder-story-how-three-22-year-olds-built-a-10-billion-ai-company-in-two-years/) (high/medium)
- BarRaiser founded by Avinash Saxena (ex-Roposo), raised $4.2M seed (May 2022, $6.2M total); at seed had done 30,000 interviews for 250+ companies via 800+ expert interviewers. — [inc42.com](https://inc42.com/buzz/saas-startup-barraiser-bags-4-2-mn-from-021-capital-gfc-others/) (high)
- Intervue.io founded 2019 by ex-Paytm engineers Rahul Arora + Pushpendra Rautela; ~$301K raised over 2 rounds; Shark Tank India deal Jan 2024 (Aman Gupta, ₹1.5cr for 2%); ~$906.8K ARR at $2.7M valuation (2024). — [getlatka.com](https://getlatka.com/companies/intervue) (low-medium)
- InCruiter founded 2018 (Anil Agarwal + Ritu Mathran); bootstrapped with ~₹20 lakh savings, later ₹20 lakh Startup India grant + ₹1cr revenue-based financing; total raised ~$254K, now in talks for $3-5M; FY25 revenue $1M, FY26 target $2.5M; 3,000+ vetted interviewers, 50+ new AI-interview clients in the past year. — [yourstory.com](https://yourstory.com/2025/08/incruiter-ai-powered-hiring-tools-recruitment), [indianstartupnews.com](https://indianstartupnews.com/funding/hr-tech-startup-incruiter-raises-rs-11m-in-undiluted-revenue-based-funding-from-getvantage-2323005) (medium)
- **Karat** (Mo Bhende ex-Microsoft, Jeff Spector ex-chief-of-staff to Melinda Gates): $110M Series C Oct 2021 at $1.1B valuation ($248M total raised); 2024 est. revenue $73.6M ARR on 600,000+ technical evaluations. — [businesswire.com](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20211013005399/en/Karat-Pioneers-the-Interviewing-Cloud-to-Transform-Technical-Hiring-Secures-$110M-in-Series-C-Funding), [getlatka.com](https://getlatka.com/companies/karat) (high/low)
- AI interview/screening startups selling to enterprises face opaque, resource-intensive sales cycles with buying committees spanning IT/legal/finance/HR, plus the burden of educating uncertain buyers. — [aifund.ai](https://aifund.ai/insights/insights-enterprise-sales/) (low)
