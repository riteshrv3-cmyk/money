# Raw research: Real estate agent, online-only, no network, no capital

Day 1 discovery, Aug 2026. Source: Workflow `four-ideas-discovery`, agents `real-estate:market-numbers`, `real-estate:complaints` (+round2), `real-estate:founder-stories`.

## Lens A — Market numbers

- No documented case of a fully online, zero-network, remote-only solo agent operating across multiple cities in India was found. Independent brokers do run mostly on WhatsApp (20-30 enquiries handled without an office). — [qvoo.io](https://qvoo.io/whatsapp-business-for-real-estate/) (low confidence)
- RERA agent registration is legally mandatory under Section 9 of the RERA Act, 2016 for any individual facilitating property sale/purchase, including part-time agents. — [legalserviceindia.com](https://www.legalserviceindia.com/Legal-Articles/rera-agent-registration-india-guide/) (high confidence)
- Operating unregistered risks ₹10,000/day penalty (max 5% of transaction value) and up to 1 year imprisonment for unfair trade practices; unregistered agents are excluded from selling RERA-registered projects. — same source (medium confidence)
- Maharashtra RERA agent registration fee: ₹10,590 (some sources say ₹10,000), via MahaRERA portal with PAN, Aadhaar, address proof, 3 years ITR, affidavit. — [registerkaro.in](https://www.registerkaro.in/post/rera-registration-fees) (medium confidence)
- MahaRERA certificate issued within ~30 days, valid 5 years. — [bankbazaar.com](https://www.bankbazaar.com/home-loan/rera-maharashtra.html) (medium confidence)
- Resale commission: 1-2% of sale price (0.5-1.5% for high-value), negotiable, paid by buyer/seller/split. — [mansionia.com](https://mansionia.com/real-estate-agent-commissions/) (medium confidence)
- Rental commission: typically 1 month's rent, usually tenant-paid. — [nobroker.in](https://www.nobroker.in/forum/what-is-real-estate-broker-commission/) (medium confidence, source has commercial bias against brokers)
- Builder/developer project commission: 1-4% of unit price (up to 5% luxury), paid by developer not buyer; channel partners source 40-70% of bookings in a typical project. — [leadrat.com](https://leadrat.com/channel-partners-in-real-estate-a-complete-2026-guide/) (medium confidence)
- Entry-level agent income ₹197,000-308,000/year (~₹16-25k/month) per salary aggregator — conflates salaried roles with independent brokers, not a solo-agent-specific figure. — [in.indeed.com](https://in.indeed.com/career/real-estate-agent/salaries) (low confidence)
- **No India-specific data on first-year income for a zero-network solo online agent exists.** General (US-context) guidance says the first 12-24 months are hardest and success depends heavily on referrals/network — which contradicts the zero-network premise. — [samsawyer.substack.com](https://samsawyer.substack.com/p/newly-licensed-and-lost) (low confidence, US context)
- **NoBroker was built explicitly to eliminate the human agent** (broker-free platform), raised $368M at ~$913M valuation — the largest online platforms are structurally designed to disintermediate, not to route business through solo agents. — [orangeowl.marketing](https://orangeowl.marketing/unicorn-chronicles/nobroker-success-story/) (medium confidence)
- No documented channel-partner/referral program for individual freelance agents on NoBroker, Housing.com or 99acres (unlike builder-side channel partner programs) was found. — [companies.naukri.com](https://companies.naukri.com/infoedge-jobs/real-estate) (low confidence)

## Lens B — Complaints

- Noida agent (Ajai Misra) booked a flat in 2014, developer never paid the 2% commission credit note despite buyer taking possession. — [vidhikarya.com](https://www.vidhikarya.com/FreeLegalAdvice/3899/commission-not-paid-by-real-estate-brokerage-firm) (medium, old case)
- Common scam pattern: agents take token/advance money before showing houses, or show already-rented units as available. — [nrilegalservice.com](https://www.nrilegalservice.com/blog/tips-to-avoid-real-estate-scams-in-india/) (low, pattern description not individual case)
- Pre-RERA, agents commonly misrepresented properties, concealed defects, took undisclosed commissions; buyers had no dedicated fraud forum. — [blog.ipleaders.in](https://blog.ipleaders.in/actions-one-can-take-malpractices-real-estate-agents/) (low, pre-RERA context)
- Builder (Ruparel, 2017) withheld agent's brokerage for over a year via shifting excuses (fund issues, then RERA licensing, then buyer-broker relative technicality); consumer forums won't hear brokerage disputes, forcing civil suits. — [kaanoon.com](https://www.kaanoon.com/224275/builder-not-paying-brokerage) (medium)
- In primary-market deals, buyers increasingly bypass agents entirely and deal directly with builders using only a lawyer for due diligence, since the builder pays the broker (not the buyer) — no financial reason for a buyer to insist on an agent. — [teamblind.com](https://www.teamblind.com/post/use-agent-or-buy-directly-for-house-in-bangalore-do8torvf) (low)

## Lens C — Founder stories

- Housing.com tried to reduce broker reliance via direct listings, grew 300%/month at peak, but failed standalone and merged with PropTiger/Elara in 2017. — [mayurpanchal.substack.com](https://mayurpanchal.substack.com/p/case-study-on-housingcom-why-did) (medium)
- Housing.com founder Rahul Yadav was ousted in 2015; valuation reportedly dropped from ₹1500cr to ₹450cr after his exit. — [outlookindia.com](https://outlookindia.com/website/story/high-jinks-at-housingcom/294287) (medium)
- Housing.com actually operated as a two-sided marketplace with ~6,000 brokers across ~40 cities — it never eliminated brokers, undercutting the "zero-network" model. — [markhub24.com](https://www.markhub24.com/post/housing-com-s-revenue-model-in-real-estate) (low)
- NoBroker's pure zero-brokerage listing model did not sustain the business alone: by FY24-25, non-subscription services (~45% of growth) and paid "personal assistant" subscriptions (~40% of revenue) had to be added. — [startuptalky.com](https://startuptalky.com/nobroker-business-model/) (low)
- NoBroker built NoBrokerHood (society management) as a recurring post-transaction revenue layer — a pivot away from pure zero-brokerage listings. — [g2.com](https://www.g2.com/products/nobrokerhood/reviews) (low)
- Zero Brokerage (Mumbai, founded 2004, commission-free model) shows only 1 employee as of 2026 — likely dormant, but no explicit shutdown account found. — [tracxn.com](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/zero-brokerage/__I_zn7Igll9iv98DlENDKHB0AZ5oONuMhGQtFp6Pto84) (low)
- Most Indian developer "channel partner programs" are informal (a phone-number list + unstructured commission promise) — structured/digital distribution is hard to displace because the market runs on informal broker relationships and trust. — [blog.campaignhq.co](https://blog.campaignhq.co/real-estate-channel-partner-automation-india-whatsapp-email-2026) (low)
- No primary founder account (blog/interview/AMA) of someone who built and failed at an online-only, zero-broker-network brokerage in India, with disclosed revenue, was found. — search-coverage gap (low)
