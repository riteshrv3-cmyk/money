# Staffer Call Script (MLA's social media / IT karyakarta)

Situation: referred informally, he is a loyal karyakarta doing IT (trusted man, not technical).
Call: phone/WhatsApp, 2-5 days before the MLA meeting, mixed language.
Goal: learn their current tools & process + make him like you + keep channel open.

## WARNING first: "he is not a threat" is wrong

You chose to treat him as no threat. Push back, with logic:
1. He is a LOYAL karyakarta - the MLA's trusted man. After your meeting, sir will ask him one question: "kay vatla tula?" (what did you think?). His one lukewarm line - "malaa kahi khaas nahi vatla" - can kill your pitch after you leave the room.
2. Your product touches HIS territory (digital work, data, reports). If the system succeeds, either he runs it or he becomes useless. He will calculate this in the first 5 minutes, even if you never say it.
3. Cost of insurance is ONE sentence in the call (given below). Cost of ignoring him is the whole deal.
So: you don't need to fear him, but you must feed him respect and a future role.

## Call structure (12-15 minutes)

### 1. Opening - guidance frame (1 min)
- "Dada, [reference name] ne tumcha number dila. Tumhi sir cha digital sagla sambhalta, tumhala ground samajta. Sir na bhetnyaadhi tumchya kadun samjun ghyaycha hota."
- (Dada, [reference] gave me your number. You handle all of sir's digital work, you understand the ground. Before meeting sir, I wanted to understand things from you.)
- Why it works: a karyakarta rarely gets called "the expert". You just made him senior.

### 2. Intel questions - current tools & process (8 min)
Ask as curiosity, not interview. Let him talk 80%.
1. "Lokanchi kama/takrari kashi yetat tumchyakde? WhatsApp var? Office la?" - How do citizen problems reach you? What happens to them after that?
2. "Kuthe lihun thevta? Excel, vahi, ka app?" - Where is it recorded - Excel, notebook, or any app?
3. "2024 chya voter list, karyakarta list - te sagla kon sambhalta? Kasa?" - Who keeps the voter and worker data, and in what form?
4. "Kadhi konta software/app try kela hota ka? Kay zala?" - Did they ever try any software or vendor? What happened? (Past vendor failures = gold. Whatever killed the last tool will try to kill yours.)
5. "Booth pramukh / karyakartyanshi sampark kasa hoto? Kon active aahe he kasa kalta?" - How do you know which workers are active?
6. "Tumcha divas kasa jato? Sagla tumhi ekta karta ka?" - What does his own day look like, is he alone doing everything?
Note his answers - each one maps to a module of your product.

### 3. Vague but exciting reveal (2 min)
- "Mi AI chi agency chalavto - company office chi kama AI ne automatic karto. America madhe rajkarnyansathi asha systems aahet - tithla Congressman chya office madhe pratyek takrar number ne track hote. Mala te ithe, Marathi madhe, aplya lokansathi banvaycha aahe. Mhanun sir na bhetnar aahe."
- (I run an AI agency - we automate office work with AI. In America politicians' offices have such systems - every complaint is tracked with a number. I want to build that here, in Marathi, for our people. That is why I am meeting sir.)
- STOP there. No modules, no comeback talk, no pricing. If he asks details: "Sir na bhetun tyanchya priority nusar tharvin. Tumchya aajchya mahitine mala khup madat zali." (I will decide as per sir's priority after meeting him. Your input today helped me a lot.)

### 4. The one-sentence insurance (30 sec) - DO NOT SKIP
- "Ani he jar zala, tar system chalvnara mansus tumhichach asnar - tumhala ground mahiti aahe, mala fakta technology yete."
- (And if this happens, the person running the system will be you - you know the ground, I only know technology.)
- This one line converts the potential enemy into the product's first champion.

### 5. Close - keep the channel open (1 min)
- "Dada, khup shiklo tumchyakadun. Meeting nantar pan tumhala call karen - tumcha experience lagel mala." (Learned a lot from you. I will call you after the meeting too - I will need your experience.)
- Then WhatsApp him the same evening: "Dhanyavad dada, chhan bolna zala. Bhetu lavkarach." Small message, big memory.

## Positive mention to sir - how it happens without asking
You chose not to ask about the MLA meeting. Fine. The positive mention still happens IF: he felt like the expert (opening), he talked 80% (questions), and he sees a role for himself (insurance line). People praise what makes them bigger.

## After the call - 3 things to write down
1. Their current process, tool by tool (this becomes your demo's "before vs after").
2. Any dead vendor story and WHY it died.
3. His personal pain points - the first feature you demo to the MLA should quietly solve HIS pain too.
