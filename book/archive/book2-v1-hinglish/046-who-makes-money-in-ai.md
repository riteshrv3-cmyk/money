# Chapter 7.1  [SPINE]
## AI industry mein paisa kaun kamata hai

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Book 1 Chapter 6.9 ka niyam bolo.

**Kabir:** Paisa neeche se aata hai, jab ek insaan apni zaroorat ke liye deta hai. Aur woh wahan rukta hai jahan sabse kam vikalp hain.

**Madhav:** Ab AI pe lagao. Neeche se upar chalo. Sabse neeche kya hai?

**Kabir:** Chip. Woh machinein jinpe yeh sab chalta hai.

**Madhav:** Uske vikalp kitne hain?

**Kabir:** Bahut kam. Aur unhe banane wale bhi bahut kam.

**Madhav:** Toh wahan paisa rukega ya nikal jaayega?

**Kabir:** Rukega.

**Madhav:** Uske upar?

**Kabir:** Woh jagah jahan machinein rakhi hain. Cloud.

**Madhav:** Uske vikalp?

**Kabir:** Kuch badi companies. Toh wahan bhi paisa rukega, lekin kam.

**Madhav:** Uske upar?

**Kabir:** Model banane wale.

**Madhav:** Unke vikalp?

**Kabir:** Kuch companies hain jo bade model bana sakti hain. Toh scarcity hai.

**Madhav:** Achha. Ab ek problem. Kuch models muft mein bant rahe hain. Chapter 2.9. Toh?

**Kabir:** Toh unka daam gir raha hai.

**Madhav:** Aur agar do models lagbhag ek jaise kaam karein, ek mehnga ek sasta?

**Kabir:** Log sasta lenge.

**Madhav:** Toh us parat pe kya ho raha hai?

**Kabir:** Vikalp badh rahe hain. Toh scarcity gir rahi hai.

**Madhav:** Aur jab scarcity girti hai toh?

**Kabir:** Paisa wahan rukna band ho jaata hai. Woh aage nikal jaata hai.

**Madhav:** Kahan?

**Kabir:** Ya toh neeche, chip wale ke paas. Ya upar.

**Madhav:** Upar kya hai?

**Kabir:** Woh products jo model pe khade hain.

**Madhav:** Unke vikalp?

**Kabir:** Bahut. Koi bhi bana sakta hai.

**Madhav:** Toh wahan paisa rukega?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Jab tak unke paas kuch aur na ho.

**Madhav:** Aur "kuch aur" kya ho sakta hai?

**Kabir:** Unka apna data. Ya unke users ka jaal. Ya woh cheezein jinse nikalna mushkil ho.

**Madhav:** Bas. Ab poori tasveer bolo.

**Kabir:** Paisa un do jagahon pe ruk raha hai jahan vikalp kam hain: sabse neeche chip, aur sabse upar woh products jinke paas apna kuch hai. Beech ki parat nichudti ja rahi hai.

### Naam

Poori parat, neeche se upar, aur har ek pe wahi sawal: **iske kitne vikalp hain?**

```
CHIP                  vikalp bahut kam       →  paisa rukta hai
                      banane wale gine-chune

BIJLI + JAGAH         seemit, aur badhana dheema   →  rukta hai
                      (yeh 2024 ke baad se ek asli seema ban gayi)

CLOUD                 kuch badi companies    →  thoda rukta hai

MODEL                 kuch companies, lekin  →  gir raha hai
                      muft models aa rahe hain

APP / PRODUCT         koi bhi bana sakta hai →  nahi rukta
                      jab tak uske paas kuch aur na ho
```

Aur woh "kuch aur," jo product wali parat pe paisa rok sakta hai:

```
APNA DATA        jo kisi aur ke paas nahi hai   (Ch 2.6, 6.6)
USERS KA JAAL    log wahan hain, isliye log wahan hain
                 (Book 1, Ch 4.1)
KAAM MEIN GHUSA  aapke roz ke kaam ka hissa ban gaya,
                 nikalna mehnga (Book 1, Ch 6.5)
BHAROSA          jahan galti ki keemat zyada hai
```

Ab teen baatein jo iss tasveer se nikalti hain.

**Ek: model wali parat sabse zyada charcha mein hai aur wahan paisa sabse tezi se gir raha hai.**

Kyunki teen cheezein ek saath ho rahi hain: models muft bant rahe hain, alag models lagbhag ek jaise kaam kar rahe hain, aur daam har saal tezi se gir raha hai.

Jo cheez badalne laayak aur sasti ho jaati hai, uspe paisa nahi rukta. **Yeh Book 1 Chapter 6.9 hai, bina kisi badlav ke.**

**Do: sabse neeche wali parat sabse mazboot hai.**

Chip banana mushkil hai, mehnga hai, aur usmein saalon lagte hain. Aur upar chahe kuch bhi ho, sabko chip chahiye.

**Teen: data ab ek scarce cheez ban rahi hai.**

Shuruaat mein woh muft tha. Ab aasan data khatam ho raha hai, log apna data band kar rahe hain, aur AI ka likha data mila hua hai (Ch 2.7).

Toh woh data jo kisi ek jagah ikattha hai, aur pakka insaan ka likha hai, woh mehnga hota ja raha hai.

**Jo cheez muft se scarce banti hai, uske maalik ki taakat achanak badh jaati hai.**

### Asli duniya se

2023 se 2026 ke beech, ek hi kaam ka daam har saal tezi se gira. Jo kaam pehle mehnga tha, woh ab uska ek chhota hissa hai.

Aur usi daur mein AI chips banane wali company duniya ki sabse badi companies mein aa gayi.

Dono baatein ek saath sach hain, aur woh ek hi tasveer hain: **beech ki parat sasti hui, aur neeche wali parat mehngi.**

Ab uska aapke liye matlab: agar aap kuch bana rahe ho jo model pe khada hai, toh aapka kharcha har saal girega. Yeh achhi khabar hai.

Aur uske saath: aapka product bhi utna hi aasan hoga banane mein, sabke liye. **Jo cheez aapke liye sasti hui, woh aapke mukable ke liye bhi sasti hui.**

Toh sawal wahi purana reh jaata hai: **aapke paas aisa kya hai jo kisi aur ke paas nahi?**

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **yeh sochna ki AI ka paisa model banane mein hai.**

Model banana bahut mehnga hai aur usmein rukta bahut kam hai, kyunki uska daam gir raha hai aur uske vikalp badh rahe hain.

Doosri galti: **yeh sochna ki "AI wrapper" ek gaali hai.**

Log kehte hain ki jo product model pe khada hai woh "sirf ek wrapper" hai.

Book 1 Chapter 2.9 se: **product ka bada hissa model ke bahar hota hai.** Kaunsa data, kaunse tools, kaunsi jaanch, kaunsa anubhav.

Sahi sawal yeh nahi hai ki woh wrapper hai ya nahi. Sahi sawal wahi purana hai: **iske kitne vikalp hain?**

Ek wrapper jiske paas apna data hai aur jo kisi ke roz ke kaam mein ghus gaya hai, woh mazboot hai. Ek wrapper jise koi ek hafte mein bana sakta hai, woh nahi.

Teesri galti: **yeh maan lena ki aaj ki tasveer kal bhi wahi rahegi.**

Do saal pehle sabse badi scarcity model mein thi. Aaj woh chip aur bijli mein hai. Do saal baad woh kahin aur ho sakti hai.

**Sawal sthir hai, jawab nahi.**

### Sochne ke liye

**1. (samajh check)** Aap ek AI product banate ho. Ek saal mein model ka daam paanch guna sasta ho jaata hai. Aapke liye achha ya bura?

> **Jawab:** Dono, aur doosra hissa log bhool jaate hain.
>
> **Achha:** aapka kharcha gira. Jo cheezein pehle mehngi thin, ab ban sakti hain.
>
> **Bura:** aapke mukable ke liye bhi gira. Aur woh cheez jo pehle sirf aap kar sakte the kyunki mehngi thi, ab koi bhi kar sakta hai.
>
> **Jo cheez sabke liye sasti ho jaati hai, woh kisi ki bhi bacha nahi hoti.** Aapka bachaav us cheez mein hona chahiye jo sasti nahi hui.

**2. (samajh check)** Do AI products hain. Ek mein log apna data daalte hain aur woh wahan jama hota hai. Doosra har baar naya kaam karta hai. Kaunsa mazboot hai?

> **Jawab:** Pehla, aur farak waqt ke saath badhta hai.
>
> Pehle mein har din jama hone wala data ek deewar ban raha hai. Nikalne ki keemat badh rahi hai, aur jo jama hua woh kisi aur ke paas nahi hai.
>
> Doosre mein har din shuruaat se shuru hota hai. Koi jama nahi ho raha.
>
> **Yeh Book 1 Ch 0.2 ka leverage hai: kya aapka kaam jama hota hai, ya har baar khatam ho jaata hai?** Ravi aur Anil, ab AI products mein.

**3. (jodne wala)** Book 1 Ch 3.6 mein tha ki companies apni cheezein muft kyun deti hain. AI models pe woh kaise lagta hai?

> **Jawab:** Wahi teesri wajah, sabse bade paimane pe: **neeche wali parat pe kabza.**
>
> Agar aapka model muft hai aur sab uspe cheezein banate hain, toh woh sabki neev ban jaata hai. Aur jo neev banata hai, woh uske aas-paas ki har cheez ki shakal tay karta hai.
>
> Aur wahi keemat bhi hai: aapne apni sabse mehngi cheez de di, aur ab paisa kahin aur se banana padega. Cloud se, ya product se, ya us data se jo aapke paas aata hai.
>
> **Jo muft de raha hai, uska ek plan hai. Woh plan dhoondhna aapka kaam hai.**
