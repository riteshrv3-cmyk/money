# Chapter 1.3  [SPINE]
## Sambhavna matlab kya

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Aage badhne se pehle ek cheez chahiye jiske bina iss kitaab ka aadha hissa samajh nahi aayega. Ek sikka uchaalta hoon. Kya aayega?

**Kabir:** Pata nahi.

**Madhav:** Bilkul sahi jawab. Ab sau baar uchaalun toh?

**Kabir:** Lagbhag pachas heads.

**Madhav:** Ab dhyaan do ki kya hua. Ek baar ke baare mein aap kuch nahi keh sakte, aur sau baar ke baare mein aap kaafi kuch keh sakte ho.

**Kabir:** Haan.

**Madhav:** **Sambhavna ek baar ke baare mein kuch nahi kehti. Woh bahut baar ke baare mein kehti hai.** Yeh sabse zaroori line hai iss chapter mein.

**Kabir:** Theek hai.

**Madhav:** Ab ek mausam wala. "Kal 70 percent baarish ka chance hai." Iska kya matlab hai?

**Kabir:** Ki baarish hone waali hai.

**Madhav:** Aur agar baarish na ho? Kya mausam wala galat tha?

**Kabir:** Haan.

**Madhav:** Nahi. Aur yeh samajhna zaroori hai. Woh yeh keh raha hai ki **jitne bhi din aise dikhte hain, unmein se lagbhag 70 mein baarish hoti hai.** Aaj un baaki tees mein se ek ho sakta hai.

**Kabir:** Toh use galat kaise saabit karenge?

**Madhav:** Ek din se kabhi nahi. Sau din lo jinke liye usne 70 percent kaha tha. Agar unmein se lagbhag 70 mein baarish hui, toh woh sahi tha. Agar 20 mein hui, toh galat.

**Kabir:** Toh ek jawab ko sahi ya galat keh hi nahi sakte.

**Madhav:** Bilkul nahi keh sakte. Aur yahi wajah hai ki log AI ke baare mein galat baatein karte hain, dono taraf. Ek galat jawab dikha kar kehte hain ki "yeh bekaar hai," aur ek achha jawab dikha kar kehte hain ki "yeh kamaal hai." Dono ek baar ki baat kar rahe hain.

**Kabir:** Aur ek baar kuch nahi batati.

**Madhav:** Ab teesra sawal, aur yeh sabse kaam ka hai. Do mausam wale hain. Pehla har din kehta hai "50 percent." Doosra kabhi 90 kehta hai, kabhi 10. Saal ke aakhir mein dono lagbhag utni hi baar sahi nikle. Kaun behtar hai?

**Kabir:** Doosra.

**Madhav:** Kyun?

**Kabir:** Kyunki pehle wale ne kabhi kuch kaha hi nahi. "50 percent" ka matlab hai "pata nahi."

**Madhav:** Bas. **Ek jawab jo kabhi galat nahi ho sakta, woh kabhi kaam ka bhi nahi hota.** Jo cheez kuch kehti hai, woh galat bhi ho sakti hai.

**Kabir:** Toh galti ka hona ek nishaani hai ki woh kuch keh raha hai.

**Madhav:** Aur ab aakhri. Ek thaila hai jismein 90 laal aur 10 neeli gendein hain. Aankh band karke ek nikaalta hoon. Kaunsi hogi?

**Kabir:** Shayad laal.

**Madhav:** Main aapko majboor karta hoon ki ek hi jawab do. Kya kahoge?

**Kabir:** Laal.

**Madhav:** Aur agar main aapse sau baar poochun, har baar thaila wahi rakh kar?

**Kabir:** Har baar laal kahunga.

**Madhav:** Toh aap sau mein se dus baar galat hoge, jaan-boojh ke, aur woh phir bhi sabse achha kaam hai jo aap kar sakte the.

**Kabir:** Haan, kyunki koi bhi doosra jawab zyada baar galat hota.

**Madhav:** Iss line ko yaad rakhna. Aage aap dekhoge ki model bilkul yehi karta hai, har shabd pe.

### Naam

**Sambhavna** yeh naapti hai ki bahut baar mein se kitni baar kuch hota hai. Woh kabhi ek baar ke baare mein nahi hoti.

Teen baatein jo aage har jagah kaam aayengi:

```
1.  EK BAAR KUCH NAHI BATATI
    ek galat jawab kuch saabit nahi karta
    ek sahi jawab bhi kuch saabit nahi karta
    ginti bahut baar se aati hai

2.  JO KUCH KEHTA HAI WOH GALAT BHI HO SAKTA HAI
    "50 percent" hamesha kehna surakshit hai aur bekaar hai
    upyogita aur galti ek hi cheez se aati hain

3.  SABSE ACHHA JAWAB BHI AKSAR GALAT HOGA
    90 laal gendein hain toh "laal" sabse achha jawab hai
    aur woh das baar mein ek baar galat hoga
    yeh nakaami nahi hai, yeh hisaab hai
```

Teesri baat sabse zyada mayne rakhti hai, isliye ek naam de deta hoon. Jab koi cheez hamesha sabse zyada sambhavna wala jawab deti hai, toh woh **hamesha kuch galtiyan karegi, aur usse kam galtiyan koi bhi tareeka nahi kar sakta.**

Yeh Chapter 5.1 mein wapas aayega, jab hum poochenge ki model cheezein bana kyun leta hai. Uska aadha jawab yahin likha hai.

### Asli duniya se

Mausam ke andaaze ko iss tarah naapa jaata hai: un sab dinon ko ikattha karo jinke liye "70 percent" kaha gaya tha, aur dekho ki unmein se kitne mein sach mein baarish hui.

Agar lagbhag 70 mein hui, toh woh andaaza **calibrated** hai, yaani uske numbers ka matlab wahi hai jo woh kehte hain.

Aaj ke mausam ke andaaze kaafi calibrated hain. Yeh ek badi baat hai, aur usmein saalon ka kaam laga.

Aur ab dhyaan dene wali baat: **AI models aksar calibrated nahi hote.** Woh apne aatmvishwas ko us tarah nahi naapte. Ek model jo 95 percent bhare andaz mein kuch kehta hai, woh 95 baar mein 95 sahi nahi hota. Woh 70 sahi ho sakta hai, ya 99.

Isiliye uska bhara hua andaz sachai ka naap nahi hai. Woh sirf uske likhne ka tareeka hai.

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **ek ghatna se nateeja nikaal lena.**

"Maine ek baar poocha, jawab galat tha, yeh bekaar hai." Ya "maine ek baar poocha, jawab kamaal tha, yeh sab kuch kar sakta hai."

Dono ek hi galti hain. Ek uchaal se sikke ke baare mein kuch pata nahi chalta.

Iska seedha nateeja: log AI ke baare mein apni raay ek ya do anubhavon pe bana lete hain, aur phir woh raay saalon chalti hai.

Sahi tareeka: **wahi kaam bees baar karo, aur ginti karo.** Kitni baar sahi, kitni baar galat, aur galat hone pe kitna bura tha. Uske baad aapke paas ek asli naap hoga, aur woh naap us kaam ke liye hoga jo aap sach mein karte ho, kisi aur ke test ke liye nahi.

Doosri galti: **"chance" ko waada samajhna.** 90 percent ka matlab hai ki dus mein se ek baar woh nahi hoga. Agar dus mein se ek baar ka nuksaan bardaasht nahi hai, toh 90 percent kaafi nahi hai, chahe woh number kitna bhi bada dikhe.

### Sochne ke liye

**1. (samajh check)** Ek doctor kehta hai "iss operation mein 95 percent safalta hai." Aapka operation fail ho jaata hai. Kya doctor jhooth bol raha tha?

> **Jawab:** Nahi. Aap un paanch mein se ek the.
>
> 95 percent ka matlab hi yeh hai ki sau mein se paanch mein aisa hoga. Aapke saath jo hua woh us number ko galat nahi karta.
>
> Number ko galat karne ka ek hi tareeka hai: sau operation dekho aur ginti karo. Agar bees fail hue, toh number galat tha.
>
> Yeh baat thandi lagti hai aur woh zaroori hai. **Ek ghatna se kabhi bhi kisi sambhavna ke baare mein kuch saabit nahi hota.**

**2. (samajh check)** Do AI models hain. Ek kabhi "mujhe nahi pata" nahi kehta. Doosra kabhi kabhi keh deta hai. Kaunsa behtar hai?

> **Jawab:** Zyadatar kaamon ke liye doosra, aur wajah wahi hai jo dono mausam walon mein thi.
>
> Jo cheez kabhi haar nahi maanti, woh aapko kabhi nahi bata sakti ki kab uspe bharosa na karo. Uska har jawab ek jaisa dikhta hai, sahi ho ya galat.
>
> Ek aisi cheez jo kabhi kabhi ruk jaati hai, aapko ek aur jaankari deti hai: uska rukna khud ek sanket hai.
>
> Yeh Chapter 5.4 mein poora khulega, jahan hum poochenge ki jawab sahi hai ya nahi, yeh kaise check karein.

**3. (jodne wala)** Madhav ne kaha ki "90 laal gendein hain toh hamesha laal kehna sabse achha hai, aur woh dus mein se ek baar galat hoga." Iska AI se kya rishta hai?

> **Jawab:** Woh seedha hai, aur woh agle chapter ka aadha hissa hai.
>
> Model har jagah wahi kar raha hai: har tukde pe woh dekhta hai ki yahan sabse zyada sambhavna kis cheez ki hai, aur wahi chunta hai.
>
> Isliye woh aksar sahi hota hai, kyunki sabse zyada sambhavna wala jawab aksar sahi hota hai.
>
> Aur isliye woh kabhi kabhi galat hota hai, aur uss galti ko hataya nahi ja sakta. Woh us tareeke ke andar hi hai.
