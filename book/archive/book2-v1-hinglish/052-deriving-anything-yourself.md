# Chapter 7.7  [SPINE]
## Kisi bhi nayi cheez ko khud derive kaise karein

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Do kitaabein khatam hone wali hain. Ek aakhri cheez deni hai, aur woh technology ki nahi hai. Batao ki iss poori kitaab mein humne kya kiya?

**Kabir:** Aapne sawal poochhe. Maine jawab nikaale.

**Madhav:** Kaunsa tareeka baar baar chala?

**Kabir:** Aap pehle problem dete the. Phir poochte the ki main kya karta.

**Madhav:** Aur naam kab aata tha?

**Kabir:** Aakhir mein. Jab cheez ban chuki hoti thi.

**Madhav:** Kyun aakhir mein?

**Kabir:** Kyunki naam pehle aata toh main use yaad karta. Aakhir mein aaya toh woh us cheez ka label ban gaya jo maine banayi thi.

**Madhav:** Ab ek aur cheez. Har baar jab aap ek jawab nikaal lete the, main kya poochta tha?

**Kabir:** "Aur uski keemat kya hai."

**Madhav:** Kyun?

**Kabir:** Kyunki kuch bhi muft nahi milta.

**Madhav:** Aur teesra? Jab aap koi jawab nikaal lete the?

**Kabir:** Aap use todne ki koshish karte the.

**Madhav:** Ab yeh sochiye: kya yeh sirf technology pe chalta hai?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Yeh kisi bhi cheez pe chalega.

**Madhav:** Chalo aazmate hain. Aap ek naye field mein ho jismein aapko kuch nahi pata. Pehla kadam?

**Kabir:** Yeh poochna ki yeh field kis dard ke liye hai.

**Madhav:** Kaise poochoge, jab aapko kuch nahi pata?

**Kabir:** Yeh poochunga ki iske bina log kya karte the.

**Madhav:** Yeh sabse achha sawal hai jo kisi naye field mein poocha ja sakta hai. Doosra kadam?

**Kabir:** Yeh dekhna ki sabse neeche kya hai. Woh cheez jispe baaki sab khada hai.

**Madhav:** Aur agar woh na dikhe?

**Kabir:** Toh poochunga: jab yeh toot ta hai toh kya hota hai. Book 1 Chapter 0.4.

**Madhav:** Teesra?

**Kabir:** Har cheez ka sauda dhoondhna. Kya deti hai, kya leti hai.

**Madhav:** Aur chautha?

**Kabir:** Apne banaye hue jawab ko todne ki koshish karna.

**Madhav:** Aur paanchva, jo iss kitaab mein har part ke aakhir mein tha?

**Kabir:** Naksha banana. Taaki nayi cheezein us par baith sakein.

**Madhav:** Bas. Yeh paanch cheezein hi asli auzaar hain. Baaki sab jo aapne seekha, woh inhi se nikla.

### Paanch kadam

**1. Poocho: iske bina log kya karte the?**

Har cheez kisi dard ke liye bani thi. Agar aap dard nahi dhoondh sakte, toh aap cheez samajh nahi sakte, sirf yaad kar sakte ho.

Aur agar dard mila hi nahi, toh do mein se ek hai: aapne dhoondha nahi, ya wahan dard tha hi nahi aur woh cheez marketing hai.

**2. Sabse neeche kya hai?**

Har cheez kisi parat pe khadi hai. Aur har parat neeche wali ko chhupa deti hai *(Book 1, Ch 0.4)*.

Agar neeche wali parat na dikhe, toh ek hi sawal poochho: **jab yeh toot ta hai toh kya dikhta hai?** Woh hamesha andar ka hissa dikha deta hai.

**3. Har cheez ka sauda kya hai?**

Jo cheez kuch deti hai, woh kuch leti hai. Agar nahi dikh raha toh aapne dhoondha nahi.

```
index    →  padhna tez, likhna dheema
cache    →  tez, purana ho sakta hai
attention→  doori se farak nahi, kaam varg mein
RAG      →  taazi jaankari, aur dhoondhna kamzor ho toh sab kharaab
tools    →  zyada kaam, zyada khatra
```

**4. Apne jawab ko todo.**

Jab aap kisi nateeje pe pahunch jao, toh use maano mat. Use todne ki koshish karo.

Book 1 Chapter 6.9 mein humne apna hi daawa chaar baar todne ki koshish ki thi. Woh bacha, aur usi se woh mazboot hua.

**Jo daawa hamla nahi jhel chuka, woh sirf ek raay hai.**

**5. Naksha banao, list nahi.**

Yeh sabse zaroori hai. Ek list mein sau cheezein hoti hain aur aap unhe bhool jaate ho.

Ek naksha mein jagahein hoti hain. Aur nayi cheez ko yaad nahi karna padta, use bas rakhna padta hai.

**Aur jis din koi cheez kisi jagah pe na baithe, us din rukna. Woh sach mein nayi hai.**

### Asli duniya se

Yeh tareeka nahi banaya gaya. Woh baar baar dobara khoja gaya hai, alag alag jagahon pe, alag naamon se.

Woh hai: cheez ko maan mat lo, use pehle sidhaanton se dobara bana kar dekho.

Aur woh dheema lagta hai. Woh dheema hai, shuruaat mein.

Uska poora faayda uss din milta hai jab kuch naya aata hai. Jisne yaad kiya tha, use naya kuch bhi ek nayi cheez lagti hai. Jisne dhaancha banaya, use woh ek jaani-pehchani cheez ka naya roop lagta hai.

**Aur woh farak har saal badhta hai.**

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **yeh sochna ki yeh sirf technical logon ke liye hai.**

Yeh paanch sawal kisi bhi cheez pe lagte hain: kanoon, dawa, paisa, sarkar, kisi bhi kaarobar pe.

Book 1 Part 0 isi ka saboot tha. Wahan humne technology se pehle paisa, value aur society ko isi tareeke se banaya tha.

Doosri galti: **yeh sochna ki dhaancha banana zyada waqt leta hai.**

Woh shuru mein zyada leta hai aur uske baad har cheez sasti hoti jaati hai *(Book 1, Ch 0.5)*.

Ek saal baad tukde wale ke paas sau alag cheezein hoti hain, jinmein se aadhi bhool chuki hoti hain. Dhaanche wale ke paas ek cheez hoti hai jismein sau jagah hain.

Teesri galti, aur yeh sabse gehri hai: **jawab ko yaad rakhna aur tareeke ko bhool jaana.**

Iss kitaab ke jawab purane ho jaayenge. Book 2 ka bahut kuch do saal mein badal jaayega.

**Yeh paanch kadam purane nahi honge.** Woh hi asli cheez hai jo aapko di ja rahi hai.

### Sochne ke liye

**1. (samajh check)** Aap ek bilkul naye field mein ho: maan lo dawa banane ka udyog. Pehle teen sawal kya poochoge?

> **Jawab:**
>
> **Ek:** iske bina log kya karte the? Yaani ek nayi dawa aane se pehle log kya karte the, aur woh kyun kaafi nahi tha.
>
> **Do:** sabse neeche kya hai? Yaani woh cheez jispe poora udyog khada hai. Shayad jaanch ka tareeka, ya kanoon, ya woh khoj jo ek baar hoti hai aur saalon chalti hai.
>
> **Teen:** har cheez ka sauda kya hai? Tez manzoori aur suraksha ke beech. Daam aur pahunch ke beech.
>
> **Aur dhyaan do: aapne dawa ke baare mein kuch nahi jaana. Aapne yeh jaana ki kya poochna hai.** Aur woh hi asli shuruaat hai.

**2. (samajh check)** Koi aapko ek nayi cheez batata hai aur aap uska dard nahi dhoondh paate. Kya nikalta hai?

> **Jawab:** Do mein se ek.
>
> **Ek:** aapne poori tarah nahi dhoondha. Ek aur sawal poochho: kaun iske liye paisa de raha hai, aur kyun?
>
> **Do:** wahan dard hai hi nahi, aur woh cheez kisi aur wajah se maujood hai. Aksar woh wajah yeh hoti hai ki koi use bech raha hai.
>
> **Yeh sawal ek bahut achha filter hai**, aur woh technology se bahar bhi utna hi kaam karta hai.

**3. (jodne wala)** Do kitaabon mein sabse zyada baar kaunsi baat dohrayi gayi?

> **Jawab:** Shayad yeh: **har cheez ka ek sauda hai, aur agar woh nahi dikh raha toh aapne dhoondha nahi.**
>
> Woh index mein tha, cache mein, taale mein, container mein, attention mein, context mein, tools mein.
>
> Aur uske bilkul paas ek doosri hai: **har cheez kisi dard ka jawab hai.**
>
> Yeh do saath mein ek poora auzaar bante hain. Kisi bhi cheez ke saamne khade ho jao aur do sawal poochho: **yeh kis dard ke liye hai, aur iski keemat kya hai?**
>
> Iske aage lagbhag kuch nahi bachta.
