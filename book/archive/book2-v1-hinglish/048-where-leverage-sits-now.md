# Chapter 7.3  [SPINE]
## Leverage ab kahan baitha hai

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Book 1 Chapter 0.2 mein Anil ne naali khodi thi. Uska leverage kya tha?

**Kabir:** Usne aisa kaam kiya jo uske bina chalta raha.

**Madhav:** Aaj ke zamaane mein woh naali kya hai?

**Kabir:** Software. Ya koi system.

**Madhav:** Aur agar software likhna lagbhag muft ho jaaye?

**Kabir:** Toh naali khodna sasta ho gaya.

**Madhav:** Aur agar naali khodna sabke liye sasta hai, toh naali ka kya mol bacha?

**Kabir:** Kam. Kyunki ab sab khod sakte hain.

**Madhav:** Toh leverage khatam ho gaya?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Woh kahin aur chala gaya hoga.

**Madhav:** Kahan? Sochte hain. Anil ki naali ka mol kis baat mein tha?

**Kabir:** Ki woh chalti rahi.

**Madhav:** Aur kis baat mein?

**Kabir:** Ki gaon ko paani chahiye tha.

**Madhav:** Aur teesri?

**Kabir:** Ki woh khod sakta tha aur baaki nahi.

**Madhav:** Ab teeno pe AI lagao. Kaunsi kamzor hui?

**Kabir:** Teesri. Ab bahut log khod sakte hain.

**Madhav:** Pehli?

**Kabir:** Wahi hai. Cheez ab bhi chalti rehti hai.

**Madhav:** Doosri?

**Kabir:** Woh bhi wahi hai. Gaon ko paani ab bhi chahiye.

**Madhav:** Toh leverage teen mein se do jagah abhi bhi hai.

**Kabir:** Haan. Sirf "main kar sakta hoon" wala hissa sasta hua.

**Madhav:** Aur ab ek nayi baat. Anil ki naali khud badi nahi hoti thi. Kya aisi cheezein hain jo khud badhti hain?

**Kabir:** Network. Book 1 Chapter 4.1. Har naya aane wala use mazboot karta hai.

**Madhav:** Aur?

**Kabir:** Data. Jitna zyada log istemaal karein, utna zyada data jama ho.

**Madhav:** Aur woh data kis kaam aata hai?

**Kabir:** Cheez ko behtar karne. Jo aur log laata hai. Jo aur data laata hai.

**Madhav:** Toh ab do kism ke leverage ho gaye.

**Kabir:** Ek jo bana kar rakh diya jaata hai. Aur ek jo apne aap badhta hai.

**Madhav:** Aur kaunsa mazboot hai jab banana sasta ho gaya ho?

**Kabir:** Doosra. Kyunki pehla koi bhi dobara bana lega.

### Naam

Leverage ke teen darje, sabse kamzor se sabse mazboot:

```
1.  BANAYI HUI CHEEZ (sthir)
    ek baar banao, chalti rehti hai
    ── AI ne ise sasta kar diya, isliye kamzor hua
       kyunki koi bhi dobara bana sakta hai

2.  JAMA HONE WALI CHEEZ
    har din kuch jud raha hai jo kal nahi tha
    data, itihaas, bharosa, aadat
    ── AI ne ise nahi chhua

3.  KHUD BADHNE WALI CHEEZ
    har naya user use sabke liye behtar bana deta hai
    network, aur woh systems jo istemaal se seekhte hain
    ── sabse mazboot, aur sabse mushkil shuru karna
```

Aur ab woh line jo iss chapter ka nichod hai:

> **AI ne pehle darje ka leverage sasta kar diya. Doosre aur teesre ko chhua bhi nahi.**

Isliye jo log pehle darje pe khade the, unki zameen khisak rahi hai. Aur jo doosre ya teesre pe hain, unke liye AI sirf ek sasta auzaar hai.

Ab ek aur farak, jo Book 1 Chapter 6.4 se aata hai aur ab sabse zyada mayne rakhta hai:

```
apni naali            koi aapko hata nahi sakta
kisi aur ki naali se  woh kal band kar sakta hai
paani lena
```

Aaj lagbhag har AI product kisi aur ke model pe khada hai. Woh kisi aur ki naali hai.

Iska matlab yeh nahi ki aisa nahi karna chahiye. Lagbhag sab yehi kar rahe hain, aur usme samajhdaari hai.

Matlab yeh hai ki **aapka leverage us naali mein nahi ho sakta.** Woh us cheez mein hona chahiye jo aapki hai: aapka data, aapke log, aapki jama hui cheez.

### Asli duniya se

2023 se lagbhag har hafte koi AI product aata hai jo kisi bade model ke upar ek achha interface rakhta hai.

Unmein se zyadatar chale nahi. Aur unki wajah lagbhag hamesha ek hi hai: unke paas sirf pehle darje ka leverage tha, jo ab sasta ho chuka hai.

Aur unmein se jo chale, unmein aksar ek hi cheez saanjha thi: unke paas kuch aisa tha jo model se nahi aata tha. Ek khaas data, ek khaas group ke log, ya kisi kaam ki gehri samajh jo bahar se nahi milti.

**Yeh Book 1 Chapter 8.5 hai, bina kisi badlav ke: jeet cheez ki quality se nahi aati.** Aur ab jab cheez banana lagbhag sabke liye mumkin hai, toh woh baat pehle se zyada sach hai.

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **yeh sochna ki AI aane se leverage badh gaya.**

Aapka leverage badha, aur sabka badha. Jab sabka badhta hai toh kisi ka nahi badhta, us rishte mein jo mayne rakhta hai.

Doosri galti: **pehle darje ke leverage ko abhi bhi bachaav samajhna.**

"Maine yeh bana liya hai" ab ek chhota bachaav hai. Woh do saal pehle bada tha.

Teesri galti, aur yeh sabse mehngi hai: **kisi aur ki naali pe apna poora ghar bana lena.**

Agar aapka poora product ek company ke model, unke daam, aur unke niyam pe khada hai, toh aapka leverage unka hai, aapka nahi. Book 1 Chapter 6.4 ka wahi sabak, sabse bade paimane pe.

Aur uska ilaaj bhi wahi hai: **aisi cheez rakho jo unki nahi hai.**

### Sochne ke liye

**1. (samajh check)** Do products hain. Ek har baar naya jawab banata hai. Doosre mein har istemaal se kuch jama hota hai. Ek saal baad kya farak hoga?

> **Jawab:** Pehla wahin khada hoga jahan shuru mein tha. Doosra ek saal aage hoga, aur woh doori har din badhegi.
>
> Aur pehle ko koi bhi dobara bana sakta hai. Doosre ko dobara banane ke liye ek saal ka istemaal chahiye.
>
> **Isliye sabse zaroori design wala sawal yeh hai: har istemaal se kya jama ho raha hai?** Agar jawab "kuch nahi" hai, toh aap har roz shuruaat se shuru kar rahe ho.

**2. (samajh check)** Aapka product ek badi company ke model pe khada hai. Kya yeh galti hai?

> **Jawab:** Nahi, aur woh lagbhag har product karta hai.
>
> Galti tab hoti hai jab aapka **poora** mol usi pe khada ho.
>
> Do sawal poochne laayak hain: agar woh kal daam das guna kar de toh? Agar woh kal khud wahi product bana le toh?
>
> Agar dono ka jawab "sab khatam" hai, toh aapke paas apna kuch hai hi nahi.
>
> **Kisi aur ki naali istemaal karo. Uspe apna ghar mat banao.**

**3. (jodne wala)** Book 1 Ch 0.2 mein Ravi bees saal chala aur uska kaam roz khatam ho gaya. Aaj Ravi kaun hai?

> **Jawab:** Woh jiska kaam har baar shuruaat se shuru hota hai aur kuch jama nahi karta.
>
> Aur woh sirf mehnat wala kaam nahi hai. Woh ek aisa product bhi ho sakta hai jo har roz naye users ko wahi cheez deta hai, aur unse kuch nahi seekhta.
>
> Anil aaj woh hai jiska kaam jama hota hai, ya jise har naya user aur mazboot karta hai.
>
> **Aur AI ne matka uthana sasta kar diya hai, na ki naali khodna bekaar.** Jo log matka uthate reh gaye, woh ab pehle se tez uthate hain, aur unka ceiling wahi hai.
