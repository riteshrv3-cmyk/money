# Chapter 7.5  [SPINE]
## Tech news kaise follow karein

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Kitaab ke shuru mein aapki shikayat kya thi?

**Kabir:** Ki har hafte kuch naya sunta hoon jo baaki sabko pehle se pata tha.

**Madhav:** Ab wahi problem alag shakal le legi. Ab aap sab kuch samajh sakte ho, toh aap sab kuch padhna chahoge.

**Kabir:** Aur uska koi ant nahi hai.

**Madhav:** Roz kitni AI ki khabarein aati hain?

**Kabir:** Bahut. Sau se zyada.

**Madhav:** Unmein se kitni mayne rakhti hain?

**Kabir:** Shayad hafte mein ek.

**Madhav:** Toh sawal kya hai?

**Kabir:** Kaunsi ek?

**Madhav:** Ab ek chhannan banate hain. Book 1 Chapter 2.2 se: kaunsa badlav mayne rakhta hai?

**Kabir:** Guna wala. Pratishat wala nahi.

**Madhav:** Toh pehla chhannan?

**Kabir:** Agar kuch das guna sasta, tez, ya bada hua hai toh dekho. 20 percent behtar ko chhod do.

**Madhav:** Achha. Doosra. Ek naya model aaya jo kisi benchmark mein aage hai. Mayne rakhta hai?

**Kabir:** Nahi, aksar. Woh har mahine hota hai. Aur benchmark mera kaam nahi naapta. *(5.3)*

**Madhav:** Aur kab mayne rakhta hai?

**Kabir:** Jab woh kuch aisa kar de jo pehle ho hi nahi sakta tha.

**Madhav:** Yaani kya badla?

**Kabir:** Sirf number nahi. Kaabiliyat ki kism.

**Madhav:** Teesra. Ek company kehti hai woh ek bada kaam kar payegi, agle saal.

**Kabir:** Woh andaza hai, jaankari nahi. Chapter 3.5. Kisi ko pata nahi ki agla model kya kar payega.

**Madhav:** Toh unhe kaise padhoge?

**Kabir:** Jo ho chuka hai use padhunga. Jo hoga uska daawa chhod dunga.

**Madhav:** Chautha. Aap kis cheez pe dhyaan doge jispe lagbhag koi charcha nahi hoti?

**Kabir:** Daam. Kyunki daam girne se naye kaam mumkin ho jaate hain.

**Madhav:** Aur?

**Kabir:** Aur woh cheezein jo model ke bahar hain. Kyunki woh main kal laga sakta hoon.

**Madhav:** Bas. Ab aakhri sawal, aur yeh sabse zaroori hai. Aap kitna padhein?

**Kabir:** Jitna kaam ka ho.

**Madhav:** Aur kaam ka kya hai?

**Kabir:** Woh jo mere kaam ko badalta hai.

**Madhav:** Aur baaki?

**Kabir:** Baaki manoranjan hai. Aur woh theek hai, jab tak main use jaankari na samjhun.

### Naam

Chaar chhannan, kaam ke kram mein:

```
1.  GUNA YA PRATISHAT?
    das guna sasta, tez, ya bada    →  dekho
    20 percent behtar               →  chhodo

2.  NAYA NUMBER YA NAYI KISM?
    benchmark pe aage               →  chhodo
    aisa kaam jo pehle ho hi nahi sakta tha  →  dekho

3.  HO CHUKA HAI YA HOGA?
    "hamne yeh kiya"                →  padho
    "agle saal yeh hoga"            →  chhodo
                                       (kisi ko pata nahi, 3.5)

4.  MERE KAAM KO BADALTA HAI?
    haan                            →  padho aur aazmao
    nahi                            →  manoranjan hai, jaankari nahi
```

Aur teen cheezein jinpe sabse kam charcha hoti hai aur sabse zyada asar padta hai:

```
DAAM GIRNA        guna wala badlav, aur khabar mein sabse peeche
                  naye kaam mumkin ho jaate hain (Ch 7.1)

MODEL KE BAHAR    naye tools, behtar RAG, naye tareeke
                  aap kal laga sakte ho, kisi ke intezaar ke bina

WOH JO TOOT       kaunsa product band hua, kaunsa API mehnga hua
                  yeh batata hai ki kya sach mein kaam nahi kar raha
```

Teesri wali sabse kaam ki hai. **Kaamyabi ki khabar sabko milti hai. Nakaami ki khabar sirf unhe milti hai jo dhoondhte hain.** Aur nakaami zyada batati hai.

Ab ek aakhri baat, aur yeh iss kitaab ka poora point hai.

Aapko har cheez padhne ki zaroorat nahi hai, kyunki ab aapke paas ek naksha hai. Jab koi nayi cheez aati hai, toh aap use uski jagah pe rakh dete ho aur aage badh jaate ho.

**Aur agar koi cheez aisi aaye jiske liye aapke naksh e mein jagah na ho, toh woh sach mein nayi hai. Us pe rukna.**

Woh saal mein do-teen baar hota hai, aur baaki sab shor hai.

### Asli duniya se

2023 se 2026 tak, har hafte "AI badal gaya" wali khabarein aati rahin.

Ab peeche mud kar dekho: kitni cheezein sach mein badli?

Kuch hi. Baaki sab wahi transformer tha, thoda bada, thode behtar data pe, thodi alag dhalai ke saath, aur uske aas-paas kuch nayi parat.

Aur jo sach mein bade badlav the, unmein se kai ne khabar mein utni jagah nahi payi jitni ek naye benchmark number ne payi.

**Yeh iss chapter ka poora saboot hai: khabar ki maatra aur badlav ki maatra ka koi rishta nahi hai.**

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **charcha ki maatra ko asar samajhna.**

Jis cheez pe sabse zyada baat ho rahi hai, woh aksar sabse kam badalti hai. Aur jo cheez chup-chaap sabki neev badal rahi hai, uspe koi baat nahi karta.

Doosri galti: **FOMO se padhna.**

Log isliye padhte hain ki kahin peeche na reh jaayein. Aur woh ulta chalta hai: aap sau cheezein padh kar bhool jaate ho, aur ek cheez bhi aazmate nahi.

**Ek cheez aazmaana das cheezein padhne se zyada batata hai.** Kyunki aazmaane se aapko woh pata chalta hai jo kisi khabar mein nahi hoti: yeh aapke kaam mein kaisa chalta hai.

Teesri galti: **daawe aur nateeje ko ek samajhna.**

Ek company ka daawa ek marketing ka kaagaz hai. Uska matlab hai ki unhe kya bechna hai, na ki kya sach hai.

Aur unmein aksar jhooth nahi hota. Bas woh un cheezon ke baare mein chup rehte hain jo kaam nahi karti.

### Sochne ke liye

**1. (samajh check)** Aaj do khabarein hain. "Naya model tark ke test mein 3 point aage." Aur "ek badi service ne apne daam aadhe kar diye." Kaunsi padhoge?

> **Jawab:** Doosri, bina soche.
>
> Teen point ek benchmark pe shor hai. Woh agle mahine palat sakta hai, aur aapke kaam se uska koi rishta nahi.
>
> Daam aadha hona ek asli badlav hai. Woh cheezein jo pehle mehngi hone ki wajah se nahi ban sakti thin, ab ban sakti hain.
>
> **Aur pehli wali ko das guna zyada charcha milegi.** Yeh iss chapter ka poora sabak hai.

**2. (samajh check)** Ek AI product ka demo dekha aur woh kamaal tha. Kya karoge?

> **Jawab:** Apne bees sawal chalao, apne asli kaam se. *(5.3)*
>
> Demo chune hue case dikhata hai. Woh jhooth nahi hai, aur woh aapke case ke baare mein kuch nahi kehta.
>
> Aur ek aur cheez dekho: **kya woh har kadam pe jaanch dikhata hai, ya sirf aakhri nateeja?** Agar sirf nateeja, toh us demo se aap yeh nahi jaan sakte ki beech mein kya hua. *(6.8)*

**3. (jodne wala)** Kitaab ke shuru mein Kabir ki shikayat thi ki har cheez "khabar" lagti hai. Ab woh kaise badla?

> **Jawab:** Ab har cheez ke liye ek jagah pehle se hai.
>
> Jab kuch naya aata hai, aap poochte ho: kaunsi parat, kaunsa dard, kaunsa sauda, andar ya bahar. Aur woh apni jagah pe baith jaata hai.
>
> Jo pehle "kuch naya" tha, woh ab "us purani cheez ka ek naya roop" hai.
>
> **Aur wahi poori kitaab ka maksad tha.** Aapne 100 cheezein yaad nahi ki. Aapne ek dhaancha banaya jismein nayi cheezein apne aap baith jaati hain.
>
> Aur jis din koi cheez nahi baithe, us din rukna. Woh sach mein nayi hai.
