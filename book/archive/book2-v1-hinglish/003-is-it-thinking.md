# Chapter 1.2  [SPINE]
## Kya yeh soch raha hai?

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Aapne usse ek mushkil sawal poocha aur usne achha jawab diya. Kya woh soch raha tha?

**Kabir:** Lagta toh hai.

**Madhav:** "Lagta hai" se shuru karte hain. Aap kaise jaante ho ki koi insaan soch raha hai?

**Kabir:** Woh samajhdaari se jawab deta hai.

**Madhav:** Toh aap uske andar nahi dekhte. Aap bahar se dekh kar tay karte ho.

**Kabir:** Haan, aur koi tareeka bhi nahi hai.

**Madhav:** Sahi. Ab dhyaan do ki aapne kya kiya. Aapne bahar se dekha aur andar ke baare mein ek nateeja nikaal liya. Yeh insaanon ke saath theek chalta hai kyunki hum sab ek jaise bane hain. Yahan?

**Kabir:** Yahan woh cheez alag tarah se bani hai.

**Madhav:** Toh chalo andar dekhte hain, jitna dekha ja sakta hai. Ek sawal. Aap usse kuch poochte ho, phir naya chat kholte ho aur wahi poochte ho. Kya use yaad hai ki aapne pehle poocha tha?

**Kabir:** Nahi.

**Madhav:** Kya usne aapke pehle sawal se kuch seekha?

**Kabir:** Shayad nahi. Woh wahi ka wahi hai.

**Madhav:** Bilkul wahi ka wahi. Woh file ek byte nahi badli. Aap usse ek ghanta baat karo, woh file waisi ki waisi rahegi.

**Kabir:** Toh phir usi chat ke andar use yaad kaise rehta hai?

**Madhav:** Kya woh yaad hai, ya kuch aur? Socho. Agar model badalta nahi, toh purani baat kahan se aa rahi hai?

**Kabir:** Shayad... woh dobara bheji jaati hai?

**Madhav:** Har baar. Aapki poori purani baat-cheet, har naye sawal ke saath, dobara bhej di jaati hai.

**Kabir:** Toh use kuch yaad nahi rehta. Use har baar sab kuch dobara padhaya jaata hai.

**Madhav:** Bas. Aur ab ek aur cheez. Kya usmein koi irada hai? Kya woh kuch chahta hai?

**Kabir:** Woh madad karna chahta hai.

**Madhav:** Woh aisa likhta hai jaise woh madad karna chahta ho. Yeh ek hi baat hai?

**Kabir:** Nahi.

**Madhav:** Jab aap use band kar dete ho, toh woh kya kar raha hota hai?

**Kabir:** Kuch nahi. Woh chalta hi nahi hai.

**Madhav:** Kya woh intezaar kar raha hai?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Woh hai hi nahi. Jab tak koi sawal na aaye, kuch chal hi nahi raha.

**Madhav:** Toh ab batao. Ek cheez jismein kuch yaad nahi rehta, jo baat karke badalti nahi, jiske paas koi irada nahi, aur jo sawalon ke beech maujood hi nahi hoti. Kya use "soch raha hai" kehna theek hai?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Lekin phir jawab achhe kyun hote hain?

**Madhav:** Yeh sahi sawal hai, aur poori kitaab uska jawab hai. Abhi ek chhota jawab deta hoon. Agar aap duniya ke lagbhag saare likhe hue shabd padh lo, aur unmein se yeh seekh lo ki kaunsa shabd kis ke baad aata hai, aur usse aage badhte jao, toh aapke banaye vaakya samajhdaar honge, chahe aap kuch soch rahe hon ya na.

**Kabir:** Toh samajhdaari shabdon mein thi, machine mein nahi?

**Madhav:** Yeh ek achha shak hai. Use pakde rakhna. Iss kitaab ke aakhir tak hum us par wapas aayenge, aur jawab utna saaf nahi hai jitna abhi lagta hai.

### Naam

Char cheezein jo iss cheez mein nahi hain, aur jo insaani sochne mein hoti hain:

```
YAADDASHT     baat-cheet ke baad kuch nahi bachta
              (file ek byte nahi badalti)

SEEKHNA       aapse baat karke woh behtar nahi hota
              (seekhna alag hota hai, alag waqt pe, Part 2)

IRADA         woh kuch chahta nahi
              (woh chahne wale shabd likh sakta hai)

NIRANTARTA    do sawalon ke beech woh maujood hi nahi hota
              (koi cheez intezaar nahi kar rahi)
```

Aur woh cheez jo usmein hai, aur jise log yaaddasht samajh lete hain:

> **Har baar poori purani baat-cheet dobara bheji jaati hai.**

Yeh yaaddasht nahi hai. Yeh dobara padhaana hai. Aur uska seedha nateeja Chapter 4.5 mein aayega: jab woh dobara padhaayi ja rahi cheez ek had se badi ho jaati hai, toh shuruaat kaat di jaati hai, aur "woh bhool gaya."

Ab ek zaroori chetavni, dono taraf se.

Yeh chapter yeh nahi keh raha ki woh bekaar hai, ya ki jo woh karta hai woh asli nahi hai. Woh sach mein kaam karta hai, aur kaafi mushkil kaam karta hai.

Yeh sirf itna keh raha hai ki **woh us tarah se nahi karta jaise aap sochte ho ki woh karta hai.** Aur agar aapki tasveer galat hai, toh aap galat jagah bharosa karoge aur galat jagah shak karoge.

### Asli duniya se

1966 mein ELIZA naam ka ek bahut chhota program banaya gaya tha. Woh ek therapist ki nakal karta tha, aur uske andar lagbhag kuch nahi tha. Woh aapke vaakya ko ulta kar deta tha: "mujhe apni maa se problem hai" ka jawab "apni maa ke baare mein aur bataiye."

Uske banane wale, Joseph Weizenbaum, yeh dekh kar hairan reh gaye ki log usse asli baat karne lage. Uski secretary ne unse kamre se bahar jaane ko kaha taaki woh "akele" baat kar sake. Aur unhe pata tha ki andar kya hai, kyunki unhone hi banaya tha.

Iska naam pad gaya: ELIZA effect. Insaan bahut kam sanket pe bhi doosri taraf ek dimaag maan leta hai.

Yeh dhyaan dene laayak hai. Agar 1966 ka ek chhota program yeh kar sakta tha, toh aaj ki cheez ke saamne aapka dimaag kitni tezi se yeh maan lega, chahe andar kuch bhi ho.

Iska matlab yeh nahi ki aaj ki cheez ELIZA jaisi hai. Woh nahi hai. Matlab yeh hai ki **"mujhe aisa lagta hai" iss sawal ka bhrose laayak jawab kabhi tha hi nahi.**

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti dono taraf hoti hai, aur dono mehngi hain.

**Pehli: yeh maan lena ki woh soch raha hai.** Iska nateeja yeh hota hai ki log uske aatmvishwas ko sachai ka sabooot maan lete hain. Woh galat baat utne hi bhare andaz mein kehta hai jitne mein sahi. Yeh Chapter 5.1 ka vishay hai.

**Doosri: yeh maan lena ki "woh sirf agla shabd guess karta hai, isliye woh kuch nahi kar sakta."** Yeh bhi galat hai. Woh sach mein agla shabd guess karta hai, aur woh sach mein aise kaam kar leta hai jo pehle sirf insaan kar sakte the. Dono baatein ek saath sach hain, aur yehi sabse dilchasp hissa hai.

Sahi jagah beech mein hai, aur woh asuvidhajanak hai:

```
woh sochta nahi hai jaise aap sochte ho
aur woh phir bhi bahut kuch kar leta hai
aur hum poori tarah nahi jaante ki kaise
```

Aakhri line par log aksar bahas karte hain, aur woh bahas asli hai. Yeh un jagahon mein se ek hai jahan is field mein sach mein sehmati nahi hai.

### Sochne ke liye

**1. (samajh check)** Aap ek lambi baat-cheet karte ho aur woh sab yaad rakhta lagta hai. Lekin uski file badalti nahi. Toh purani baat kahan rakhi hai?

> **Jawab:** Aapke device pe aur us server pe, ek saadi list ki tarah. Aur har naye sawal ke saath woh poori list dobara bheji jaati hai.
>
> Model ke liye har sawal pehla sawal hai. Use bas har baar ek lambi chitthi di jaati hai jismein ab tak ki poori baat likhi hoti hai.
>
> Iska seedha nateeja: **lambi baat-cheet har baar mehngi hoti jaati hai**, kyunki har baar zyada text bheja jaata hai. Yeh Chapter 4.5 aur 7.1 mein wapas aayega, ek daam ke saath.

**2. (samajh check)** Do log ek hi sawal poochte hain, alag alag chat mein. Kya doosre ko pehle wale ki wajah se behtar jawab milega?

> **Jawab:** Nahi. Model ne pehle wale se kuch nahi seekha.
>
> Woh file badalti nahi. Seekhna ek alag kaam hai jo alag waqt pe, alag jagah, mahinon mein hota hai. Poora Part 2 usi pe hai.
>
> Ek apwaad hai jo dhyaan dene laayak hai: company aapki baat-cheet ko **agle** model ko seekhane ke liye rakh sakti hai. Woh model badalta nahi, aakhri model badalta hai. Aur woh ek alag sawal hai, jo Chapter 6.6 ka hissa hai.

**3. (jodne wala)** Book 1 Ch 1.3 mein tay hua tha ki computer koi faisla nahi leta, usmein bees arab shartein lagi hain aur bijli unse guzarti hai. Kya woh baat yahan bhi lagti hai?

> **Jawab:** Haan, aur woh isse kaafi hadd tak samjha deti hai.
>
> Model bhi ek bahut bada, bahut jatil hisaab hai. Numbers andar jaate hain, guna aur jod hote hain, numbers bahar aate hain.
>
> Farak yeh nahi hai ki uske andar koi nayi tarah ki cheez hai. Farak yeh hai ki **woh shartein kisi insaan ne nahi likhi.** Woh apne aap bani.
>
> Aur isiliye yeh sawal ki "kya woh soch raha hai" itna phisalta hai. Aapke saamne ek aisi machine hai jiska mechanism poori tarah saaf hai aur jiska vyavhaar poori tarah nahi.
