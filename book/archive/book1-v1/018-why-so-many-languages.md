# Chapter 3.3  [SPINE]
## Itni saari languages kyun hain

---

### Samvaad

**Kabir:** Agar sab languages ka kaam ek hi hai, machine ko nirdesh dena, toh itni saari kyun hain? Sau se zyada.

**Madhav:** Tumhare ghar mein kitne chaaku hain?

**Kabir:** Chaar paanch.

**Madhav:** Sabka kaam kaatna hi hai. Phir chaar kyun?

**Kabir:** Roti ka alag, sabzi ka alag, bada wala alag.

**Madhav:** Aur agar sirf ek rakhna ho?

**Kabir:** Rakh sakte hain, lekin har kaam thoda mushkil ho jaayega.

**Madhav:** Bas. Ab languages ka bhi wahi hai. Lekin unke chunav thode alag hote hain. Ek sawal se shuru karte hain. Tumne likha: `x = 5`. Machine ko yeh kaise pata chalega ki 5 ek number hai, akshar nahi?

**Kabir:** Kyunki 5 number hai.

**Madhav:** Chapter 1.4 yaad karo. Andar sirf numbers hain. Matlab bahar se aata hai. Toh machine ko kaise pata?

**Kabir:** Kisi ko batana padega.

**Madhav:** Kaun bataye? Do tareeke hain. Ek: likhne wala pehle se likh de, "yeh khaana numbers ke liye hai." Do: koi na kuch likhe, aur chalte waqt machine dekhe ki usmein kya pada hai.

**Kabir:** Pehla zyada kaam hai likhne mein.

**Madhav:** Aur uska faayda?

**Kabir:** Agar main galti se usmein akshar daal doon toh pehle hi pakda jaayega.

**Madhav:** Aur doosre mein?

**Kabir:** Chalte waqt pata chalega. Ya shayad chalta rahega aur galat jawab dega.

**Madhav:** Toh ek tareeka likhne mein dheema aur bharosemand hai, doosra likhne mein tez aur risky hai. Kaunsa behtar hai?

**Kabir:** Chhote kaam ke liye doosra. Bade ke liye pehla.

**Madhav:** Yeh sirf ek chunav hai. Aise dozen chunav hote hain, aur har language unhe alag tarah se chunti hai. Ek aur lo. Jab tumhe RAM mein jagah chahiye, kaun de?

**Kabir:** Machine de degi.

**Madhav:** Aur jab kaam khatam, wapas kaun le?

**Kabir:** Machine hi le legi?

**Madhav:** Kaise pata chalega ki kaam khatam ho gaya?

**Kabir:** Hmm. Ya toh main bataun, ya woh khud dekhti rahe ki ab yeh kisi ke kaam ka nahi hai.

**Madhav:** Dono hote hain. Agar tum batao toh tez chalta hai lekin bhool gaye toh jagah bharti jaayegi. Agar machine khud dekhe toh surakshit hai lekin woh dekhne mein bhi waqt lagta hai, aur woh waqt kabhi bhi aa sakta hai, beech mein.

**Kabir:** Toh game mein woh beech mein aa gaya toh atak jaayega.

**Madhav:** Bilkul. Isiliye game aur operating system aksar aisi languages mein likhe jaate hain jahan aap khud batate ho. Aur website aksar aisi mein jahan machine khud dekhti hai.

**Kabir:** Toh koi language sabse achhi nahi hai.

**Madhav:** Koi bhi nahi. Har ek ne kuch chunav kiye, aur har chunav mein kuch mila aur kuch gaya. Ab aakhri sawal, aur yeh sabse kaam ka hai. Tumne C seekh li. Ab Python seekhne mein kitna lagega?

**Kabir:** Pata nahi. Shuru se?

**Madhav:** Shuru se kya kya seekhna padega? Shartein?

**Kabir:** Nahi, woh toh wahi hain.

**Madhav:** Ginti, dohrav, numbers rakhna, tulna?

**Kabir:** Woh sab bhi wahi hain.

**Madhav:** Toh kya naya hai?

**Kabir:** Sirf likhne ka tareeka. Aur woh chunav jo unhone alag kiye.

**Madhav:** Toh kitna waqt lagega?

**Kabir:** Hafta, do hafte.

**Madhav:** Aur pehli language seekhne mein?

**Kabir:** Mahine.

**Madhav:** Kyunki pehli baar tum language nahi seekh rahe the. Tum sochne ka tareeka seekh rahe the. Woh ek baar seekhna padta hai.

### Naam

Woh chunav jinke aadhaar pe languages alag hoti hain, unmein sabse bade do yeh hain:

```
TYPES: pehle batana ki khaane mein kya aayega, ya nahi
├── pehle batao (C, Java, Rust, TypeScript)
│   likhne mein dheema, galtiyan pehle pakdi jaati hain
└── mat batao (Python, JavaScript, Ruby)
    likhne mein tez, galtiyan chalte waqt

MEMORY: RAM ki jagah wapas kaun leta hai
├── tum khud (C, C++)
│   tez aur poora control, aur bhoolne pe program toot ta hai
├── machine khud dekhti rehti hai (Java, Python, Go, JavaScript)
│   surakshit, lekin beech mein ruk sakta hai
└── compiler pehle hi tay kar deta hai (Rust)
    tez aur surakshit dono, lekin likhna mushkil
```

Aur teesri baat, jo aksar sabse zyada mayne rakhti hai aur kitaabon mein sabse kam likhi jaati hai: **pehle se bana hua saaman.**

Ek language isliye nahi chuni jaati ki woh sundar hai. Woh isliye chuni jaati hai ki uske aas-paas pehle se hazaaron cheezein bani hui hain jo aapko dobara nahi banani padengi.

Python AI mein isliye nahi hai ki woh AI ke liye achhi hai. Woh isliye hai ki AI ka saara saaman Python ke liye pehle se bana hua hai. Aur woh saaman isliye bana kyunki shuru ke kuch log Python istemaal kar rahe the. Ek baar shuru ho jaaye toh khud ko badhata rehta hai.

Yeh baat Chapter 8.5 mein dobara aayegi, jahan hum poochenge ki ek technology jeetati kyun hai. Aksar jawab "behtar thi" nahi hota.

### Asli duniya se ek example

JavaScript 1995 mein lagbhag **das din** mein banayi gayi thi, ek browser mein chhote-mote kaam karne ke liye. Kisi ne nahi socha tha ki woh badi cheezein banane ke kaam aayegi.

Uska design jaldbaazi mein hua aur usmein aisi cheezein reh gayin jinhe aaj tak galtiyan maana jaata hai.

Aaj woh duniya ki sabse zyada istemaal hone wali languages mein se ek hai.

Wajah unke design mein nahi hai. Wajah yeh hai ki woh browser ke andar thi, aur browser har machine pe tha. Jo language wahan pehle se maujood thi, wahi jeet gayi.

Aur jab log uski kamiyon se pareshan hue, toh unhone use hataya nahi. Unhone uske upar ek nayi parat banayi, TypeScript, jo uski shakal sudhaar deti hai aur phir use wapas JavaScript mein badal deti hai.

Ek aur parat, phir se. Purani cheez hatayi nahi gayi, chhupa di gayi.

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **yeh sochna ki "kaunsi language seekhun" ek bada faisla hai.**

Log mahine iss sawal pe atak jaate hain. Kaunsi sabse achhi hai, kaunsi ka future hai, kaunsi mein naukri hai.

Lekin pehli language seekhne ka lagbhag saara waqt language pe nahi jaata. Woh sochne ke tareeke pe jaata hai: cheezein rakhna, shartein lagana, dohrav, tukdon mein todna. Yeh sab har language mein ek hi hai.

Ek baar woh aa gaya toh doosri language ek hafte ka kaam hai.

Yeh galti tempting isliye hai ki bahar se languages bilkul alag dikhti hain. Alag shabd, alag nishaan. Andar se woh lagbhag ek jaisi hain.

### Sochne ke liye

**1. (samajh check)** Ek hospital ki machine ka software likhna hai jo mareez ko dawai deti hai. Kaunse chunav karoge, aur kyun?

> **Jawab:** Types pehle batao. Memory ka control khud lo, ya aisi language lo jo compile ke waqt hi tay kar de.
>
> Wajah: yahan galti ki keemat jaan hai. Aap chahte ho ki jitni bhi galtiyan pakdi ja sakti hain, woh chalne se pehle pakdi jaayein.
>
> Aur aap nahi chahte ki machine beech mein apne aap ruk jaaye jagah saaf karne ke liye, kyunki woh ruk-ruk kar chalna ek dawai dene wali machine mein khatarnak hai.
>
> Dhyaan do: yeh chunav "achhi language" ka nahi hai. Yeh **is kaam ke liye sahi chunav** ka hai.

**2. (samajh check)** Aapka dost kehta hai "Python dheemi hai isliye woh ek ghatiya language hai." Kya jawab doge?

> **Jawab:** Ki speed ek chunav ka nateeja hai, kami nahi.
>
> Python ne likhne ki aasani chuni aur chalne ki speed di. C ne ulta kiya.
>
> Sawal yeh nahi hai ki kaunsi tez hai. Sawal yeh hai ki aapke kaam mein mehnga kya hai: machine ka waqt, ya insaan ka waqt.
>
> Zyadatar kaamon mein insaan ka waqt mehnga hota hai. Isiliye dheemi languages itni zyada istemaal hoti hain.

**3. (jodne wala)** Chapter 0.4 kehta tha ki har parat neeche wali ko chhupa deti hai. TypeScript ka udaharan uss baat se kaise judta hai?

> **Jawab:** Seedha judta hai, aur ek nayi baat bhi batata hai.
>
> JavaScript mein kamiyan thin. Unhe theek karne ka matlab hota duniya ke har browser ko badalna, jo mumkin hi nahi tha.
>
> Toh logon ne kuch nahi hataya. Unhone upar ek parat rakh di.
>
> Nayi baat yeh hai: **jo cheez ek baar phail jaati hai, use hataya nahi ja sakta, sirf dhaka ja sakta hai.** Isiliye technology mein purani galtiyan hamesha zinda rehti hain, bas unke upar naye kapde aa jaate hain.
>
> Yeh yaad rakhna. Jab bhi aapko lage ki koi cheez bewajah ulajhi hui hai, aksar wajah yeh hoti hai ki neeche kuch purana hai jise koi hata nahi sakta.
