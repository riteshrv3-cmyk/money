# Glossary

Har shabd, aur woh chapter jahan aapne use kamaaya.

---

**abstraction** (0.4) — neeche wali cheez ko chhupa dena, taaki uske upar wala usse soche bina kaam kar sake. Poori computer ki duniya isi ka dher hai.

**adder** (1.5) — switchon se bana woh hissa jo do numbers jodta hai. Usse ghatana, guna aur bhaag sab nikalte hain.

**AND / OR / NOT** (1.3) — teen shartein. AND: dono chalu hon. OR: koi ek. NOT: ulta. In teeno se har woh cheez ban sakti hai jo koi computer karta hai.

**API** (6.4) — tay-shuda tareeka jisse ek program doosre se baat karta hai. Ek waada, ek darwaza, aur ek taala, teeno.

**assembly** (3.1) — machine code ki woh shakal jismein har nirdesh ko ek chhota naam mil jaata hai.

**backup** (5.7) — kisi purane waqt ki copy, alag rakhi hui. Woh aapki apni galti se bachata hai. Jaanchi na ho toh backup hai hi nahi.

**bandwidth** (4.3) — ek second mein kitna data aa sakta hai. Sadak kitni chaudi hai. Badhayi ja sakti hai.

**BGP** (4.7) — woh tareeka jisse padosi machinein ek doosre ko batati hain ki kahan se kya pahuncha ja sakta hai.

**binary** (1.4) — sirf do nishaanon wali ginti, 0 aur 1.

**binary search** (5.2) — har kadam pe aadha hissa chhod dena. Das crore mein se ek cheez lagbhag sattaais kadam mein.

**bit** (1.4) — ek switch ki haalat. Ek haan-na. Sabse chhoti cheez.

**bug** (7.1) — galti. Woh isliye rehti hai kyunki haalatein anginat hain aur poora jaanchna namumkin hai.

**byte** (2.1) — aath bit. Aam taur pe ek angrezi akshar.

**cache** (5.5) — paas rakhi hui copy. Woh tez isliye hai kyunki woh purani ho sakti hai. Woh do alag baatein nahi hain.

**cascading failure** (6.6) — bojh khud ko badhata jaata hai. Log intezaar chhod kar dobara koshish karte hain, jisse bojh aur badhta hai.

**CDN** (5.5) — duniya bhar mein rakhi copies, taaki cheez user ke paas se aaye. Speed ke liye bhi, aur paise ke liye bhi.

**certificate authority** (4.6) — woh teesra jo batata hai ki koi sach mein wahi hai jo keh raha hai. Uski list aapke device mein pehle se hoti hai.

**CI** (7.2) — woh machine jo har badlav pe saari jaanch apne aap chalati hai.

**client** (4.1 / 6.3) — woh cheez jo sawal poochti hai. User ke device pe chalti hai, isliye uspe kabhi bharosa nahi kiya jaata.

**cloud** (6.5) — kisi aur ki machinein, kirye pe. Per machine mehnga, aur phir bhi aksar sahi.

**compiler** (3.1, 3.2) — poore program ko pehle numbers mein badal deta hai, phir woh chalta hai.

**compression** (2.1) — dohrav hatao, ya sirf farak rakho. Woh dohrav pe chalta hai, isliye jahan dohrav nahi wahan bekaar hai.

**congestion control** (4.3) — dheere se badhao, kuch khone lage toh peeche hato. Isse poori sadak bina kisi manager ke baat jaati hai.

**container** (7.4) — program aur uski saari cheezein ek dabbe mein, taaki woh har machine pe ek jaisa chale.

**CPU** (2.3) — woh hissa jahan nirdesh chalte hain. Uthao, samjho, karo, baar baar.

**database** (5.1) — woh program jo chaar problem sambhalta hai: dhoondhna, ek saath likhna, beech mein rukna, dohrav.

**DNS** (4.5) — naam ko pate mein badalne wala jaal. Ek pedh, jismein har shakha apna hissa sambhalti hai.

**encoding** (1.4) — woh tay-shuda list jo batati hai ki kaunsa number kis cheez ka matlab hai. Matlab andar nahi hota, isi list se aata hai.

**front / back** (6.3) — dikhne wala hissa user ke device pe, na dikhne wala server pe. Sach hamesha peeche tay hota hai.

**git** (7.3) — har badlav ka record. Uska asli faayda purani copy nahi, himmat hai.

**HTTP** (4.4) — web ka protocol. Kya maanga jaa sakta hai aur jawab kaisa dikhega.

**index** (5.2) — peeche wali list. Padhna tez karta hai, likhna dheema, aur jagah leta hai.

**interpreter** (3.2) — chalte chalte translate karta hai, line by line.

**IP address** (4.2) — machine ka pata. Woh network mein jagah batata hai, zameen pe jagah nahi.

**latency** (4.3, 6.7) — ek chakkar mein kitna waqt. Sadak kitni lambi hai. Roshni ki seema hai, isliye badhayi nahi ja sakti.

**load balancer** (6.1) — saamne khadi cheez jo users ko machinon pe baantti hai.

**lock-in** (6.5) — jitni kisi ki khaas cheezein istemaal ki, utna wahan se nikalna mushkil.

**logic gate** (1.3) — bijli ko shart ke hisaab se rokne ya jaane dene wala hissa.

**machine code** (3.1) — seedhe numbers, jinhe chip samajhti hai.

**N+1** (6.7) — ek sawal se sau cheezein aayin, aur phir har cheez ke liye ek alag sawal. Speed ki sabse aam galti.

**NAT** (4.2) — ek public pate ke peeche kai andar wale pate. Isiliye aapka phone bahar se pahuncha nahi ja sakta.

**network effect** (4.1) — har naya judne wala pehle se jude hue sabki value badha deta hai.

**open source** (3.6) — jiska code sabke liye khula ho. Uski wajah aksar daan nahi, hisaab hoti hai.

**operating system** (3.4) — woh manager jo baari, jagah aur saaman baantta hai, aur sabko ek doosre se alag rakhta hai.

**overflow** (1.5) — jagah khatam ho gayi. Machine rukti nahi, woh chup-chaap galat jawab de deti hai.

**packet** (4.3) — bade sandesh ka ek chhota tukda, jispe pata aur number likha hota hai.

**process** (3.5) — chalta hua program. File ek nuskha hai, process us nuskhe se bana khaana.

**program** (1.6) — nirdesh, jo khud bhi numbers hain. Isiliye machine badle bina kaam badal jaata hai.

**protocol** (4.4) — pehle se tay niyam. Woh bheja nahi ja sakta, isliye dono taraf pehle se hona chahiye.

**public key / private key** (4.6) — ek chaabi jo band karti hai aur sabko di ja sakti hai, doosri jo kholti hai aur kisi ko nahi.

**queue** (5.6) — kaam ki kataar. Woh kaam kam nahi karti, woh intezaar hata deti hai.

**RAM** (2.4) — tez memory jo bijli jaate hi khaali ho jaati hai.

**registers** (2.3) — CPU ke ekdam paas ke chhote khaane. Sabse tez, sabse kam.

**replica** (5.7) — turant banti nakal. Machine kharaab hone se bachati hai, aapki galti se bilkul nahi.

**routing** (4.7) — har machine sirf agla kadam jaanti hai. Koi poora raasta nahi jaanta.

**scale up / scale out** (6.1) — ek machine ko bada karo, ya kai chhoti lagao.

**server** (4.1) — woh machine jo ek jagah rehti hai aur jo bhi aaye uska jawab deti hai.

**SQL / NoSQL / graph** (5.3) — data ki teen shakalein. Shakal data se nahi, us sawal se aati hai jo aap sabse zyada poochoge.

**stateless** (6.1) — machine ke andar aisi koi jaankari na ho jo sirf usi ke paas ho.

**TCP / UDP** (4.3) — har tukda pahunchna zaroori, ya pahunche toh theek na pahunche toh chalo aage.

**TLS / HTTPS** (4.6) — woh taala. Woh sirf itna kehta hai ki raaste mein koi padh nahi raha aur naam wahi hai jo dikh raha hai.

**transaction** (5.4) — ya toh sab kaam ho, ya kuch bhi na ho. Aadha kabhi nahi.

**transistor** (1.1) — chip ke andar ka chhota switch. Use doosra switch daba sakta hai, aur wahi sab kuch mumkin banata hai.

**Unicode** (1.4) — duniya ki lagbhag har likhavat ke liye ek hi list.

**uptime** (6.2) — kitna waqt chalu raha. Naukon mein ginte hain, aur har nau daam ko lagbhag guna kar deta hai.

**version control** (7.3) — dekho git.

**XOR** (1.5) — "dono alag hain" wali shart. Adder ka aadha hissa.
