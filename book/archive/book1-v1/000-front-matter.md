## Yeh kitaab aapko batati nahi hai

Zyada kitaabein aapko ek definition deti hain aur ummeed karti hain ki aap maan lo. Aap padhte ho, sar hilate ho, aur ek hafte baad kuch bacha nahi hota.

Aap yeh already jaante ho ki yeh tareeka aap pe kaam nahi karta.

Toh yeh kitaab ulta chalti hai.

Har chapter aapke saamne ek problem rakhta hai. Asli problem, jismein log hote hain. Phir poochta hai ki aap kya karte. Aap sochte ho. Aap kuch nikaalte ho. Aur phir kitaab batati hai ki jo cheez aapne abhi banayi, woh already exist karti hai, aur duniya use is naam se bulati hai.

Aap naam yaad nahi kar rahe. Aap cheezein bana rahe ho, aur naam baad mein utha rahe ho.

Knowledge sirf isi tarah se tikta hai.

---

## Do log

Yeh kitaab do logon ki baat-cheet se chalti hai.

**Madhav.** Umar saath ke aas-paas. Poori zindagi cheezein banayi hain, pehle machine, phir software. Ab padhata hai. Jawab seedha nahi deta, kyunki uska maanna hai ki jo jawab diya jaaye woh tikta nahi.

**Kabir.** Umar pachchees. Padhai poori ho chuki hai. Kai cheezein shuru ki hain, koi khatam nahi hui. Roz AI istemaal karta hai aur use bilkul samajh nahi aata ki andar kya hai. Har hafte kisi nayi cheez ka naam sunta hai jo baaki sabko pehle se pata tha. Woh sust nahi hai, aur woh bewakoof toh bilkul nahi hai. Uske paas bahut saare tukde hain jo aapas mein judte nahi.

Kabir aksar galat hota hai. Uske galat jawab iss kitaab ka sabse kaam ka hissa hain, kyunki woh wahi galtiyan hain jo lagbhag har koi karta hai. Jab woh galat ho, toh ruk kar socho ki aap bhi wahi sochte the ya nahi.

## Chaar vaade

**Koi shabd aapki zaroorat se pehle nahi aayega.** Aap kabhi aisa word nahi milenge jiski kami aapne pehle mehsoos na ki ho. Agar koi word bold mein dikhe, toh matlab do page pehle aapne woh idea bana liya tha, aur ab bas uska label mil raha hai.

**Bina wajah kuch exist nahi karta.** Technology ki har cheez kisi insaan ne banayi thi, jiske paas ek problem thi. Yeh kitaab hamesha pehle problem deti hai. Agar kabhi kuch bewajah lage, toh woh likhne ki galti hai, aapki nahi.

**Ek chapter, ek idea.** Chapters jaan-boojh ke chote hain. Do ya teen page. Ek nayi cheez. Agar chapter khatam karke aap us ek idea ko bol sako, toh woh chapter kaam kar gaya.

**Baat hamesha aap tak wapas aati hai.** Har chapter aise khatam hota hai jo aapne khud dekha hai, aksar isi hafte.

## Do kitaabein, aur yeh pehli kyun hai

```
BOOK 1   THE MACHINE
         Switches, languages, networks, data, serving.
         Jo 2005 mein sach tha aur 2045 mein bhi sach rahega.
         Yeh kitaab kabhi purani nahi hogi.

BOOK 2   THE THINKING MACHINE
         Probability, learning, meaning, transformers, agents.
         Yeh har saal thodi badalti rahegi.
```

Book 2 zyada exciting lagti hai, aur log wahin se shuru karna chahte hain. Problem yeh hai ki Book 2 ka har chapter Book 1 ki cheezon pe khada hai. AI ek file hai numbers ki, jo ek machine pe chalti hai, ek network ke through aati hai, aur ek database se judti hai.

Pehle machine, phir thinking machine. Us order mein kuch bhi udhaar nahi lena padta.

## Sawal jo chapter ke aakhir mein aate hain

Har chapter do ya teen sawal ke saath khatam hota hai. Teen tarah ke sawal hain:

```
SAMAJH CHECK    is chapter ka idea pakda ya nahi
JODNE WALA      yeh chapter pehle wale kis cheez se judta hai
DESIGN SAWAL    ek part mein ek baar. Kuch banane ka sawal.
```

Yeh test nahi hain. Computer ki zaroorat nahi. Likhne ki bhi nahi, sirf design wale sawal ke liye.

Ek minute baitho har sawal pe, aage badhne se pehle.

Har sawal ka jawab usi ke neeche likha hai. Pehle apna jawab socho, sahi ho ya galat, phir neeche padho. Galat jawab jo aapne socha, woh sahi jawab se behtar hai jo aapko diya gaya.

Design sawalon ke jawab `WORKBOOK.md` mein likhna. Woh iss kitaab ka asli output hai.
