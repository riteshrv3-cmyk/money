# Chapter 1.2  [SPINE]
## Yeh maan kar chal raha hoon

---

### Samvaad

**Kabir:** Ek baat poochun? Iske liye mujhe maths aani chahiye?

**Madhav:** Kitni aati hai?

**Kabir:** School wali. Uske baad chhoot gayi.

**Madhav:** Do aur do?

**Kabir:** Chaar.

**Madhav:** Aadha kitna hota hai?

**Kabir:** Sau ka pachas.

**Madhav:** Bas. Poori kitaab ke liye itni kaafi hai.

**Kabir:** Sach mein? Computer toh poora maths hi hai.

**Madhav:** Computer ke andar maths hai. Use samajhne ke liye maths nahi chahiye. Yeh do alag baatein hain, aur log inhe hamesha mila dete hain.

**Kabir:** Farak samjhao.

**Madhav:** Tumhare pet ke andar kya ho raha hai abhi?

**Kabir:** Khaana pach raha hai.

**Madhav:** Usmein kaunse kemikal lag rahe hain, bata sakte ho?

**Kabir:** Nahi.

**Madhav:** Phir bhi tum yeh samajhte ho ki khaana kaise pachta hai. Khaya, tuta, sharir ne le liya, bacha hua nikal gaya. Yeh samajh galat hai?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Bas gehri nahi hai.

**Madhav:** Woh galat nahi hai. Woh **ek parat ki** samajh hai. Usse aap khaane ke faisle le sakte ho, doctor se baat kar sakte ho, aur yeh samajh sakte ho ki bimari kyun hui. Kemikal ki zaroorat sirf tab padti hai jab tum nayi dawai banao.

**Kabir:** Toh yeh kitaab mujhe kis parat pe le jaayegi?

**Madhav:** Utni gehri jitni faisle lene ke liye chahiye. Tum yeh samajh jaoge ki har cheez kaam kaise karti hai aur kyun waisi hai. Tum yeh nahi seekhoge ki use banayein kaise.

**Kabir:** Toh main code likhna nahi seekhunga.

**Madhav:** Nahi. Aur yeh main pehle din bata raha hoon, taaki teesre hafte mein tumhe dhokha na lage.

**Kabir:** Toh phir faayda kya hai?

**Madhav:** Do aadmi socho. Ek ko code likhna aata hai lekin naksha nahi pata. Doosre ko naksha pata hai lekin code nahi aata. Kaunsa aadmi yeh tay kar sakta hai ki kya banaya jaaye?

**Kabir:** Doosra.

**Madhav:** Aur kaunsa ek hafte mein code seekh sakta hai, kyunki uske paas har cheez ke liye jagah pehle se hai?

**Kabir:** Doosra hi.

**Madhav:** Ulta karna mushkil hai. Code se naksha nahi banta. Log saalon code likhte hain aur naksha phir bhi nahi hota.

**Kabir:** Aur mujhe kya kya pehle se aana chahiye?

**Madhav:** Teen cheezein. Padhna aata ho. Ginti aati ho. Aur tumne kabhi phone ya computer istemaal kiya ho.

**Kabir:** Bas?

**Madhav:** Bas. Aur ek cheez jo maine maan li hai aur woh hunar nahi hai: har chapter ke baad tum ek minute ruk kar sochoge. Agar woh nahi karoge toh yeh kitaab kaam nahi karegi, chahe tumhe kitni bhi maths aati ho.

### Naam

Yeh chapter iss kitaab ka **contract** hai. Dono taraf se.

**Jo maan kar chal raha hoon:**

```
aap padh sakte ho
aap gin sakte ho, aur aadha-dugna samajhte ho
aapne kabhi phone ya computer istemaal kiya hai
aap har chapter ke baad ek minute rukoge
```

**Jo nahi maan raha:**

```
aapko maths aati hai
aapne pehle kabhi coding ki hai
aap kisi technical shabd ka matlab jaante ho
aapko yaad hai school mein kya padha tha
```

**Jo yeh kitaab degi:**

```
har parat kaam kaise karti hai, aur kyun waisi hai
kisi bhi nayi cheez ko naksh e pe rakhne ki taakat
ek product ko kaagaz pe design karne ki samajh
AI se woh nikaalna jo expert nikaalte hain
```

**Jo yeh kitaab nahi degi:**

```
code likhne ki kabiliyat
kisi language mein maharat
haath se kaam karne ka tajurba
dikhane laayak koi banaya hua saaman
```

Samajh pehle aati hai aur use chhoda nahi ja sakta. Kabiliyat alag cheez hai, woh karne se aati hai, aur woh iske baad aati hai.

### Asli duniya se ek example

Ek building ka architect aur ek mistri.

Architect ne shayad zindagi mein ek deewar nahi banayi. Woh cement nahi ghol sakta, sariya nahi bandh sakta. Us hisaab se woh mistri se kam kaabil hai.

Lekin architect yeh tay karta hai ki building kaisi hogi, kitni manzil sahi hai, kahan wazan padega, kahan roshni aayegi, aur kya cheez giregi.

Mistri ko yeh sab nahi aata, aur usne poori zindagi deewarein banayi hain.

Dono kaam ke hain. Dono ko paisa milta hai. Lekin building ki shakal architect tay karta hai.

Yeh kitaab aapko architect banati hai. Mistri banna alag padhai hai, aur bahut aasan ho jaati hai jab naksha pehle se dimaag mein ho.

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **yeh sochna ki samajhne se pehle karna zaroori hai.**

"Pehle coding seekh lo, phir sab samajh aa jaayega." Yeh salaah har jagah milti hai.

Woh kabhi kabhi kaam karti hai, aur bahut mehngi hai. Kyunki bina naksha ke code seekhne ka matlab hai hazaaron chhote tukde yaad karna bina yeh jaane ki woh kis cheez ka hissa hain. Log saal-do-saal lagate hain aur unhe abhi bhi nahi pata ki jo cheez woh likh rahe hain woh kahan chal rahi hai.

Yeh galti tempting isliye hai ki karne se **turant kuch dikhta hai**. Pehle din kuch chal jaata hai screen pe. Samajhne mein pehle hafte kuch nahi dikhta.

Aur ek aur wajah: karne ki salaah dene wale zyadatar log woh hain jinke paas naksha **already hai**. Unhe yaad nahi ki woh unhe kab aur kaise mila, isliye woh maan lete hain ki woh karne se hi aa gaya tha.

### Sochne ke liye

Har sawal pe ek minute socho, phir neeche jawab padho.

**1. (samajh check)** Madhav ne kaha ki khaana pachne ki "ek parat ki samajh" galat nahi hai, bas gehri nahi hai. Aisi teen cheezein batao jo aap roz istemaal karte ho aur jinki aapko ek parat ki samajh hai. Kya kabhi us kami se aapka kaam ruka?

> **Jawab:** Aam jawab: gaadi, bijli, bank, dawai, internet.
>
> Aur zyadatar logon ke liye kaam kabhi nahi rukta, kyunki woh un cheezon ko sirf **istemaal** karte hain.
>
> Kaam tab rukta hai jab aap kuch chunna chahte ho ya banana chahte ho. Do dawaiyon mein se behtar chunni ho. Do phone mein se behtar chunna ho. Kisi ko batana ho ki kya banaya jaaye.
>
> Isse ek saaf rule nikalta hai: **jab tak aap sirf istemaal kar rahe ho, ek parat kaafi hai. Jis din chunna ya banana ho, us din nahi.**

**2. (samajh check)** Kitaab kehti hai ki woh coding nahi sikhayegi. Kabir ne poocha "toh faayda kya hai." Ab aap uska jawab do, apne shabdon mein, ek line mein.

> **Jawab:** Kai theek jawab hain. Sabse mazboot yeh hai:
>
> **Code likhna aaj sabse sasti cheez hai. Yeh jaanna ki kya likhna chahiye, sabse mehngi.**
>
> Aur Chapter 0.2 ki bhasha mein: code likhna ek hunar hai jo lakhon logon ke paas hai, toh uska scarcity dial neeche hai. Yeh tay karna ki kya banaya jaaye, woh bahut kam log kar paate hain.
>
> Ek aur seedha jawab: is kitaab ke baad koi bhi nayi technology aayegi toh aapko uske liye jagah dhoondhni nahi padegi. Woh khabar nahi lagegi.

**3. (jodne wala)** Chapter 0.5 mein Kabir ne kaha tha ki tukdon mein seekhne se har cheez "khabar" lagti hai. Iss chapter ka architect wala example uss baat se kaise judta hai?

> **Jawab:** Architect ke paas ek dhaancha hai: wazan, roshni, jagah, kharcha. Jab woh koi nayi building dekhta hai, toh use nayi cheez nahi dikhti. Use dikhta hai ki wahi jaani-pehchani cheezein iss baar kaise jodi gayi hain.
>
> Mistri jo sirf deewarein banata aaya hai, use har nayi design ek nayi cheez lagti hai.
>
> Wahi farak Kabir ka hai. Jab dhaancha nahi hota toh har cheez alag se aati hai aur alag se bhoolni padti hai. Jab dhaancha hota hai toh nayi cheez sirf ek farak hoti hai, poori cheez nahi.
