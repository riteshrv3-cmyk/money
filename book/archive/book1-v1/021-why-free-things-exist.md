# Chapter 3.6  [SPINE]
## Free cheezein exist kyun karti hain

---

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Chapter 0.1 ka niyam bolo.

**Kabir:** Value nahi di toh tokens nahi.

**Madhav:** Ab ek problem hai. Linux ek operating system hai. Duniya ke lagbhag saare servers uspe chalte hain, aur har Android phone ke andar bhi wahi hai. Uski keemat zero hai. Koi bhi utha kar istemaal kar sakta hai, badal sakta hai, aur bech bhi sakta hai.

**Kabir:** Toh usne banane wale ko kya diya?

**Madhav:** Yehi sawal hai. Aur Linux akela nahi hai. Python free hai. Woh database jo aadhi duniya chalata hai, free hai. Woh saara AI ka saaman jo abhi tum sunte ho, bahut sa free hai.

**Kabir:** Toh log ise banate kyun hain?

**Madhav:** Pehle ek aur sawal. Ek company ko ek database chahiye. Do raaste hain: khud banaye, ya khareede. Ab teesra raasta socho.

**Kabir:** Kisi aur ke banaye hue ko istemaal kare, free mein.

**Madhav:** Aur agar usmein koi kami ho?

**Kabir:** Toh woh use theek kar le.

**Madhav:** Ab woh theek kiya hua hissa uske paas hai. Do raaste: apne paas rakhe, ya wapas de de. Rakhne mein kya hoga?

**Kabir:** Jab agla version aayega, toh use apna hissa dobara jodna padega. Har baar.

**Madhav:** Hamesha ke liye. Aur de de toh?

**Kabir:** Toh woh agle version mein khud aa jaayega. Uska kaam khatam.

**Madhav:** Toh company apna kaam free mein kyun de rahi hai?

**Kabir:** Kyunki rakhne mein use zyada mehnga pad raha hai.

**Madhav:** Bas. Yeh daan nahi hai. Yeh hisaab hai.

**Kabir:** Lekin sabse pehla banane wala? Jab koi company thi hi nahi?

**Madhav:** Woh log alag wajah se banate hain. Kabhi unhe khud woh cheez chahiye hoti hai aur milti nahi. Kabhi woh naam kamana chahte hain, jo aage kaam deta hai. Kabhi unhe woh problem hi dilchasp lagti hai.

**Kabir:** Aur woh naam se kya kamate hain?

**Madhav:** Chapter 0.2 ke dials lagao. Jisne woh cheez banayi jo hazaaron log roz istemaal karte hain, uski scarcity kahan hai?

**Kabir:** Bahut upar. Bahut kam log woh bana sakte hain, aur ab woh saabit bhi hai.

**Madhav:** Toh use tokens mile ya nahi?

**Kabir:** Seedhe nahi mile. Lekin uske teeno dials upar chale gaye.

**Madhav:** Ab ek aur wajah, jo sabse badi hai. Ek company kyun apni bani hui cheez free mein de degi, jaan-boojh ke, shuru se?

**Kabir:** Samajh nahi aaya.

**Madhav:** Socho. Tumhare paas ek cheez hai jo log istemaal karte hain, aur ek cheez hai jo uske upar chalti hai aur bikti hai. Kaunsi free karoge?

**Kabir:** Neeche wali. Taaki zyada log upar wali tak aayein.

**Madhav:** Aur agar woh neeche wali sab jagah pahunch jaaye, toh baaki sabko kya karna padega?

**Kabir:** Usi ke hisaab se chalna padega.

**Madhav:** Toh free dene se kya mila?

**Kabir:** Woh cheez sabki ban gayi, aur uske aas-paas ki har cheez uske hisaab se banegi.

**Madhav:** Bas. Free hona ek chaal bhi ho sakti hai, daan bhi, aur hisaab bhi. Teeno ek saath chalte rehte hain.

### Naam

Aisa software jiska asli code sabke liye khula ho, use **open source** kehte hain.

Char alag wajahein, aur teeno mein se koi bhi "achhai" nahi hai:

```
1. APNA KHARCHA KAM KARNA
   theek kiya hua hissa wapas do, taaki har baar dobara na jodna pade

2. NAAM AUR SAABITI
   scarcity ka dial upar, dikhaane laayak kaam
   (aksar naukri aur mauke isi se aate hain)

3. NEECHE WALI PARAT PE KABZA
   apni parat free karo taaki upar wali parat bik sake

4. KHUD KO ZAROORAT THI
   koi banata hai kyunki use chahiye tha aur mil nahi raha tha
```

Aur ab ek zaroori farak, jo log hamesha mila dete hain:

```
FREE ka matlab "muft"          →  paisa nahi lagta
FREE ka matlab "khula"          →  code dikhta hai, badal sakte ho
```

Aksar dono saath hote hain, lekin hone zaroori nahi hain. Kuch software muft hai lekin uska code bilkul band hai. Aap use istemaal kar sakte ho aur andar dekh nahi sakte.

Jab bhi koi cheez muft mile, ek hi sawal poochna kaafi hai: **iska kharcha kaun utha raha hai, aur use badle mein kya mil raha hai?** Iska jawab hamesha hota hai. Kabhi woh chanda hai, kabhi company ka hisaab, kabhi aapka data, aur kabhi upar wali parat jo bikti hai.

### Asli duniya se ek example

1991 mein ek 21 saal ka Finnish student, Linus Torvalds, ne shauk mein ek operating system ka core likhna shuru kiya. Usne likha ki yeh "kuch bada nahi hoga."

Aaj woh Linux hai. Woh duniya ke lagbhag saare web servers pe chalta hai, har Android phone ke andar hai, aur lagbhag har badi cloud company usi pe khadi hai.

Ab dilchasp hissa. Aaj Linux mein sabse zyada code aise programmers se aata hai jinhe badi companies **tankhwah** deti hain yeh kaam karne ke liye. Woh companies ise daan nahi maan rahin. Unke liye yeh sasta hai: sabko ek hi neev chahiye, aur usko akele sambhalna kisi ek ke liye bahut mehnga hai.

Toh sabse bada muft software duniya ke sabse bade karobaar chala raha hai, aur unhi ke paise se chal raha hai.

Yeh Chapter 0.1 ke niyam ko todta nahi hai. Value di ja rahi hai aur mil bhi rahi hai. Bas woh tokens ki shakal mein nahi guzarti.

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **yeh sochna ki free software ghatiya hota hai.**

Log maante hain ki jiska paisa nahi liya gaya woh kamzor hoga, ya usmein koi chaal hogi.

Sachai lagbhag ulti hai. Jo cheezein sabse zyada bhrose ki hain, woh aksar khuli hain: Linux, aur woh cryptography jo aapke bank ki suraksha karti hai, aur woh database jo aspatal chalate hain.

Wajah seedhi hai: jab code sabke saamne khula ho, toh hazaaron log usmein kami dhoondh sakte hain. Band code mein sirf usi company ke log dekh sakte hain.

Yeh galti templing isliye hai ki baaki har cheez mein daam aur quality ka rishta hota hai. Software mein woh rishta toota hua hai, kyunki software ki nakal banane ka kharcha lagbhag zero hai. Ek hi cheez ek aadmi ke paas ho ya das karod ke paas, banane wale ka kharcha wahi rehta hai.

Aur yeh ek gehri baat hai: **jis cheez ki nakal muft hai, uska daam uski quality nahi batata.** Yeh baat aage software ke poore karobaar ko samjhati hai, aur Book 2 mein AI models pe bhi lagegi.

### Part 3 ka gate

**1.** Ek dost kehta hai "computer Python samajhta hai." Aap kya kahoge? *(nahi aaya toh 3.1)*

> **Jawab:** Computer sirf apni chip ke numbers samajhta hai. Python ek insaani suvidha hai, jise ek doosra program numbers mein badalta hai.

**2.** Aapke phone mein tees app "khule" hain lekin chip mein aath hisse hain. Yeh kaise chal raha hai? *(nahi aaya toh 3.4, 3.5)*

> **Jawab:** Woh chal nahi rahe. Zyadatar ruki hui haalat mein hain, RAM mein pade hue. OS baari baantta hai, itni jaldi ki aapko pata na chale.

**3.** Ek company apna bana hua software free mein de deti hai. Chapter 0.1 ka niyam toota? *(nahi aaya toh 3.6)*

> **Jawab:** Nahi. Value di bhi ja rahi hai aur mil bhi rahi hai, bas tokens ki shakal mein nahi. Kabhi apna kharcha bachta hai, kabhi scarcity ka dial upar jaata hai, kabhi upar wali parat bikne lagti hai.
