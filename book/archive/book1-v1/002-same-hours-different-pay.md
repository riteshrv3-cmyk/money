# Chapter 0.2  [SPINE]
## Same hours, different pay

---

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Usi gaon mein do log hain. Ravi paani dhota hai, sooraj se pehle nikalta hai, andhere mein lautta hai, bees saal se. Ek din chhutti nahi li. Uska ghar chhota hai. Meera haddi jodti hai. Kuch hafte chaar ghante kaam karti hai, kuch hafte bilkul nahi. Uska ghar gaon mein sabse bada hai.

**Kabir:** Toh gaon Ravi ke saath galat kar raha hai.

**Madhav:** Kaun kar raha hai galat? Naam batao.

**Kabir:** Panchayat.

**Madhav:** Koi panchayat nahi hai. Kisi ne rate tay nahi kiye. Har aadmi apni marzi se de raha hai.

**Kabir:** Toh phir aisa hua kaise?

**Madhav:** Yehi sawal hai. Teen tareeke se dekhte hain. Pehla: Ravi aaj paani na laaye toh tumhara kya jaayega?

**Kabir:** Mujhe khud nadi tak jaana padega. Ek ghanta.

**Madhav:** Aur Meera aaj tumhari tooti haddi na jode toh?

**Kabir:** Haddi tedhi jud jaayegi.

**Madhav:** Kitne din ke liye?

**Kabir:** Hamesha ke liye. Main zindagi bhar langdaunga.

**Madhav:** Toh ek ghanta, aur chaalis saal. Dono ne mehnat ki. Lekin dono ne jo cheez roki, woh barabar hai?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Bilkul nahi.

**Madhav:** Toh pehli baat: value mehnat se nahi aati. Woh us problem ke size se aati hai jo tum hatate ho. Ab doosra sawal. Paani gaon mein aur kitne log dho sakte hain?

**Kabir:** Sab. Matka uthana koi hunar nahi hai.

**Madhav:** Aur haddi kitne jod sakte hain?

**Kabir:** Sirf Meera.

**Madhav:** Ravi kal chhod de toh?

**Kabir:** Dopahar tak koi aur dho raha hoga.

**Madhav:** Meera kal chhod de toh?

**Kabir:** Das saal koi nahi hoga.

**Madhav:** Toh gaon ko Meera ko rokna padega. Ravi ko rokna zaroori nahi hai. Yeh zulm hai ya hisaab?

**Kabir:** Hisaab. Lekin bura lagta hai.

**Madhav:** Bura lagna aur galat hona alag cheezein hain. Ab teesra aadmi. Anil naam ka banda Ravi ko roz aate-jaate dekhta hai. Do saal lagakar woh nadi se gaon tak ek naali khodta hai. Do saal tak woh aisa kuch nahi banata jo koi pee sake. Phir naali poori ho jaati hai aur paani roz subah khud aata hai. Anil ghar chala jaata hai. Ab ginti karo. Ravi ne kitne saal kaam kiya?

**Kabir:** Bees.

**Madhav:** Uske bees saal ka kaam aaj kahan hai?

**Kabir:** Khatam. Jo paani dhoya woh pee liya gaya.

**Madhav:** Anil ne kitne saal kaam kiya?

**Kabir:** Do.

**Madhav:** Aur uske do saal ka kaam aaj kahan hai?

**Kabir:** Naali mein. Woh abhi bhi chal rahi hai.

**Madhav:** Anil ne Ravi se zyada mehnat ki?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Bahut kam ki.

**Madhav:** Toh farak kya tha?

**Kabir:** Anil ki mehnat ek cheez ke andar chali gayi, aur woh cheez uske bina bhi chalti rahi.

**Madhav:** Ab teeno ko saath rakho. Kisi ko kitna milega, yeh kya tay karta hai?

**Kabir:** Problem ka size. Kitne log woh kaam kar sakte hain. Aur kaam tikta hai ya khatam ho jaata hai.

**Madhav:** Teeno jodkar, ya guna karke?

**Kabir:** Guna. Kyunki agar ek bhi zero ho toh kuch nahi bachta. Anil ki naali se agar koi paani na peeta, toh woh kitni bhi durlabh hoti, uska kuch nahi milta.

**Madhav:** Sahi. Ab formula dobara bolo, aur mujhe batao ki usmein kya nahi hai.

**Kabir:** Size, guna kitne kam log, guna kitni baar wapas aata hai. Ghante usmein nahi hain.

**Madhav:** Ek baar bhi nahi. Aur yeh us gaon mein sach tha jahan na paisa tha, na malik, na koi economy. Yeh kisi ka banaya hua rule nahi hai. Yeh exchange se apne aap nikalta hai.

**Kabir:** Toh phir sab log yeh kyun maante hain ki ghanton ka paisa milta hai?

**Madhav:** Kyunki jab teeno dials neeche hon, tab ghante hi ek matra lever bachta hai. Aur jo ek matra lever bachta hai, woh mechanism lagne lagta hai.

---

### Naam

Teen cheezein, guna hokar:

```
        jo problem aap solve karte ho, uska size
    x   kitne kam log woh solve kar sakte hain
    x   aapka kaam kitni baar wapas aata rehta hai
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────
    =   aapko kitna milta hai
```

Doosri cheez ka naam hai **scarcity**. Teesri ka naam hai **leverage**.

Ghante formula mein nahi hain. Na guna, na jode, kahin nahi.

Lagbhag har koi maanta hai ki use ghanton ka paisa milta hai. Woh mechanism nahi hai. Woh woh hai jo bachta hai jab mechanism aapki pahunch se bahar ho.

---

### Asli duniya se ek example

2014 mein Facebook ne WhatsApp ko lagbhag 19 billion dollar mein khareeda.

Us waqt WhatsApp mein **55 log** kaam karte the, jinmein sirf 32 engineers the. Aur unke app ko lagbhag 45 crore log har mahine istemaal karte the.

Ek engineer, lagbhag ek crore chalees lakh users.

Formula pe chalao. Size: logon ko ek doosre se baat karni hai, jo seedhi zaroorat hai. Scarcity: us waqt bahut kam log itne bade paimane pe chalne wala messaging system bana sakte the. Leverage: poora upar. Code ek baar likha gaya aur woh raat-din chalta raha, chahe likhne wala so raha ho.

Ab isi ke saath ek postman ki tulna karo, jo bhi sandesh pahunchane ka hi kaam karta hai. Wahi zaroorat. Postman roz chalta hai aur uska kaam har shaam khatam ho jaata hai.

Ravi aur Anil, doosri shakal mein. Field nahi badla. Position badli.

---

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **yeh maan lena ki sahi field chun lena hi asli faisla hai.**

Log saal-do-saal iss sawal pe atak jaate hain: doctor banun ya engineer, coding karun ya business. Jaise ek darwaza sahi ho aur baaki galat.

Lekin har field mein dono taraf ke log hain. Padhane mein bhi crore kamane wale hain aur mushkil se guzara karne wale bhi. Ilaaj mein bhi. Khaane mein bhi. Ek hi field ke andar ka farak, do alag fieldon ke farak se kahin bada hai.

```
PADHANA
  das students wala tutor        ←→  jiski kitaab ek crore log padhte hain

KHAANA
  ek kitchen mein cook            ←→  jiske do sau kitchen hain

ILAAJ
  roz tees mareez dekhne wala     ←→  jisne woh ilaaj banaya jo sab istemaal karte hain

SANGEET
  shaadi mein bajane wala          ←→  jiski recording sote waqt bajti rehti hai
```

Yeh galti tempting isliye hai kyunki field ka faisla **dikhta hai**. Uska ek naam hai, ek degree hai, log poochte hain. Position ka faisla dikhta nahi. Uska koi naam nahi hota aur woh ek din mein nahi hota, isliye lagta hai ki woh faisla hai hi nahi.

### Map pe

```
        KAM                                          ZYADA
        │                                              │
size    chhoti problem, bina bhi chal jaaye  ←→  badi problem, bina nahi chalti
scarce  bahut log kar sakte hain             ←→  bahut kam log kar sakte hain
leverage kaam karte hi khatam                ←→  kaam aapke bina bhi aata rehta hai
```

Teen dials. Jo milta hai woh teeno ka guna hai, isiliye ek dial ko neeche se upar karna sab kuch badal sakta hai, aur kisi dial ko chhue bina sirf zyada der kaam karne se lagbhag kuch nahi badalta.

### Sochne ke liye

Har sawal pe ek minute socho, phir neeche jawab padho.

**1. (samajh check)** Ravi Anil ki naali dekhta hai aur sochta hai ki use aur mehnat karni chahiye. Woh bees ki jagah chalees matke roz dhone lagta hai aur uski kamai lagbhag dugni ho jaati hai. Meera aur Anil ki nahi badalti. Iss tareeke se uski kamai zyada se zyada kahan tak ja sakti hai, aur wahan ruk kyun jaati hai?

> **Jawab:** Ek din mein jitne matke ek insaan ka sharir dho sakta hai, bas wahan tak. Shayad saath, shayad assi. Uske baad kuch nahi.
>
> Ceiling ki wajah yeh hai ki Ravi apna **waqt** bech raha hai, aur waqt roz utna hi milta hai. Uska leverage dial zero pe atka hai, aur zero se guna karo toh mehnat kitni bhi ho, jawab chhota hi rehta hai.
>
> Yeh important isliye hai ki Ravi ka faisla galat nahi hai. Zyada mehnat se sach mein zyada milta hai. Bas woh raasta ek deewar pe khatam hota hai, aur woh deewar uske sharir mein hai. Anil ke raaste mein aisi koi deewar nahi thi.

**2. (samajh check)** Teen dials mein se kaunsa ek aadmi sabse jaldi badal sakta hai, aur kaunsa sabse dheere? Har ek ke liye actually kya chahiye, woh socho.

> **Jawab:** Sabse tez **problem ka size** hai. Aap kal se hi badi problem pe kaam karna shuru kar sakte ho. Kisi ki ijaazat nahi chahiye, sirf yeh chunna hai ki kis problem pe waqt lagana hai. Ravi agar kal se naali khodna shuru kare toh usne yeh dial usi din ghuma diya.
>
> Sabse dheema **scarcity** hai. Woh cheez seekhne mein saal lagte hain jo bahut kam log kar sakte hain. Meera ke saalon ka koi shortcut nahi tha.
>
> **Leverage** beech mein hai, aur uski shakal alag hai. Woh dheere-dheere nahi badhta. Woh ek din chalu hota hai. Anil ki naali do saal tak zero thi, phir ek din poori. Isliye leverage sabse zyada chhoda jaata hai: uska bada hissa aisa dikhta hai jaise kuch ho hi nahi raha.

**3. (jodne wala)** Pichhle chapter mein paisa "value dene ka record" tha. Anil ko do saal baad bhi paani ka hissa milta rehta hai. Woh record kis cheez ka hai, aur woh value di kab gayi thi?

> **Jawab:** Woh record us naali ka hai, na ki aaj ke kisi kaam ka. Value do saal pehle di gayi thi, jab woh khod raha tha.
>
> Ravi roz value deta hai aur roz record kamata hai. Anil ne do saal ek saath value di aur aisa record kamaya jo baar baar kaam aata hai.
>
> Toh gaon Anil ko "kuch na karne" ke paise nahi de raha. Woh us kaam ke paise de raha hai jo poora ho chuka hai aur ab bhi kaam kar raha hai. Yeh Chapter 0.1 wale rule ka seedha nateeja hai: value di gayi thi, isliye tokens aa rahe hain.
>
> Yahin par leverage ki asli definition milti hai: **aisi value dena jo ek baar di jaaye aur baar baar mile.**
