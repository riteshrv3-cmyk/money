### Roz ke sawal

```
"Mera phone dheema hai / app band ho rahe hain"

   →  3.5 process, aur "khula" hona matlab "chal raha" nahi

   →  4.3 bandwidth aur latency alag cheezein hain

"Yeh app muft kaise hai?"
   →  3.6 free cheezein exist kyun karti hain
   →  6.8 kharcha kaun utha raha hai
```
