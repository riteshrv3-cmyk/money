### Naam

Daava wahi rehta hai, aur ab woh mazboot hai kyunki usne chaar hamle jhel liye:

> **Paisa system mein sirf ek jagah se ghusta hai: jab ek insaan apni zaroorat ke liye deta hai. Baaki har cheez us paise ka batwara hai.**

Lekin jaanch se teen nayi baatein nikli hain, jo daave mein nahi thin.

**Ek: raasta lamba ho sakta hai.**

```
seedha       insaan  →  aap
ek kadam     insaan  →  company  →  aap
kai kadam    insaan  →  company  →  cloud  →  chip  →  aap
ghooma hua   insaan  →  vigyapan  →  app  →  aap
                        (aur wahi insaan doosre sire pe bhi)
udhaar pe    insaan  →  (aage kabhi)  →  nivesh  →  aap
```

**Do: raasta lamba hone ka matlab hai ki aapko dikhta nahi ki kaun de raha hai.** Aur jab aapko nahi dikhta, toh aap yeh bhi nahi dekh paate ki woh kab rukega. Chip banane wale ko pata nahi hota ki uska paisa kis insaan ki kaunsi zaroorat se aa raha hai.

**Teen, aur yeh sabse zaroori hai: paisa kahan se aata hai aur kahan rukta hai, yeh do alag sawal hain.**

Yeh daava sirf pehle sawal ka jawab deta hai. Doosre ka jawab Chapter 0.2 se aata hai: value, scarcity, leverage.

Linux mein paisa hai, bahut hai. Woh Linux mein nahi rukta. Protocol likhne walon ne bahut value di aur bahut kam paisa liya (Chapter 4.8). Cloud company ke paas paisa rukta hai, kyunki jo cheez sabko chahiye aur bahut kam log de sakte hain, wahi rokti hai.

**Paisa neeche se aata hai aur wahan rukta hai jahan scarcity hoti hai.**

---

### Asli duniya se ek example

Ek chip banane wali company ke paas aaj duniya ki lagbhag saari AI wali chips ka bahut bada hissa hai.

Uska paisa kahan se aata hai? Woh AI companies ko bechti hai. Woh companies apni cheezein doosri companies ko bechti hain. Woh companies apne product aam logon ko bechti hain. Aur woh log apni zaroorat ke liye paisa dete hain.

Chaar kadam. Har kadam pe kuch paisa rukta hai.

Ab sawal yeh hai ki sabse zyada kahan rukta hai. Jawab wahan hai jahan sabse kam vikalp hain.

Aur yahi wajah hai ki us chip company ke paas itna paisa ruk raha hai. Uske product ka koi aasan vikalp nahi hai. Baaki har kadam pe kai vikalp hain.

Chapter 0.2 ka doosra dial, poore paimane pe. **Scarcity tay karti hai ki paisa kahan rukta hai, chahe woh kahin aur se aaya ho.**

---

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **yeh sochna ki "main sirf companies ko bechta hoon, toh aam log mere liye mayne nahi rakhte."**

Har chain aakhir mein kisi insaan pe khatm hoti hai. Agar woh insaan rukta hai, toh poori chain rukti hai, chahe aap us se paanch kadam door ho.

Iska seedha nateeja: log samajh nahi paate ki jab mandi girti hai toh unka karobaar kyun gir jaata hai, jabki unka aur aam logon ka koi seedha rishta nahi hai.

Doosri galti, aur yeh ulti hai: **yeh sochna ki jo cheez muft di ja rahi hai uska koi kharcha nahi hai.**

Har muft cheez ka kharcha koi utha raha hai. Sawal sirf yeh hai ki kaun, aur badle mein kya. Chapter 3.6 aur 6.8 dono yeh sawal poochh chuke hain, aur woh hamesha jawab deta hai.

### Part 6 ka gate

**1.** Aapne das machinein lagayin aur site phir bhi dheemi hai. Sabse pehle kahan dekhoge? *(nahi aaya toh 6.1)*

> **Jawab:** Us jagah jahan sab das milti hain, aksar database. Bojh hata nahi, khisak gaya.

**2.** Aapka system 100 per second sambhal sakta hai aur 101 aa rahe hain. Woh thoda dheema hoga ya baith jaayega? *(nahi aaya toh 6.6)*

> **Jawab:** Baith sakta hai. Log intezaar chhod kar dobara koshish karenge, jisse bojh khud ba khud badhta jaayega.
>
> Systems dheere-dheere kharaab nahi hote. Woh ek seema tak theek chalte hain aur phir jhatke mein girte hain.

**3.** Ek app 45 crore users ko 55 logon se chala. Do wajahein batao. *(nahi aaya toh 6.8, 0.2)*

> **Jawab:** Ek: uska product likhawat pe khada tha, jo sabse sasti cheez hai rakhne aur bhejne mein.
>
> Do: uska leverage bahut upar tha. Har naya user lagbhag koi naya kharcha nahi laata tha.
