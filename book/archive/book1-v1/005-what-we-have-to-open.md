# Chapter 0.5  [SPINE]
## Toh ab hume kya kholna padega

---
### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Ab tak tumhare paas do cheezein hain. Ek naksha, jo batata hai ki society ka kaam kahan hota hai. Aur ek formula, jo batata hai ki kisi ko kitna milega. Ab in dono ko saath rakho aur mujhe batao ki tumhe seekhna kya chahiye.

**Kabir:** Level chaar. AI.

**Madhav:** Kyun?

**Kabir:** Kyunki wahi sabse upar hai.

**Madhav:** Sabse upar hone se kya milta hai?

**Kabir:** Zyada... paisa?

**Madhav:** Pichhle chapter mein hi humne dekha ki level se kuch nahi milta. Dobara socho. Formula ke teen dials lo, ek-ek karke. Pehla, problem ka size. Woh kis level pe tay hota hai?

**Kabir:** Level ek pe. Insaan ki zaroorat pe.

**Madhav:** Aur woh list badalti hai?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Paanch hazaar saal se wahi hai.

**Madhav:** Toh us dial ko samajhne ke liye tumhe kya seekhna padega?

**Kabir:** Kuch bhi nahi. Main khud insaan hoon, mujhe pata hai logon ko kya chahiye.

**Madhav:** Bas. Pehla dial khula hua hai. Ab doosra, scarcity. Woh kis cheez pe khada hai?

**Kabir:** Iss baat pe ki kitne log woh kaam kar sakte hain.

**Madhav:** Aur kaunsa level aisa hai jise sabse kam log samajhte hain?

**Kabir:** Level teen. Jo chhupa hua hai.

**Madhav:** Toh scarcity ka dial apne aap kahan sabse upar hoga?

**Kabir:** Wahin, jahan chhupa hua hai.

**Madhav:** Ab teesra. Leverage. Anil ki naali. Aaj ke zamane mein woh "naali" kya hai?

**Kabir:** Software. Ya koi system. Koi machine.

**Madhav:** Woh kis level pe baithte hain?

**Kabir:** Teen pe. Phir se teen pe.

**Madhav:** Toh teeno dials ek saath kahan ishaara kar rahe hain?

**Kabir:** Ek dial khula hai, aur baaki do ek hi chhupi hui parat ke peeche hain.

**Madhav:** Bas. Yeh poori kitaab ka tark hai, ek line mein. Ab ek chetavni, kyunki tum abhi ek galat nateeja nikalne wale ho. Kya main yeh keh raha hoon ki level teen pe jaane se tum ameer ho jaoge?

**Kabir:** Nahi... kyunki har level pe neeche wale log bhi hain.

**Madhav:** Sahi. Main sirf itna keh raha hoon ki jo do dials tumhare haath mein hain, dono ek hi darwaze ke peeche hain. Darwaza kholna zaroori hai. Kaafi nahi hai. Ab aakhri sawal, aur yeh sabse practical hai. Us parat ko samajhne ke do tareeke hain. Batao.

**Kabir:** Ek toh yeh ki jo cheez saamne aaye woh padh lo. Aaj database, kal cloud.

**Madhav:** Tum abhi tak yehi kar rahe the.

**Kabir:** Haan.

**Madhav:** Aur nateeja?

**Kabir:** Har hafte kuch naya sunta hoon jo baaki sabko pehle se pata tha.

**Madhav:** Kyun? Tum sust toh nahi ho.

**Kabir:** Kyunki har cheez alag se aati hai. Uske liye mere paas koi jagah nahi hoti.

**Madhav:** Aur jab jagah na ho toh har nayi cheez kya lagti hai?

**Kabir:** Khabar. Aur khabar hamesha der se aati hai.

**Madhav:** Toh doosra tareeka kya hoga?

**Kabir:** Neeche se poora dhaancha banana. Pehle jagah banao, phir cheezein apne aap baith jaayengi.

**Madhav:** Woh dheema lagta hai.

**Kabir:** Shuru mein.

**Madhav:** Aur baad mein?

**Kabir:** Baad mein... har agli cheez sasti ho jaati hai. Kyunki mujhe yaad nahi karni padti, sirf jagah dhoondhni padti hai.

**Madhav:** Toh hum kahan se shuru karenge?

**Kabir:** Sabse neeche se. Jo meri zindagi se sabse door cheez lagti hai.

**Madhav:** Ek switch se. Chaar hafte lagenge. Uske baad AI tumhe ek alag duniya nahi, ek aur chapter lagega.

### Naam

Iss kitaab ka baaki hissa woh doosra tareeka hai, poora ka poora.

Aur order ulta lagega, toh yeh saaf keh deta hoon. Hum sabse neeche se shuru karenge, ek switch se, jo aapki rozmarra ki zindagi se sabse door cheez lagti hai.

Woh isliye ki har parat sirf tabhi samajh aati hai jab uske neeche wali parat pehle se khadi ho. Beech se shuru karoge toh udhaar lena padega, aur udhaar liya hua gyaan hi woh gyaan hai jo tikta nahi.

Chaar hafte lagenge. Uske baad Book 2 mein jo AI hai, woh khud ba khud ek chapter jaisa lagega, na ki ek alag duniya.

---

### Asli duniya se ek example

Jab koi nayi programming language ya naya framework aata hai, ek cheez baar baar dikhti hai.

Naya seekhne wala usmein mahine lagata hai. Purana kaam karne wala usmein ek hafta lagata hai.

Wajah yeh nahi hai ki woh tez hai. Wajah yeh hai ki uske paas dhaancha pehle se hai. Jab woh nayi cheez padhta hai, toh use naye ideas nahi mil rahe. Use yeh mil raha hai ki jo ideas woh pehle se jaanta hai, unhe iss baar kis naam se bulaya gaya hai aur kaunse do-teen faisle alag liye gaye hain.

Woh yaad nahi kar raha. Woh sirf farak note kar raha hai.

Yehi cheez har layer pe kaam karti hai. Ek baar aap samajh gaye ki data store kaise hota hai, toh har naya database ek naya subject nahi rehta. Woh ek jaana-pehchana subject ban jaata hai jismein kuch alag chunav kiye gaye hain.

Yeh iss kitaab ka poora vaada hai. Aap 112 cheezein yaad nahi karoge. Aap ek dhaancha banaoge, aur uske baad nayi cheezein yaad karne ki zaroorat hi nahi padegi.

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **yeh sochna ki neeche se shuru karna dheema raasta hai.**

Aisa lagta hai ki seedha kaam ki cheez pe jaana tez hoga. AI seekhna hai toh AI padho, switch se kya matlab.

Yeh pehle do hafte tak sach lagta hai, aur uske baad ulta ho jaata hai. Kyunki jo aadmi tukdon mein seekh raha hai, use har nayi cheez zero se seekhni padti hai. Jo aadmi dhaancha bana raha hai, uski har agli cheez pichhli se sasti hoti jaati hai.

Ek saal baad tukde wale ke paas sau alag cheezein hoti hain, jinmein se aadhi bhool chuki hoti hain. Dhaanche wale ke paas ek cheez hoti hai jismein sau jagah hain.

Yeh galti tempting isliye hai ki tukdon mein seekhne mein **shuruaat mein zyada tez lagta hai**. Aapko roz kuch naya pata chalta hai. Dhaancha banane mein shuruaat mein kuch bhi kaam ka nahi milta. Ravi aur Anil, phir se, ab seekhne mein.

### Ek cheez jo saath chalegi

Iss kitaab mein hum ek chhote se product ko saath lekar chalenge:

**Ek chat app, jo aapke saare messages sambhal ke rakhta hai, aur unmein se kuch bhi dhoondh sakta hai.**

Bas itna. Aisa lagta hai ki ek aadmi ek hafte mein bana le.

Har part ke aakhir mein hum us app pe wapas aayenge. Aur har baar pata chalega ki uske andar ek aur parat chhupi thi jo pichhli baar dikhi hi nahi thi.

Kitaab ke aakhir tak aap us app ko poora khol chuke honge, upar se neeche tak, aur us waqt aapko yeh bhi pata hoga ki use banane mein kitna waqt lagta.

### Part 0 ka gate

Aage badhne se pehle in teen sawalon ka jawab do. Agar nahi aata, toh bataye gaye chapter pe wapas jao.

**1.** Ek aadmi hafte mein saath ghante kaam karta hai aur bahut kam kamata hai. Uski kamai badhane ke teen alag raaste batao, aur har raaste ke liye batao ki kaunsa dial ghum raha hai. *(nahi aaya toh 0.2)*

> **Jawab:** Teen raaste, teen dials.
>
> **Badi problem pe jaana.** Wahi hunar, lekin aisi jagah lagana jahan na hone ka nuksaan bada ho. Ek hi kaam karne wala aadmi kisi aur jagah kai guna kama sakta hai, sirf isliye ki wahan uska rukna zyada mehnga padta hai. Dial: size.
>
> **Aisa kuch seekhna jo kam log kar sakte hain.** Dheema, saalon ka kaam. Dial: scarcity.
>
> **Aisi cheez banana jo uske bina chale.** Ek auzaar, ek system, ek likhi hui cheez, ek business jismein doosre log kaam karein. Dial: leverage.
>
> Ek chauthi cheez bhi log aksar kehte hain: aur zyada ghante. Woh dial hai hi nahi, aur uski ek deewar hai. Chapter 0.2 ka pehla sawal wahi tha.

**2.** Level 3 ki koi cheez uthao, aur teen level 2 wale kaam batao jinhe woh guna karti hai. Phir batao ki woh level 1 ki kaunsi zaroorat tak pahunchti hai. *(nahi aaya toh 0.3)*

> **Jawab:** Ek udaharan: GPS.
>
> Woh delivery ko guna karta hai (ek banda 4 ki jagah 25 order kar leta hai), taxi ko guna karta hai (bina raasta poochhe kahin bhi), aur samaan bhejne wali company ko guna karta hai (poora truck kahan hai, hamesha pata).
>
> Level 1 tak: khaana, judav, aur samaan ka pahunchna.
>
> Sahi jawab kai hain. Check yeh hai ki aapne teeno kaamon ke liye wahi ek auzaar rakha, aur teeno alag business the. Jo cheez sirf ek hi business ko guna karti hai, woh aksar level 3 nahi, us business ka ek hissa hoti hai.

**3.** Ek achhi technology aur ek buri technology mein, aapko kaunsi zyada samajh aati hai, aur aisa kyun hai? *(nahi aaya toh 0.4)*

> **Jawab:** Buri wali.
>
> Buri technology aapko andar ka hissa dikhati hai kyunki woh use chhupa nahi paati. Woh atakti hai, error deti hai, aur aapko andar jaakar theek karna padta hai. Us process mein aapko pata chal jaata hai ki andar kya hai.
>
> Achhi technology kuch nahi dikhati. Woh bas chal jaati hai.
>
> Isiliye purane zamane ke log apni cheezein zyada samajhte the. Woh hoshiyaar nahi the. Unki cheezein zyada kharaab thin.
