# Chapter 1.1  [SPINE]
## Ek switch dabane se kya hota hai

---

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Deewar pe jo switch hai, use dabao.

**Kabir:** Dabaya. Light jal gayi.

**Madhav:** Ab batao, tumne kiya kya?

**Kabir:** Switch dabaya.

**Madhav:** Switch ne kya kiya?

**Kabir:** Light chalu ki.

**Madhav:** Nahi. Switch ne light ko chhua bhi nahi. Woh deewar mein hai, light chhat pe hai. Switch ne kya kiya?

**Kabir:** Bijli jaane di.

**Madhav:** Kaise?

**Kabir:** Taar jud gaya. Pehle raasta toota hua tha, ab jud gaya.

**Madhav:** Bas. Ek switch sirf ek kaam karta hai: raasta jodta hai ya todta hai. Aur uski kitni haalatein hain?

**Kabir:** Do. Juda hua, ya toota hua.

**Madhav:** Sirf do? Aadha juda hua nahi ho sakta?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Ya toh bijli jaa rahi hai ya nahi jaa rahi.

**Madhav:** Ise likhne ka ek tareeka batao. Sabse chhota tareeka.

**Kabir:** Haan ya na. Chalu ya band.

**Madhav:** Aur agar sirf do nishaan istemaal karne hon?

**Kabir:** 1 aur 0.

**Madhav:** Theek hai. Ab ek sawal poochta hoon jo ajeeb lagega. Tumhare ghar mein kitne switch hain?

**Kabir:** Pandrah, bees.

**Madhav:** Aur tumhare phone ke andar?

**Kabir:** Phone mein switch kahan hote hain?

**Madhav:** Poocha maine. Andaza lagao.

**Kabir:** Kuch nahi honge. Woh toh chip hai.

**Madhav:** Ek aam phone ki chip mein aaj kuch **arab** switch hote hain. Itne chhote ki ek baal ki mothai mein hazaaron aa jaayein. Lekin kaam wahi karte hain jo deewar wala karta hai. Raasta jodo ya todo.

**Kabir:** Arab switch se kya hoga? Woh toh sirf light jalate hain.

**Madhav:** Yehi iss poori kitaab ka sawal hai. Ek switch se kuch nahi hota. Do switch se thoda kuch hota hai. Arab switch se woh sab hota hai jo tumne aaj subah se ab tak kiya hai. Har photo, har message, har video, har jawab jo tumhe AI ne diya.

**Kabir:** Yeh maanna mushkil hai.

**Madhav:** Maanna mat. Hum ise banayenge. Agle kuch chapter mein tum khud dekh loge ki ek switch se do tak, do se ginti tak, ginti se faisle tak, aur faisle se har cheez tak kaise pahunchte hain. Us din tumhe maanne ki zaroorat nahi padegi.

**Kabir:** Aur beech mein kuch aur bhi hai? Ya bas switch hi switch?

**Madhav:** Bas switch. Poori kitaab mein hum ek bhi nayi cheez neeche nahi daalenge. Jo bhi aayega, wahi arab switch alag tareeke se jude honge.

---

### Naam

Woh chhota switch jo chip ke andar hota hai, use **transistor** kehte hain.

Woh deewar wale switch se sirf teen tarah se alag hai:

```
DEEWAR KA SWITCH                TRANSISTOR
haath se dabana padta hai   →   bijli se hi dabta hai
ek second mein ek baar      →   ek second mein arbon baar
ek ungli jitna bada         →   itna chhota ki dikhta hi nahi
```

Teesri baat sabse zaroori hai, aur woh yeh hai: **ek transistor ko doosra transistor daba sakta hai.**

Iska matlab hai ki switch ek doosre ko control kar sakte hain, bina kisi insaan ke. Aur wahi cheez sab kuch mumkin banati hai. Aage do chapter isi ek baat pe hain.

---

### Asli duniya se ek example

Pehla transistor 1947 mein bana, Bell Labs mein. Woh haath mein pakadne laayak tha.

1971 mein Intel ne ek chip banayi jismein 2,300 transistor the.

Aaj ek aam phone ki chip mein lagbhag 15 se 20 arab transistor hote hain.

Ginti karo: 2,300 se 20,00,00,00,000 tak, pachas saal mein. Aur unmein se har ek wahi ek kaam karta hai jo deewar wala switch karta hai.

Poori computer ki duniya mein jo kuch bhi hua hai, woh iss ek cheez ke chhota aur sasta hone se hua hai. Koi naya idea nahi aaya. Wahi switch, bas zyada.

---

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **yeh sochna ki computer ke andar kuch aur bhi hai.**

Log maante hain ki kahin koi jaadu hai. Koi aisi cheez jo sirf engineers ko pata hai. Kuch aisa jo samajh se bahar hai.

Andar kuch nahi hai. Sirf switch hain, aur woh ek doosre ko dabate hain.

Yeh galti tempting isliye hai kyunki jo cheez computer karta hai woh switchon se bahut door lagti hai. Ek gaana, ek chehra, ek jawab. In cheezon aur "chalu ya band" ke beech ki doori itni badi hai ki dimaag maanne se mana kar deta hai.

Woh doori asli hai. Lekin usmein koi jaadu nahi hai, sirf parat pe parat hai. Aur hum har parat ko ek-ek karke rakhenge.

---

### Ek cheez jo poori kitaab mein chalegi

Iss kitaab mein hum ek chhote product ko saath lekar chalenge:

> **Ek chat app. Aapke saare messages sambhal ke rakhta hai, aur unmein se kuch bhi dhoondh sakta hai.**

Bas itna. Sunne mein lagta hai ek aadmi ek hafte mein bana le.

Har part ke aakhir mein hum iss app pe wapas aayenge, aur har baar uske andar ek nayi parat khulegi. Abhi ke liye sirf ek sawal apne dimaag mein rakh lo:

**Jab aap ek message type karte ho, woh message jaata kahan hai?**

Kitaab ke aakhir tak aapko iska poora jawab pata hoga, upar se neeche tak, aur yeh bhi ki aisa app banane mein kitna waqt lagta.

### Sochne ke liye

Har sawal pe ek minute socho, phir neeche jawab padho.

**1. (samajh check)** Ek switch ki do haalatein hain. Do switch ko saath rakho. Ab kitni alag haalatein ban sakti hain? Teen switch se?

> **Jawab:** Do switch se chaar. Teen se aath.
>
> Ginti aise karo: pehla switch do haal mein ho sakta hai, aur uski har haalat mein doosra bhi do haal mein ho sakta hai. Toh 2 x 2 = 4. Teesra aaya toh phir se dugna, 8.
>
> Yeh chhota sa hisaab poori kitaab ka sabse taakatwar hisaab hai. Har naye switch pe **dugna**. Das switch se 1,024. Bees se dus lakh se zyada.
>
> Isiliye arab switch itni badi cheez hai. Aur isiliye ek transistor ka chhota hona itna mayne rakhta hai.

**2. (samajh check)** Madhav ne kaha ki ek transistor ko doosra transistor daba sakta hai, aur yeh sabse zaroori baat hai. Deewar wale switch ke saath aisa nahi ho sakta. Kyun mayne rakhta hai ki ek switch doosre ko dabaye?

> **Jawab:** Kyunki tab insaan ki zaroorat khatam ho jaati hai.
>
> Deewar wale switch ko har baar koi ungli chahiye. Yaani woh utni hi baar chal sakta hai jitni baar koi aadmi use dabaye.
>
> Jab ek switch doosre ko dabata hai, toh ek switch dabane se ek poori shrinkhla chal padti hai, aur uske liye kisi ki zaroorat nahi. Aap ek baar tay kar dete ho ki kaun kise dabayega, aur uske baad machine khud chalti hai.
>
> **Wahi "khud chalna" hi poori technology hai.** Chapter 0.2 ki bhasha mein: yahi Anil ki naali hai. Ek baar bana do, phir woh aapke bina chalti rahe.

**3. (jodne wala)** Chapter 0.4 kehta tha ki har technology apne neeche wali cheez ko chhupa deti hai. Aapne pandrah saal phone istemaal kiya aur aaj tak transistor ke baare mein nahi socha. Kya yeh phone ki kami hai ya uski kaamyaabi?

> **Jawab:** Uski kaamyaabi. Poori tarah se.
>
> Agar phone istemaal karne ke liye aapko transistor samajhna padta, toh woh ek bekaar phone hota.
>
> Lekin ab woh doosri baat bhi yaad karo jo Kabir ne 0.4 mein khud nikaali thi: chhupi hui cheez ko aap istemaal kar sakte ho, uske upar kuch bana nahi sakte.
>
> Isliye phone theek hai. Sirf aapka kaam ab yeh hai ki uss deewar mein ek darwaza banao.
