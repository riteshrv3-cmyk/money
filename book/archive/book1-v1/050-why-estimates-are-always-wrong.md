# Chapter 7.5  [SPINE]
## Estimate hamesha galat kyun hota hai

---

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Ek kaam hai. Kisi se poocho kitna waqt lagega. Jawab kya milega?

**Kabir:** Do din.

**Madhav:** Aur asli mein?

**Kabir:** Paanch. Ya dus.

**Madhav:** Yeh hamesha hota hai, har jagah, har company mein. Toh log seekhte kyun nahi?

**Kabir:** Shayad woh jaan-boojh ke kam batate hain.

**Madhav:** Kabhi kabhi. Lekin woh log bhi galat hote hain jo apne liye anumaan lagate hain aur jinpe koi dabaav nahi hai. Toh wajah kuch aur hai. Chalo dhoondhte hain. Jab aap "do din" kehte ho, toh aap kis cheez ka waqt gin rahe ho?

**Kabir:** Kaam karne ka.

**Madhav:** Kaunsa kaam? Jo aapko dikh raha hai, ya jo dikh nahi raha?

**Kabir:** Jo dikh raha hai.

**Madhav:** Aur Chapter 7.1 mein humne kya dekha tha? Ek chhote se kaam mein kitni haalatein thin?

**Kabir:** Anginat. Aur zyadatar dikhti nahi.

**Madhav:** Toh aap kis cheez ka anumaan laga rahe the?

**Kabir:** Sirf seedhe raaste ka. Jahan sab kuch theek chalta hai.

**Madhav:** Aur asli waqt kahan jaata hai?

**Kabir:** Un raaston mein jinka pata baad mein chalta hai.

**Madhav:** Bas. Ab doosri baat. Aap dus chhote kaam ginte ho, har ek do ghante ka. Kul bees ghante. Sahi?

**Kabir:** Haan.

**Madhav:** Aur beech mein? Ek kaam se doosre pe jaane mein? Kisi se poochne mein? Kisi ke jawab ka intezaar karne mein?

**Kabir:** Woh maine gina hi nahi.

**Madhav:** Aur agar aapko kisi doosre insaan ka jawab chahiye, jo do din baad milta hai?

**Kabir:** Toh mera kaam do ghante ka hai aur do din lag gaye.

**Madhav:** Toh dusra kaaran kya hai?

**Kabir:** Intezaar. Jo kisi ke kaam mein nahi ginta lekin waqt leta hai.

**Madhav:** Ab teesri baat, aur yeh sabse gehri hai. Ek kaam jise aap pehle kar chuke ho, uska anumaan kaisa hota hai?

**Kabir:** Kaafi sahi.

**Madhav:** Aur jo aapne kabhi nahi kiya?

**Kabir:** Bahut galat.

**Madhav:** Kyun?

**Kabir:** Kyunki mujhe pata hi nahi ki usmein kya kya hai.

**Madhav:** Toh anumaan asal mein kis cheez ka naap hai?

**Kabir:** Iss baat ka ki main kitna jaanta hoon.

**Madhav:** Bas. **Anumaan waqt ka naap nahi hai. Woh gyaan ka naap hai.** Aur isiliye woh us kaam mein sabse galat hota hai jo sabse naya ho, jahan uski sabse zyada zaroorat hoti hai.

---

### Naam

Teen kaaran, aur teeno alag hain:

```
1. JO DIKHTA NAHI
   aap seedhe raaste ka anumaan lagate ho
   asli waqt tedhe raaston mein jaata hai (Chapter 7.1)

2. INTEZAAR
   kaam do ghante ka, jawab ka intezaar do din
   yeh kisi ke ghante mein nahi ginta

3. GYAAN
   jaana-pehchana kaam: anumaan theek
   naya kaam: anumaan bahut galat
   aur jo sabse zaroori kaam hote hain woh aksar naye hote hain
```

Do cheezein jo iss se nikalti hain aur kaam aati hain:

**Ek: anumaan ek range hai, ek number nahi.**

"Do din" jhoot hai. "Do se aath din, aur agar woh purani cheez tedhi nikli toh do hafte" sach hai.

Log ek number maangte hain kyunki uspe plan banana aasan hai. Aur ek number dene ka matlab hai ki aap apni anischitta chhupa rahe ho, jo baad mein kisi aur ke sar phootegi.

**Do: sabse achha ilaaj anumaan sudhaarna nahi hai. Woh kaam chhota karna hai.**

Ek do-mahine ka kaam kabhi sahi nahi aanka jaayega. Ek do-din ka kaam aksar aank liya jaayega.

Isliye achhi teamein bade kaam ko chhote tukdon mein todti hain, aur har tukda apne aap mein kuch kaam ka hota hai. Woh isliye nahi ki chhota anumaan zyada sahi hai. Woh isliye ki jaldi pata chal jaata hai ki hum kitne galat the.

Aur ek teesri baat, jo Chapter 7.1 se seedhi aati hai: **jo kaam ek purani cheez ko chhoota hai, uska anumaan hamesha sabse galat hota hai**, kyunki us purani cheez mein kya kya hai, yeh kisi ko poora nahi pata.

---

### Asli duniya se ek example

Sydney Opera House ka anumaan tha: saat lakh dollar aur chaar saal.

Woh bana das saal baad, aur uska kharcha lagbhag pandrah guna se zyada nikla.

Yeh software nahi tha. Yeh 1950 ke dashak ki baat hai.

Aur wajah wahi teeno thin: kisi ne aisi shakal pehle banayi hi nahi thi (gyaan), design banate banate badalta raha (jo dikhta nahi), aur kai alag logon ke faisle ek doosre ka intezaar karte rahe (intezaar).

Yeh iss chapter ki sabse zaroori baat hai: **yeh software ki bimari nahi hai.** Yeh har us kaam ki shakal hai jo pehle kabhi nahi kiya gaya.

Jo kaam dohraya jaata hai, uska anumaan sahi lagta hai. Ek jaisi sau building banao aur sauvi ka anumaan lagbhag theek hoga.

Software mein anumaan isliye zyada galat lagta hai kyunki **software mein dohraya jaane wala kaam hota hi nahi.** Agar woh dohraya ja sakta hai, toh usse copy kar liya jaata hai. Jo bacha rehta hai woh hamesha naya hota hai.

---

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **yeh maan lena ki anumaan behtar tajurbe se sahi ho jaayega.**

Tajurba jaane-pehchane kaam ka anumaan sudhaarta hai. Naye kaam ka nahi. Aur tees saal ka tajurba rakhne wale log bhi naye kaam mein bahut galat hote hain.

Iska seedha nateeja: companies anumaan sahi karwane ki koshish mein bahut waqt jalati hain, jab woh waqt kaam chhota karne mein lagta toh zyada faayda hota.

Doosri galti: **buffer daal dena aur maan lena ki problem hal ho gayi.**

Aap dus din ka anumaan pandrah kar dete ho. Aur woh kaam bees din leta hai. Kyunki jo cheez aapko dikh nahi rahi thi, woh 50 pratishat jodne se dikhne nahi lagti.

Teesri galti, aur yeh sabse mehngi hai: **anumaan ko waada samajhna.**

Anumaan yeh hai ki aaj kitna pata hai. Waada yeh hai ki kya hoga. Jab dono ko ek maan liya jaata hai, toh log anumaan hi dena band kar dete hain, ya bahut bade dene lagte hain taaki phanse na. Aur us din se aapke paas asli jaankari aani band ho jaati hai.

### Part 7 ka gate

**1.** Ek team kehti hai "hamare code mein koi bug nahi hai, humne poori jaanch ki hai." Kya yeh mumkin hai? *(nahi aaya toh 7.1)*

> **Jawab:** Nahi. Haalatein anginat hain, isliye poora jaanchna namumkin hai.
>
> Jaanch se yeh saabit hota hai ki jo aapne socha woh theek chalta hai. Jo aapne nahi socha, uske baare mein woh kuch nahi kehti.

**2.** Program aapke laptop pe chalta hai, server pe nahi. Sabse aam wajah? *(nahi aaya toh 7.4)*

> **Jawab:** Do machinon pe kuch alag hai, aur aksar woh cheez hai jo aapne mahine pehle lagayi thi aur bhool gaye.
>
> Hal container hai: program apni saari cheezein saath leke chalta hai.

**3.** Ek kaam ke liye "do din" bataya gaya aur usmein do hafte lage. Kaun galat tha? *(nahi aaya toh 7.5)*

> **Jawab:** Shayad koi nahi. Anumaan waqt ka naap nahi hai, gyaan ka naap hai.
>
> Do din ka matlab tha "jitna mujhe dikh raha hai woh do din ka hai." Baaki bara din un cheezon mein gaye jo us waqt dikh hi nahi rahi thin.

### Chat app pe wapas

```
Aapke chat app mein bug hain. Abhi bhi. Yeh nakaami nahi hai,
kyunki uski haalatein anginat hain aur unhe poora jaancha ja
hi nahi sakta.

Uske paas jaanch hai, taaki purani galtiyan wapas na aayein.
Uska poora itihaas rakha hai, taaki koi bhi badlav wapas liya
ja sake. Aur woh ek dabbe mein chalta hai, taaki har machine pe
bilkul ek jaisa rahe.

Aur jab kisi ne poocha tha ki isse banane mein kitna lagega,
jawab galat tha. Woh hamesha galat hota hai, kyunki woh sirf
us hisse ka anumaan tha jo us waqt dikh raha tha.
```

Ab sirf ek cheez bachi hai. Aapne har parat alag alag dekh li.

**Ab unhe ek saath rakh kar dekhte hain, ek asli product mein.** Yeh Part 8 hai, aur woh iss kitaab ka aakhri part hai.
