# Chapter 4.8  [DEPTH]
## Internet ke rules kaun banata hai

---

*DEPTH chapter. Yahan technology nahi, taakat hai. Agar aap yeh samajhna chahte ho ki paisa aur asar kahan baithta hai, toh yeh Part 4 ka sabse kaam ka chapter hai.*

---

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Pichhle chapter mein tay hua ki internet ek sehmati hai. Ab sawal: woh sehmati likhi kisne?

**Kabir:** Kisi sarkar ne?

**Madhav:** Kaunsi? Internet 190 se zyada deshon mein hai.

**Kabir:** Toh United Nations jaisa kuch?

**Madhav:** Nahi. Ab main tumhe ek cheez batata hoon jo lagbhag kisi ko pata nahi hoti. Internet ke bade niyam ek aise samooh se aate hain jiski koi sadasyata nahi hai. Koi bhi aa sakta hai. Koi vote nahi hota.

**Kabir:** Toh faisla kaise hota hai?

**Madhav:** Log baat karte hain jab tak kaafi log sehmat na ho jaayein. Woh use "rough consensus" kehte hain.

**Kabir:** Aur agar koi na maane?

**Madhav:** Kuch nahi hota. Koi saza nahi hai. Koi force nahi hai.

**Kabir:** Toh log maante kyun hain?

**Madhav:** Yehi asli sawal hai. Socho. Agar tum apna alag protocol banao toh kya hoga?

**Kabir:** Mujhse koi baat nahi kar payega.

**Madhav:** Toh taakat kahan hai?

**Kabir:** Niyam mein nahi. Iss baat mein ki baaki sab kya maan rahe hain.

**Madhav:** Bas. Ab ek aur jagah dekhte hain. Naam ki jad kaun sambhalta hai?

**Kabir:** Woh toh koi hoga hi. Chapter 4.5 mein tha ki jad sabse upar hai.

**Madhav:** Ek sanstha hai jo yeh tay karti hai ki kaunse naye tarah ke naam banenge, aur kaun unhe sambhalega.

**Kabir:** Woh kiski hai?

**Madhav:** Kisi ki nahi, aur uski kahani dilchasp hai. Kai saal tak woh America ki sarkar ke ek theke pe chalti thi. Duniya ke baaki deshon ko yeh pasand nahi tha. 2016 mein woh sarkar se alag ho gayi.

**Kabir:** Toh ab woh sabki hai?

**Madhav:** Ab woh ek aisi sanstha hai jismein sarkarein, companies aur aam log sab baithte hain. Behtar hai. Lekin dhyaan do ki woh jagah abhi bhi ek hi hai.

**Kabir:** Aur bharose wali list? Jo phone mein pehle se hoti hai?

**Madhav:** Achha sawal. Woh list kaun tay karta hai?

**Kabir:** Phone banane wala? Ya browser banane wala?

**Madhav:** Bilkul. Ek chhoti si sanstha hai jismein browser banane wali companies aur certificate dene wale baithte hain, aur woh niyam tay karte hain. Lekin aakhri faisla browser banane wale ka hai. Woh kisi ko bhi list se hata sakte hain.

**Kabir:** Aur agar hata dein?

**Madhav:** Us certificate dene wale ka karobaar khatam. Aisa hua hai, ek se zyada baar.

**Kabir:** Toh chaar-paanch companies duniya ka bharosa tay karti hain.

**Madhav:** Ab teen jagahein gin lo jahan asli taakat hai.

**Kabir:** Protocol likhne wale. Naam ki jad. Aur bharose wali list.

**Madhav:** Aur ek chauthi bhi hai, jo sabse purani hai. Woh taar kiske hain, aur woh kahan se guzarte hain.

### Naam

Chaar jagahein jahan internet ki taakat sach mein baithti hai:

```
1. PROTOCOL LIKHNE WALE
   koi sadasyata nahi, koi vote nahi, sirf sehmati
   taakat isliye hai ki baaki sab unke likhe niyam maan lete hain

2. NAAM KI JAD
   ek sanstha tay karti hai ki naye naam kaise banenge
   aur kaunsa desh ya sanstha kaunsa hissa sambhalega

3. BHAROSE WALI LIST
   gine-chune browser aur phone banane wale
   jo chaahein use bharose se bahar kar sakte hain

4. TAAR
   samundar ke neeche ke bade taar, aur woh jagahein
   jahan bade jaal aapas mein milte hain
```

Ab woh baat jo iss chapter ki asli seekh hai.

Chapter 4.7 mein tay hua tha ki internet ka koi maalik nahi hai. Woh sach hai. Lekin **"koi maalik nahi" ka matlab "koi taakat nahi" nahi hota.**

Taakat wahan chali jaati hai jahan sehmati zaroori hoti hai. Jahan sabko ek hi cheez maanni padti hai, wahan jo us cheez ko sambhalta hai, uske paas asar aa jaata hai, chahe uska koi kanooni haq na ho.

Aur ab Chapter 0.2 ke dials lagao. Ek protocol likhne wale ko kitna paisa milta hai?

Aksar bahut kam. Kai toh muft mein karte hain.

Lekin unka asar arbon logon pe padta hai. Toh unka leverage kalpana se bahar hai aur unka paisa lagbhag zero.

Yeh Chapter 0.1 ke niyam ko todta nahi. Value di gayi, aur bahut di gayi. Woh tokens mein wapas nahi aayi, kyunki unhone woh cheez banayi jise sirf muft rehne pe hi kaam karna tha.

**Jo cheez sabki sehmati se chalti hai, uska daam koi nahi le sakta. Isliye us jagah taakat toh hoti hai, paisa nahi.** Paisa uske upar wali parat pe baithta hai.

### Asli duniya se ek example

2011 mein ek certificate dene wali Dutch company, DigiNotar, hack ho gayi. Hamlawaron ne uske naam se nakli certificate banaye, jinmein Google ka bhi ek tha, aur unhe Iran mein logon ki baatein padhne ke liye istemaal kiya gaya.

Jab yeh pata chala, toh browser banane walon ne mil kar us company ko apni bharose wali list se hata diya.

Us company ke certificate duniya bhar mein turant bekaar ho gaye. Kuch hi hafton mein woh diwaaliya ho gayi.

Kisi adalat ne faisla nahi diya. Kisi sarkar ne band nahi kiya. Chaar-paanch browser banane walon ne apni ek list se ek naam hata diya, aur woh company khatam ho gayi.

Yeh saaf tarah dikhata hai ki asli taakat kahan baithi hai: kanoon mein nahi, us list mein jo pehle se sabke device mein pad chuki hai.

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **yeh sochna ki technology neutral hai aur uske faisle technical hote hain.**

Log maante hain ki protocol isliye chuna gaya ki woh behtar tha, aur niyam isliye aise hain ki technically sahi hain.

Bahut se faisle technical the. Lekin bahut se nahi. Kaunse naam ban sakte hain, kaun certificate de sakta hai, taar kis desh se guzarte hain, kaunsi baat encrypt hogi aur kaunsi nahi. Yeh sab faisle hain, aur faisle koi leta hai.

Yeh galti tempting isliye hai ki technology gani t aur logic jaisi lagti hai, aur unmein raay ki jagah nahi hoti. Lekin kaunsa hisaab kiya jaaye, yeh hamesha ek chunav hai.

Aur agar aap yeh samajhna chahte ho ki paisa aur asar kahan jaate hain, toh yeh sabse kaam ki aadat hai: **jab bhi koi technology dekho, poocho ki iske niyam kaun tay karta hai, aur agar woh kal badal de toh kya hoga.**

### Part 4 ka gate

**1.** Aapka phone bahar se pahuncha nahi ja sakta. Toh WhatsApp ka message aapke phone pe pahunchta kaise hai? *(nahi aaya toh 4.2)*

> **Jawab:** Aapka phone pehle bahar jaata hai aur ek server se juda rehta hai jiska pakka pata hai. Message us server pe aata hai aur woh use aage bhejta hai.
>
> Do phone kabhi seedha baat nahi karte. Beech mein hamesha koi hota hai.

**2.** Ek video call mein awaaz kat jaati hai aur wapas aa jaati hai, lekin jo chhoot gaya woh dobara nahi milta. File download mein aisa nahi hota. Kyun? *(nahi aaya toh 4.3)*

> **Jawab:** Do alag tareeke. Video call mein tukda kho jaaye toh chhod diya jaata hai, kyunki purani awaaz kisi kaam ki nahi. File mein har tukda dobara maanga jaata hai, kyunki ek bhi tukda gaya toh file toot jaayegi.

**3.** Ek site pe taala hai. Kya aapka data wahan surakshit hai? *(nahi aaya toh 4.6)*

> **Jawab:** Taala sirf itna kehta hai ki raaste mein koi padh nahi raha, aur naam wahi hai jo dikh raha hai.
>
> Woh us site ke maalik ke baare mein kuch nahi kehta. Thagne wali site pe bhi taala hota hai.

### Chat app pe wapas

```
Aapka message packet mein tootta hai.
Har packet pe aapka pata aur manzil ka pata likha hai.
Woh bees ajnabi machinon se guzarta hai, aur koi bhi machine
poora raasta nahi jaanti.

Woh TCP se jaata hai, kyunki ek bhi shabd khona theek nahi.
Woh taale mein band hai, isliye raaste wale padh nahi sakte.
Woh seedha doosre phone pe nahi jaata, kyunki doosra phone
bahar se pahuncha hi nahi ja sakta. Woh ek server tak jaata hai.

Aur woh server ka naam ek list mein ek line hai, jo kisi aur ke paas hai.
```

Ab woh server. Usne aapka message le liya. Aur ab?

**Usse woh sambhal kar kahan rakhega, aur agar aap chhe saal baad usmein se ek shabd dhoondho, toh woh kaise milega?** Yeh Part 5 hai.
