        Everything true in 2005 and still true in 2045.
        Never needs revision.

## Standing rules

```
LANGUAGE      Half and half. English sentence structure, Hindi where it
              lands naturally. Technical nouns ALWAYS stay English and are
              never translated. Hindi carries reasoning and connective
              tissue only.

TERMS         No term appears before the reader has needed the thing.

ONE IDEA      One new idea per chapter. Synthesis chapters are marked
              as such and are the only exception.

DIAGRAMS      Text drawings only. Every explanation written so it never
              needs a picture.

"LAYER"       Society uses LEVELS. Networks use STAGES. Neural networks
              keep LAYERS, because that word is fixed in the real world.

MARKING       Every chapter marked [SPINE] or [DEPTH].
              Book 2 chapters also carry a version and month in the footer.

BINDING       Where it is genuinely true, a part ends with one line
              connecting it back to value, scarcity and leverage from
              Chapter 0.2. Only where true, roughly six parts. A binding
              line that has nothing to say teaches the reader to skip
              that position.

MAP           Appears at the end of every part. The final map is designed
              before writing begins; every earlier version is that same
              map with pieces hidden.

EXERCISES     Three types.
              (a) samajh check, every chapter
              (b) jodne wala sawal, links to an earlier part
              (c) design sawal, once per part
              Answer sits DIRECTLY BELOW its own question, in a
              quote block. Design answers go into WORKBOOK.md.

CHAPTER       1 Problem      2 Sawal        3 Nikaalte hain
SHAPE         4 Naam         5 Asli duniya se ek example (named, real)
              6 Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain
              7 Map pe       8 Sochne ke liye, with answers below each

GATE          Every part ends with 3 questions answerable only if the
              whole part landed, plus an explicit instruction on which
              chapter to reread if not.

DISPUTED      Where the field genuinely disagrees, say so in three lines
              and give both sides. Roughly eight places across both books.
```

Running example, one per book, each complete inside its own book.
Book 1: a chat app that stores and searches your messages.
Book 2: the same app, but it answers questions about your documents.
