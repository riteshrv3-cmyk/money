# Chapter 1.6  [SPINE]
## Ek machine sab kuch kyun kar sakti hai

---

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Ek sawal jo shayad tumne kabhi socha hi nahi. Tumhare paas ek chaaku hai, ek chammach hai, ek taala hai. Kya ek chaaku taala ban sakta hai?

**Kabir:** Nahi.

**Madhav:** Aur tumhara phone? Woh camera hai, ghadi hai, radio hai, kitaab hai, calculator hai, naksha hai, aur chitthi bhejne ki machine bhi.

**Kabir:** Haan.

**Madhav:** Toh phone ke andar itni saari alag machinein hain?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Ek hi chip hai.

**Madhav:** Toh ek hi machine itni saari cheezein kaise ban jaati hai? Aur chaaku kyun nahi ban paata?

**Kabir:** Kyunki phone mein alag-alag app hain.

**Madhav:** App hai kya?

**Kabir:** Ek program.

**Madhav:** Aur program hai kya?

**Kabir:** Nirdeshon ki list. Kya karna hai, kis order mein.

**Madhav:** Aur woh list rakhi kahan hai?

**Kabir:** Phone ke andar.

**Madhav:** Kis shakal mein?

**Kabir:** Numbers mein. Kyunki andar sirf numbers hi hote hain.

**Madhav:** Ab ruko. Yeh baat abhi tumhare haath se nikal gayi, isliye dobara bolo. Program kis shakal mein rakha hai?

**Kabir:** Numbers ki tarah.

**Madhav:** Aur photo kis shakal mein rakhi hai?

**Kabir:** Numbers ki tarah.

**Madhav:** Toh machine ke liye program aur photo mein kya farak hai?

**Kabir:** Koi... koi farak nahi hai?

**Madhav:** Koi farak nahi hai. Dono numbers hain. Farak sirf itna hai ki machine ek ko **padhti** hai aur doosre ko **karti** hai.

**Kabir:** Yeh ajeeb hai.

**Madhav:** Yeh ajeeb hai aur yehi sab kuch hai. Ab socho. Agar nirdesh bhi sirf numbers hain, aur numbers badle ja sakte hain, toh machine kya kar sakti hai?

**Kabir:** Alag numbers daal do toh woh alag kaam karegi.

**Madhav:** Machine badalni padegi?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Sirf numbers badalne padenge.

**Madhav:** Toh ab batao, chaaku taala kyun nahi ban sakta?

**Kabir:** Kyunki chaaku ki shakal hi uska kaam hai. Use badalne ke liye chaaku ko hi badalna padega.

**Madhav:** Aur computer?

**Kabir:** Computer ki shakal uska kaam nahi hai. Uska kaam uske andar rakhe numbers hain. Woh badal do, kaam badal gaya.

**Madhav:** Bas. Isiliye ek hi machine camera bhi hai aur ghadi bhi. Machine kabhi nahi badalti. Sirf list badalti hai.

**Kabir:** Toh jab main ek naya app install karta hoon...

**Madhav:** Tum machine mein kuch nahi jodte. Tum sirf numbers ka ek naya set daalte ho.

**Kabir:** Aur woh number kahan se aate hain?

**Madhav:** Koi insaan unhe likhta hai. Seedhe numbers mein nahi, kyunki woh kaafi takleef deh hai. Woh lagbhag angrezi jaisi shakal mein likhta hai aur ek doosra program use numbers mein badal deta hai.

**Kabir:** Ek program doosre program ko banata hai?

**Madhav:** Haan. Aur woh banane wala program bhi khud numbers hi hai. Yeh Part 3 hai, abhi mat kholo.

**Madhav:** Aakhri sawal. Agar ek machine har woh kaam kar sakti hai jiske liye nirdesh likhe ja sakein, toh kaunsa kaam woh nahi kar sakti?

**Kabir:** Jiske liye nirdesh likhe hi na ja sakein.

**Madhav:** Ise yaad rakhna. Poori kitaab mein aur poori Book 2 mein, har seema isi ek line se nikalti hai.

### Naam

Woh numbers jo nirdesh hain, unhe **program** kehte hain.

Aur woh khoj ki nirdesh bhi data ki tarah hi rakhe ja sakte hain, use **stored program** kehte hain. Yeh 1940 ke aas-paas ki baat hai aur yeh computer ka sabse bada ek idea hai.

Farak samajhne ke liye:

```
CHAAKU jaisi machine
├── shakal hi kaam hai
├── naya kaam chahiye toh nayi machine
└── ek machine, ek kaam

COMPUTER jaisi machine
├── shakal khaali hai, kaam andar ke numbers mein hai
├── naya kaam chahiye toh naye numbers
└── ek machine, anginat kaam
```

Iska ek naam aur hai: aisi machine ko **general purpose** kehte hain. Poori duniya mein aisi machinein bahut kam hain. Lagbhag har cheez jo insaan ne banayi, woh ek kaam ki hai. Computer un ginti ki cheezon mein se hai jiska kaam pehle se tay nahi hota.

Aur yahi wajah hai ki Chapter 0.3 mein level 4 alag nikla tha. Kabir ne wahan kaha tha ki AI "auzaar banane ka auzaar" hai. Woh isliye mumkin hua kyunki uske neeche jo machine hai, woh khud pehle se hi kisi bhi kaam ke liye khaali thi.

### Asli duniya se ek example

Dusre vishwa yudh ke waqt ENIAC naam ki ek badi machine banayi gayi thi, hisaab ke liye. Woh ek kamre jitni thi.

Use naya kaam dene ke liye log usmein **taar badalte the**. Switch haath se ghumate the, plug nikaal kar doosri jagah lagate the. Ek naya program lagane mein kai din lag jaate the, aur woh kaam zyadatar auraton ki ek team karti thi jo us machine ki asli programmer thin.

Kuch hi saal baad wali machinein alag thin. Unmein program bhi wahin rakha jaata tha jahan data rakha jaata tha. Naya kaam dene ke liye taar nahi badalte the, sirf naye numbers bhar dete the.

Woh farak, taar badalne se numbers badalne tak, computer ka asli janm hai. Uske pehle woh ek machine thi. Uske baad woh anginat machinein ban gayi.

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **software ko koi alag jaadui cheez samajhna.**

Log "hardware" aur "software" ko do alag duniya maante hain. Ek cheez jo hai, aur ek cheez jo... pata nahi kya hai. Kuch aisa jo hawa mein hai.

Software hawa mein nahi hai. Software woh numbers hain jo machine ke andar rakhe hain, aur woh utne hi asli hain jitni ek photo.

Iss galti ka seedha nateeja hai: log yeh nahi samajh paate ki software ko banane mein itna waqt aur paisa kyun lagta hai, jab "usme kuch hai hi nahi." Aur woh yeh bhi nahi samajh paate ki software gayab kyun ho sakta hai, kharaab kyun ho sakta hai, ya use jagah kyun chahiye.

Yeh galti tempting isliye hai ki software ko aap chhoo nahi sakte. Lekin bijli ko bhi nahi chhoo sakte, aur woh bilkul asli hai.

Sahi tasveer yeh hai:

> Hardware woh machine hai jo nirdesh maanti hai.
> Software woh nirdesh hain.
> Dono numbers hain. Ek switchon mein baitha hai, doosra unke beech se guzarta hai.

### Part 1 ka gate

Aage badhne se pehle in teen sawalon ka jawab do.

**1.** Ek dost kehta hai "computer bahut hoshiyaar hai." Aap Part 1 ke aadhaar pe kya kahoge? *(nahi aaya toh 1.3)*

> **Jawab:** Computer mein hoshiyaari kahin nahi hai. Usmein switch hain jo shartein banate hain, aur bijli un sharton se guzarti hai.
>
> Hoshiyaari, agar kahin hai, toh un nirdeshon mein hai jo kisi insaan ne likhe. Machine unhe samajh kar nahi chal rahi, woh unhe chala rahi hai.

**2.** Aapke phone mein camera app aur calculator app dono hain. Andar kya alag hai? *(nahi aaya toh 1.6)*

> **Jawab:** Sirf numbers alag hain. Machine bilkul wahi hai.
>
> Yeh sunne mein chhota lagta hai lekin yehi wajah hai ki ek chhoti si cheez aapki jeb mein sau alag machinon ka kaam kar leti hai.

**3.** Ek file mein numbers hain: 200, 15, 99. Yeh photo hai, gaana hai, ya nirdesh? *(nahi aaya toh 1.4)*

> **Jawab:** Sirf numbers dekh kar bataya hi nahi ja sakta. Woh teeno ho sakte hain.
>
> Matlab andar nahi hota. Matlab iss baat mein hota hai ki unhe kaunsi list se padha jaa raha hai, aur machine unhe padhne wali hai ya karne wali.

### Chat app pe wapas

Yaad karo, hum ek chat app saath lekar chal rahe hain: aapke messages sambhalne aur unhe dhoondhne wala.

Part 1 ke baad aapko itna pata hai:

```
Aapka message akshar nahi hai. Woh numbers hai.
Woh numbers switchon mein rakhe hain, 0 aur 1 ki shakal mein.
App khud bhi numbers hai, sirf woh numbers jo "kiye" jaate hain.
Aur jo machine app chala rahi hai, woh app ke liye banayi hi nahi gayi.
Woh khaali thi. App ne use apni shakal di.
```

Ek sawal jo abhi khula hai, aur agla part usi pe hai:

**Aapke ek message ko kitni jagah chahiye? Aur ek photo ko usse kitni zyada?**
