# Chapter 8.5  [SPINE]
## Ek technology jeetati kyun hai, doosri marti kyun

---

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Iss kitaab mein aapne kai baar dekha ki ek behtar cheez haar gayi. Ginao.

**Kabir:** IPv6 behtar tha aur 26 saal mein bhi aadhi duniya tak nahi pahuncha. JavaScript das din mein bani aur jeet gayi. Aur log behtar messaging app pe nahi jaate.

**Madhav:** Toh "behtar" kaafi nahi hai. Phir kya kaafi hai? Ek-ek karke dekhte hain. IPv6 kyun nahi phaila?

**Kabir:** Kyunki sabko ek saath badalna padta, aur akele badalne se kisi ko faayda nahi tha.

**Madhav:** JavaScript kyun jeeti?

**Kabir:** Kyunki woh browser ke andar pehle se thi. Jo wahan tha, wahi jeeta.

**Madhav:** Messaging app?

**Kabir:** Kyunki mere log wahan hain. Akele jaane se main akela ho jaunga.

**Madhav:** Ab teeno ko dekho. Kya saanjha hai?

**Kabir:** Teeno mein faisla akele ka nahi tha. Woh sabka milkar tha.

**Madhav:** Bas. Ab ek chauthi wajah bhi hai. Chapter 3.3 mein humne dekha tha ki Python AI mein kyun hai. Yaad hai?

**Kabir:** Kyunki uske aas-paas saara saaman pehle se bana hua tha.

**Madhav:** Aur woh saaman kyun bana?

**Kabir:** Kyunki kuch log use istemaal kar rahe the.

**Madhav:** Toh?

**Kabir:** Toh jo aage nikal gaya, woh aur aage nikalta gaya.

**Madhav:** Aur ab paanchvi, jo sabse kam dikhti hai. Do cheezein hain, dono barabar achhi. Ek ko chalane mein aur badalne mein aasani hai, doosri technically behtar hai lekin uske saath kaam karna dukhdayi hai. Kaunsi jeetegi?

**Kabir:** Pehli.

**Madhav:** Kyun? Log toh behtar cheez chahte hain.

**Kabir:** Kyunki jo cheez istemaal karne mein aasan hai use zyada log istemaal karenge, aur phir woh aage nikal jaayegi.

**Madhav:** Aur ab aakhri sawal, jo aapke apne kaam ka hai. Agar aap kuch banate ho, toh in paanch mein se aap kis pe kaam kar sakte ho?

**Kabir:** Behtar banane pe... jo shayad sabse kam kaam ka hai.

**Madhav:** Aur baaki chaar?

**Kabir:** Woh log ke aapas ke rishton pe hain, cheez pe nahi.

### Paanch wajahein

```
1. PEHLE SE WAHAN HONA
   jo jagah pe pehle se maujood tha, woh jeet jaata hai
   (JavaScript browser mein tha)

2. SABKO EK SAATH BADALNA PADE
   agar badlav ka faayda tabhi mile jab sab badlein,
   toh koi nahi badlega
   (IPv6, 26 saal)

3. LOG WAHAN HAIN
   network wali cheezon mein aap cheez nahi chunte,
   aap yeh chunte ho ki kiske saath rahoge
   (Chapter 4.1)

4. AAS-PAAS KA SAAMAN
   jo aage nikla, uske aas-paas cheezein banti hain,
   jisse woh aur aage nikal jaata hai
   (Python aur AI)

5. ISTEMAAL KARNE MEIN AASANI
   dukhdayi behtar cheez se aasan theek cheez aksar jeet jaati hai
```

Aur ab woh baat jo iss chapter ki asli seekh hai:

**Paanchon mein se ek bhi "cheez kitni achhi hai" nahi hai.**

Achha hona ek shart hai. Ek cheez itni buri nahi honi chahiye ki kaam hi na kare. Uske baad, jeet lagbhag poori tarah in paanch pe tay hoti hai.

Yeh nirasha ki baat lag sakti hai, aur woh nahi hai. Iska ulta matlab yeh hai: **agar aap in paanch ko samajh lo, toh aap unpe kaam kar sakte ho.**

```
behtar banana        →  sabse mushkil, sabse kam asar
aasan banana         →  aksar behtar banane se zyada asar
kisi jagah ghus jana  →  jahan log pehle se hain
chhote group se shuru →  jahan poora group ek saath aa sake
saaman banane dena    →  doosron ko apne upar banane dena (Ch 6.4)
```

Teesra aur chautha wala Chapter 4.1 ki us problem ka jawab hai jo tab khuli chhodi gayi thi: **network wali cheez shuru kaise hoti hai jab uski shuruaati value zero hai?**

Jawab: poore bazaar se mat ladho. Ek chhota group dhoondho jo poora ek saath aa sake, jahan network aandar hi poora ho jaaye. Ek college, ek company, ek sheher, ek khaas kaam. Wahan value zero se turant upar chali jaati hai, kyunki sab jo aapko chahiye wahi hain.

### Asli duniya se ek example

Facebook ne ek college se shuru kiya. Sirf ek. Phir kuch aur college.

Yeh dheema tareeka lagta hai. Woh sabse tez tareeka tha, aur wajah ganit hai.

Ek college mein do hazaar log hain. Unmein se sab ek doosre ko jaante hain ya jaan sakte hain. Toh jaise hi kuch sau log aaye, un logon ke liye network **poora** ho gaya. Unka har dost wahan tha.

Ab isko ulta karo. Agar woh poori duniya mein ek saath khulte, toh ek karod log aane par bhi kisi ke liye uske apne das dost wahan hone ka koi bharosa nahi tha. Woh ek karod log ek khaali kamre jaisa lagta.

Yeh Chapter 4.1 ka ganit hai, ulta karke istemaal kiya gaya: **rishte ginti se tez badhte hain, isliye chhoti jagah mein poori bhar jaana bade bazaar mein bikhre rehne se behtar hai.**

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **yeh sochna ki behtar product jeetega.**

Yeh sabse mehngi galti hai jo kuch banane wale karte hain. Woh saal lagate hain kuch behtar banane mein, aur phir hairan hote hain ki log kyun nahi aaye.

Log isliye nahi aate ki woh moorkh hain. Woh isliye nahi aate ki unke liye badlav ki keemat aapke behtar hone se zyada hai. Unhe apne log chhodne padenge, apni aadat badalni padegi, apna purana data le jaana padega.

**Aapka behtar hona unki keemat se bada hona chahiye, aur woh keemat aksar bahut badi hoti hai.**

Doosri galti, ulti taraf se: **yeh maan lena ki jab bada khilaadi maujood hai toh kuch naya ho hi nahi sakta.**

Naye jeet te hain, aur woh aksar in raaston se aate hain:

```
naya paimana      jab kuch itna sasta ho jaaye ki naye kaam khul jaayein
naya raasta       jab log kisi nayi jagah pe pahunch jaayein
                  (jaise sab desktop se phone pe aa gaye)
purane ka bojh    jab bade khilaadi apne purane waadon mein
                  bandhe hon (Chapter 6.4) aur mud na sakein
chhoti jagah      jahan bada khilaadi dhyaan nahi de raha
```

Teesri galti: **yeh sochna ki jo aaj jeet raha hai, uska jeetna kisi vajah se tay tha.**

Aksar woh timing thi. Wahi cheez do saal pehle marti aur do saal baad late hoti.

### Sochne ke liye

**1. (samajh check)** Aap ek nayi cheez bana rahe ho jo tabhi kaam ki hai jab bahut log uspe hon. Kahan se shuru karoge?

> **Jawab:** Ek chhoti, poori jagah se.
>
> Ek college, ek daftar, ek sheher, ek khaas kaam, ek ilaaka. Aisi jagah jahan aapke shuruaati users ke liye **saare** log wahin hon.
>
> Poore bazaar mein bikhre hue das hazaar users se ek jagah ke poore do hazaar behtar hain, kyunki dusre mein network poora hai aur pehle mein khaali.
>
> Aur woh jagah chhoti hona ek nuksaan nahi hai. Woh hi ek matra tareeka hai.

**2. (samajh check)** Ek bade khilaadi ke saamne aapka product har tarah se behtar hai. Log phir bhi nahi aa rahe. Kya soch rahe ho?

> **Jawab:** Badlav ki keemat naapo.
>
> Unhe kya chhodna padega? Apne log? Apna purana data? Apni aadat? Apne doosre systems se jo jud gaya hai woh?
>
> Aur phir poocho: kya mera behtar hona un sab se bada hai?
>
> Aur ek kaam ka raasta: **keemat kam karo, faayda badhane ki jagah.** Purana data le aane do. Dono jagah kaam karne do. Ek hissa aisa banao jo unki purani cheez ke saath chale.

**3. (jodne wala)** Chapter 0.2 ke teen dials the: size, scarcity, leverage. Yeh paanch wajahein kis dial pe lagti hain?

> **Jawab:** Zyadatar leverage pe, aur wahi sabse kaam ki baat hai.
>
> Jo cheez pehle se jagah pe hai, jispe log hain, aur jiske aas-paas saaman bana hai, woh har naye user se aur mazboot hoti jaati hai bina uske maalik ki mehnat ke.
>
> Yeh Chapter 4.1 wala leverage ka teekha roop hai: **ek baar banao, aur phir har naya aane wala use aur bada kar de.**
>
> Aur isliye yeh paanch wajahein sirf technology ki baat nahi hain. Woh yeh batati hain ki technology mein sabse bada leverage kahan banta hai, aur woh cheez ki quality mein kabhi nahi banta.
