### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Chaar hafte pehle tumne kaha tha ki har hafte kuch naya sunte ho jo baaki sabko pehle se pata tha.

**Kabir:** Haan.

**Madhav:** Ab ek khabar padhta hoon. "Ek nayi database company ne kaha hai ki unka system purane se sau guna tez hai kyunki woh sab kuch memory mein rakhta hai." Kya sawal poochoge?

**Kabir:** Memory mein rakhta hai matlab RAM mein. Toh bijli jaane pe kya hoga?

**Madhav:** Aur?

**Kabir:** RAM mehngi hai aur chhoti hai. Toh kitna data aa sakta hai?

**Madhav:** Aur?

**Kabir:** Aur "sau guna tez" kis kaam mein? Padhne mein ya likhne mein?

**Madhav:** Chaar hafte pehle tum kya poochte?

**Kabir:** Kuch nahi. Main bas maan leta ki woh tez hai.

**Madhav:** Ek aur. "Ek nayi cheez aayi hai jo aapke code ko apne aap kai machinon pe chala deti hai."

**Kabir:** Toh mera code khaali hona chahiye, warna das copy das alag jhoot bolengi.

**Madhav:** Aur?

**Kabir:** Aur bojh hat ta nahi, khiskta hai. Toh database ka kya?

**Madhav:** Ek aakhri. "Ek naya protocol aaya hai jo internet ko dugna tez kar dega."

**Kabir:** Sabko ek saath badalna padega?

**Madhav:** Haan.

**Kabir:** Toh shayad woh das saal lagega, ya kabhi nahi aayega. IPv6 ki tarah.

**Madhav:** Ab batao, kya badla?

**Kabir:** Pehle har khabar ek nayi cheez thi. Ab woh ek jaani-pehchani jagah pe aa kar baithti hai.

**Madhav:** Aur jab kuch aisa aaye jo sach mein naya ho?

**Kabir:** Toh mujhe pata chal jaayega ki woh sach mein naya hai. Kyunki uske liye meri jagah nahi hogi.

**Madhav:** Aur woh sabse zaroori cheez hai jo yeh kitaab de sakti thi.

### Aakhri check

Yeh Book 1 ka gate hai. Har sawal do ya teen parton se aata hai.

**1.** Aapki site das hazaar users pe theek chal rahi thi aur das lakh pe baith gayi. Do bilkul alag wajahein batao, aur har ek ke liye ek alag ilaaj.

> **Jawab:**
>
> **Ek: kahin poori list padhi ja rahi hai.** Kaam data ke saath seedha badhta hai. Das hazaar pe woh dikha nahi, das lakh pe saamne aa gaya. Ilaaj index hai (5.2), badi machine nahi.
>
> **Do: seema paar ho gayi aur shrinkhla chal padi.** Log intezaar chhod kar dobara koshish kar rahe hain, jisse bojh khud badh raha hai (6.6). Ilaaj seema pe mana karna hai, machine badhana nahi.
>
> Farak pehchanne ka tareeka: pehli mein cheez dheere dheere dheemi hui hogi jaise data badha. Doosri mein woh ek din achanak giri hogi.

**2.** Ek dost kehta hai "maine apna data do jagah rakha hai, ab woh surakshit hai." Kya kahoge?

> **Jawab:** Ki woh machine kharaab hone se surakshit hai, apni galti se bilkul nahi (5.7).
>
> Replica wahi karta hai jo asli karta hai. Aap galti se kuch mita do toh woh dono jagah mit jaayega, turant.
>
> Uske liye purana backup chahiye. Aur woh backup tabhi backup hai jab use wapas laakar dekha gaya ho.

**3.** Ek naya app banane wala aapse poochta hai: "search feature banane mein kitna waqt lagega?" Aap kya poochoge, jawab dene se pehle?

> **Jawab:** Kam se kam yeh:
>
> Kis cheez mein dhoondhna hai, sirf likhawat ya photo bhi? Milte-julte shabd chalenge ya bilkul wahi? Kitna data hai? Nateeje kis kram mein aayein, aur "sahi kram" ka matlab kya hai? Galat likhne pe kya ho? Kuch na mile toh kya dikhe?
>
> Aur phir batana ki yeh ek range hai, ek number nahi (7.5), kyunki search un kaamon mein hai jinka "sahi jawab" saaf nahi hota (8.2).

**4.** Ek khabar: "nayi chip 40 pratishat tez hai." Doosri khabar: "ek nayi cheez ne AI chalane ka kharcha 30 guna kam kar diya." Kaunsi zyada mayne rakhti hai?

> **Jawab:** Doosri, aur bahut zyada (2.2).
>
> 40 pratishat ek hi paimane ke andar ka sudhaar hai. Jo cheezein pehle ho sakti thin woh thodi behtar ho gayin.
>
> 30 guna sasta hone ka matlab hai ki woh sab kuch jo pehle mehnga hone ki wajah se banaya hi nahi ja sakta tha, ab ban sakta hai. Woh paimana badal deta hai.
>
> **Pratishat mein badlav khabar hai. Guna mein badlav badlav hai.**

**5.** Aap ek product design kar rahe ho. Uska ek hissa yeh hai ki users video daal sakein. Kis cheez pe sabse pehle sochoge?

> **Jawab:** Kharche pe (6.8).
>
> Ek ghanta video lagbhag ek karod text message ke barabar hai. Aur bhejna rakhne se kai guna mehnga hai.
>
> Toh video wala product kabhi bhi text wale product jaisa muft nahi ho sakta. Uska poora karobaari dhaancha alag hoga: shuru se paise ka raasta chahiye, ya bahut sakht seemayein.
>
> Aur yeh technical faisla nahi hai. Yeh sabse bada karobaari faisla hai jo iss product mein hai.

### Aap ab kya kar sakte ho

Kitaab ke shuru mein teen vaade the. Ab unhe check karo.

**Ek: koi bhi nayi technology sun kar use naksh e pe rakh dena.**

Aap yeh kar sakte ho. Teen sawal kaafi hain:

```
1. Yeh kis parat pe hai?
   switch, ya language, ya network, ya data, ya serving,
   ya banane ka tareeka

2. Yeh kis dard ke liye bani thi?
   Agar dard nahi bata sakte, toh shayad woh cheez marketing hai

3. Iska sauda kya hai?
   Har cheez kuch deti hai aur kuch leti hai
   Agar nahi dikh raha toh aapne dhoondha nahi
```

**Do: ek product kaagaz pe design karna.**

Aap yeh kar sakte ho. Chapter 8.1 ka naksha, 8.2 ki chaar pehchaanein, aur 8.3 ke paanch hisse. Woh teen milkar ek asli spec ban jaate hain.

**Teen: AI se woh nikaalna jo expert nikaalte hain.**

Yeh adhoora hai, aur woh jaan-boojh ke hai. Iska aadha hissa Chapter 8.3 mein aa chuka hai: jo aadmi spec likh sakta hai, uske liye AI ek bahut bada guna hai.

Baaki aadha Book 2 hai, kyunki uske liye yeh jaanna zaroori hai ki AI andar se kaam kaise karta hai, aur woh kya nahi kar sakta.

### Ab aage

Book 1 khatam.

Aapne sabse neeche se shuru kiya, ek switch se, aur upar chadhte gaye. Aur ab neeche wali poori parat, jo Chapter 0.4 mein "sab chhupa hua" likhi thi, woh khuli hui hai.

**Book 2, The Thinking Machine**, wahin se shuru hogi jahan Book 1 ne chhoda: ek machine jo sirf numbers rakhti hai aur shartein chalati hai. Aur usmein hum poochenge ki us machine se woh cheez kaise bani jo baat karti hai.

Us kitaab ka har chapter iss kitaab pe khada hai. Model ek file hai numbers ki. Woh ek machine pe chalta hai jismein RAM aur chip hai. Woh ek network se aata hai. Uske jawab cache mein rakhe jaate hain. Uska kharcha usi hisaab se nikalta hai jo Chapter 6.8 mein tha.

Kuch bhi naya nahi hoga. Sirf ek aur parat, isi dher pe.

Aur agar Book 1 ne apna kaam kiya hai, toh Book 2 aapko ek nayi duniya nahi lagegi. Woh ek aur chapter lagega.
