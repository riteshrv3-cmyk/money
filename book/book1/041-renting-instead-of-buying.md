# Chapter 6.5  [SPINE]
## Kharidne ki jagah kiraye pe lena

---

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Aapko ek website chalani hai. Kitni machinein chahiye?

**Kabir:** Pata nahi. Ek se shuru karta hoon.

**Madhav:** Aur agar kal das hazaar log aa gaye?

**Kabir:** Aur machinein chahiye.

**Madhav:** Kitni der mein mil jaayengi, agar aap khareedte ho?

**Kabir:** Order karo, aaye, lagao. Kuch hafte.

**Madhav:** Aur woh das hazaar log kitni der rukenge?

**Kabir:** Kuch second.

**Madhav:** Toh khareedna kaam nahi karta. Ab ulta socho. Aapne das machinein pehle se khareed leen, aur log aaye hi nahi.

**Kabir:** Toh paisa gaya. Woh machinein khaali padi rahengi.

**Madhav:** Toh do taraf se nuksaan hai. Kam khareedo toh chook jaate ho, zyada khareedo toh paisa jalta hai. Aur pehle se pata nahi hota ki kitni chahiye.

**Kabir:** Toh kiraye pe le lo. Jitni chahiye utni.

**Madhav:** Kaun dega?

**Kabir:** Koi jiske paas bahut saari machinein hon.

**Madhav:** Woh itni machinein rakhega hi kyun?

**Kabir:** Kyunki bahut saare log usse maang rahe hain.

**Madhav:** Aur sab ek saath maangenge?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Kisi ka bojh subah zyada hoga, kisi ka raat ko. Kisi ka December mein, kisi ka June mein.

**Madhav:** Toh usko kitni machinein rakhni padengi? Sabke sabse bade bojh ka jod?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Usse kam. Kyunki sabke bade bojh ek waqt pe nahi aate.

**Madhav:** Bas. Wahi poora karobaar hai. Ab ek aur baat. Aapne ek machine kiraye pe li. Uska daam kya hoga, khareedne se zyada ya kam?

**Kabir:** Zyada hoga. Usko munafa bhi chahiye.

**Madhav:** Toh phir log kirya kyun lete hain?

**Kabir:** Kyunki jab zaroorat nahi hoti tab paisa nahi lagta. Aur turant milti hai.

**Madhav:** Aur agar aapko pata ho ki aapko poore saal, chaubees ghante, dus machinein chahiye hi chahiye?

**Kabir:** Toh khareedna sasta hoga.

**Madhav:** Kitna sasta? Aam taur pe teen se paanch guna.

**Kabir:** Itna zyada?

**Madhav:** Isliye jab company chhoti hoti hai toh kirya samajhdaari hai, aur jab bahut badi ho jaati hai toh kabhi kabhi wapas khareedne mein chali jaati hai. Ab aakhri sawal. Aapne apna saara system kisi ek kiraye wale pe daal diya. Kya mila aur kya gaya?

**Kabir:** Mila: mujhe machinein nahi sambhalni padeen. Gaya: ab main uspe nirbhar hoon.

**Madhav:** Aur woh daam badha de toh?

**Kabir:** Toh mujhe dena padega, ya sab kuch uthakar kahin aur le jaana padega.

**Madhav:** Kitni mehnat lagegi uthane mein?

**Kabir:** Bahut. Kyunki maine unki cheezein istemaal ki hongi jo kahin aur milti hi nahi.

### Naam

Kisi aur ki machinein kiraye pe lene ka naam hai **cloud**.

Uske teen darje hote hain, aur farak yeh hai ki aap kitna khud sambhalte ho:

```
KHAALI MACHINE
├── woh machine deta hai, baaki sab aap
├── sabse zyada control, sabse zyada kaam
└── aksar sabse sasta

TAIYAAR JAGAH
├── aap code deto ho, woh chalata hai
├── OS, update, bada hona woh sambhalta hai
└── beech ka daam

POORA KAAM
├── database, email, login: banaya banaya
├── aap sirf istemaal karte ho
└── sabse mehnga, sabse aasan
```

Cloud ka asli faayda machine ka daam nahi hai. Woh teen cheezein hain:

```
1. TURANT MILNA        das machinein paanch minute mein
2. JITNA CHAHIYE UTNA  raat ko kam, din mein zyada
3. AADMI KAM           machine, bijli, AC, jaal: unka sar dard
```

Aur uski keemat bhi teen hain:

```
1. DAAM ZYADA          poore waqt chalne wali cheez ke liye 3-5 guna
2. NIRBHARTA           unke daam, unke niyam, unka band hona
3. FANS JAANA          jitni unki khaas cheezein istemaal ki,
                       utna nikalna mushkil
```

Teesri baat pe ek naam hai: **lock-in**. Aur woh muft cheezon se shuru hota hai. Har aasan aur sasti cheez jo sirf ek jagah milti hai, woh ek aur dhaaga hai jo aapko wahin baandhta hai.

---

### Asli duniya se ek example

Dropbox ne apne shuruaati saalon mein sab kuch Amazon ke cloud pe rakha tha. Woh us waqt bilkul sahi faisla tha: unhe apna product banana tha, data centre nahi.

Jab woh bahut bade ho gaye, unhone apna khud ka system banaya aur apna zyadatar data usmein le aaye. Kai saal lage. Unhone bataya ki isse unhe do saal mein lagbhag 7.5 crore dollar ki bachat hui.

Ab dhyaan do ki dono faisle sahi the, alag alag waqt pe.

Shuru mein kirya sahi tha, kyunki tab paisa aur waqt banane mein lagna chahiye tha, na ki machinein sambhalne mein. Baad mein khareedna sahi tha, kyunki bojh pakka ho chuka tha aur bahut bada tha.

**Sahi jawab company ke size aur waqt ke saath badalta hai.** Yeh un chhote se sawalon mein se ek hai jinka jawab hamesha "depends" hota hai, aur woh "depends" ginta ja sakta hai.

---

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **yeh sochna ki cloud sasta hai.**

Cloud lagbhag hamesha mehnga hai, per machine. Uska faayda daam mein nahi hai.

Uska faayda yeh hai ki aap sirf utna dete ho jitna istemaal karte ho, aur aapko machine sambhalne wale log nahi rakhne padte. Ek chhoti company ke liye woh do cheezein daam se kahin badi hain.

Lekin agar aapka bojh pakka hai aur poore waqt chalta hai, toh aap ek suvidha ke liye teen se paanch guna de rahe ho jiski aapko zaroorat hi nahi.

Doosri galti: yeh maan lena ki cloud pe hai toh hamesha chalega. Chapter 6.2 mein hum dekh chuke hain ki cloud bhi baithta hai, aur jab baithta hai toh aap kuch nahi kar sakte.

Teesri galti, aur yeh sabse mehngi hai: **bina soche unki khaas cheezein istemaal karte jaana.**

Har baar jab aap unki koi aisi cheez lete ho jo kahin aur nahi milti, toh nikalna thoda aur mushkil ho jaata hai. Ek din aapko pata chalta hai ki aap ja hi nahi sakte, aur us din aapke paas mol-bhav ki koi taakat nahi bachti.

### Sochne ke liye

**1. (samajh check)** Ek app hai jismein din mein 10,000 log aate hain aur raat ko koi nahi. Kirya ya kharid?

> **Jawab:** Kirya, aur saaf faisla hai.
>
> Kyunki bojh ubhaar-chadhaav wala hai. Khareedoge toh aapko sabse bade waqt ke hisaab se khareedna padega, aur woh machinein raat bhar khaali padi rahengi.
>
> Kirye mein aap raat ko machinein kam kar sakte ho aur us waqt ka paisa hi nahi dete.
>
> **Ubhaar-chadhaav wala bojh = kirya. Pakka aur poore waqt ka bojh = kharid.** Yeh sabse saaf niyam hai jo iss sawal pe lagta hai.

**2. (samajh check)** Ek cloud company aisi database deti hai jo bahut aasan hai aur sirf unke yahan milti hai. Aap use loge?

> **Jawab:** Le sakte ho, lekin aankhein khuli rakh kar.
>
> Faayda asli hai: kam kaam, tez shuruaat.
>
> Keemat yeh hai ki us din se aapka jaana mushkil ho gaya. Aur woh mushkil dikhta nahi, kyunki jab tak aap ja nahi rahe tab tak koi dard nahi hota.
>
> Ek achhi aadat: **poocho ki iska koi aisa vikalp hai jo har jagah milta hai?** Agar haan, toh sochna chahiye ki jo aasani mil rahi hai woh us aazadi se badi hai ya nahi.

**3. (jodne wala)** Chapter 6.4 kehta tha ki kisi aur ke API pe khada hona khatra hai. Cloud usse kaise judta hai?

> **Jawab:** Woh wahi baat hai, sabse badi shakal mein.
>
> API pe khade ho toh ek cheez kisi aur ki hai. Cloud pe khade ho toh **poori zameen** kisi aur ki hai.
>
> Uske daam, uske niyam, uska band hona, sab aap pe seedha lagta hai.
>
> Iska matlab yeh nahi ki cloud galat hai. Lagbhag har company usi pe khadi hai, aur wajah achhi hai.
>
> Matlab sirf itna hai ki yeh ek chunav hai, majboori nahi. Aur har chunav ki keemat hoti hai, jo Chapter 5.4 se hum jaante hain.
