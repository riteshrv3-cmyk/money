# Chapter 0.1  [SPINE]
## The village: paisa exist hi kyun karta hai

---

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Ek gaon socho. Sau log, ek valley mein. Paani hai, zameen hai, patthar hai, lakdi hai. Paisa nahi hai, kyunki abhi tak kisi ne invent hi nahi kiya. Sabko khaana chahiye, paani chahiye, chhat chahiye, bimari mein ilaaj chahiye. Ab batao, woh jiyenge kaise?

**Kabir:** Har banda apna kaam kar lega. Apna khaana ugayega, apna ghar banayega.

**Madhav:** Achha. Toh jis din uski haddi tootegi, us din?

**Kabir:** Woh... khud jod lega?

**Madhav:** Kisne sikhaya use haddi jodna?

**Kabir:** Theek hai. Woh kaam nahi karega.

**Madhav:** Aur bhi kuch nahi chalega. Ek aadmi saat kaam kare toh saaton kharaab karega. Din mein itni roshni hi nahi hai. Gaon zinda toh rahega, lekin jaanwaron ke level pe. Toh doosra tareeka batao.

**Kabir:** Kaam baant lein. Bees kheti karein, das paani laayein, bees ghar banayein, das ilaaj seekhein.

**Madhav:** Haan. Ab dhyaan do ki kya hua. Gaon ko nayi zameen mili?

**Kabir:** Nahi.

**Madhav:** Naye auzaar? Naye log?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Kuch bhi naya nahi mila.

**Madhav:** Phir bhi ab woh pehle se zyada banate hain. Sirf ek arrangement badla, aur output badh gaya. Yeh yaad rakhna, aage kaam aayega. Ab agla sawal. Kisan ke paas anaaj hai, chhat nahi. Mistri ke paas hunar hai, khaane ko kuch nahi. Kya karenge?

**Kabir:** Aasan hai. Kisan anaaj dega, mistri chhat banayega.

**Madhav:** Bilkul. Ab vaidya. Usne mochi ka haath theek kiya. Vaidya ko joote chahiye.

**Kabir:** Toh mochi joote de dega. Hisaab barabar.

**Madhav:** Aur teen mahine baad vaidya ko phir joote chahiye. Iss baar mochi bilkul theek-thaak hai. Usko vaidya se kuch nahi chahiye.

**Kabir:** Toh vaidya intezaar karega ki mochi bimar pade.

**Madhav:** Toh vaidya ki roti iss baat pe tiki hai ki log bimar padein?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Yeh toh galat hai.

**Madhav:** Galat hai, aur yeh koi ajeeb case bhi nahi hai. Master ko anaaj chahiye lekin kisan ke bachche bade ho gaye. Pehredaar ko chhat chahiye lekin mistri ka ghar bana hua hai. Seedha exchange sirf tab chalta hai jab do log ek hi waqt pe ek doosre ka kaam chahte hon.

**Kabir:** Toh likh lo. Ek register banao. Vaidya ne mochi ka kaam kiya, likh do. Baad mein mochi se le lega.

**Madhav:** Achha jawab. Sach mein achha, aur duniya ne yeh kiya bhi hai. Lekin do sawal. Register rakhega kaun?

**Kabir:** Koi ek aadmi.

**Madhav:** Aur jis din usne apna naam thoda bada likh diya?

**Kabir:** Hmm.

**Madhav:** Doosra sawal. Sau log the toh register chal gaya. Ab das hazaar log hain, alag gaon hain, log aate jaate hain. Kitne register?

**Kabir:** Bahut zyada. Aur kisi ko kisi pe bharosa bhi nahi hoga.

**Madhav:** Toh register ka idea sahi hai, uski shakal galat hai. Ab socho. Aisi kaunsi cheez ho jo mochi le lega, chahe use ilaaj bilkul nahi chahiye?

**Kabir:** Aisi cheez jo uske kaam ki ho. Anaaj?

**Madhav:** Kabhi kabhi. Aur jis din uske paas anaaj bhara ho?

**Kabir:** Toh phir... aisi cheez jo woh kisi aur ko de sake.

**Madhav:** Ruko. Yeh important hai. Woh use kyun lega?

**Kabir:** Kyunki use pata hai ki baaki sab bhi lenge.

**Madhav:** Bas. Woh cheez uske kisi kaam ki nahi hai. Woh use isliye leta hai kyunki use bharosa hai ki aage badha dega. Ab batao, us cheez mein kya kya hona chahiye?

**Kabir:** Sadni nahi chahiye. Warna jab tak main use karun, khatam.

**Madhav:** Aur?

**Kabir:** Uthane mein aasan ho. Bhaari patthar se kaam nahi chalega.

**Madhav:** Aur?

**Kabir:** Nakli banana mushkil ho. Warna koi bhi bana lega aur bina kuch diye sab le lega.

**Madhav:** Aur? Ek chhoti cheez khareedni ho toh?

**Kabir:** Toh woh tukdon mein bat jaani chahiye.

**Madhav:** Aur aakhri, sabse zaroori?

**Kabir:** Sab log use maanein. Ek bhi na maane toh chain toot jaati hai.

**Madhav:** Tumne abhi paisa invent kar diya.

### Naam

Jo cheez Kabir ne nikaali, uska naam hai **paisa**.

Ab dhyaan do ki woh actually hai kya, kyunki yahin par lagbhag sab log galat samajhte hain.

Paisa daulat nahi hai. Paisa **ek record hai ki aapne kisi ko value di**, jise aap gaon mein kisi ke bhi saamne rakh sakte ho.

Anaaj daulat hai. Chhat daulat hai. Judi hui haddi daulat hai. Tokens doosre logon ke kaam pe ek claim hain.

Aur Kabir ka register wala jawab galat nahi tha. Paisa hi ek register hai, bas woh kisi ek aadmi ke paas nahi rakha. Woh sabke haathon mein tukdon mein baant diya gaya hai. Isiliye use kisi rakhwaale pe bharose ki zaroorat nahi padti.

Ek doosri cheez bhi paisa chupke se karta hai. Jo aadmi leta hai lekin deta kuch nahi, uske paas kuch jama nahi hota. Paise se pehle yeh dikhna mushkil tha.

---

### Asli duniya se ek example

1945 mein ek British arthshastri, R. A. Radford, ne apne war-time prisoner camp ke baare mein likha. Har kaidi ko Red Cross se ek jaisa parcel milta tha: cigarettes, jam, butter, chocolate, biscuits.

Kisi ne paisa jaari nahi kiya. Koi sarkar nahi thi. Phir bhi kuch hi hafton mein cigarettes paisa ban gaye.

Aur exactly wahi hua jo samvaad mein nikla. Jo aadmi smoke nahi karta tha woh bhi cigarettes leta tha, kyunki use pata tha ki baaki sab lenge. Log cigarettes mein daam bolne lage: ek shirt itni cigarettes, ek chocolate utni. Jab parcel late aate the toh cigarettes kam ho jaati thin aur har cheez sasti ho jaati thi. Jab zyada aate the toh daam chadh jaate the.

Kisi ne yeh design nahi kiya. Kuch sau log, ek jagah band, aur exchange ki problem ne apne aap paisa bana diya. Aur usne wahi paanch shartein poori kin jo Kabir ne ginayi thin.

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **paise ko hi daulat samajh lena.**

Isse ek khatarnak nateeja nikalta hai. Log sochte hain ki agar sabke paas zyada paisa aa jaaye toh sab ameer ho jaayenge. Sarkarein bhi kabhi kabhi yehi sochti hain.

Lekin paisa ek claim hai, cheez nahi. Zyada claim banane se cheezein nahi banti. Sirf har claim chhota ho jaata hai.

Yeh galti tempting isliye hai kyunki ek aadmi ke level pe woh sach lagti hai. Agar sirf aapke paas zyada paisa aa jaaye, toh aap sach mein zyada cheezein khareed sakte ho. Dimaag ise sab pe laagu kar deta hai. Lekin ek aadmi ka zyada paisa doosron ke claims ka hissa cheen kar aata hai. Sabka zyada paisa kisi se kuch nahi cheenta, aur isliye kuch deta bhi nahi.

### Sochne ke liye

Har sawal pe ek minute socho, phir neeche jawab padho.

**1. (samajh check)** Raat-o-raat gaon ke har aadmi ke paas das guna zyada tokens aa jaate hain. Anaaj utna hi hai, ghar utne hi hain, joote utne hi hain. Cheezon ka daam kya hoga? Aapka jawab tokens ke baare mein kya batata hai?

> **Jawab:** Daam lagbhag das guna ho jaayenge. Kisi ka kuch nahi badla.
>
> Wajah: tokens ka koi apna kaam nahi hai. Woh sirf iss baat ke record hain ki gaon ki cheezon pe aapka kitna hissa hai. Cheezein utni hi hain, toh hissa bhi utna hi hai. Sirf ginti ke number bade ho gaye.
>
> Isse ek aur baat nikalti hai: **paisa gaon ki daulat nahi hai. Woh us daulat ko baantne ka tareeka hai.** Camp wale example mein bilkul yehi dikha, jahan zyada cigarettes aate hi har cheez mehngi ho gayi.

**2. (samajh check)** Ek aadmi ko tokens ka bada dher viraasat mein milta hai aur woh zindagi bhar kisi ko koi value nahi deta. Woh khaata hai, ghar mein rehta hai, bimari mein ilaaj paata hai. Kya use gaon se asli value mil rahi hai? Agar haan, toh woh value banayi kisne, aur gaon ko badle mein kya mila?

> **Jawab:** Haan, use bilkul asli value mil rahi hai. Woh anaaj kisi kisan ne ugaya, woh ghar kisi mistri ne banaya.
>
> Value kisi aur ne banayi, aur gaon ko iss aadmi se badle mein kuch nahi mila.
>
> Lekin dhyaan do: value kisi waqt di zaroor gayi thi, sirf iss aadmi ne nahi di. Jisne di, usne woh record iss aadmi ko de diya. Tokens ki khaas baat yahi hai, ki woh haath badal sakte hain. Woh yeh nahi batate ki kaam kisne kiya, sirf yeh ki record ab kiske paas hai.
>
> Yeh paise ki kamzori hai, bug nahi. Agar tokens haath na badal sakein toh mochi wala deadlock wapas aa jaayega.

**3. (samajh check)** Paise ne mochi wala deadlock saaf kar diya. Ek aisi problem batao jo gaon ke paas paise se pehle nahi thi, aur ab hai.

> **Jawab:** Kai sahi jawab hain. Kuch mazboot wale:
>
> **Jama karna.** Anaaj sad jaata tha, isliye koi use jama nahi kar sakta tha. Tokens sadte nahi, isliye ab ek aadmi bahut saara hissa rok ke rakh sakta hai jabki baaki ke paas kam ho.
>
> **Kaam se kat jaana.** Pehle dikhta tha ki kisne kya diya. Ab sirf ek number reh gaya. Tokens yaad nahi rakhte ki woh kaise aaye. Chori aur imaandaari ke tokens ek jaise dikhte hain.
>
> **Nakli banana.** Ab shoonya se claim banane ka matlab hai gaon ki daulat ka hissa bina kuch diye le lena.
>
> **Paise ke liye kaam.** Pehle aap us cheez ke liye kaam karte the jo kisi ko chahiye thi. Ab aap tokens ke liye kaam kar sakte ho, chahe jo bana rahe ho woh kisi ke kaam ka ho ya na ho.
