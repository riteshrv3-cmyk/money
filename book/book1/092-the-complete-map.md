  1.  Andar sirf numbers hain. Matlab hamesha bahar se aata hai.

  2.  Har parat neeche wali ko chhupati hai.
      Isiliye achhi technology samajh nahi aati.

  3.  Machine badalti nahi. Sirf numbers badalte hain.

  4.  Hisaab sasta hai. Data laana mehnga hai.
      Jab kuch dheema ho, pehle poocho ki data kahan se aa raha hai.

  5.  Har cheez ka ek sauda hai.
      Agar nahi dikh raha, toh aapne dhoondha nahi.
        index    → padhna tez, likhna dheema
        cache    → tez, aur purana ho sakta hai
        taala    → sahi, aur dheema
        container→ ek jaisa, aur alagav poora nahi

  6.  Bojh hat ta nahi. Woh khiskta hai.
      Ek jagah baanto, agli jagah pe pahunch jaata hai.

  7.  Systems dheere-dheere kharaab nahi hote.
      Woh ek seema tak theek chalte hain, phir jhatke mein girte hain.

  8.  Jo jaancha nahi gaya, woh hai hi nahi.
      Backup, doosri machine, wapas jaane ka raasta, sab.

  9.  Har cheez kisi dard ka jawab hai.
      Dard nahi, toh cheez nahi chahiye.

 10.  Jeet cheez ki quality se nahi aati.
      Woh pehle wahan hone se, logon ke wahan hone se,
      aas-paas ke saaman se, aur aasani se aati hai.
