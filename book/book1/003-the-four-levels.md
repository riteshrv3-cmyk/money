# Chapter 0.3  [SPINE]
## Society ke chaar levels

---
### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Gaon badh gaya. Sau log ab ek crore hain. Valley sheher ban gayi. Ek cheez toot gayi jiske baare mein kisi ne socha hi nahi tha. Batao kya?

**Kabir:** Bheed ho gayi?

**Madhav:** Usse gehri baat. Gaon mein tumhe pata tha ki Meera haddi jodti hai. Uske ghar tak chal ke ja sakte the. Ab ek crore logon mein tumhe kitne log pata hain?

**Kabir:** Do sau, teen sau.

**Madhav:** Toh aaj subah tumne jo chawal khaya, woh kisne ugaya?

**Kabir:** Pata nahi.

**Madhav:** Paani kis pipe se aaya, kisne daali?

**Kabir:** Pata nahi.

**Madhav:** Wahi kaam abhi bhi ho raha hai. Roz. Lekin purana arrangement ise organise nahi kar sakta, kyunki purana arrangement ek doosre ko jaanne pe khada tha. Toh ab yeh chalta kaise hai? Sabse neeche se shuru karo. Ek insaan ko chahiye kya?

**Kabir:** Khaana, paani, ghar, ilaaj, suraksha, padhai. Aur kisi ka saath.

**Madhav:** Aur? Ek cheez chhoot rahi hai.

**Kabir:** Kuch aisa jisse din bitane laayak lagein.

**Madhav:** Haan. Ab batao, yeh list gaon wale ke liye alag thi?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Bilkul yehi thi.

**Madhav:** Paanch hazaar saal pehle?

**Kabir:** Wahi.

**Madhav:** Toh yeh list hilti nahi. Sab kuch iske upar khada hai. Ab agla kadam. Meera sau logon ko sambhal leti thi. Ek crore ko khaana kaun khilayega?

**Kabir:** Ek aadmi toh nahi kar sakta.

**Madhav:** Toh?

**Kabir:** Log mil kar karenge. Ek group.

**Madhav:** Aur woh group kis kis ko khilayega?

**Kabir:** Un logon ko jinhe woh jaanta bhi nahi.

**Madhav:** Bas, yahi scale ka jawab hai. Ek aadmi unhe serve karta hai jinhe woh jaanta hai. Ek group unhe serve karta hai jinhe usmein se koi nahi jaanta. Ab teesra kadam. Har group ek hi sawal poochta hai. Kya?

**Kabir:** Zyada paisa kaise kamayein?

**Madhav:** Usse pehle wala sawal. Woh zyada logon tak kaise pahunchein, bina zyada aadmi lagaye?

**Kabir:** Koi machine. Koi auzaar.

**Madhav:** Hal. Pahiya. Likha hua hisaab. Telephone. Ab dhyaan se batao: hal kitne logon ko khaana khilata hai?

**Kabir:** Sabko. Usise toh kheti hoti hai.

**Madhav:** Nahi. Hal ne aaj tak ek bhi aadmi ko khaana nahi diya. Woh kisi ke muh mein kuch nahi daalta.

**Kabir:** Toh phir woh karta kya hai?

**Madhav:** Kisan ko guna karta hai. Aur kisan logon ko khilata hai.

**Kabir:** Haan. Auzaar seedha insaan ko serve nahi karta. Woh us cheez ko badata hai jo insaan ko serve kar rahi hai.

**Madhav:** Ise yaad rakhna, agle chapter ka poora chapter isi pe hai. Ab aakhri kadam. Ab tak har auzaar ek hi kaam karta tha. Hal hal chalata hai. Use mana kar hisaab-kitaab nahi karwa sakte. Sahi?

**Kabir:** Sahi.

**Madhav:** Aur aaj?

**Kabir:** Aaj aisi cheez hai jo hisaab bhi likhti hai, message bhi, scan bhi padhti hai, raasta bhi banati hai.

**Madhav:** Toh woh ek kaam ka auzaar hai?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Woh... auzaar banane ka auzaar hai.

**Madhav:** Ab poora dhaancha bolo. Neeche se upar.

**Kabir:** Insaan ki zaroorat. Uske upar group jo use scale pe poora karta hai. Uske upar auzaar jo group ko guna karta hai. Aur uske upar woh cheez jo auzaaron ko behtar karti hai.

**Madhav:** Chaar levels. Aur aakhri sawal: paisa in mein kahan ghusta hai?

**Kabir:** Sabse upar? AI companies ke paas toh bahut paisa hai.

**Madhav:** Ghusta kahan hai, rukta kahan hai, yeh alag sawal hai. Jab tum khaana khareedte ho, tumhare haath se paisa kis level pe nikalta hai?

**Kabir:** Level ek pe. Meri zaroorat pe.

**Madhav:** Bas. Paisa hamesha sabse neeche se ghusta hai, jab ek insaan apni zaroorat ke liye deta hai. Uske baad woh upar chadhta hai. Upar wale levels paisa banate nahi, woh neeche se aaya hua paisa aapas mein baantte hain.

### Naam

Chaar levels. Har ek apne neeche wale ko serve karne ke liye hai.

```
    ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │  4   ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE             │   auzaaron ko behtar karta hai
    └───────────────────┬──────────────────────┘
                        ↓ serve karta hai
    ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │  3   TECHNOLOGY                          │   group ko guna karta hai
    └───────────────────┬──────────────────────┘
                        ↓ serve karta hai
    ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │  2   ORGANISATIONS (business)            │   ajnabiyon ko scale pe serve
    └───────────────────┬──────────────────────┘
                        ↓ serve karta hai
    ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │  1   HUMAN NEEDS                         │   sab kuch isi wajah se hai
    └──────────────────────────────────────────┘

    Service NEECHE ki taraf behti hai.
    Paisa UPAR ki taraf, aur woh sirf level 1 pe hi ghusta hai,
    jab ek insaan apni zaroorat ke liye paise deta hai.
```

Teen cheezein check karne laayak hain, kyunki saaf-suthra diagram inhe chhupa deta hai.

**Har zaroorat business se poori nahi hoti.** Maa apne bachche ko khila rahi hai, yeh level 1 seedha serve ho raha hai, na koi organisation, na paisa kahin. Insaan ko ek doosre se jo chahiye, uska bahut bada hissa kabhi transaction tha hi nahi aur kabhi hoga bhi nahi.

**Har business ko technology se madad nahi milti.** Kuch ko mushkil se chhoti hai. Kuch jaan-boojh ke mana karte hain, aur wahi mana karna hi unka product hai.

**Har technology ko AI behtar nahi karta.** Paani ki pipe technology hai. Usmein sochne wali machine ke liye karne ko kuch hai hi nahi.

Yeh stack ek jhukav batata hai, kanoon nahi. Jahan levels gayab hain, aksar wahi jagah sabse interesting hoti hai.

### Asli mein

Koi bhi zaroorat uthao, uske upar chaaron levels abhi baithe milenge.

```

              farm               tractor

sehat     →   hospital       →   scanner,      →   scan padhna
                                 dawaiyan

gyaan     →   school,        →   chhapai,      →   har sawal ka jawab
              university         video             dene wala tutor

suraksha  →   police,        →   radio,        →   pattern pakadna
              insurance          camera

```

Har column bhara hua hai. Us table ka har level 4 wala entry aapse chhota hai.

### Asli duniya se ek example

Aaj raat aap phone se khaana order karte ho. Chaaron levels ek saath chal rahe hain, aur aap sirf ek button dekhte ho.

```
LEVEL 1   Bhookh. Bas. Yeh cheez badalti hi nahi.

LEVEL 2   Ek company jo aapko un rasoiyon se jodti hai jinse aap
          kabhi mile nahi. Restaurant khaana banata hai, delivery
          wala laata hai, dono aapke liye ajnabi hain.
          Yeh woh "group" hai jo ajnabiyon ko scale pe serve karta hai.

LEVEL 3   Phone, internet, GPS, map, payment system.
          Inmein se koi bhi aapko khaana nahi khilata.
          Yeh sab us company ko guna karte hain.
          GPS ke bina ek delivery wala ek din mein 4 order kar paata.
          GPS ke saath 25.

LEVEL 4   Kitne order kahan se aayenge iska andaaza.
          Kaunsa raasta chuna jaaye. Kaunsa delivery wala kaunse
          order ke liye. Yeh level 3 ke auzaaron ko behtar karta hai.
```

Ab neeche se upar ginti karo. Level 1 hazaaron saal purana hai. Level 2 ki shakal purani hai, dhaabe hamesha the. Level 3 ke tukde 20 se 40 saal purane hain. Level 4 lagbhag 10 saal purana hai.

Ek order, chaar levels, aur unki umron mein hazaaron saal ka farak.

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **yeh maan lena ki upar wale level pe hone se apne aap zyada paisa milta hai.**

"AI mein chala jaaunga" sunne mein aisa lagta hai jaise woh khud ek jawab hai. Woh nahi hai. Woh sirf batata hai ki aap kaunsi manzil pe khade honge, na ki us manzil pe aapka kya haal hoga.

Level 4 pe hazaaron log naukri karte hain, ghante bechte hain, aur unka leverage dial bilkul zero pe hai. Woh bas Ravi hain, ek nayi manzil pe. Udhar Level 1 pe koi kisan hai jiske paas apni zameen hai aur woh Ravi se kai guna kama raha hai.

Yeh galti tempting isliye hai kyunki level dikhta hai aur position nahi. Level ka naam hota hai jo aap kisi ko bata sakte ho. Position ka koi naam nahi hota.

Do maps ko saath rakhna hi asli kaam hai: **level batata hai ki aap kis kaam ke aas-paas ho, dials batate hain ki us kaam se aapko kya milega.**

---

### Map pe

Ab aapke paas do map hain jo aapas mein fit hote hain.

Pehla map, pichhle chapter se, batata hai ki **kisi ko kitna milega yeh kya tay karta hai**: value, scarcity aur leverage.

Yeh map batata hai ki **society mein saara kaam hota kahan hai**: chaar levels, har ek apne neeche wale ko serve karta hua.

Dono ko saath rakho aur ek cheez dikhti hai: har level pe aise log hain jo teeno dials pe neeche hain, aur aise bhi jo teeno pe upar hain. Koi level apne aap ameer nahi hai. Sirf levels ke andar ki positions hoti hain.

### Sochne ke liye

Har sawal pe ek minute socho, phir neeche jawab padho.

**1. (samajh check)** Pichhle hafte aapne kisi cheez ke paise diye. Use neeche ki taraf trace karo: kaunsi organisation ne di, woh organisation kis technology pe depend karti hai, aur woh aakhir mein us list mein se kaunsi zaroorat poori karti hai? Agar aap list tak pahunch hi nahi paate, toh woh us cheez ke baare mein kya batata hai jo aapne khareedi?

> **Jawab:** Zyadatar cheezein 2 ya 3 kadmon mein list tak pahunch jaati hain. Chai → dukaan → supply chain → khaana. Recharge → telecom company → tower aur fibre → judav.
>
> Agar aap list tak nahi pahunch paate, toh do mein se ek baat hai.
>
> **Ek:** woh cheez kisi aur ki zaroorat poori karti hai, aapki nahi. Business ke liye khareedi gayi cheezein aksar aisi hoti hain. Woh list tak pahunchti hain, bas kisi aur ke raaste se.
>
> **Do:** woh zaroorat ki nahi, chaah ki cheez hai. Yeh galat nahi hai, insaan hamesha aisi cheezein khareedte aaye hain. Lekin dhyaan rakho: zaroorat ki cheezein mandi mein tikti hain aur chaah ki cheezein sabse pehle katti hain.

**2. (jodne wala)** Chapter 0.2 ke teen dials yaad karo. Kya level 4 pe hone se apne aap leverage mil jaata hai? Ek aisa aadmi socho jo level 4 pe kaam karta hai aur jiska leverage phir bhi kam hai.

> **Jawab:** Nahi, bilkul nahi milta.
>
> Ek aadmi jo kisi company mein AI ka kaam karta hai aur ghante ke hisaab se tankhwah leta hai, uska leverage utna hi hai jitna Ravi ka. Woh chhutti pe jaaye toh uski kamai rukti hai. Kaam woh kar raha hai, lekin jo cheez baar baar chalti rahegi uska maalik koi aur hai.
>
> **Leverage level se nahi aata. Woh iss baat se aata hai ki jo cheez aapke bina chalti rahegi, uspe aapka haq hai ya nahi.**
>
> Isi tarah, level 1 pe bhi bahut leverage ho sakta hai. Jis kisan ke paas apni zameen hai, uske paas ek aisi cheez hai jo har saal fasal deti hai. Zameen uski naali hai.

**3. (design sawal, iss part ka)** Ek zaroorat chuno jo aapko lagta hai theek se poori nahi hoti. Uske chaaron levels likho jaisi woh aaj hain. Phir woh jagah nishaan lagao jahan koi level gayab hai ya kamzor hai. Yeh `WORKBOOK.md` mein likho.

> **Jawab:** Iska ek sahi jawab nahi hai, kyunki yeh aapka apna kaam hai. Lekin dekhne ke liye do cheezein batata hoon.
>
> **Gayab level 3:** ek business chal raha hai lekin usko guna karne wala koi auzaar nahi hai. Log haath se register bhar rahe hain, phone pe hisaab kar rahe hain. Yeh sabse aam khaali jagah hai aur sabse aasan bharne wali.
>
> **Gayab level 2:** zaroorat asli hai lekin use scale pe serve karne wala koi group hi nahi hai. Yeh dhoondhna mushkil hai aur milne pe bada hota hai.
>
> Ek chetavni: khaali jagah dikhne ka matlab hamesha mauka nahi hota. Kabhi kabhi level isliye khaali hai kyunki wahan paisa nahi hai, ya kisi ne koshish karke chhod diya. Chapter 8.5 mein hum isi pe wapas aayenge.
