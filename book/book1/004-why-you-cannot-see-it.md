### Naam

Neeche wali cheez ko chhupa dene ke iss tareeke ka naam hai **abstraction**.

Yeh technology ka sabse zaroori aur sabse kam samjha jaane wala idea hai. Poori computer ki duniya iske alaawa lagbhag kuch bhi nahi hai: parton ka dher, jahan har parat neeche wali ko chhupa deti hai, taaki uske upar wala usse soche bina kaam kar sake.

```
aap ek app istemaal karte ho     taaki program ke baare mein na sochna pade
program ek language istemaal     taaki 1 aur 0 ke baare mein na sochna pade
language ek OS istemaal karti    taaki chip ke baare mein na sochna pade
OS chip istemaal karta hai       taaki electron ke baare mein na sochna pade
```

Koi bhi saari parton ko gehrai se nahi jaanta. Sab kisi chhupi hui parat pe khade hain. Yeh aapke gyaan ka gaddha nahi hai. Yeh poora design hai.

### Asli mein

Jab bijli pehli baar sheheron mein aayi, log ise samajhte the. Ghar ke malik apna fuse badalte the aur unhe pata hota tha ki circuit kaise banta hai. Woh isliye nahi ki woh aaj ke logon se hoshiyaar the. Woh isliye ki technology abhi itni achhi nahi thi ki gayab ho sake.

Ab bijli aapke liye ek switch hai. Behtar technology, kam samajh. Yeh exchange har ek cheez ke saath hua hai.

Aur wahi abhi AI ke saath ho raha hai, sirf bahut tezi se. Aap ek box mein type karte ho aur jawab aa jaata hai. Beech ka sab kuch pehle hi gayab ho chuka hai, jabki cheez bani hue paanch saal bhi theek se nahi hue.

### Asli duniya se ek example

1958 mein Leonard Read ne ek chhota lekh likha jiska naam tha "I, Pencil". Usmein ek pencil apni kahani khud sunati hai.

Uski baat ek hi thi: **duniya mein ek bhi aisa insaan nahi hai jo ek pencil bana sake.**

Lakdi kisi ne kaati, aari kisi aur ne banayi, us aari ka steel kisi teesre ne, us steel ka loha kisi khadaan se. Graphite Sri Lanka se, mitti Mississippi se, mom, rang, rubber, us rubber ko jodne wala kemikal. Us pencil ke peeche hazaaron log hain, alag deshon mein, jo ek doosre ko jaante tak nahi, aur jinmein se koi bhi akela pencil nahi bana sakta.

Ab yeh yaad rakho aur socho ki ek phone kya cheez hai. Ya ek AI model.

Aur ab dhyaan do ki iss baat ne aapko chaunkaya. **Pencil sabse simple cheez hai jo aap soch sakte ho.** Woh bhi chhupi hui hai. Yeh soch ki "mujhe technology samajh nahi aati" us pencil se hi shuru ho jaati hai.

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **yeh sochna ki technology samajh na aana meri kami hai.**

Yeh baat andar hi andar chalti rehti hai. Log sochte hain ki baaki sabko yeh sab pata hai aur woh akele peeche reh gaye. Isse sharm aati hai, aur sharm ki wajah se log sawal poochna band kar dete hain, jisse gaddha aur bada hota hai.

Lekin jo abhi aapne dekha, uske hisaab se yeh galat hai. Cheezein banai hi is tarah gayi hain ki samjhe bina chal jaayein. **Aapko woh nahi dikh raha jo dikhane ke liye banaya hi nahi gaya tha.**

Ab yahan par ek baat saaf kar deta hoon, kyunki iska ulta bhi galat hai.

Yeh iss baat ka bahana nahi hai ki samajhne ki koshish hi na ki jaaye. Pichhla section abhi bata chuka hai ki chhupi hui cheez ko aap istemaal toh kar sakte ho, lekin uspe kuch bana nahi sakte. Toh kami aapki nahi hai, aur keemat phir bhi aapko hi chukani padti hai.

Sahi baat yeh hai: **peeche hona aapki galti nahi hai, lekin usse nikalna aapka hi kaam hai.**

### Sochne ke liye

Har sawal pe ek minute socho, phir neeche jawab padho.

**1. (samajh check)** Aaj aapne jo cheezein istemaal ki hain, unmein se ek chuno, aur us sabki list banao jo woh aapse chhupa rahi hai. Kahan tak neeche ja sakte ho, jab tak aapko sach mein pata na ho ki neeche kya hai?

> **Jawab:** Zyadatar log 2 ya 3 kadam neeche jaakar ruk jaate hain. Nal se pipe tak, pipe se tanki tak, aur uske baad andhera.
>
> Woh rukne ki jagah hi kaam ki cheez hai. Woh batati hai ki aapki abstraction ki deewar kahan khadi hai.
>
> Aur dhyaan do ki har cheez ke liye woh deewar alag jagah hai. Jo cheez aapne khud thodi bahut kholi hai, uske liye aap gehre ja paate ho. Yeh saabit karta hai ki deewar aapke dimaag ki seema nahi hai. Woh sirf yeh hai ki aapne ab tak kya khola aur kya nahi.

**2. (samajh check)** Chapter kehta hai ki achhi technology gayab ho jaati hai. Toh phir ek achhi technology aur ek aisi cheez mein kya farak hai jo hai hi nahi? Aap doosron ko kaise samjhaoge ki koi chhupi hui cheez wahan maujood hai?

> **Jawab:** Farak tab dikhta hai jab woh **ruk jaaye**.
>
> Jo cheez hai hi nahi, woh ruk nahi sakti. Achhi technology jab rukti hai toh poora ka poora andar ka jaal ek saath saamne aa jaata hai. Light jaane ke din aapko pata chalta hai ki bijli ka grid maujood tha.
>
> Isiliye zyadatar log technology ke baare mein sirf uske toot-ne se seekhte hain. Aur isiliye woh seekh tukdon mein rehti hai: aapko sirf woh hissa dikhta hai jo us din toota tha.
>
> Ek doosra tareeka bhi hai: keemat dekho. Har chhupi hui cheez ko koi na koi chala raha hai aur uska kharcha uth raha hai. Jahan paisa ja raha hai, wahan kuch maujood hai. Part 7 mein hum yehi karenge.

**3. (jodne wala)** Chapter 0.2 kehta tha ki pay value, scarcity aur leverage se aata hai. Agar abstraction ka matlab hai ki bahut kam log neeche wali parat samajhte hain, toh us parat ko samajhne wale ke liye scarcity ka dial kahan baithta hai? Aur woh dial waqt ke saath kis taraf khisak raha hai?

> **Jawab:** Jitni gehri parat, utna upar scarcity ka dial. Yeh seedha nikal aata hai: samajhne wale kam hain, isliye jo samajhta hai woh durlabh hai.
>
> Dooosra hissa zyada important hai, aur uska jawab do tarah se sach hai.
>
> **Ek taraf:** har naya abstraction ek purani parat ko scarce se common bana deta hai. Pehle server chalane ke liye machine ka gehra gyaan chahiye tha. Cloud aane ke baad kam log ko uski zaroorat rahi. Us parat ka dial neeche khisak gaya.
>
> **Doosri taraf:** wahi abstraction ek nayi, aur upar wali parat bana deta hai, jahan dial ekdum upar hai, kyunki cheez nayi hai aur abhi kisi ko samajh nahi aayi.
>
> Toh scarcity gayab nahi hoti. Woh upar khisak jaati hai.
>
> Yeh iss poori kitaab ka sabse kaam ka nateeja hai, aur Book 2 isi jagah pe khadi hai. AI abhi wahi nayi parat hai.
