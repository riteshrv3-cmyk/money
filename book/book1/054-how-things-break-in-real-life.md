# Chapter 8.4  [DEPTH]
## Asli zindagi mein cheezein kaise tootti hain

---

*DEPTH chapter. Ismein sirf ghatnaayein hain. Har ek kisi na kisi chapter ki baat ko asli duniya mein saabit karti hai.*

---

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Ab tak humne dekha ki cheezein kaise chalti hain. Ab dekhte hain ki woh kaise girti hain. Ek andaza lagao: bade systems mein sabse zyada nuksaan kis wajah se hota hai? Hacker? Machine kharaab hona?

**Kabir:** Hacker.

**Madhav:** Nahi. Sabse zyada nuksaan un badlaon se hota hai jo khud us company ke logon ne kiye.

**Kabir:** Apne hi log?

**Madhav:** Ek settings ki line badli. Ek naya version daala. Ek command galat jagah chali. Yeh sabse aam wajah hai, har saal, har jagah.

**Kabir:** Toh badlav hi khatra hai.

**Madhav:** Aur badlav zaroori bhi hai. Toh sawal yeh nahi hai ki badlav rok do. Sawal yeh hai ki badlav ko kaise kiya jaaye ki galti chhoti rahe.

**Kabir:** Kaise?

**Madhav:** Do niyam. Pehla: ek saath sab jagah mat badlo. Thodi jagah badlo, dekho, phir aage badho.

**Kabir:** Aur agar galat nikla?

**Madhav:** Doosra niyam: wapas jaane ka raasta hamesha taiyaar rakho, badlav se pehle.

**Kabir:** Aur agar wapas jaane ka raasta hi kaam na kare?

**Madhav:** Toh woh raasta hai hi nahi. Chapter 5.7 wali baat: jo jaancha nahi gaya woh hai hi nahi.

### Chaar ghatnaayein

**1. Ek naam gayab hua, aur duniya ruk gayi**

2021 mein ek badi social media company ke saare product lagbhag chhe ghante ke liye poori duniya se gayab ho gaye.

Server chal rahe the. Data theek tha. Lekin ek badlav ke baad unke pate baantne wali jaankari internet se hat gayi, aur naam ka jawab dene ka koi raasta nahi bacha.

Aur ek nateeja aur nikla: unke office ke darwaze bhi unhi systems se chalte the. Log andar nahi ja paaye us cheez ko theek karne jisne unhe bahar kar diya tha.

```
Chapter 4.5   naam poore internet ki neev mein hai
Chapter 6.2   koi ek jagah aisi na ho jo sab rok de
```

**2. Aath machinein, saat pe naya code**

2012 mein ek trading company ne naya code daala. Aath machinein thin, saat pe naya gaya, ek chhoot gayi.

Us ek machine pe purana code chalta raha, aur uska ek bahut purana hissa naye code ke saath milkar galat kaam karne laga.

45 minute mein lagbhag 44 crore dollar chale gaye. Company us hafte lagbhag khatam ho gayi.

```
Chapter 7.1   galti hisson ke BEECH mein thi, kisi ek hisse mein nahi
Chapter 7.4   haath se daalna hi asli galti thi
```

**3. Paanch bachaav, chaar bekaar**

2017 mein ek company ke engineer ne raat ko, thake hue, ek command galat server pe chala di. 300 GB asli data mit gaya.

Unke paas paanch alag bachaav the. Chaar us waqt kaam nahi kar rahe the: kuch chup-chaap band ho chuke the, kuch se banti hui files khaali thin, kisi ki kabhi jaanch nahi hui thi.

Paanchva ek chhe ghante purana snapshot tha, jo kisi ne kisi aur kaam ke liye banaya tha. Usi se woh bache.

```
Chapter 5.7   jo bachaav jaancha nahi gaya, woh bachaav hai hi nahi
```

**4. Ek cloud service baithi, hazaaron site baith gayin**

Ek badi cloud company ki ek service kuch ghante ke liye baith gayi. Uske saath duniya bhar ki hazaaron websites aur apps baith gayin.

Un hazaaron mein se har ek ne apna system mazboot banaya tha: kai machinein, backup, sab kuch.

Lekin sabke neeche ek hi cheez thi.

```
Chapter 6.2   aapka system utna hi mazboot hai jitni uski
              sabse kamzor NEECHE WALI parat
Chapter 6.5   cloud ki nirbharta
```

### Chaaron mein saanjha kya hai

```
KISI MEIN BHI HACKER NAHI THA
KISI MEIN BHI MACHINE KHARAAB NAHI HUI
KISI MEIN BHI CODE MEIN GALTI NAHI THI
```

Chaaron mein:

```
1. badlav apne hi logon ne kiya
2. woh cheez jo toot ti hai woh aksar HISSON KE BEECH hoti hai
3. bachaav maujood tha aur kaam nahi kar raha tha
4. neeche wali parat pe nirbharta, jise koi yaad nahi rakhta
```

Aur ek aur baat, jo sabse zaroori hai: **har ghatna mein cheez ek jhatke mein giri, dheere-dheere nahi.** Chapter 6.6 wali baat, asli duniya mein.

---

### Do niyam jo har jagah kaam karte hain

**1. Thoda badlo, dekho, phir aage badho.**

Naya code pehle ek pratishat logon ko do. Kuch der dekho. Theek lage toh das pratishat. Phir sab.

Iska naam alag alag jagah alag hai, lekin soch ek hi hai: **agar kuch galat hai toh woh ek pratishat ko dikhe, sabko nahi.**

**2. Wapas jaane ka raasta pehle banao, badlav se pehle.**

Aur woh raasta ek button hona chahiye, ek plan nahi. Kyunki jab cheez giri hui hoti hai, tab kisi ke paas plan padhne ka waqt nahi hota.

Aur teesra, jo in dono se nikalta hai: **jab kuch toota ho, tab sabse pehle use theek karo, phir dhoondho ki kyun toota tha.** Log ulta karte hain, aur system tab tak toota rehta hai jab tak wajah na mil jaaye.

---

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **yeh sochna ki galti kisi aadmi ki thi.**

Har ghatna ke baad ek aadmi milta hai jisne button dabaya tha. Aur woh sabse bekaar jawab hai.

Sawal yeh nahi hai ki usne button kyun dabaya. Sawal yeh hai ki **ek aadmi ke ek button se poora system kaise gir sakta tha.**

Agar ek aadmi ki ek galti se 44 crore dollar ja sakte hain, toh problem us aadmi mein nahi hai. Problem us system mein hai jismein aisi galti mumkin thi.

Isiliye achhi teamein ghatna ke baad aadmi ka naam nahi dhoondhti. Woh yeh dhoondhti hain ki kaunsi cheez ne is galti ko itna aasan bana diya, aur use kaise mushkil banaya jaaye.

Doosri galti: **yeh sochna ki yeh sirf badi companies ke saath hota hai.**

Yeh chhoti jagahon pe zyada hota hai. Bade systems ki ghatnaayein sirf isliye mashhoor hoti hain ki woh dikhti hain, aur unhe likh diya jaata hai.

### Sochne ke liye

**1. (samajh check)** Aapki team naya code hafte mein ek baar, shukravaar shaam ko daalti hai. Kya problem hai?

> **Jawab:** Do.
>
> **Ek:** hafte bhar ke badlav ek saath jaate hain. Kuch galat ho toh pata hi nahi chalega ki kis badlav se hua. Chhote badlav dhoondhna aasan banate hain.
>
> **Do:** shukravaar shaam ka matlab hai ki agar kuch toota toh koi maujood nahi hoga.
>
> Isiliye kai jagah "shukravaar ko kuch mat daalo" ek niyam hai. Aur behtar jagahon pe badlav itne chhote aur itne aam ho jaate hain ki din ka farak hi khatam ho jaata hai.

**2. (samajh check)** Ek ghatna ke baad manager kehta hai "aage se dhyaan rakhna." Kya yeh kaafi hai?

> **Jawab:** Nahi, aur woh lagbhag bekaar hai.
>
> "Dhyaan rakhna" kisi cheez ko nahi badalta. Agla aadmi bhi dhyaan rakh raha tha.
>
> Kaam ka jawab yeh hai: **us galti ko namumkin ya mushkil kaise banaya jaaye?**
>
> Command chalane se pehle poochha jaaye? Asli server pe alag rang dikhe? Ek aadmi akela na kar sake? Wapas jaane ka button ho?
>
> **Yaad rakhne ki jagah dhaancha badlo.** Yaddasht har baar fail hoti hai. Dhaancha nahi.

**3. (jodne wala)** Chaaron ghatnaon mein bachaav maujood tha. Teen mein woh kaam nahi kar raha tha. Yeh iss kitaab ki kaunsi baat dohrata hai?

> **Jawab:** Chapter 5.7: **jo jaancha nahi gaya, woh hai hi nahi.**
>
> Aur uska ek gehra roop yeh hai: bachaav ke saath ek khaas problem hai. Woh chup-chaap kharaab hota hai.
>
> Ek website toot jaaye toh turant pata chalta hai. Ek backup toot jaaye toh saalon tak kisi ko pata nahi chalta, kyunki woh sirf us din kaam aata hai jab zaroorat padti hai.
>
> Isiliye bade systems mein backup ko baar baar **wapas laakar** dekha jaata hai, apne aap, aur agar woh na aaye toh alarm bajta hai.
>
> **Jo cheez sirf bure din kaam aati hai, use achhe din jaancha jaana chahiye.**
