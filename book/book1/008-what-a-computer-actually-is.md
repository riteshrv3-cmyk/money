# Chapter 1.3  [SPINE]
## Computer actually hai kya

---

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Ek switch ki do haalatein hain. Ab do switch ek taar mein ek ke baad ek lagao. Bulb jalega kab?

**Kabir:** Jab dono chalu hon. Ek bhi band hua toh raasta toota.

**Madhav:** Aur agar dono switch alag-alag raaston pe hon, aur dono raaste bulb tak jaate hon?

**Kabir:** Toh ek bhi chalu ho toh bulb jal jaayega.

**Madhav:** Ruko. Tumne abhi jo bola, use dhyaan se suno. Pehle case mein tumne kya kaha?

**Kabir:** Dono chalu hon tabhi.

**Madhav:** Aur doosre mein?

**Kabir:** Ek bhi chalu ho toh kaafi.

**Madhav:** Tumne abhi do faisle banaye. Sirf taar jodne ke tareeke se. "Dono" aur "koi ek." Yeh sirf bijli nahi hai. Yeh ek shart hai.

**Kabir:** Theek hai, lekin yeh toh bahut chhoti baat hai.

**Madhav:** Ek teesra tareeka bhi hai. Aisa switch socho jo ulta chalta hai. Bijli aaye toh raasta tode, na aaye toh jode.

**Kabir:** Woh kis kaam ka?

**Madhav:** Woh "nahi" hai. Ab tumhare paas teen cheezein hain: **dono**, **koi ek**, aur **nahi**. Ab main tumse ek badi baat kahunga aur tum use abhi maanoge nahi. In teen cheezon se, kaafi saari lagakar, tum har woh sawal hal kar sakte ho jiska jawab haan ya na mein ho.

**Kabir:** Har sawal? Jaise?

**Madhav:** Jaise: yeh do numbers barabar hain? Yeh number us number se bada hai? Yeh tareekh guzar chuki hai? Yeh password sahi hai?

**Kabir:** Yeh sab sirf "dono, koi ek, nahi" se?

**Madhav:** Sirf isse. Ek udaharan lo. Do switch, A aur B. Main jaanna chahta hoon ki dono alag hain ya nahi. Batao kaise pata karoge.

**Kabir:** Hmm. Agar A chalu hai aur B band, toh alag hain. Ya A band aur B chalu, tab bhi alag hain.

**Madhav:** Toh usko teen cheezon mein likho.

**Kabir:** "A aur B-nahi" wala case. Ya "A-nahi aur B" wala case. Koi ek ho jaaye toh alag hain.

**Madhav:** Tumne abhi taaron se tulna banayi. Kisi ne tumhe formula nahi diya. Ab yeh socho: agar do cheezein barabar hain ya nahi, yeh switchon se pata chal sakta hai, toh kya cheez nahi chal sakti?

**Kabir:** Pata nahi. Shayad kuch bhi nahi.

**Madhav:** Ab teesri baat, aur yeh sabse zaroori hai. Pichhle chapter mein maine kaha tha ki ek transistor doosre ko daba sakta hai. Iska matlab kya hua?

**Kabir:** Ki ek shart ka jawab doosri shart mein daala ja sakta hai.

**Madhav:** Aur uska jawab teesri mein. Aur aage. Kitni door tak?

**Kabir:** Jitne switch hon.

**Madhav:** Aur tumhare phone mein kitne hain?

**Kabir:** Bees arab.

**Madhav:** Toh tumhare paas bees arab shartein hain, ek doosre mein daali hui, aur woh ek second mein arbon baar chal sakti hain. Ab batao, computer kya hai?

**Kabir:** Switch ka dhher, jo ek doosre ko dabate hain, aur milkar shartein banate hain.

**Madhav:** Aur uske alaawa?

**Kabir:** Kuch nahi?

**Madhav:** Kuch nahi. Ab poori kitaab mein hum yehi karenge: dekhte jaayenge ki iske upar parat pe parat kaise chadhi.

### Naam

Woh teen cheezein jo Kabir ne banayi, unke naam hain **AND**, **OR** aur **NOT**.

```
AND    dono chalu hon tabhi        (ek ke baad ek taar)
OR     koi ek chalu ho toh kaafi   (alag alag raaste)
NOT    ulta kar do                 (chalu ho toh band)
```

Inhe **logic gates** kehte hain. Gate isliye ki woh bijli ko rok bhi sakte hain aur jaane bhi de sakte hain, shart ke hisaab se.

Aur jo baat Kabir ne khud nikaali, uska naam bhi hai. Yeh saabit ho chuka hai ki **in teen se** har woh cheez banayi ja sakti hai jo koi bhi computer karta hai. Aur jodna, ghatana, tulna, yaad rakhna, sab.

Toh ek computer ki poori definition itni hai:

> Bahut saare switch, aise jude hue ki woh AND, OR aur NOT banayein, aur ek ka jawab doosre mein jaaye.

Iske alaawa computer mein kuch nahi hai. Baaki sab jo aap dekhte ho, wo iske upar chadhi hui parat hai.

### Asli duniya se ek example

1937 mein ek 21 saal ka student, Claude Shannon, ne apni master's thesis likhi. Usne dikhaya ki bijli ke switchon ke jaal aur tark ke niyam bilkul ek jaisi cheez hain.

Us waqt switch phone ke exchange mein lagte the, call jodne ke liye. Kisi ne unhe sochne wali cheez nahi maana tha.

Shannon ne dikhaya ki agar aap switch ko tark ki tarah likh sakte ho, toh aap tark ko switch se bana bhi sakte ho.

Woh thesis lagbhag har digital cheez ki neev hai jo uske baad bani. Aur usmein koi nayi machine nahi thi, sirf yeh dekhna tha ki do purani cheezein ek hi cheez hain.

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **yeh sochna ki computer "sochta" hai.**

Log kehte hain computer ne decide kiya, computer ko laga, computer samajh gaya. Yeh sirf bolne ka tareeka nahi hai, log sach mein aisa mehsoos karte hain.

Computer koi faisla nahi leta. Usmein bees arab shartein pehle se lagi hui hain, aur bijli un shartों mein se guzar jaati hai. Jo bahar aata hai, woh jawab hai.

Ek chaabi wale taale se socho. Sahi chaabi ghumti hai, galat nahi ghumti. Taale ne kuch soch kar faisla nahi kiya. Uski shakal aisi hai. Computer ek taala hai jismein bees arab pins hain aur unhe har second badla ja sakta hai.

Yeh galti tempting isliye hai ki bahar se dono ek jaise dikhte hain. Aap sawal poochte ho, jawab aata hai. Andar kya hua, woh dikhta nahi.

Yeh baat Book 2 mein bahut kaam aayegi, jab hum AI kholenge. Wahan yeh galti sabse zyada hoti hai.

### Sochne ke liye

Har sawal pe ek minute socho, phir neeche jawab padho.

**1. (samajh check)** Ek ghar mein aisa taala lagana hai jo tabhi khule jab do log ek saath apni chaabi lagayein. Kaunsa gate hai yeh? Ab aisa taala jo kisi ek chaabi se bhi khul jaaye?

> **Jawab:** Pehla AND hai. Doosra OR.
>
> Bank ki tijoriyon mein sach mein AND lagta hai: do alag logon ki chaabi, ek saath. Ghar ke darwaze pe OR hota hai: aapki chaabi ya ghar ke kisi aur ki, dono chalti hain.
>
> Dhyaan do ki yeh koi computer ki cheez nahi hai. Yeh sirf shart hai. Computer ne shart invent nahi ki, usne use bijli mein daal diya.

**2. (samajh check)** Madhav ne kaha ki AND, OR aur NOT se har haan-na wala sawal hal ho sakta hai. Ek aisa sawal sochne ki koshish karo jo iss tareeke se hal na ho.

> **Jawab:** Agar sawal ka jawab pakka haan ya na hai, aur jo cheezein us jawab ko tay karti hain woh sab pata hain, toh woh hal ho jaayega. Iska koi apwaad nahi hai. Yeh saabit ho chuka hai.
>
> Jo sawal hal nahi honge, woh do tarah ke hain:
>
> **Jinka jawab haan-na mein hai hi nahi.** "Yeh gaana achha hai?" Yahan pehle yeh tay karna padega ki achhe ka matlab kya hai. Woh tay ho jaaye toh sawal hal ho jaayega, lekin jawab aapki di hui paribhasha ka hoga, sachai ka nahi.
>
> **Jinka jawab tay karne wali cheezein pata hi nahi hain.** "Kal baarish hogi?" Yeh switch ki kami nahi hai, yeh jaankari ki kami hai.
>
> Yeh farak yaad rakhna. Book 2 mein AI ki lagbhag saari seemayein inhi do mein se ek hain.

**3. (jodne wala)** Chapter 1.1 mein aapne dekha ki har naye switch pe haalatein dugni ho jaati hain. Ab jodo: agar bees arab switch hain, toh kya sach mein har mumkin haalat kabhi istemaal hoti hai?

> **Jawab:** Nahi, bilkul nahi. Aur farak kalpana se bahar hai.
>
> Bees arab switch se jitni haalatein banti hain, woh brahmand ke saare paramanuon se bhi behisaab zyada hain. Unmein se ek bahut hi chhota hissa hi kabhi kaam mein aata hai.
>
> Toh phir itne switch kyun? Kyunki aapko pehle se nahi pata ki kaunsi haalat chahiye hogi. Machine ko har mumkin cheez ke liye taiyaar rehna padta hai, isliye nahi ki sab hongi, balki isliye ki koi bhi ho sakti hai.
>
> Yeh soch aage kai jagah dikhegi: **jagah isliye nahi banayi jaati ki woh bhar jaayegi, balki isliye ki pehle se pata nahi ki kya aayega.**
