# Chapter 6.6  [SPINE]
## Jo aap type karte hain uska kya hota hai

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Aap ek chat box mein kuch likhte ho. Woh kahan jaata hai?

**Kabir:** Kisi company ke server pe. Chapter 1.1.

**Madhav:** Raaste mein koi padh sakta hai?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Taala hai. Book 1 Ch 4.6.

**Madhav:** Aur doosre sire pe?

**Kabir:** Wahan woh khula hai. Taala sirf raaste mein bachata hai.

**Madhav:** Toh wahan uska kya hota hai?

**Kabir:** Model use padhta hai.

**Madhav:** Model ek hisaab hai. Woh padh kar bhool jaata hai. Uske alaawa?

**Kabir:** Woh kahin likha ja sakta hai.

**Madhav:** Kyun likhega koi?

**Kabir:** Taaki main apni purani chat dekh sakun.

**Madhav:** Aur?

**Kabir:** Agar kuch galat ho toh dekh sakein.

**Madhav:** Aur?

**Kabir:** Agle model ki training ke liye. Chapter 2.2.

**Madhav:** Teen alag wajahein, teen alag matlab. Ab batao: in teeno mein aapke liye sabse bada farak kaunsa hai?

**Kabir:** Teesra. Kyunki tab meri baat kisi aur ke model ka hissa ban jaayegi.

**Madhav:** Aur nikalne ka koi rasta hai?

**Kabir:** Chapter 2.3 ke hisaab se nahi. Woh arbon numbers mein bikhar jaayegi.

**Madhav:** Ab ek company kehti hai "hum aapke data pe train nahi karte." Kya isse aapki baat rakhna band ho jaata hai?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Woh sirf teesri wajah hata rahe hain. Baaki do abhi bhi hain.

**Madhav:** Toh do alag sawal ho gaye.

**Kabir:** Ek: kya woh rakha jaata hai. Do: kya woh training mein jaata hai.

**Madhav:** Aur teesra bhi hai. Kaun dekh sakta hai?

**Kabir:** Unke apne log? Agar kuch jaanchna ho toh.

**Madhav:** Aur sarkar, agar kanooni maang aaye?

**Kabir:** Toh jo rakha hai woh diya ja sakta hai.

**Madhav:** Ab ek aakhri baat, aur yeh sabse zyada nazarandaaz hoti hai. Aapne AI se apni ek file ke baare mein poocha. Kya woh file bheji gayi?

**Kabir:** Haan, poori.

**Madhav:** Aur usmein kya kya tha?

**Kabir:** Pata nahi. Shayad woh sab jo maine kabhi usmein likha.

**Madhav:** Toh aapne kya bheja?

**Kabir:** Maine socha maine ek sawal bheja. Maine poori file bhej di.

### Naam

Teen alag sawal, aur log inhe hamesha mila dete hain:

```
1.  KYA WOH RAKHA JAATA HAI?
    lagbhag hamesha haan, kuch waqt ke liye
    wajah: aapki history, jaanch, galti dekhna, kanoon

2.  KYA WOH TRAINING MEIN JAATA HAI?
    yeh alag sawal hai
    aur yeh aksar badla ja sakta hai (settings ya plan se)

3.  KAUN DEKH SAKTA HAI?
    unke kuch log, jaanch ke liye
    aur kanooni maang aane pe, jo rakha hai woh diya ja sakta hai
```

Aur ek chauthi baat, jo sabse zyada chhoot ti hai:

> **Aap sirf apna sawal nahi bhejte. Aap woh sab bhejte ho jo sawal ke saath jaata hai.**

Poori purani chat (Ch 1.2). Woh file jo aapne di. Woh document jo RAG ne chuna (Ch 6.2). Woh code jo agent ne padha (Ch 6.5).

Aur zyadatar mein aapko yeh dikhaya nahi jaata ki kya kya bheja gaya.

Aam taur pe do alag tarah ke istemaal hote hain, aur unke niyam alag hote hain:

```
AAM CHAT PRODUCT     history rakhi jaati hai
                     training mein jaana aksar default hota hai,
                     aur band kiya ja sakta hai

KAAROBARI / API      aksar training mein nahi jaata, default se
                     rakhne ka waqt kam hota hai
                     kyunki companies isi shart pe aati hain
```

**Yeh niyam badalte rehte hain. Yeh un chapters mein se hai jo sabse jaldi purana hoga.** Jo nahi badlega woh yeh teen sawal hain, aur unhe poochna aapki aadat honi chahiye.

### Asli duniya se

Ek baar baar hone wali ghatna: kisi company ke log apne andar ke kaagaz AI mein daal dete hain, aur kuch mahine baad company ko pata chalta hai ki uska andar ka data bahar ja chuka hai.

Kisi ne chori nahi ki. Kisi ne kanoon nahi toda. Logon ne bas apna kaam aasan karne ke liye ek box mein text daala.

Aur wahi teen sawal kisi ne nahi poocha tha.

Isliye ab bahut si companies do mein se ek raasta chunti hain: ya toh aisi service jismein data training mein na jaaye aur kam waqt rakha jaaye, ya apna model apne server pe.

Aur ab aap jaante ho ki doosra raasta kyun mumkin hai: **model ek file hai** (Ch 2.9). Agar aapke paas woh file hai, toh aapka data kahin nahi jaata.

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **taale ko poori suraksha samajhna.**

Taala raaste mein bachata hai. Doosre sire pe aapka text khula hai. *(Book 1, Ch 4.6)*

Doosri galti: **"hum train nahi karte" ko "hum rakhte nahi" samajhna.**

Yeh do alag baatein hain. Zyadatar services rakhti hain, kuch din se kuch mahine, chahe woh train na karein.

Teesri galti, aur yeh sabse aam hai: **yeh bhool jaana ki poora sandarbh jaata hai.**

Aapne ek chhota sawal poocha, aur uske saath do ghante ki purani chat gayi, jismein shayad aapka koi password ya kisi customer ka naam tha.

**Ek saada niyam jo yaad rakhne laayak hai:**

> Agar aap woh cheez ek ajnabi vendor ko email nahi karoge, toh use AI ke box mein bhi mat daalo, jab tak aapne teen sawal na poochhe hon.

### Sochne ke liye

**1. (samajh check)** Aapki company kehti hai "AI istemaal karo lekin customer ka data mat daalo." Aap ek file ke baare mein sawal poochte ho. Kya khatra hai?

> **Jawab:** Poori file jaati hai, sirf aapka sawal nahi.
>
> Aur us file mein kya kya hai, yeh aapko poora yaad nahi hota. Ek purana naam, ek email, ek number, jo kabhi kisi ne daala tha.
>
> **Aapne niyam nahi toda. Aapne yeh nahi socha ki bheja kya ja raha hai.**
>
> Ilaaj: sirf zaroori hissa bhejo, poori file nahi. Aur agar poori bhejni hi hai, toh pehle dekho ki usmein kya hai.

**2. (samajh check)** Ek company apna model apne server pe chalati hai. Usne kya bacha liya aur kya kho diya?

> **Jawab:** Bacha liya: unka data kahin nahi jaata. Poori tarah unke control mein.
>
> Kho diya: unhe machinein chalani padengi, jo mehngi hain (Ch 4.1). Aur khule model aksar sabse bade models se thode peeche hote hain.
>
> **Yeh Book 1 Ch 6.5 wala kirya-ya-kharid wala sawal hai, ab data ke saath.**
>
> Aur jawab wahi hai: uss baat pe nirbhar karta hai ki galti ki keemat kya hai. Ek hospital ke liye ek jawab hai, ek blog ke liye doosra.

**3. (jodne wala)** Book 1 Ch 4.2 mein tha ki beech mein hamesha koi hota hai aur woh sab dekhta hai. Yahan uska poora roop kya hai?

> **Jawab:** Yeh usi baat ka sabse bada roop hai.
>
> AI ke saath aap sirf ek sandesh nahi bhej rahe. Aap apni soch, apna kaam, apna code, apne documents, aur apne sawal bhej rahe ho, roz.
>
> **Aur woh sab kuch ek hi jagah ikattha ho raha hai.**
>
> Yeh sirf privacy ka sawal nahi hai. Book 1 Chapter 6.9 ke hisaab se, yeh yeh bhi batata hai ki scarcity kahan ban rahi hai. Jiske paas woh data hai, uske paas aage kya hoga, iska sabse saaf naksha hai.
>
> Chapter 7.1 mein hum isi pe wapas aayenge.
