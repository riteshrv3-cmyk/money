# Chapter 5.5  [SPINE]
## Jo cheezein sab galat samajhte hain

*Yeh chapter Part 1 se 5 tak ki har baat ko ek jagah pe rakh kar dohrata hai, un galtiyon ke roop mein jo sabse zyada hoti hain.*

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Ab main aapko das baatein sunata hoon jo log kehte hain. Aap batao ki har ek mein kya galat hai.

**Kabir:** Theek.

**Madhav:** "AI mere phone ke andar hai."

**Kabir:** Zyadatar nahi. Phone ek khidki hai. Model kisi building mein hai. *(1.1)*

**Madhav:** "Woh mujhse baat karke seekh raha hai."

**Kabir:** Uski file badalti hi nahi. Purani baat dobara bheji ja rahi hai. *(1.2, 2.2)*

**Madhav:** "Woh soch raha hai."

**Kabir:** Usmein yaaddasht nahi, irada nahi, aur sawalon ke beech woh maujood hi nahi hota. *(1.2)*

**Madhav:** "Woh sirf agla shabd guess karta hai, isliye kuch nahi kar sakta."

**Kabir:** Yeh bhi galat hai. Woh sach mein agla shabd chunta hai, aur woh sach mein mushkil kaam kar leta hai. Dono ek saath sach hain. *(1.4)*

**Madhav:** "Woh sab kuch jaanta hai, usne internet padha hai."

**Kabir:** Usne ek chhaana hua hissa dekha, ek tarikh tak, aur usmein se sirf patterns bache. *(2.3, 2.6, 2.7)*

**Madhav:** "Bada model hamesha behtar hota hai."

**Kabir:** Size teen cheezon mein se ek hai. Chhota naya model bade purane se behtar ho sakta hai. *(3.5)*

**Madhav:** "Hallucination ek bug hai jo theek ho jaayega."

**Kabir:** Woh us tareeke ke andar hai. Kam ho sakta hai, khatam nahi. *(5.1)*

**Madhav:** "Agar woh aatmvishwas se keh raha hai toh shayad sahi hai."

**Kabir:** Aatmvishwas dhaala gaya tha, kyunki logon ne use pasand kiya. Uska sach se rishta nahi hai. *(2.8)*

**Madhav:** "Yeh benchmark mein 95 percent laata hai, toh mera kaam kar lega."

**Kabir:** Benchmark ek aam kaam naapta hai, aur mera kaam aam nahi hai. *(5.3)*

**Madhav:** Aur aakhri: "yeh sab jaadu hai, samjha nahi ja sakta."

**Kabir:** Mechanism poori tarah saaf hai. Jo samjha nahi ja sakta woh andar ke numbers ka matlab hai. Do alag baatein. *(3.4)*

**Madhav:** Aap poori kitaab ka nichod bol chuke ho.

### Das galtiyan, aur unke sahi roop

```
1.  "AI mere phone mein hai"
    →  aksar nahi. Phone khidki hai, model kisi building mein.

2.  "Woh mujhse seekh raha hai"
    →  uski file sthir hai. Purani baat dobara bheji jaati hai.
        Haan, aapki baat AGLE model ko sikha sakti hai.

3.  "Woh soch raha hai"
    →  koi yaaddasht nahi, koi irada nahi, sawalon ke beech
        woh maujood hi nahi.

4.  "Woh sirf guess karta hai, isliye bekaar hai"
    →  bhi galat. Woh guess karta hai AUR mushkil kaam karta hai.
        "Sirf" wala shabd hi galti hai.

5.  "Usne internet padh liya, sab jaanta hai"
    →  chhaana hua hissa, ek tarikh tak, aur sirf patterns.

6.  "Bada matlab behtar"
    →  size, data aur dhalai, teen cheezein. Size ek hai.

7.  "Hallucination theek ho jaayega"
    →  kam hoga. Khatam nahi. Woh tareeke ke andar hai.

8.  "Aatmvishwas se kaha, toh shayad sahi hai"
    →  aatmvishwas dhaala gaya hai. Woh naap nahi hai.

9.  "Benchmark achha hai toh mera kaam ho jaayega"
    →  apna chhota test banao. Bees sawal kaafi hain.

10. "Yeh jaadu hai"
    →  mechanism saaf hai. Numbers ka matlab andhera hai.
        Do alag cheezein.
```

### Teen aur, jo kam dikhti hain lekin mehngi hain

**Gyaan aur aankh ki seema ko mila dena.** Woh akshar nahi gin pata kyunki use akshar dikhte hi nahi. Woh aapke company ke niyam nahi jaanta kyunki woh uske data mein the hi nahi. Dono "nahi kar sakta" jaise dikhte hain aur dono ke ilaaj alag hain. *(5.2)*

**Model aur product ko ek samajhna.** Jo aap istemaal karte ho woh ek poora dher hai. Us dher ka bada hissa model ke bahar hai, aur aksar problem wahin hoti hai. *(2.9)*

**Yeh maan lena ki jo aaj nahi ho sakta woh kabhi nahi hoga, ya jo aaj ho sakta hai woh hamesha rahega.** Aankh ki seemayein tezi se hat rahi hain. Andar ki seemayein lagbhag nahi hilee hain. Farak jaanna hi asli hunar hai. *(5.2)*

### Asli duniya se

Do ulti galtiyan, dono aam, aur dono mehngi.

Ek taraf woh log jo maante hain ki yeh cheez kal sab kuch kar legi. Woh apne systems aise banate hain jaise aaj ki seemayein arzi hain, aur phir woh seemayein unke product mein baithi reh jaati hain.

Doosri taraf woh log jinhone ek baar ek galat jawab dekha aur nateeja nikaal liya ki yeh bekaar hai. Woh do saal peeche reh jaate hain aur unhe pata bhi nahi chalta.

Dono ne ek hi galti ki: **ek ya do ghatnaon se poori tasveer bana li.** Chapter 1.3 wali baat: sambhavna ek baar ke baare mein kuch nahi kehti.

Sahi jagah beech mein hai aur woh asuvidhajanak hai. Yeh cheez kuch kaamon mein kamaal hai, kuch mein bekaar, aur farak pehle se saaf nahi dikhta. Ise naapna padta hai, aur apne kaam pe naapna padta hai.

### Sochne ke liye

**1. (samajh check)** In dus mein se kaunsi galti aap khud karte the?

> **Jawab:** Iska koi sahi jawab nahi hai. Lekin do sabse aam hain, aur dono ke saath aap shayad reh chuke ho.
>
> Aatmvishwas ko sanket samajhna. Woh sabse chupke se nuksaan karti hai, kyunki jab jawab sahi hota hai tab bhi aap galat wajah se bharosa kar rahe hote ho.
>
> Aur yeh sochna ki woh aapse seekh raha hai. Yeh galti aapko har baar dobara wahi cheezein samjhaate rehne pe majboor karti hai, aur uska koi faayda nahi hota.

**2. (samajh check)** Ek dost kehta hai "maine ChatGPT se poocha aur usne galat jawab diya, yeh sab hype hai." Aap kya kahoge?

> **Jawab:** Ki ek ghatna se kuch saabit nahi hota, dono taraf. *(1.3)*
>
> Aur phir do sawal poochunga: kaunsa sawal tha, aur kaise poocha tha?
>
> Agar woh ek khaas, chhota tathya tha, toh galat hona lagbhag pakka tha (5.1). Agar sawal mein apna jawab daala tha, toh model us taraf khinch gaya (5.4).
>
> **Zyadatar "yeh bekaar hai" wale anubhav us kaam se aate hain jismein woh sabse kamzor hai, us tareeke se poocha gaya jo sabse kamzor hai.**

**3. (jodne wala)** Book 1 Ch 0.4 mein tha ki achhi technology gayab ho jaati hai, isliye samajh nahi aati. Kya AI ke saath wahi ho raha hai?

> **Jawab:** Bilkul, aur bahut tezi se.
>
> Aap ek box mein type karte ho aur jawab aa jaata hai. Beech ka sab kuch chhupa hua hai: tokens, sandarbh, temperature, filters, tools.
>
> Aur Book 1 ka wahi sabak yahan bhi lagta hai: **jo chhupa hua hai use aap istemaal kar sakte ho, uske upar kuch bana nahi sakte, aur yeh nahi keh sakte ki uske saath kya mumkin hai.**
>
> Yeh das galtiyan asal mein ek hi galti ke das roop hain: **cheez ko bahar se dekh kar andar ke baare mein nateeja nikaal lena.**
