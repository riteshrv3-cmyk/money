# Chapter 7.2  [SPINE]
## Ek aadmi 2026 mein kya bana sakta hai

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Book 1 Chapter 8.2 mein chaar pehchaanein thin: kya banana aasan hai aur kya mushkil. Unhe bolo.

**Kabir:** Sahi jawab saaf hai ya dhundhla. Waqt ki sakhti. Paisa ya kanoon juda hai ya nahi. Aur naya kaam ya purani cheez chhoona.

**Madhav:** Ab batao ki AI ne inmein se kaunsi badli.

**Kabir:** Shayad pehli? Ab sahi jawab dhundhla ho toh bhi kaam ho sakta hai?

**Madhav:** Kya AI ka jawab bharosemand hai jab sahi jawab dhundhla ho?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Wahin toh woh sabse zyada bhatakta hai.

**Madhav:** Toh pehli pehchaan badli?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Woh waisi ki waisi hai.

**Madhav:** Doosri? Waqt ki sakhti?

**Kabir:** Nahi. AI dheema hai, tez nahi.

**Madhav:** Teesri? Paisa aur kanoon?

**Kabir:** Aur mushkil ho gayi, kyunki ab ek aur cheez hai jo galti kar sakti hai.

**Madhav:** Chauthi? Purani cheez chhoona?

**Kabir:** Woh shayad thodi aasan hui. AI purana code padh kar samjha deta hai.

**Madhav:** Thodi. Toh chaaron mein se koi bhi poori tarah nahi badli. Phir badla kya?

**Kabir:** Likhne ki speed.

**Madhav:** Aur woh kitna hissa tha?

**Kabir:** Pata nahi. Aadha?

**Madhav:** Ek naye product mein likhna shayad chauthai hai. Baaki kya hai?

**Kabir:** Tay karna ki kya banana hai. Design. Jaanch. Chalana. Logon tak pahunchana.

**Madhav:** Toh agar likhna do guna tez ho gaya, toh poora kaam kitna tez hua?

**Kabir:** Bahut kam. Shayad ek chauthai.

**Madhav:** Toh phir log kyun kehte hain ki sab kuch badal gaya?

**Kabir:** Kyunki shuruaat bahut tez ho gayi. Pehla chalta hua roop ab ek din mein ban jaata hai.

**Madhav:** Aur aage ka kaam?

**Kabir:** Woh utna hi hai.

**Madhav:** Bas. **Shuruaat sasti hui hai, poora karna nahi.**

### Naam

Chaar pehchaanein, aur AI ka unpe asar:

```
1.  SAHI JAWAB SAAF HAI YA NAHI      lagbhag nahi badla
    dhundhla jawab wahin hai jahan AI sabse kamzor hai

2.  WAQT KI SAKHTI                    nahi badla
    AI dheema hai

3.  PAISA YA KANOON                   thoda mushkil hua
    ek aur cheez jo galti kar sakti hai, aur jiski
    galti samjhayi nahi ja sakti (Ch 2.1)

4.  NAYA YA PURANA                    thoda aasan hua
    purana code padhna aur samajhna sasta ho gaya
```

Aur jo sach mein badla:

```
LIKHNA           bahut sasta          ← sabse bada badlav
PADHNA/SAMAJHNA  bahut sasta          ← kam charcha, bada asar
PEHLA ROOP       ghanton mein          ← isliye lagta hai sab badal gaya

DESIGN           utna hi
JAANCH           utni hi (aur zyada zaroori, Ch 5.4)
CHALANA          utna hi (poori Book 1)
LOGON TAK        utna hi (Book 1, Ch 8.5)
```

Ab do baatein jo iss chapter ki jaan hain.

**Ek: ek aadmi ab wahi bana sakta hai jo pehle chhoti team banati thi, agar kaam pehli pehchaan mein aasan ho.**

Yeh sach mein bada badlav hai. Woh cheezein jo pehle "iske liye do log chahiye" thin, ab ek aadmi kar leta hai.

Aur woh pehli pehchaan wahi rehti hai: **jinka sahi jawab saaf hai.**

**Do: aur wahi baat aapke mukable ke liye bhi sach hai.**

Yeh Chapter 7.1 ka wahi sabak hai. Jo aapke liye sasta hua, woh sabke liye sasta hua.

Toh "main isse jaldi bana sakta hoon" ab koi bachaav nahi hai. Woh do saal pehle ek bachaav tha.

Jo bachaav hai woh wahi hai jo pehle bhi tha, aur ab pehle se zyada:

```
kya aapke paas aisa data hai jo kisi aur ke paas nahi?
kya aap us problem ko jaante ho jise koi aur nahi jaanta?
kya log aapke paas hain? (Book 1, Ch 4.1)
kya aapka kaam jama hota hai, ya har baar khatam?
```

**Banane ki kshamta sasti hui. Jaanna ki kya banana hai, mehngi hi rahi.**

### Asli duniya se

Ek jaani-pehchani cheez baar baar dikhi hai: koi ek aadmi ek hafte mein ek product banata hai, uska demo bahut achha hota hai, aur woh product kabhi asli istemaal tak nahi pahunchta.

Aur uski wajah lagbhag hamesha wahi teen hoti hain: jaanch nahi thi, chalana mushkil nikla, ya logon tak pahunchne ka koi rasta nahi tha.

Yaani woh teen cheezein jo AI ne nahi badli.

Aur ulta bhi hua hai: log jo pehle se kisi problem ko gehrai se jaante the, unhone AI ke saath woh cheezein banayi jo pehle unke bas ke bahar thin.

**Farak yeh nahi tha ki kaun AI istemaal kar raha tha. Farak yeh tha ki kaun jaanta tha ki kya banana hai.**

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **shuruaat ki speed ko poore kaam ki speed samajhna.**

Pehla chalta hua roop ab bahut jaldi ban jaata hai, aur woh chaunkane wala hai. Aur uske baad ka kaam lagbhag utna hi hai.

Iska seedha nateeja: log aadhe-adhoore products se bhar jaate hain, aur unmein se kuch bhi poora nahi hota.

Doosri galti: **yeh sochna ki AI ne "coding seekhna" bekaar kar diya.**

Book 1 Chapter 7.1 se: aapko yeh jaanna padta hai ki jo bana woh sahi hai ya nahi. Aur AI apni galti khud nahi pehchan sakta (Ch 5.2).

Toh jaanne ki zaroorat kam nahi hui. Woh khisak gayi: **likhne se jaanchne ki taraf.**

Teesri galti: **yeh maan lena ki jo aap bana sakte ho woh koi aur nahi bana sakta.**

Do saal pehle "main isse bana sakta hoon" ek bachaav tha. Ab woh nahi hai.

### Sochne ke liye

**1. (samajh check)** Aapko ek idea aaya aur aapne ek hafte mein uska chalta hua roop bana liya. Ab sabse bada khatra kya hai?

> **Jawab:** Yeh maan lena ki mushkil hissa ho gaya.
>
> Jo hua woh sabse aasan hissa tha, aur wahi hissa sabse zyada sasta hua hai.
>
> Jo bacha hai: kya woh sach mein kisi ki problem hal karta hai, kya woh chalta rahega, kya log use dhoondh payenge, aur kya koi aur do din mein wahi nahi bana lega.
>
> **Book 1 Ch 8.5 ka wahi sawal: jeet cheez ki quality se nahi aati.**

**2. (samajh check)** Do log ek hi din ek hi tarah ka AI product banate hain. Ek chal jaata hai, doosra nahi. Sabse sambhavit wajah?

> **Jawab:** Ek jaanta tha ki kiski problem hal kar raha hai, doosra nahi.
>
> Jab banane ka daam gir jaata hai, toh farak banane mein nahi rehta. Woh us jaankari mein reh jaata hai jo banane se pehle thi.
>
> Aur woh jaankari kahin muft nahi milti. Woh us kaam ke andar rehne se aati hai.
>
> **Isliye AI ne un logon ko sabse zyada faayda diya jo pehle se kisi problem ke andar the**, na ki un logon ko jo ek tool dhoondh rahe the.

**3. (jodne wala)** Book 1 Ch 0.2 ke teen dials pe yeh sab lagao.

> **Jawab:**
>
> **Size:** nahi badla. Problem kitni badi hai, yeh AI se tay nahi hota.
>
> **Scarcity:** yahan sabse bada badlav hua, aur woh khisak gaya. "Code likh sakta hoon" ki scarcity gir gayi. "Jaanta hoon ki kya banana hai" aur "jaanch sakta hoon ki yeh sahi hai" ki scarcity badh gayi.
>
> **Leverage:** aasan hua. Ek aadmi ab woh cheez bana sakta hai jo uske bina chalti rahe.
>
> **Toh formula wahi hai. Bas ek dial ka matlab badal gaya.** Aur jo log purane matlab pe khade hain, unke paanv ke neeche se zameen khisak rahi hai.
