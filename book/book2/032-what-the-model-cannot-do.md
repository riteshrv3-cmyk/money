# Chapter 5.2  [SPINE]
## Model kya nahi kar sakta

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Ab ek list banate hain. Woh kya nahi kar sakta? Lekin har ek ke liye wajah batao, sirf naam nahi.

**Kabir:** Woh gin nahi sakta.

**Madhav:** Wajah?

**Kabir:** Use akshar dikhte hi nahi (Ch 1.5). Aur ginna ek kaam hai, likhawat nahi (Ch 1.4).

**Madhav:** Kya woh hamesha nahi gin sakta?

**Kabir:** Agar main use akshar alag alag karke doon toh gin leta hai.

**Madhav:** Toh yeh pakki seema hai ya aankh ki seema?

**Kabir:** Aankh ki. Woh hataayi ja sakti hai.

**Madhav:** Achha. Agla.

**Kabir:** Bade numbers ka hisaab.

**Madhav:** Wajah?

**Kabir:** Woh hisaab kar nahi raha. Woh yeh chun raha hai ki aise sawal ke baad kaunsa number aksar aata hai.

**Madhav:** Aur agar main use calculator de doon?

**Kabir:** Toh woh hisaab calculator se karwa lega.

**Madhav:** Toh yeh bhi pakki seema nahi hai. Agla.

**Kabir:** Woh nahi jaanta ki use kya nahi pata.

**Madhav:** Wajah?

**Kabir:** Uske andar khaali jagah nahi dikhti (Ch 2.3, 5.1).

**Madhav:** Kya yeh tool se hat sakta hai?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Yeh andar ki baat hai.

**Madhav:** Bas. Yeh pehli asli seema hai. Aage.

**Kabir:** Woh apni galti nahi pehchan sakta.

**Madhav:** Kabhi kabhi toh pehchan leta hai. Aap bolte ho "yeh galat hai" aur woh maan leta hai.

**Kabir:** Lekin woh tab bhi maan leta hai jab main galat hoon.

**Madhav:** Toh woh kya kar raha hai?

**Kabir:** Woh galti nahi pehchan raha. Woh mere saath sehmat ho raha hai. Chapter 2.8.

**Madhav:** Aur yeh pakki seema hai?

**Kabir:** Kaafi hadd tak, kyunki woh dhalai se aayi hai.

**Madhav:** Ab do aur, jo sabse gehri hain. Woh lamba plan bana kar uspe tik nahi paata. Kyun?

**Kabir:** Kyunki woh ek-ek token karke aage badhta hai. Uske paas koi jagah nahi hai jahan poora plan rakha ho aur woh use dekhta rahe.

**Madhav:** Uska plan kahan hai?

**Kabir:** Usi likhawat mein jo woh ban chuka hai.

**Madhav:** Toh agar woh beech mein bhatak gaya?

**Kabir:** Toh use pata bhi nahi chalega, kyunki ab uska naya "plan" wahi bhatka hua text hai.

**Madhav:** Aur aakhri. Kya woh sach mein nayi cheez soch sakta hai?

**Kabir:** Pata nahi. Woh naye jode banata hai, un cheezon ke jo pehle se hain.

**Madhav:** Kya insaan kuch aur karta hai?

**Kabir:** Yeh mushkil sawal hai.

**Madhav:** Yeh un jagahon mein se ek hai jahan main aapko jawab nahi de sakta, aur jo koi aapko poore vishwas se jawab de, uspe shak karna.

### Naam

Seemayein do kism ki hain, aur farak sabse kaam ki cheez hai:

```
AANKH KI SEEMA (hataayi ja sakti hai)
├── akshar ginna          →  akshar alag karke do
├── bada hisaab           →  calculator do (Ch 6.2)
├── taazi jaankari        →  search do
├── aapka apna data       →  saath bhejo
└── lambe document        →  chun kar bhejo

ANDAR KI SEEMA (nahi hat ti)
├── nahi jaanta ki kya nahi pata
├── apni galti khud nahi pehchan sakta
├── aatmvishwas ka sachai se rishta nahi
├── lambe plan pe khud tik nahi paata
└── uska "irada" nahi hai, sirf likhawat hai
```

Aur yeh farak hi poora Part 6 hai. **Pehli list tools se hat ti hai. Doosri list ke liye insaan chahiye.**

Ab andar wali seemaon mein se ek pe alag se dhyaan dena zaroori hai, kyunki woh sabse mehngi hai:

> **Woh apne kaam ki jaanch khud nahi kar sakta.**

Aap use kaho "check karo," aur woh check karne jaisa text likhega. Woh text bhi usi tareeke se bana hai: sabse sambhavit shabd. Woh ek asli jaanch nahi hai.

Isliye har aisa system jo AI ko AI se jaanchwaata hai, woh ek gehri kamzori pe khada hai. Kuch faayda hota hai, aur woh utna nahi hai jitna lagta hai.

Ek aakhri baat, jo dhyaan dene laayak hai. Naye models jawab dene se pehle apne liye kuch soch kar likhte hain, aur usse mushkil kaam kaafi behtar hote hain.

Woh andar wali seema ko hata nahi raha. Woh use aankh ki seema mein badal raha hai: **jo cheez pehle andar honi thi, woh ab likhawat mein aa gayi, jahan model use dekh sakta hai.**

Kitna badalta hai, ispe abhi bahas hai. Lekin dhaancha wahi purana hai: cheez ko us shakal mein le aao jismein woh dikhe.

### Asli duniya se

2023 se pehle, ek aam sawal tha: "AI se hisaab karwao aur woh galat karta hai."

Aaj zyadatar bade products mein woh problem lagbhag gayab hai. Model ne ganit nahi seekha. Use ek calculator de diya gaya.

Isi tarah taazi jaankari: model ki cutoff wahi hai (Ch 2.6), lekin product use search se jod deta hai.

Yeh yaad rakhne laayak hai: **pichhle do saal ki bahut si "AI behtar ho gaya" wali baatein model ki nahi, uske aas-paas ki parat ki hain.**

Aur isse ek kaam ki aadat nikalti hai: jab bhi koi kahe ki AI ab yeh kar sakta hai, poochna ki **model behtar hua hai, ya use ek tool mil gaya hai?** Dono asli hain, aur unke matlab bilkul alag hain.

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **dono kism ki seemaon ko ek samajhna.**

Log dekhte hain ki AI akshar nahi gin pata aur nateeja nikaalte hain ki woh bewakoof hai. Woh ek aankh ki seema hai, aur woh das second mein hat ti hai.

Aur ulta bhi hota hai: log dekhte hain ki AI ne calculator se hisaab kar liya aur nateeja nikaalte hain ki ab woh sochta hai. Woh bhi galat hai. Uske andar kuch nahi badla.

Doosri galti: **yeh maan lena ki jo seema aaj hai woh hamesha rahegi.**

Aankh ki seemayein tezi se hat rahi hain. Andar ki seemayein lagbhag nahi hilee hain.

Toh agar aap kuch bana rahe ho, toh dhyaan se chuno ki aap kis pe daanv laga rahe ho:

```
aankh ki seema pe daanv    →  woh shayad ek saal mein hat jaayegi
andar ki seema pe daanv    →  woh shayad rahegi
```

### Sochne ke liye

**1. (samajh check)** Aap chahte ho ki AI aapke company ke niyam ke hisaab se jawab de. Yeh kaunsi seema hai, aur ilaaj kya hai?

> **Jawab:** Aankh ki seema. Woh niyam uske data mein the hi nahi.
>
> Ilaaj: woh niyam use bhejo, sawal ke saath.
>
> Aur agar niyam bahut lambe hain, toh pehle chuno ki iss sawal ke liye kaunsa hissa chahiye, phir woh bhejo. Chapter 6.2.
>
> **Model ko sikhaane ki koshish mat karo. Use dikha do.**

**2. (samajh check)** Aap AI se ek jawab lete ho aur phir usi AI se poochte ho "kya yeh sahi hai?" Kitna bharosa karoge?

> **Jawab:** Bahut kam, aur do wajahon se.
>
> **Ek:** woh jaanch nahi kar raha, woh jaanch jaisa text likh raha hai.
>
> **Do:** woh aapse sehmat hone ki taraf dhaala gaya hai (Ch 2.8). Aap poochoge "yeh sahi hai?" toh "haan" ek sambhavit jawab hai.
>
> Thoda faayda hota hai, khaaskar agar aap use asli jaankari saath do aur poochho ki kya jawab usse mel khaata hai.
>
> **Lekin ek cheez khud ko nahi jaanch sakti. Yeh Chapter 5.4 ka poora aadhaar hai.**

**3. (jodne wala)** Chapter 1.5 mein "strawberry" wali problem thi, aur ilaaj tha akshar alag karke dena. Kya wahi soch andar ki seemaon pe bhi lagti hai?

> **Jawab:** Lagti hai, aur woh unhe poori tarah nahi hataati.
>
> "Cheez ko us shakal mein do jismein woh dikhe" har jagah kaam karta hai. Lambe plan ko likhwa lo, taaki woh use dekh sake. Har kadam ko alag karwao, taaki galti chhoti rahe.
>
> Yeh sach mein madad karta hai. Aur woh apni hi likhi cheez ko us tarah se nahi jaanch sakta jaise ek doosra insaan karta.
>
> **Toh dikhana kaafi hai jab problem dekhne ki ho. Jab problem judge karne ki ho, tab dikhane se kaam nahi chalta.**
