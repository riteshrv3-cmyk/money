# Chapter 1.1  [SPINE]
## Enter dabane ke baad kya hota hai

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Aap ek chat box mein sawal likhte ho aur Enter dabate ho. Jawab kahan se aata hai?

**Kabir:** Server se. Mera phone toh jaanta nahi.

**Madhav:** Book 1 ka kaunsa chapter?

**Kabir:** 4.2. Mera phone bahar se pahuncha nahi ja sakta, isliye beech mein hamesha koi server hota hai.

**Madhav:** Toh aapka sawal packet mein tootta hai, taale mein band hota hai, bees ajnabi machinon se guzarta hai, aur kisi building mein pahunchta hai. Wahan tak sab kuch Book 1 hai. Ab batao, wahan aisa kya hai jo Book 1 mein nahi tha?

**Kabir:** Pata nahi. Ek program hoga.

**Madhav:** Program toh hai. Lekin usmein ek file bhi hai. Aur woh file bahut badi hai.

**Kabir:** Kitni?

**Madhav:** Kuch sau GB. Aur usmein sirf numbers hain.

**Kabir:** Book 1 mein toh sab kuch numbers hi tha.

**Madhav:** Sahi. Toh farak kya hai? Ek photo bhi numbers hai, ek program bhi numbers hai. Yeh teesri cheez kya hai?

**Kabir:** Pata nahi.

**Madhav:** Sochte hain. Ek photo ke numbers kisne tay kiye?

**Kabir:** Camera ne. Jo saamne tha uske hisaab se.

**Madhav:** Ek program ke numbers kisne tay kiye?

**Kabir:** Kisi insaan ne likha, aur translator ne numbers banaye.

**Madhav:** Aur iss file ke numbers kisne tay kiye?

**Kabir:** Bhi kisi insaan ne?

**Madhav:** Nahi. Aur yahi poori kitaab ka sabse zaroori jawab hai. **Yeh numbers kisi ne likhe hi nahi.** Woh apne aap bane.

**Kabir:** Apne aap ka matlab?

**Madhav:** Ek tareeka chalaya gaya, bahut saare examples pe, aur us tareeke ke baad yeh numbers bache. Koi insaan nahi bata sakta ki inmein se kisi ek number ka kya matlab hai.

**Kabir:** Toh woh kaam kaise karte hain?

**Madhav:** Woh poora Part 2 hai. Abhi sirf itna pakdo ki teesri tarah ka number aa gaya hai:

**Kabir:** Photo wale numbers duniya se aaye. Program wale numbers insaan se aaye. Aur yeh... process se aaye.

**Madhav:** Bas. Ab agla sawal. Aapka sawal us machine tak pahuncha. Machine ne file kholi. Aur phir?

**Kabir:** Woh sochti hai?

**Madhav:** Yeh sabse aam jawab hai aur woh galat hai. Agla chapter usi pe hai. Abhi ek aur cheez dekho. Jawab aapki screen pe kaise aata hai, ek saath ya thoda thoda?

**Kabir:** Thoda thoda. Shabd ek ek karke aate hain.

**Madhav:** Kya woh dikhane ka andaaz hai, ya sach mein waise hi ban raha hai?

**Kabir:** Pata nahi. Purane apps mein toh woh dikhawa hota tha.

**Madhav:** Yahan woh sach hai. Woh sach mein ek waqt mein ek tukda banata hai, aur har tukda bante hi bhej diya jaata hai.

**Kabir:** Toh woh poora jawab pehle se nahi jaanta?

**Madhav:** Nahi. Jab woh pehla shabd bhej raha hota hai, tab use dusva shabd pata nahi hota.

**Kabir:** Yeh ajeeb hai.

**Madhav:** Yeh sabse zaroori baat hai jo iss poori kitaab mein hai. Ise yaad rakhna.

### Naam

Woh badi file jismein numbers hain, use **model** kehte hain.

Teen tarah ke numbers, ab tak:

```
photo ke numbers      duniya se aaye     (camera ne naapa)
program ke numbers    insaan se aaye     (kisi ne likha)
model ke numbers      process se aaye    (kisi ne nahi likha)
```

Teesri tarah nayi hai, aur woh poori Book 2 ki jad hai.

Aur woh doosri baat, jo abhi chhoti lagti hai:

> **Model ek waqt mein ek tukda banata hai, aur agla tukda banane se pehle use pichhla dikhta hai.**

Iss ek line se aage bahut kuch nikalta hai: woh bhool kyun jaata hai, woh cheezein bana kyun leta hai, uska kharcha kaise nikalta hai, aur pehla shabd baaki se dheema kyun hota hai.

### Asli duniya se

Jab aap ChatGPT ya Claude mein type karte ho, aapka sawal aksar doosre desh mein ek building tak jaata hai, jahan hazaaron khaas chips lagi hain. Wahan woh model file pehle se khuli padi hai, RAM mein, chalne ke liye taiyaar.

Woh file har sawal ke liye dobara nahi khulti. Woh ek baar khulti hai aur mahinon khuli rehti hai, kyunki use kholna hi mehnga hai. Book 1 Chapter 2.4 wali baat: chalne ke liye cheez ko tez memory mein aana padta hai.

Aur isiliye aap uska istemaal kirye pe karte ho, khareed kar nahi. Ek insaan ke liye woh machinein rakhna bemaani hai jab woh din mein bees sawal poochta hai.

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **yeh sochna ki AI aapke phone ke andar hai.**

Zyadatar mamlon mein woh nahi hai. Aapka phone ek khidki hai. Sawal bahar jaata hai, jawab bahar se aata hai.

Iska seedha nateeja: log poochte hain ki AI bina internet ke kyun nahi chalta, aur woh yeh nahi samajh paate ki jo woh type karte hain woh kisi aur ki machine pe jaata hai. Doosri baat ka matlab Chapter 6.6 mein khulega.

Chhote model phone pe bhi chal sakte hain, aur woh badh rahe hain. Lekin jo sabse bade aur sabse kaabil hain, woh aaj bhi kisi building mein hain, aur uski wajah seedhi hai: unhe kuch sau GB RAM chahiye, aur aapke phone mein aath hai.

### Sochne ke liye

**1. (samajh check)** Model ek file hai. Aapke phone mein 256 GB hain aur model 400 GB ka hai. Aur agar woh 100 GB ka hota?

> **Jawab:** Rakhne mein aa jaata. Chalne mein phir bhi nahi.
>
> Chalne ke liye use RAM mein aana padta hai, aur phone mein 8 se 16 GB RAM hai. *(Book 1, Ch 2.4)*
>
> **Rakhna aur chalana do alag sawal hain.** Log inhe hamesha mila dete hain. Isiliye "model phone mein aa jaayega" ka matlab lagbhag hamesha yeh hota hai ki koi bahut chhota model banaya gaya hai, na ki bada model kisi tarah ghus gaya.

**2. (samajh check)** Jawab shabd ke shabd aata hai, aur model ko agla shabd pehle se pata nahi hota. Isse kya nikalta hai jo aapne khud dekha ho?

> **Jawab:** Kai cheezein, aur aap sab dekh chuke ho.
>
> Woh beech mein rok diya ja sakta hai, aur jo bana hai woh bana rehta hai.
>
> Lamba jawab chhote se zyada waqt leta hai, seedha anupaat mein, kyunki har tukda alag se banta hai.
>
> Aur woh apni hi shuruaat mein phans sakta hai. Agar usne pehla vaakya galat disha mein shuru kar diya, toh baaki poora usi disha mein banta chala jaata hai, kyunki har agla tukda pichhle ko dekh kar banta hai.

**3. (jodne wala)** Book 1 Ch 4.2 kehta tha ki beech mein baithne wala sab dekhta hai. Yahan uska kya matlab hai?

> **Jawab:** Ki jo bhi aap us box mein likhte ho, woh kisi doosri company ki machine pe pahunchta hai aur wahan padha ja sakta hai.
>
> Taala (Ch 4.6) sirf raaste mein bachata hai. Jo doosre sire pe baitha hai, uske liye woh khula hai.
>
> Yeh koi ilzaam nahi hai, yeh dhaancha hai. Kya rakha jaata hai, kitni der, aur kaun dekh sakta hai, yeh Chapter 6.6 ka poora vishay hai.
