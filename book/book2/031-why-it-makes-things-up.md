# Chapter 5.1  [SPINE]
## Yeh cheezein bana kyun leta hai

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Aap ek kitaab ke baare mein poochte ho jo hai hi nahi. Woh uska poora saaransh bata deta hai, lekhak ke naam ke saath. Kya hua?

**Kabir:** Usne jhooth bola.

**Madhav:** Jhooth bolne ke liye kya chahiye?

**Kabir:** Sach pata ho, aur phir kuch aur kaho.

**Madhav:** Kya use pata tha ki woh kitaab nahi hai?

**Kabir:** Shayad nahi.

**Madhav:** Toh woh jhooth nahi hai. Ab poori wajah nikalte hain, tukdon mein. Uska kaam kya hai?

**Kabir:** Agla token chunna, jo sabse sambhavit ho.

**Madhav:** Aapne poocha "iss kitaab ka saaransh do." Ab aise sawal ke baad, likhawat mein, aksar kya aata hai?

**Kabir:** Ek saaransh.

**Madhav:** Toh sabse sambhavit agla token kya hai?

**Kabir:** Saaransh ki shuruaat.

**Madhav:** Kya "yeh kitaab maujood nahi hai" sambhavit hai?

**Kabir:** Kam. Log aisa likhte hi nahi hain aksar.

**Madhav:** Toh woh saaransh shuru kar deta hai. Aur ek baar shuru ho gaya toh?

**Kabir:** Har agla shabd pichhle ko dekh kar aayega. Woh us saaransh ko poora karta jaayega.

**Madhav:** Toh galti kahan hui?

**Kabir:** Pehle token pe. Aur uske baad sab kuch usi ke upar khada ho gaya.

**Madhav:** Ab doosri wajah. Chapter 2.3 se: model mein us kitaab ka data tha?

**Kabir:** Nahi. Woh kitaab hai hi nahi.

**Madhav:** Toh us jagah pe uske andar kya hai?

**Kabir:** Kuch nahi. Ya milti-julti kitaabon ka nichod.

**Madhav:** Aur woh nichod kaisa dikhta hai, jab woh use padta hai?

**Kabir:** Shayad utna hi thos jitna asli gyaan.

**Madhav:** Bas. **Uske andar "mujhe pata hai" aur "mujhe nahi pata" ke liye do alag jagahein nahi hain.** Dono ek jaise lagte hain.

**Kabir:** Toh woh yeh keh hi nahi sakta ki use nahi pata.

**Madhav:** Kabhi keh deta hai, aur woh bhi ek seekhi hui aadat hai, na ki koi asli jaanch. Ab teesri wajah. Chapter 2.8 se: use kis cheez ke liye dhaala gaya tha?

**Kabir:** Insaan ki pasand ke liye.

**Madhav:** Aur insaan ko kaunsa jawab zyada pasand aata hai, "mujhe nahi pata" ya ek poora, aatmvishwas bhara saaransh?

**Kabir:** Doosra.

**Madhav:** Toh humne use iss taraf dhakela hai.

**Kabir:** Toh yeh bug nahi hai.

**Madhav:** Yeh teen alag cheezon ka seedha nateeja hai, aur teeno humne khud banayi hain.

### Naam

Ise **hallucination** kehte hain, aur naam thoda bhraamak hai. Woh koi bimari nahi hai. Woh design ka nateeja hai.

Teen wajahein, aur teeno alag hain:

```
1.  USKA KAAM HI YEH HAI
    har haal mein agla sambhavit token dena
    "kuch nahi" uska jawab ho hi nahi sakta

2.  USMEIN KHAALI JAGAH NAHI DIKHTI
    jo bacha nahi, uski jagah khaali nahi hoti (Ch 2.3)
    milta-julta nichod utna hi thos lagta hai jitna asli gyaan

3.  AATMVISHWAS KE LIYE DHAALA GAYA
    insaanon ne bhare andaz wale jawab chune (Ch 2.8)
    "nahi pata" ko kam pasand kiya gaya
```

Aur ek chauthi baat jo iss sab ko badtar banati hai: **ek baar shuru ho gaya toh woh aage badhta hai.** Har agla token pichhle ko dekh kar aata hai (Ch 1.1), toh ek galat shuruaat ek poore galat paragraph mein badal jaati hai.

Ab do baatein jo iss chapter ki jaan hain.

**Ek: yeh hataya nahi ja sakta.**

Woh kam kiya ja sakta hai, kaafi kam. Woh khatam nahi ho sakta, kyunki woh us tareeke ke andar hai jo Chapter 1.3 mein tha: hamesha sabse sambhavit chuno, aur kabhi kabhi woh galat hoga.

Jo koi kahe ki unhone ise "hal" kar diya hai, woh ya toh kuch aur keh raha hai ya galat hai.

**Do: yeh sabse zyada wahan hota hai jahan sabse kam dikhta hai.**

```
sabse kam    aam, baar baar likhi gayi baatein
             (data mein hazaaron baar thin)

sabse zyada  khaas naam, tarikhein, numbers, hawaale,
             chhote ilaake ki baatein, naye events
             (data mein ek ya do baar thin, ya bilkul nahi)
```

Aur wahi cheezein aksar sabse zyada bharose laayak dikhti hain, kyunki woh thos hoti hain. **Ek hawala jhooth nahi lagta. Woh ek hawala lagta hai.**

### Asli duniya se

2023 mein ek vakeel ne adaalat mein ek kaagaz jama kiya jismein purane mukadmon ke hawaale the. Woh hawaale AI se aaye the.

Un mukadmon mein se kai maujood hi nahi the. Naam asli lagte the, number sahi shakal ke the, adaalat ke naam sahi the. Sab kuch waisa hi dikhta tha jaisa ek asli hawala dikhta hai.

Woh isliye asli lagte the ki model ne yahi seekha tha: **hawale kaise dikhte hain.** Usne yeh nahi seekha tha ki kaunse hawale maujood hain.

Aur yeh iss poore chapter ka sabse saaf udaharan hai. Model ne apna kaam theek kiya. Usne aisi cheez banayi jo us jagah pe sambhavit thi. Bas woh sach nahi thi, aur sach naapa hi nahi gaya tha (Ch 2.4).

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti: **hallucination ko ek bug samajhna jise theek kiya jaayega.**

Log intezaar karte hain ki agla model ise hata dega. Woh use kam karega. Woh use khatam nahi karega, kyunki woh us tareeke ka hissa hai.

Iska seedha nateeja: log apne systems aise banate hain jaise yeh problem kal khatam ho jaayegi. Woh nahi hogi. Use maan kar banana padega.

Doosri galti: **yeh sochna ki woh sirf mushkil sawalon pe hota hai.**

Ulta. Woh mushkil sawal ko mushkil maan sakta hai. Woh ek chhote, saadhaaran, khaas sawal pe pura aatmvishwas se galat ho jaata hai, kyunki us baat ka data hi kam tha.

**Model ki kamzori kaam ki mushkil se nahi, data ki kami se aati hai.** Yeh Chapter 2.6 wali baat hai aur woh yahan sabse zyada kaam ki hai.

Teesri galti: **usse poochna ki "kya tumhe pakka pata hai?"**

Woh us sawal ka bhi wahi kaam karega: sambhavit jawab dena. Aur "haan, mujhe pakka pata hai" ek sambhavit jawab hai.

Uska aatmvishwas jaanch nahi hai. Woh sirf aur likhawat hai.

### Sochne ke liye

**1. (samajh check)** Aap do sawal poochte ho. "Newton ka doosra niyam kya hai?" Aur "mere sheher ke us chhote college ki 2019 ki fees kya thi?" Kis mein hallucination ka khatra zyada hai, aur kyun?

> **Jawab:** Doosre mein, bahut zyada.
>
> Newton ka niyam data mein lakhon baar aaya. Woh mazbooti se bacha hai.
>
> Us college ki 2019 ki fees shayad ek do baar aayi ho, ya bilkul nahi. Us jagah pe kuch bacha hi nahi.
>
> Aur woh chup nahi reh sakta. Toh woh ek aisa number likhega jo aisi jagah pe sambhavit hai, aur woh number bilkul asli lagega.
>
> **Sawal jitna khaas aur chhota, khatra utna zyada.** Yeh ulta lagta hai aur yeh sabse kaam ki baat hai.

**2. (samajh check)** Ek AI product kehta hai "hamne hallucination 90 percent kam kar diya." Kya woh sach ho sakta hai?

> **Jawab:** Haan, aur woh shayad model ka kaam nahi hai.
>
> Aisa aksar model ke bahar wali parat se hota hai: jawab dene se pehle asli source dhoondho aur woh saath bhejo (Chapter 6.2), aur jawab ko us source ke saath jaancho.
>
> Yaani unhone model ko "sach" nahi sikhaya. Unhone use har baar sach saamne rakh diya.
>
> **Aur wahi lagbhag har asli hal hai.** Model ki yaaddasht se nahi, saamne rakhi jaankari se.

**3. (jodne wala)** Chapter 2.4 mein tha ki "jo aap naapte ho wahi milta hai." Hallucination us baat ka kaunsa roop hai?

> **Jawab:** Woh us baat ka sabse seedha nateeja hai.
>
> Training mein naapa gaya: agla token kitna sambhavit hai. Sach kabhi naapa hi nahi gaya.
>
> Dhalai mein naapa gaya: insaan ko kaunsa jawab pasand aaya. Sach phir bhi naapa nahi gaya.
>
> Toh model us cheez mein kamaal hai jo naapi gayi, aur uska sach ke saath rishta sirf ittefaqan hai: sach aksar sambhavit hota hai, isliye woh aksar sahi hota hai.
>
> **Jahan sach aur sambhavna alag ho jaate hain, wahan model sambhavna ke saath jaayega. Har baar.**
