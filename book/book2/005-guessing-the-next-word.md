# Chapter 1.4  [SPINE]
## Agla shabd guess karna

### Samvaad

**Madhav:** Ek vaakya poora karo. "Main baazar ja raha hoon, doodh aur..."

**Kabir:** Bread.

**Madhav:** Aapne kaise chuna?

**Kabir:** Bas aa gaya.

**Madhav:** Kyun aaya? "Doodh aur haathi" kyun nahi aaya?

**Kabir:** Kyunki woh bewakoofi hai.

**Madhav:** Aapko kaise pata?

**Kabir:** Kyunki maine hazaaron baar aisi baatein suni hain. Doodh ke saath bread aata hai, haathi nahi.

**Madhav:** Toh aapne kya kiya, us pal mein?

**Kabir:** Maine woh chuna jo iss jagah pe sabse zyada baar aata hai.

**Madhav:** Bas. Ab ek aur. "Bharat ki rajdhani hai..."

**Kabir:** Dilli.

**Madhav:** Kya aapne yeh soch kar nikaala, ya yaad se?

**Kabir:** Yaad se.

**Madhav:** Ab dhyaan do. Dono baar aapne wahi kaam kiya: jo aksar aata hai woh chuna. Ek baar woh bhasha ki aadat thi, ek baar woh gyaan tha.

**Kabir:** Lekin dono mera ek hi kaam tha.

**Madhav:** Bahar se ek hi. Aur ab yeh dekho: agar koi cheez sirf "agla shabd chunna" kar sake, aur bahut achhe se kar sake, toh usse kya kya nikal aayega?

**Kabir:** Vaakya toh sahi banenge.

**Madhav:** Aur "Bharat ki rajdhani hai" ka jawab?

**Kabir:** Woh bhi. Yaani gyaan bhi aa jaayega.

**Madhav:** Aur "2 + 2 hai"?

**Kabir:** Woh bhi, agar woh hazaaron baar likha dekha ho.

**Madhav:** Aur "yeh code galat kyun hai" ka jawab?

**Kabir:** Agar hazaaron baar aise sawal aur jawab likhe gaye hon, toh shayad woh bhi.

**Madhav:** Toh ab ek ajeeb baat saamne aa rahi hai. Aisa kya hai jo insaan ne kabhi likha hi na ho?

**Kabir:** Bahut kam.

**Madhav:** Isliye ek cheez jo sirf agla shabd chunti hai, woh bahut saare kaam kar leti hai. Woh un kaamon ko karna nahi seekhi. Woh un kaamon ke baare mein likhe gaye shabd seekhi.

**Kabir:** Yeh dono ek jaise lagte hain.

**Madhav:** Kabhi kabhi woh ek jaise hote hain aur kabhi bilkul nahi. Ek sawal se farak dikha deta hoon. Agar main aapse poochun "iss vaakya mein kitne akshar hain," toh aap kya karoge?

**Kabir:** Ginunga.

**Madhav:** Aur agar aap sirf agla shabd chun sakte ho, gin nahi sakte?

**Kabir:** Toh main woh number likhunga jo aisi jagah pe aksar likha jaata hai.

**Madhav:** Aur woh sahi hoga?

**Kabir:** Shayad nahi.

**Madhav:** Yeh Chapter 5.2 ka aadha hissa hai. Abhi bas itna dekho ki **jo kaam likhawat mein aksar dikhta hai woh aasan hai, aur jo kaam sach mein karna padta hai woh mushkil hai**, chahe insaan ke liye doosra wala aasan ho.

### Naam

Woh poora tareeka aisa hai:

```
1.  ab tak ka saara text dekho
2.  har mumkin agle tukde ke liye ek sambhavna nikalo
3.  unmein se ek chuno
4.  use text ke aakhir mein jodo
5.  wapas 1 pe
```

Bas. Iske alaawa kuch nahi hai. Poori cheez yahi hai.

Do baatein jo isse seedhi nikalti hain:

**Ek: gyaan aur bhasha alag nahi rakhi gayi.** "Dilli" wahi tarah se aata hai jaise "bread" aata hai. Model ke andar ek jagah nahi hai jahan sach rakha ho aur doosri jagah jahan vyakaran ho. Sab ek hi cheez hai.

Isliye woh sach aur achhe lagne wale jhooth mein farak nahi kar sakta, kyunki uske liye dono ek hi kism ke hain. Chapter 5.1.

**Do: jo cheez likhawat mein aksar dikhti hai woh aasan hai.**

```
AASAN                          MUSHKIL
kisi cheez ko samjhana         akshar ginna
code likhna jaisa aksar likha  bade numbers ka hisaab
kisi shaili mein likhna        chaalis kadam ka plan
kisi baat ka saaransh          apni hi galti pehchanna
```

Daayan column ka har kaam insaan ke liye aasan hai aur iss tareeke ke liye mushkil. Woh isliye mushkil hai ki uske liye kuch **karna** padta hai, aur yeh cheez karti nahi, woh likhti hai.

Aur ab woh baat jo pichhle chapter se judti hai. Har kadam pe woh sabse zyada sambhavna wala tukda chunta hai, ya uske aas-paas ka. Toh:

> **Woh aksar sahi hoga, kyunki sabse sambhavit jawab aksar sahi hota hai.**
> **Aur woh kabhi kabhi galat hoga, aur woh galti hataayi nahi ja sakti.**

### Asli duniya se

Aapka phone jab typing mein agla shabd sujhata hai, woh bilkul yehi kaam kar raha hai, sirf bahut chhote paimane pe. Woh pichhle kuch shabd dekhta hai aur agla sujhata hai.

Farak sirf paimane ka hai, tareeke ka nahi.

Phone wala shayad pichhle do ya teen shabd dekhta hai. Ek bada model pichhle hazaaron shabd dekhta hai, aur uske andar arbon numbers hain jo yeh tay karte hain ki kya aata hai.

Woh farak itna bada hai ki nateeja alag kism ka lagne lagta hai. Lekin agar aap poochho ki woh kya kar raha hai, jawab wahi hai jo aapke keyboard ka hai.

Yeh Book 1 Chapter 2.2 wali baat hai: **paimana kaafi badal jaaye toh woh apne aap mein ek nayi cheez ban jaata hai.**

### Yahan log kya galat samajhte hain

Sabse aam galti, aur yeh do ulti shakalon mein aati hai.

**Pehli: "yeh sirf agla shabd guess karta hai, isliye yeh kuch samajhta nahi."**

Yeh vaakya sach hai aur uska nateeja galat hai. Woh sach mein agla shabd chunta hai. Aur woh sach mein aise kaam kar leta hai jo pehle sirf insaan kar sakte the.

"Sirf" wala shabd hi galti hai. Agla shabd theek se chunne ke liye bahut kuch chahiye: vyakaran, tathya, tark ka dhaancha, sandarbh, aur woh sab jo shabdon mein chhupa hota hai.

**Doosri: "yeh sab kuch samajhta hai."**

Yeh bhi galat hai, aur uska nateeja mehnga hai. Woh gyaan aur achhe lagne wale jhooth mein farak nahi karta, kyunki uske andar woh farak likha hi nahi hai.

Sahi jagah asuvidhajanak hai: **woh ek kaam kar raha hai jo bahut simple hai, aur us kaam ka nateeja bahut jatil hai.**

### Sochne ke liye

**1. (samajh check)** Aap model se poochte ho ki "strawberry" mein kitne 'r' hain. Woh galat jawab deta hai. Woh yeh kaam kyun nahi kar pata jab woh usse mushkil kaam kar leta hai?

> **Jawab:** Kyunki woh akshar dekhta hi nahi. Yeh agle chapter ka poora vishay hai.
>
> Aur usse pehle ek aur wajah hai jo iss chapter se aati hai: ginna ek **kaam** hai, likhawat nahi. Uske liye ek-ek karke aage badhna padta hai aur ginti yaad rakhni padti hai.
>
> Woh sirf yeh chun raha hai ki aisi jagah pe kaunsa number aksar likha hota hai.
>
> **Jo kaam likhawat mein dikhta hai woh aasan hai. Jo kaam karna padta hai woh mushkil hai.**

**2. (samajh check)** Model ke andar "sach" alag se nahi rakha hai. Isse kya nikalta hai jo aapke liye kaam ka hai?

> **Jawab:** Ki woh aapko yeh nahi bata sakta ki use kis baat ka pakka pata hai aur kis ka nahi.
>
> Ek asli tathya aur ek achha lagne wala jhooth uske liye ek hi kism ke hain. Dono bas aise shabd hain jo us jagah pe theek baithte hain.
>
> Iska seedha nateeja aapke liye: **uska aatmvishwas ek sanket nahi hai.** Woh sahi baat aur galat baat ek hi andaz mein kahega.
>
> Yeh Chapter 5.4 ka poora vishay hai, aur woh iss kitaab ka sabse practical chapter hai.

**3. (jodne wala)** Pichhle chapter mein tha ki sabse achha jawab bhi aksar galat hoga. Yahan uska kya roop hai?

> **Jawab:** Har ek shabd pe woh daanv lag raha hai.
>
> Ek jawab mein paanch sau shabd hain, toh paanch sau chunav hue. Har chunav zyadatar sahi hai. Lekin agar har chunav 99 baar mein 99 sahi ho, tab bhi paanch sau mein se kuch galat honge.
>
> Aur ek galat shabd ke baad, baaki poora jawab usi galat shabd ko dekh kar banta hai. *(Chapter 1.1)*
>
> Isliye chhoti galtiyan aksar chhoti nahi rehti. Woh aage jaakar poora vaakya mod deti hain. Yeh Chapter 5.1 mein wapas aayega.
